## Core model

- Daily bias comes first. Do not drop to the hourly without a one-sided higher-timeframe bias.
- Continuation and reversal are framed mechanically from previous-day high, previous-day low, and wick-to-wick equilibrium.
- Equilibrium divides the range into the half that should hold for continuation:
  - bullish continuation: upper half / EQ respect
  - bearish continuation: lower half / EQ respect

## Candle numbering

- Candle 2 is the swing point.
- Candle 3 is the candle after Candle 2.
- Candle 4 is the continuation candle if the structure is correct.
- In practice, the model often looks for a Candle 2 closure first, but a Candle 3 closure can be ideal when it creates a new protected swing.

## Higher-timeframe POI logic

- Entry work happens inside a higher-timeframe POI, not in random mid-range space.
- The POI is usually a fair value gap, opposing candle, order block, or protected-swing area inside the correct half of the previous day's range.
- Reversal days are framed from the previous-day extreme.
- Continuation days are framed from equilibrium plus the correct half of the range.

## Continuation sequence

- Bias candle closes directionally.
- Mark previous-day high/low and EQ.
- Price should open and first move against the continuation direction:
  - bullish continuation prefers open-low behavior
  - bearish continuation prefers open-high behavior
- Price should respect EQ on the timeframe pair being used.
- Inside the correct half, wait for POI reaction, then Candle 2 / Candle 3 closure or CISD confirmation.
- First target is the previous-day high/low. Extension depends on wick quality.

## Reversal sequence

- Price trades into previous-day high or low.
- Small wick supports immediate expansion.
- Large wick downgrades immediate expansion and often requires waiting for Candle 3 or the next candle.
- Lower-timeframe CISD confirms the wick and allows the trade back into range.
- First target is usually EQ; second target can be the opposite side of the prior range.

## Invalidation and downgrade rules

- If price closes through the expected POI instead of respecting it, the setup is degraded.
- If EQ fails on the wrong side, continuation probability drops sharply.
- If price already expanded before the new session, often wait for the next daily closure instead of forcing a continuation.
- Protected swings stop being trustworthy after liquidity is hit; the next sweep can be the real move.

## What the indicator should teach visually

- PDH / EQ / PDL must be explicit on-chart.
- The active higher-timeframe POI should be visually obvious.
- The chart should show whether a Candle 2 formed inside the POI.
- The chart should show whether a Candle 3 closure confirmed the setup.
- Entry-readiness should not appear before HTF bias, POI interaction, and closure progression are visible.
