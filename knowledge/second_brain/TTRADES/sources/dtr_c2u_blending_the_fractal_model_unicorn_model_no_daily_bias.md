# DTR | C2U - Blending The Fractal Model & Unicorn Model - No Daily Bias

## TL;DR
A "T Talk" guest episode: TTrades introduces (00:11-00:31) the guest "Day Trading Realtor" (DTR), who presents almost all of it. His C2U ("C2 unicorn") model is POI-based and needs no daily bias: an hourly (or HTF) candle 2 forms at a POI, and on the 5m (or the paired LTF) a unicorn (breaker + overlapping FVG) forms inside that C2 wick within 10-12 candles or fewer. He enters during C2 on the unicorn instead of waiting for the C2 closure and a candle 3 entry as in TTFM. Context comes from AM Trades' 7-hour-candle daily profile and ADR. Targets are "low hanging fruit" (2-2.5R as a funded trader). Everything here is the guest's method, not TTrades' teaching; some parts are withheld as paid content (01:40).

## Definitions
- **C2U / C2 unicorn**: guest's model; a TTFM candle 2 at a POI whose wick contains an LTF unicorn (00:50-00:59, 04:35).
- **Unicorn (model)**: ICT pattern; breaker with an FVG, "the most powerful algorithmic reversal pattern" per ICT (03:28); formed when price closes through the breaker (05:28-05:32); learned from Ash Trades / Unicorn University (01:20).
- **Points of interest** (as he uses them): swing highs/lows, opposing range candles, FVGs, previous day/session/week highs/lows (03:58-04:14), hourly IFVG, 50% of the previous 7-hour candle/wick (07:34-08:05), daily C2 T-spot (15:53).
- **7-hour candle daily profile**: AM Trades' expansion-model profile; 7-hour candles, the 1:00-8:00 a.m. candle = London candle (05:55-06:21).
- **London C2 / London reversal**: London 7-hour candle forming a candle 2 at a POI (06:21-06:27).
- **T-spot** (heard "t-spot", "tpot", "te- spot"): TTrades term, here a POI that exists only once C2 has closed; daily C2 T-spot is open-ended until an hourly close past it (11:47, 15:51-16:29). Exact geometry not defined in this video.
- **Low hanging fruit target**: nearest obvious liquidity (Asia high, NY open, London low) (12:23, 14:15).

## Procedure / checklist
1. 7-hour chart: read the daily profile (London C2/reversal? Asia made the high/low?) and HTF FVG / opposing candles (05:55, 09:33).
2. Check ADR (6-month): remaining range vs distance to target; or already exceeded → reversal (06:42, 13:16).
3. Mark hourly swing points reaching into POIs; prefer stacked POIs (03:48, 07:28).
4. Drop to 5m (or paired LTF): during the C2 candle, wait for a unicorn formed in ≤10-12 candles (04:48, 08:08).
5. Enter on the unicorn inside C2 (tag-in), without waiting for C2 closure (08:19).
6. Target low hanging fruit, 2-2.5R; optional trim/trail for more (12:23, 14:41).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Trading unicorns that form slowly over hours or far down the candle body; they are consolidation (05:08-05:39).
- Expecting every trade to run straight to target; some are losses or marathon holds (20:09).
- Copying someone else's entries without knowing why you enter (22:41-22:52).

## Conflicts and open questions
- Directly conflicts with TTFM practice: TTFM waits for a valid C2 closure and enters in candle 3; C2U enters inside C2 on the unicorn (08:19). Guest method, not TTrades' teaching.
- "No daily bias" is the guest's position; TTrades' own videos usually lead with daily bias. The quoted TTrades remark (02:57) is second-hand.
- The 10-12 candle limit is counted on the LTF (5m/1m) from where? Start point of the count (sweep, breaker origin) is not defined.
- Unicorn definition (breaker + FVG overlap rules, which closure confirms) is assumed, not defined here.
- T-spot geometry is not defined; "until we get an hourly closure past this daily t-spot" (16:17) is the only invalidation given.
- ADR lookback "last 6 months" (06:47) for NQ; other examples do not say the lookback.
- Stops are never stated for any example.
