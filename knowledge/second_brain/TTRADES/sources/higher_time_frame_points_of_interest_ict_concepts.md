# Higher Time Frame Points of Interest - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
When to drop to the lower timeframe to look for an entry model: only when price is at a higher-timeframe point of interest, so you are not "searching all the time". HTF is relative to the entry timeframe: he enters on 1m and 15s and watches the 15m, 1h and 4H for POIs. The POIs he lists: highs and lows (equal, session, daily), fair value gaps, order blocks, a fair value gap fill, OTE of a range, and internal range liquidity (he calls it "five things"). Walk-through on one unnamed chart: lows as magnets, entries after highs/lows run, retracements into FVG / OTE / premium of a displacement leg, counter-trend scalps only with a confirmed lower-timeframe model, and internal range liquidity before highs. Companion to "Build An Entry Model".

## Definitions
- **Point of interest (POI)**: HTF level where a lower timeframe entry model is sought: highs/lows, FVG, OB, FVG fill, OTE, internal range liquidity (00:36, 00:52).
- **Internal range liquidity**: liquidity inside the current dealing range (e.g. internal highs) taken before external highs (00:52, 04:47).
- **OTE of a range**: optimal trade entry zone of a displacement leg (02:15, 03:43).
- **Relief bounce**: retrace after an aggressive move (04:17).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Set POI timeframes relative to entry timeframe (1m/15s -> 15m/1h/4H) (00:07).
2. Mark the POI list: highs/lows, FVGs, OBs, FVG fills, OTE of the range, internal range liquidity (00:36, 00:52).
3. Wait until price reaches a POI (00:52).
4. Ask direction: trend continuation or retracement? (02:44)
5. Drop to the lower timeframe and require a confirmed entry model (04:17).
6. Target next liquidity: internal range liquidity before external highs; retracements to OTE (04:47, 05:22).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Searching for entry models all the time instead of at POIs (00:52).
- Entering at a POI without a confirmed lower timeframe model (04:17).
- Treating a counter-trend retracement long as a trend reversal (02:44).
