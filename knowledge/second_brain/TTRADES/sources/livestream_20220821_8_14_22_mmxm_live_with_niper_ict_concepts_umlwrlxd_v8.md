## Q&A
- **Q (10:00):** How do you confirm the original consolidation? **A:** [guest] Look at the Asia session; Asia highs/lows and the previous daily low chopping together is accumulation. It can also be relative equal lows or highs, any area of liquidity.
- **Q (13:30):** How do you know whether the sell side of the curve will take out the original consolidation lows or reverse before reaching them? **A:** [guest] Look from a higher timeframe; after a previous daily/weekly/session high is taken, see whether a market structure break follows. The safe trade is to wait for the OTE FVG retrace and the break of structure: the second distribution leg gives the higher confirmation that the lows will be taken. Protected highs form after the shift and after the BOS.
- **Q (16:10):** Which key levels do you watch for smart money reversals, and how do you validate that one occurred? **A:** [guest] Wait for the reaction after liquidity is taken; not every random high is an SMR. Consider time: entries are within kill zones. The SMR is validated by price breaking swing lows with displacement, then retracing into the OTE FVG and continuing.
- **Q (16:40):** How do you mark out buyside liquidity? **A:** Obvious highs, previous day highs, relative equal highs, double tops; for sellside double bottoms, session lows, previous daily/weekly lows; retail support and resistance are liquidity. TTrades shows obvious lows on ES 1H: look for the most obvious level and don't overthink.
- **Q (21:10):** Which timeframe is most important for the model? **A:** [guest] The model and price are fractal: 1m to daily. He is watching a daily sell model on ES. On higher timeframes (weekly, daily, 4H) expect the buy side of the curve to give two opportunities before reaching the premium array; below that, usually one.
- **Q (22:10):** Do the consolidations on the buy side and sell side of the curve line up at the same levels? **A:** [guest] No, the boxes do not have to line up; it is however the algorithm delivers price.
- **Q (23:20):** Was ICT's YouTube Market Maker series enough to learn? **A:** Mostly yes, plus learning from other people; trading plan development 6 and 7 recommended.
- **Q (25:10):** Where does 'the curve' come from? **A:** [guest] It is just order flow: price moving up in a bullish trend is the buy side of the curve.
- **Q (29:10):** Do you focus on institutional order flow or market structure for higher-timeframe trends? **A:** Neither of them focuses on IOF.
- **Q (29:10):** Why mark the midnight and 8:30 opening prices? **A:** On buy days buy below them, on sell days sell above them. Below both = deep discount, look for buys; above both = deep premium, expect a retrace into discount. Improves risk-to-reward and stops you selling the bottom.
- **Q (29:10):** How much should I risk per trade? **A:** Depends on account and tolerance; not advice; they personally use about 1%.
- **Q (32:30):** Order block or fair value gap for entries? **A:** Both prefer fair value gaps; indices respect FVGs very cleanly. With a large FVG, a breaker can narrow it down.
- **Q (32:30):** Do you do top-down analysis from the monthly? **A:** TTrades: uses daily and weekly a little, most analysis on 1H/15m, sometimes trades a 15-second chart and then does not care about the monthly. [guest] Sniper: daily/4H/1H analysis, entries on 15m/5m/1m.
- **Q (32:30):** Which indicators do you use? **A:** An ATR trailing stop for lower timeframes and an ICT sessions indicator.
- **Q (34:00):** Typical risk management? **A:** Risk the exact same amount every trade and stop when wrong.
- **Q (36:20):** What does your entry checklist look like? **A:** [guest] For a sell: liquidity taken (PDH, PWH) or a higher-timeframe premium FVG tapped; drop to 15m/1H to see a market structure break (no break = still buy side of the curve, no trade); retrace into OTE FVG or order block; enter; target PDL, session lows, relative equal lows or trendline liquidity. Must sell in a premium with clear lows below.

A slight break below followed by an aggressive move back up is a liquidity grab to continue higher. No FVG means no retracement entry.
- **Q (38:20):** How can I trade power of three? **A:** Accumulation, manipulation, distribution: if thinking longs and price drops below the 8:30 and midnight opens, that is the manipulation where you look for entry before range expansion up.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Selling at the bottom / in discount. Fix: Use the midnight and 8:30 opens (premium vs discount) and only sell in premium. (29:10)
- Treating a small wick below a low as a break of structure. Fix: Require displacement that creates a fair value gap; otherwise it is a liquidity grab. (38:20)
- [guest] Calling any random high taken a smart money reversal. Fix: Wait for the reaction (displacement breaking swing lows) after liquidity is taken, within kill zones. (16:10)
- Overtrading on very low timeframes (TTrades admits taking too many trades on the 15-second chart). Fix: Target 2-3 trades a day (TTrades) / one per session with a daily loss cap (guest). (59:00)
- [guest] Not journaling, so mistakes are never learned from. Fix: Record confluence, feelings and outcome for every trade. (55:00)
