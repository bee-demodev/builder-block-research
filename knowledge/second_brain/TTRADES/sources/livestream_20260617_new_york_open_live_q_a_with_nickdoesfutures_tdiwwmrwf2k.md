## Mistakes and warnings
- Looking at charts too much, skipping meals, not leaving the chair all day (likely Nick). Fix: Plan the day first and fit trading into a fixed window (09:30-11:30) with commitments afterwards. (18:50)
- Getting over-conservative (capital preservation) once funded and never reaching payout. Fix: Trade the funded account like the eval minus the risk; care less. (48:30)
- Adding to a position without moving the stop, which raises risk above the initial plan. Fix: Move the stop when adding so total risk stays at the initial risk. (10:50)
- Reading lower-timeframe market structure with no higher-timeframe context. Fix: Get a daily candle 2 closure first and only mark structure in that direction. (1:02:10)
- Overtrading. Fix: Limit yourself to one trade a day. (1:10:10)
- Taking late continuation entries (e.g. a breaker after the move already ran). Fix: Skip them; there is no risk-to-reward left. (48:30)
- Trading the chop after the protected high is broken on a pre-FOMC morning. Fix: Once the opening-drive idea is invalidated, stop; wait for FOMC or the next day. (1:13:20)
- Shorting while a market-moving speaker (Trump) is live. Fix: Stand aside until the speech is over; use a headline feed. (26:50)
- Deciding reversal vs continuation from a single candle closure. Fix: Read the phase of price at the level: V-shape reversal, consolidation, or retracement then continuation. (1:13:20)
