# Top Down Analysis | Daily Bias To Entries - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
How he goes from daily bias to an entry in three timeframes: the daily chart gives the bias and target (previous day high/low, swing points, daily OB/FVG), the hourly gives the point of interest (order block, breaker plus FVG) formed around midnight / London / pre-New York, and the 5m gives the entry (breaker after a high-low-higher-high-lower-low, FVG / consequent encroachment), with SMT as confirmation. He first studies an MMXM Trader Twitter example (daily FVG fill, hourly shift plus breaker/FVG, 5m FVG entry), then three NQ examples from August (the 24th, Tuesday the 15th, Thursday the 17th; year not said). Minimum 2R, runners toward the daily target (previous day low or a swing low). Weekly profile (e.g. "Tuesday has made the high of the week") frames which days expand.

## Definitions
- **Point of interest (POI)**: the hourly array (OB, breaker plus FVG) aligned with daily bias where the entry timeframe is watched (01:23, 03:49).
- **Breaker (as used)**: the candle of a high, low, higher high, lower low swing that gets displaced through (04:20, 09:09).
- **Runners**: position left on after the minimum 2R (04:50, 09:41).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Daily: read structure (swings, failed displacement), mark previous day high/low, daily OB/FVG; decide bias and target (02:44, 05:22).
2. Weekly profile: which day made the weekly extreme, which days should expand (08:11).
3. Hourly: mark midnight open; watch London and pre-New York; find an OB / breaker plus FVG aligned with bias (03:13, 06:25).
4. 5m: wait for price to reach the hourly POI; look for SMT and a reaction (06:59).
5. Entry: breaker after high-low-higher-high-lower-low, or FVG CE; stop on the high (04:20, 07:39).
6. Minimum 2R, runners to previous day low / daily swing low (04:50, 09:41).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: MMXM Twitter trade (00:57-02:17)
- Daily swing low, fill of daily FVG, target sell-side low. Hourly: POI filled, bearish structure shift, retrace to breaker plus FVG. 5m: lower highs/lows, 5m FVG fill short.
### Example 2: NQ daily / 1h / 5m, August 24 (02:17-05:22)
- Swing low taken, 2.5 SD reached, aggressive Monday-Wednesday rally, failed displacement over a swing high -> bearish. PDH/PDL and a daily OB marked. Hourly: midnight open; London sweeps highs without displacing; New York moves above daily open, closes back in, displaces lower = hourly OB POI. 5m: FVG into the OB, breaker after high-low-higher-high-lower-low; filled before 9:30; expansion at 9:30; previous day low reached.
### Example 3: NQ, Tuesday August 15 (05:22-08:11)
- Daily up-close candle; Monday closed into it. Hourly: price into the OB, midnight open, displacement before New York, sweep under a low into an FVG; up-close candle reaches below a low and closes below = POI. 5m: SMT at the high, extra sweep without taking the high, displacement lower FVG; CE entry, stop on the high, target just below PDL (min 2R); hit.
### Example 4: NQ, Thursday August 17 (08:11-10:22)
- Tuesday made the high of the week; up-close daily candle resisting; targets PDL and a swing point. Hourly: up-close candle into an FVG displaced lower = OB. 5m: 8:30 news, reach into the OB, high-low-higher-high then lower low = breaker paired with displacement; tagged, 2R almost immediately, swing low hit.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Do not want price to return to the high above a breaker; that invalidates (04:20, 09:41).
- POI must align with daily bias, not just any hourly array (02:44).

## Conflicts and open questions
- Example 3: he wants the daily up-close candle to "close and reach below previous day low" to become an OB, but then trades the hourly OB toward PDL before that daily close; the daily confirmation is anticipated, not waited for.
- Weekly profile is referenced but its rules come from another video.
- Year of the August dates is not said (weekday names fit 2023, implied).
- "Minimum 2R" is fixed, runner exit rules are not.
