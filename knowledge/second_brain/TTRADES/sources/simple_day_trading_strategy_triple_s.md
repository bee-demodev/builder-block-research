# Simple Day Trading Strategy - Triple(S)

## TL;DR
Early, pre-ICT stock/options video. "Triple S" (stupid simple strategy): plot previous week high/low, previous day high/low, previous day close and current day open; watch the 5m chart of a few stocks (AMD, NVIDIA, Tesla) for breaks, bounces and rejections at those levels; only trade with the overall market (SPX/SPY/futures) and the higher-timeframe trend. Exits: first target a nearby 5m bar / the opening price, scale out, stop to breakeven, trail behind 5m bars. Two examples (NVDA long off a daily level, TSLA inside-bar break). Historically useful as the origin of his previous-week/day level focus and correlated-asset confirmation, not as TTFM rules.

## Definitions
- **Triple S**: "stupid simple strategy" (00:00).
- **Levels** (on-screen slide at 03:41): previous week high/low (orange), previous day high/low (turquoise), previous day close (light grey), current day open (white) (00:29-01:02).
- **Play**: a break, bounce or rejection at a plotted level on the 5m (01:33; slide).

## Procedure / checklist
On-screen slide (frame at 03:41), consistent with speech:
1. Plot previous week H/L, previous day H/L, previous day close, current day open.
2. Watch 5m price action (breaks / bounces / rejections; "strat" combos; 5m closes above/below level; candlestick formations).
3. Play with the overall market: SPY/SPX/ES 5m; trade in the same direction.
4. Check higher timeframes: 15m/30m/1h and daily; "trend is your friend".
5. Exit on a 5m close back through the level; scale at the open price, breakeven, trail 5m bars (02:58-03:25).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: NVDA 5m long (02:30-03:55)
- Price tries to break below a daily level, rejects and closes above; entry on the retest or break of that candle. AMD and SPX also moving up. Stop: close below the daily level. First target a nearby 5m bar (small), so target the opening price; scale out, stop to breakeven, trail 5m bars; stopped out "at 8.15"; the contracts gained 50%.

### Example 2: TSLA 5m short (03:55-04:58)
- Consolidated on the daily level and the opening price; take the break of the inside bar or, for less chop, the later break down; stop on a close above that candle; "over 100%" trade.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Trading against SPX direction or the daily trend "will just increase your chances of being wrong" (01:33, 02:01).
- Breakouts in gap context are lower probability than bounces (03:55).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Stopped out here at 8.15" (03:25): unclear whether 8:15 is a time (unlikely for US stocks pre-market) or a price; returns quoted are option contract % gains.
- Pre-ICT content: stop on a 5m close back through the level differs from his later swing-based stops; treat as historical.
- Stock/options trading on equities, not futures; instruments outside the TTFM futures focus.
