# Validating Swing Points Using Lower Timeframe Confirmation (CISD)

## TL;DR
Core TTFM validity rule: a higher-timeframe candle-2 or candle-3 closure is a valid swing point only when the lower timeframe (paired timeframe) inside that HTF candle shows a CISD, i.e. price reached an important level (swept a high/low or tapped an FVG) and then closed through the series of candles that delivered price into it. Both parts are required: an HTF closure without an LTF CISD is invalid (wait or ignore), and an LTF CISD without an HTF closure "means nothing". A failed CISD usually leads to a consolidation that gets swept on one side and traded to the other. Quality matters: a V-shaped reversal forming the CISD is ideal, while a close-over after the candle consolidated for half its duration is usually just consolidation. Entries then come from a continuation order block / protected swing inside the HTF candle. His TTFM indicator only prints the model (T-spot, EQ) when both requirements are met.

## Definitions
- **CISD (change in the state of delivery)**: order-block formation at a trend change; important level reached, then a close through the series of candles that delivered into it (0:18-0:46).
- **Valid fractal model (swing point)**: HTF candle-2 or candle-3 closure + LTF CISD inside that HTF candle (0:46-3:16, 9:52).
- **Candle-2 closure**: candle sweeps the previous candle's extreme and closes back inside (1:17-1:25, 3:57).
- **Candle-3 closure**: no candle-2 closure, but candle 3 closes beyond candle 2's opening price (2:18-2:59).
- **Continuation order block**: LTF continuation (new CISD / protected swing) formed after the initial CISD, at a POI, used for entry (9:11-9:38).
- **T-spot / EQ**: indicator-printed zones inside the next HTF candle (5:22).

## Procedure / checklist
1. HTF: is there a candle-2 closure (sweep + close back inside) or a candle-3 closure (beyond candle 2's open)? If not, ignore any LTF CISD (0:54, 2:04, 2:57).
2. LTF inside that HTF candle: find the extreme, find the series of candles (can be one) that made it, check for a close through it (1:25-1:40, 4:24).
3. Grade the CISD: V-shaped reversal = good; close-over after consolidating ~half the HTF candle = consolidation (8:13-8:40).
4. If no CISD: wait or skip; expect consolidation and a sweep of one side (6:43-7:08).
5. If valid: look for continuation in the next HTF candle (candle 3 or 4), entering on a continuation order block / protected swing at a POI; T-spot and EQ from the indicator (5:18-5:32, 9:11-9:52).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Taking an HTF closure as a reversal without the LTF CISD; the swing often gets run (5:32-6:14).
- Trading an LTF CISD with no HTF closure behind it (2:04).
- Counting a candle-3 "closure" that never closed beyond candle 2's open (2:57).
- Accepting a slow close-over after half the candle consolidated (8:23).
- Entering at the first POI touch without a continuation (9:21).

## Conflicts and open questions

- "Important level" (0:32) = sweep of a high/low or an FVG tap; whether other POIs (order blocks, breakers) count is not said here.
- Example 2's timeframe pairing (HTF for the 5m check) is not stated; 1h is implied by his standard pairing but not shown.
