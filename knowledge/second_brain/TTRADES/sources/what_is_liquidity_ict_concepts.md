# What is Liquidity? - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Early ICT-era lesson taught by examples on ES. Liquidity is where stops sit: above the most obvious highs (buy-side, shorts' stops) and below the most obvious lows (sell-side, longs' stops), plus internal highs/lows inside a range where traders would realistically place stops. Once a pool is taken, look for the setup in the opposite direction; untaken relative equal highs/lows become targets for later. Entry after a stop run is the "2022 mentorship setup" on the 1m: a short-term high broken with displacement, then a long from the FVG in the discount of the displacement range. He prefers the 15m to see the pools. Pre-TTFM vocabulary.

## Definitions
- **Liquidity**: resting stop orders at obvious highs and lows (00:06).
- **Buy-side liquidity**: stops of shorts above highs (00:35). **Sell-side liquidity**: stops of longs below lows (00:35-01:05).
- **Relative equal highs/lows**: clustered highs/lows; stops pool there; targets if untaken (01:36, 03:46-04:19).
- **2022 mentorship setup**: ICT's model as he uses it: stop run, short-term swing broken with displacement, FVG entry in the discount (long) of the displacement range (03:13-03:46).

## Procedure / checklist
1. On the 15m, mark the obvious range high and low, then the internal swings where realistic stops sit (02:10-02:41).
2. Wait for a pool aligned with your bias to be run (02:41).
3. Drop to the 1m; wait for a short-term high (for longs) broken with displacement (03:13).
4. Draw a Fib over the displacement range; enter at the FVG only if it is in discount (03:46).
5. Target untaken liquidity, e.g. relative equal highs/lows (04:19).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: ES 5m, range with equal highs, date not readable (00:35-02:10)
- Obvious high/low marked; internal low taken (sell-side), then a cluster of equal highs identified as the next target; once taken, look for the short (01:05-01:36).

### Example 2: ES 15m -> 1m, range-bound session (02:10-03:46)
- Bullish case: internal stops below are run; on the 1m a short-term high is broken with displacement leaving an FVG; Fib over the displacement range shows the FVG in discount = valid long (02:41-03:46). No stop/target stated.

### Example 3: untaken equal highs and lows (03:46-04:19)
- Neither relative equal pool is hit; after a structure shift, a short from the upper area targets the relative equal lows (04:19).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Buying the sweep directly is possible but riskier than waiting for displacement and the FVG (03:13).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Most obvious" highs/lows is subjective; no swing size or lookback is defined.
- "Relative equal" tolerance not defined.
- Discount threshold: only "discount" of the displacement range (below 50%?) is implied, not stated.
- Displacement here is not quantified (see what_is_displacement_ict_concepts for his range-vs-distance comparison).
- Frames show ES 15m and 1m; dates not readable.

## Glossary additions
- `2022 mentorship setup`: ICT's 2022 model (sweep, displacement over short-term swing, FVG in discount/premium).
- `FIB` (Fib): Fibonacci tool over the displacement range for premium/discount.
- Mishearings: "Anyone that go along now" = "anyone that got long now" (01:05).
