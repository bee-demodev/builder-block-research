# Targets With Standard Deviation Projections - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Target method TTrades learned from his friend AM Trades. Context: price runs into a higher-timeframe PD array, forms a short-term high, trades over it and back into the range, shifting structure. The move that took the high (from the swept short-term swing to the extreme) is the manipulation leg; draw a fib over it and project into the delivery side: targets at the 2-2.5 standard deviations and the 4th. Prefer targets where liquidity (highs/lows, FVGs) lines up with a projection. After the 4th SD, a retracement back into the range is possible if displacement stops. Four ES examples (15m/5m, 1h, 2m, daily -> 5m).

## Definitions
- **Manipulation leg**: the move that swept the short-term high (or low) before price came back into the range and shifted structure; the anchor for projections (00:36, 02:40, 06:37).
- **Standard deviation projection**: fib drawn over the manipulation leg (high to low for bearish, low to high for bullish) with extension levels at 1, 2, 2.5, 4 (00:36). Fib settings were shown on screen, not read out (01:06).
- **Sell side of the curve**: the bearish delivery phase after the shift (00:36).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Identify an HTF PD array / liquidity (1h FVG, OB mean threshold, FVG CE, 4H FVG, daily OB) (01:35, 03:46, 04:47, 06:37).
2. On the LTF wait for a sweep of a short-term swing and a close back into the range / structure shift (00:07, 02:10).
3. Mark the manipulation leg; draw the fib over it and project (02:40, 05:25).
4. Target 2-2.5 SD, then 4 SD, preferably where liquidity lines up (00:36, 04:16).
5. After the 4th SD, consider a retracement back into the range if displacement stops (01:06, 03:09).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Projections are "useful without structure aligning" only as a target tool; the stronger case is when a projection lines up with liquidity (04:16, 08:10).
- Do not hold blindly at the 4th SD: if displacement stops, expect a retracement back into the range (01:06, 03:09).
