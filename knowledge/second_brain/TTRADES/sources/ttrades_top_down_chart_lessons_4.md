# TTrades Top-Down Chart Lessons #4

## TL;DR
Review of a live ES long scalp (chart dated Tue 09 Dec 2025). He ranks NQ / ES / YM by location relative to the previous-day low, candle type and SMT: NQ is weakest (it took its previous-day low), YM strongest. Once NQ reverses off its previous-day low he wants longs, but skips YM because it has already expanded over its highs and trades the "middle asset" ES, which has failure swings / equal highs above as the draw. Scalping pairing: 1h for context (a small-wick hourly reversal candle supports expansion), 15m for the swing point (candle 2 closure, candle 3 let close at 9:30 with NQ), 1m for entry (a new low with NQ-vs-ES SMT, close back over the down-close series). Management: partial at 2R, more at the highs, exit the rest rather than wait a tick or two for the limit; runners trailed to a new protected swing and stopped out.

## Definitions
- **Middle asset**: of NQ/ES/YM, the one between the strongest and weakest; traded when the strongest has already expanded beyond its liquidity and the weakest is only just reversing (2:23-2:58).
- **Previous-day low SMT**: one index takes its previous-day low while the others do not (0:35, 1:13).
- **Opposing candle (series)**: the series of down-close candles that made the low; a close back over it = continuation / CISD (4:38).
- **Supports expansion (hourly)**: reversal candle with context and a small wick off the previous hour (3:50, 6:41).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Context (1h, 30m view across NQ/ES/YM): draw = equal highs / failure swings; check previous-day-low SMT (0:27-0:40).
2. Rank assets: location vs previous-day low, candle type (expansion vs reversal), SMT (0:40-1:35).
3. 15m: wait for the open; weakest index manipulates and reverses off its previous-day low -> look long on stronger assets; exclude one already over its highs -> middle asset (1:35-2:58).
4. 15m swing on the traded asset: candle-2 closure, let candle 3 (the 9:30 candle) close in line with the correlated index (2:58-3:33, 6:51).
5. Check the 1h candle supports expansion (3:50).
6. 1m: no nearby protected low -> wait for a new low; SMT vs NQ; close over the down-close series = continuation order block; enter (4:10-4:54).
7. Stop tight below bodies; target highs; partial at 2R and at intermediate highs; exit at the target if the limit misses; trail runners to new protected swings (5:02-6:26).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: ES long scalp, 1h -> 15m -> 1m, Tue 09 Dec 2025 (dates on frames at 0:50 and 5:49) (0:10-7:49)
- 30m/1h view: NQ closest to (and through) its previous-day low, YM bullish expansion candle, NQ a reversal candle; NQ weakest, YM strongest (0:40-1:35).
- 15m after the open: NQ manipulates and trades into its previous-day low, then a reversal closure; failure swings above and ES equal highs = draw; YM excluded (over highs) -> ES (1:35-2:58).
- ES 15m: candle-2 closure, candle-3 continuation; waits for NQ's 9:30 15m candle to close (2:58-3:33, 6:51-7:09).
- 1m with 15m and 1h overlays: 15m candle-2 closure, CISD, continuation; hourly small-wick reversal candle; only a distant protected low -> waits for a new low (3:33-4:22).

- Management: partial at 2R, more at the intermediate highs, limit at the final high not filled -> exited; runners at breakeven, trailed to a new protected swing after a sweep + close over; stopped out on a return to that low; price later respected the original protected swing and ran higher (5:26-7:09).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Shorting an asset with failure swings above from the open (1:42).
- Buying the strongest asset after it has already expanded over its highs (2:27).
- Entering with a stop at a distant protected low instead of waiting for a new low (4:10-4:22).
- Waiting for a limit at the exact target and giving back the position for a tick or two (5:42-5:57).
