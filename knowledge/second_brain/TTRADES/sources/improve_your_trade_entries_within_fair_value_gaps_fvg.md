# Improve Your Trade Entries Within Fair Value Gaps (FVG)

## TL;DR
Two-minute early TTrades ICT tip: three ways to tighten an entry inside a fair value gap. (1) Put a 0.5 fib on the FVG and enter at its midpoint instead of the near edge: lower risk, some missed trades, but a lower win rate is then acceptable. (2) Look left for an order block or breaker lining up with the FVG and use the order block's open. (3) Drop to a smaller timeframe and use an imbalance / order block inside the larger FVG (daily FVG → hourly order block; 5m FVG → 1m order block, whose open landed close to the FVG's 0.5). Feeds TTFM entry refinement inside a HTF PD array.

## Definitions
- **FVG 0.5**: midpoint of the fair value gap drawn with a fib (00:06-00:34).
- **Order block (bearish, here)**: series of up-close candles to the left of the FVG; its open is the entry (00:34-01:04).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Mark the FVG (00:06).
2. Option A: fib it, entry at 0.5 (00:34).
3. Option B: look left for an order block / breaker overlapping the FVG; entry at the OB open (00:34-01:04).
4. Option C: drop a timeframe (daily → hourly, 5m → 1m) and use the imbalance / order block inside the FVG (01:04-01:35).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: FVG with 0.5 fib (00:06-00:34)
- Entry moved from the gap's edge to its 0.5; risk reduced. Instrument/timeframe not stated (later called the 5m example).

### Example 2: FVG with order block to the left (00:34-01:04)
- Series of up-close candles = order block lining up with the FVG; entry at its open.

### Example 3: daily FVG → hourly order block (01:04-01:35)
- Large daily FVG range narrowed with an hourly order block as reference.

### Example 4: 5m FVG → 1m order block (01:35-02:05)
- On the 1m, an order block to the left within the 5m FVG; its open is the refined entry, close to the FVG 0.5.
