# Intro to Market Structure Shifts, Fair Value Gaps, and Displacement - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
One of TTrades' earliest ICT primers. Market structure = highs and lows (short-term, swing, relative equal); liquidity (stops) rests beyond them, and once it is taken you want it delivered in the direction of your bias. In a downtrend watch the lower highs: a market structure shift is when one is broken with displacement. Displacement = an energetic move through structure that leaves a fair value gap. A fair value gap = a three-candle break in price delivery where candle 1 and candle 3 wicks do not overlap. Entry = the FVG left by the MSS. One example (the 24th; SMT, broadening formation, FVG touched): 1m and 5m MSS + FVG entries toward relative equal highs. Foundation vocabulary for later TTFM CISD and FVG rules.

## Definitions
- **Market structure**: highs and lows — short-term, swing, relative equal (00:06).
- **Market structure shift (MSS)**: break of the last lower high (in a downtrend) with displacement (01:05-01:36).
- **Displacement**: energetic move through structure, leaving a FVG; "a distance" reached quickly before retracing (01:36-02:08).
- **Fair value gap (FVG)**: three-candle pattern where candle 1 and candle 3 wicks do not overlap — a break in price delivery (02:08-02:37).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Know the bias and where liquidity rests (00:33).
2. Wait for liquidity taken in the opposite direction (03:05).
3. Watch the last lower highs (for longs); wait for one to break with displacement (01:05-01:36).
4. Draw the FVG created by the displacement; enter there (02:37).
5. Target relative equal highs / opposite liquidity; re-enter on later MSS + FVG (03:35).

## Mistakes and warnings
- A break of a high without displacement / FVG is not an MSS (01:36, implied by the definition).
