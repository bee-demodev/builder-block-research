# TTrades Top-Down Chart Lessons #2

## TL;DR
Review of TTrades' own ES long from the previous week (5m chart dated Wed 27 Aug '25). Weekly reversal candle (aggressive drop then aggressive rally, FVGs both sides) -> bullish week; Monday consolidates after Friday's expansion, Tuesday sweeps Monday and reverses, so Wednesday is the continuation day that should respect the upper half of Tuesday's candle. Hourly CISD confirms the reversal; intraday a consolidation's low is swept into an FVG in the upper half of the prior daily candle. On the 5m the 9:00 candle forms an HTF wick + CISD; he wants the overlap of 0.5 of the wick and 0.5 of the CISD to hold, but R:R to the short-term highs is too poor on 5m and 3m, so he refines to 1m and enters on the break of the high with the stop on the 1m low (target just over 4R, partial at 2R, trailed out). He picked ES over NQ because YM was strongest and NQ weakest.

## Definitions
- **V-shaped reversal**: aggressive move lower met by an aggressive move higher, leaving FVGs on both sides (00:25-00:41).
- **Reversal day / continuation day**: Tuesday sweeps Monday's consolidation and reverses; Wednesday continues (01:28-01:45, 10:36-10:47).
- **T-spot**: overlap of 0.5 of the HTF wick and 0.5 of the CISD leg, where price must hold (04:56-05:22). Not named as a box; he refers to "my model and the T-spot".
- **Fractalize**: apply the daily candle logic to a lower-timeframe candle (08:21-08:37).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Weekly: identify reversal candle -> weekly direction and target (previous high) (00:41-01:01).
2. Daily: map phases (expansion, reversal, consolidation); pick the continuation day after the reversal day (01:01-01:45, 10:29-10:50).
3. Hourly: confirm the reversal with a close through the down-close candles; mark 0.5 of the previous daily candle; price should stay in its upper half (01:48-02:52).
4. Intraday: if consolidation, wait for the range low to be swept into a POI in the upper half (03:22-03:53).
5. Choose the index by relative strength (04:02-04:21).
6. 5m: HTF wick + CISD; hold area = overlap of the two 0.5 levels; POI FVG inside it (04:27-05:36).
7. Entry only if partial at short-term targets gives 2R; else refine (3m, rarely 1m) and seek continuations (05:38-07:59).
8. Stop at the protected low; partial 2R, partial at highs, trail (08:43-10:05).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Entering where you can't partial at short-term targets with decent R:R (05:38-05:57).
- Extending the target to make R:R work; price may reverse at the level (07:51-07:59).
- Trading reversal days without strong context; continuations are easier (02:36-02:42, 05:57-06:07).
