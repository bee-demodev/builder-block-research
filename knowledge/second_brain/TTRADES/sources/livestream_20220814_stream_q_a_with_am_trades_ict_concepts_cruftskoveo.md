## Q&A
- **Q (04:00):** Is the daily on ES/NQ showing inducement, so downside should come next? **A:** [guest] The 4,300 equal highs draw the eye and the imbalance above is a draw; trading above them is not a short signal by itself. If they are taken, look for hourly signals that hint at downside. There is also a fair value gap below.
- **Q (04:20):** Do you use the opening price every 90-minute cycle? **A:** [guest] No. Looks at the midnight opening price, the daily opening price or the RTH open only.
- **Q (05:30):** How to backtest gold? **A:** Host does not trade gold; method is the same for any market: open a 15m chart, go back to a day, mark out what you see, watch what happens when price returns to those areas and drop to lower timeframes there. Soft4FX-type tools allow real-time-style backtesting.
- **Q (08:50):** Difference between market structure shift and market structure break? **A:** [guest] Mostly terminology. He uses market structure break for a break of an intermediate-term swing point (more significant, higher timeframe) and market structure shift for an intraday shift such as a 30m high broken after taking a low.
- **Q (08:50):** How do you get your daily bias? **A:** [guest] He does not do daily bias much. He looks at weekly bias, the days of the week that should form the high/low, follows order flow, and the market maker model gives the narrative. Host adds: if not doing that, look on the daily for relative equal highs and fair value gaps where price is likely to draw.
- **Q (11:00):** In the market maker buy/sell model, do you enter on the intermediate high/low or the failed swing? **A:** [guest] Prefers the failed swing on the second leg: the first leg's retracement to the FVG makes the intermediate-term point; the second leg gives the failed swing that targets completion of the model (the silver bullet).
- **Q (11:00):** Can these concepts be used on stocks? **A:** Host: yes, he looks at the 5m and uses OTE from low to high, but it is less clean because stocks lack the overnight market structure. Later (TSLA 2m) he adds he does not watch stocks much because of the absence of structure; an aggressive break of structure then a single up-close candle could be a short even outside OTE.
- **Q (14:10):** Why not trade gold? **A:** Host watches only NASDAQ and ES. [guest] finds enough with indexes, Bitcoin and occasionally EUR; not watching too many things is an advantage.
- **Q (14:10):** How do you use the market maker buy/sell model for bias? **A:** [guest] Mark higher-timeframe POIs: premium arrays make sell models, discount arrays make buy models. Wait for price to hit the POI, then look left; on the lower timeframe look for consolidation/accumulation, liquidity left (equal lows), a smart money reversal, then the intermediate-term high and the silver bullet FVG entry, stop above, partials, target sell side. Walked through on an ES 3m replay of 2022-08-11.
- **Q (19:40):** I can only trade from my phone because of my 9-5; what do you recommend? **A:** Sit on the 15m chart; when a point of interest is reached, look at the phone and go to a lower timeframe.
- **Q (19:40):** Explain breakers, and how many trades do you take a week? **A:** [guest] A breaker forms when price makes a high, then a lower low after a run; the down-close candle before the run on buy side becomes a bearish breaker once price breaks below (opposite for bullish). He takes fewer than two trades a day, around eight a week; market maker setups are rarer but high probability. 15m is the lowest timeframe for higher-timeframe narrative; hourly to daily is the sweet spot even for day trades.
- **Q (21:10):** How do you manage stops while in a trade? **A:** Host: take the first partial at the nearby lows and move the stop to break even, or trail market structure on a 2m/1m chart under each new higher low. [guest] Moves to break even only when the first target is hit; before that he does not touch the stop even if structure shifts; then scales and lets the rest run. Host: taking 50% off at 2R and leaving the stop still nets 1R if stopped.
- **Q (24:10):** Best way to learn how price delivers? **A:** [guest] Backtesting: apply what you watch to a real chart, annotate moves, write down what you see, screenshot; hindsight is where you learn.
- **Q (26:10):** Is the silver bullet formed after the intermediate high in the sell model (and vice versa)? **A:** [guest] Yes, after the intermediate-term swing point is created. Later: the ITH confirms the silver bullet; place the stop near the intermediate-term high (a defended stop) and enter on the second retracement into an FVG.
- **Q (27:00):** Explain algorithmic / advanced market structure. **A:** [guest] Structure means nothing out of context. Intermediate-term swing points form when price trades back into an FVG and moves away. When bearish, want intermediate-term lows broken (MSB) and intermediate-term highs unviolated; then a short-term high/failed swing and the sell-off. It replaces basic higher-high/lower-low reading with FVGs and short/intermediate/long-term swings. Refers to ICT 2022 mentorship episodes 12-13.

With fixed risk, a 2:1 or 3:1 R:R at 50% win rate is profitable; with inconsistent size even 75% can lose. Shows his position-size spreadsheet.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Moving the stop to break even too fast gets you taken out before the real move. Fix: Wait for displacement over highs and a higher low before moving it; or leave it until the first target. (22:20)
- Buying at the high of the day and week with no prior liquidity run. Fix: Wait for a run of liquidity and then the move in your direction. (31:00)
- Inconsistent position size: even a 75% win rate can lose money. Fix: Risk the same dollar amount every trade, size to the stop. (34:30)
- Getting caught up in very low timeframes. Fix: Use them only to reduce risk inside a higher-timeframe POI; zoom back out for targets. (41:00)
- [guest] Jumping around ICT content and picking up random concepts. Fix: Master the 2022 mentorship series first. (48:00)
- Quitting a job to trade while anxious about money. Fix: Keep other income so trading is not the only source of money. (53:50)
