# ICT Index Futures Weekly Recap - Week of 5/16/22

## TL;DR
An early (May 2022) ICT-style weekly recap of ES/NQ for Mon 2022-05-16 to Fri 2022-05-20. Weekly idea: the prior week failed to take a daily low, Friday retraced up, so he expected a move up into a daily order block and then a drop to take the low. Mon-Wed are hindsight only; Thursday and Friday were traded live. Entries are pre-TTFM ICT vocabulary: liquidity taken, market structure shift (MSS), retracement into FVG/order block, stop on the high or above the FVG, targets at an HTF FVG or a liquidity level. Uses SMT ("divergence") between ES and NQ to pick the weaker index to short.

## Definitions
- **Market structure shift (MSS)**: taking a short-term low/high against the prior move (01:31, 02:14). ICT term, not TTFM CISD.
- **Divergence**: his early name for SMT between ES and NQ (03:31).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: Mon-Tue 2022-05-16/17, hindsight (01:00-01:36)
- Below the daily OB, expecting a retracement up; no swing lows taken, so no trades. Tuesday reaches the area and turns bearish; possible MSS entry, not liked.

### Example 2: Wed 2022-05-18, 5m, hindsight (01:36-02:44)
- He was asleep; looks for liquidity taken at his wake time; daily still has room to the low. 5m: short-term low taken, MSS down, retrace into FVG and OB, short, stop on the highs, target 39.05 (spoken; most likely ES 3905), the start of a 1h FVG.

### Example 3: Thu 2022-05-19, NQ 1m, live (02:44-04:25)
- Shorts only after the daily range breakdown. Liquidity taken above, 15m SMT shows NQ weaker than ES. 1m: MSS takes a low, retrace, sell; stop above the FVG (the high was too much risk); target 11,850 for liquidity; reached "by like two points" (04:20). Tweets shown as proof.

### Example 4: Fri 2022-05-20, NQ 5m/1m, live (04:25-06:04)
- Plan from a 1h order block: take the highs, then the equal lows not broken in RTH. Morning: move up into the OB, highs taken, then down through all targets. 1m entries: sell the retrace into the OB after lows are taken, or after the next low is taken and price retraces into an FVG; held all day, about a 500 point move (05:49). Week summary: daily target, move into the daily OB, lows taken.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Do not be greedy: exit at the start of the HTF FVG (02:37).
- His chart times were on Mountain time, not Eastern, so the session times shown are off (00:51).

## Conflicts and open questions
- Pre-TTFM vocabulary (MSS, order block, divergence) rather than CISD/SMT/PSP; mapping MSS to CISD is implied only.
- "39.05" target (02:23) is ambiguous: no instrument is named for Wednesday; it most likely means ES 3905.
- "8 11 850" (03:58) is garbled; at 04:16 he says 11,850 on NQ.
- Charts were in Mountain time, so frames show times about 2 hours off ET.
- Recording date is not stated; the Friday is described as "this morning" (04:52), which suggests 2022-05-20, not confirmed.
