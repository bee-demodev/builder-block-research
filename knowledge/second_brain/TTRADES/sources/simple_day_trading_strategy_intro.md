# Simple Day Trading Strategy - Intro

## TL;DR
His earliest, pre-ICT style intro: plot previous week high/low, previous day high/low, previous day close and the day's opening price, then take actionable trades off those levels on the 5-minute chart using candlestick formations / "the Strat" closes above and below. Example on Tesla: a shooter candle tests a level and closes below it; enter on the close or break, target previous 5m candles, and trail runners on previous 5m candles. Described as a scalping strategy. No TTFM concepts.

## Definitions
- **Shooter candle**: candle that tests a level and rejects (Strat term), not defined (01:03).
- **The Strat**: candle-close system referenced as a trigger source (01:03).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Plot previous week high/low, previous day high/low/close (00:01-00:30).
2. At open, plot the opening price (00:30).
3. On 5m, wait for a candlestick/close trigger at a level (01:03).
4. Enter on close or break; target previous 5m candles; trail runners on prior 5m candles (01:03-02:06).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: TSLA 5m short (01:03-01:34)
- Shooter candle tests a level, closes below; entry on the break; target previous 5m candles; worked.

### Example 2: TSLA 5m long and trailed short (01:34-02:06)
- Break and close above a level: long. Break through the previous-day close with a little resistance: entry, stop trailed on previous 5m candles until stopped; "over 10 points".
