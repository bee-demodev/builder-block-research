# Fractalizing Entries -Q&A with Ben - ICT Concepts (2022-09-11)

> [!info] Converted from the livestream analysis JSON (an Opus agent read the stream's frames and transcript).

## TL;DR
Guest stream from the 2022 ICT era: TTrades only introduces his friend Ben and relays viewer questions; almost all teaching is Ben's. Ben's lesson is 'market fractals': a higher-timeframe 'sponsorship' model (weekly/4H point of interest, market-maker sell model) is re-found on lower 'execution' timeframes (1H, 15m, 5m, 1m) to get the same target with a much smaller stop, i.e. a higher R and shorter time in the trade. He walks a hindsight S&P 500 example (August 2022 rejection of a weekly FVG near 4325, down to the 4247 sell-side pool and lower) from weekly down to 1-minute, then a NAS100 long from Friday 9 Sep 2022 (5m and 1m entries off an hourly FVG after a sell-side raid, including a 1-minute 'silver bullet' re-entry). The Q&A covers R:R and journaling, order blocks vs FVGs vs breakers, the PD array matrix, breaker vs mitigation block, kill zones, DXY as his barometer, SMT, stop runs vs real shifts, daily bias, backtesting, impulse/retracement and external-to-internal liquidity, purge-and-revert, and win rate vs strike rate. No live trades; markets reopen during the stream (~18:00 NY Sunday).

## Q&A
- **Q (06:20):** Should I watch ICT's monthly core content or just the 2022 mentorship? **A:** [guest] Be consistent and accurate with the 2022 series first; core content adds complication and delays progress; if the YouTube model works, don't change it.
- **Q (10:50):** Why isn't the big wick before it used as the swing high on the 4H? **A:** [guest] You can use it; it is the more conservative stop and less likely to be hit, but the candle high where the shift originated is still valid; the wick is a deviation from the range grabbing liquidity.
- **Q (15:00):** Mitigated vs unmitigated? **A:** [guest] Mitigation is a revisit; unmitigated means the FVG was left open. Unfilled weekly gaps are where price seeks rebalancing, so they serve as targets.
- **Q (16:40):** What is sponsorship? **A:** [guest] Where the original idea comes from; a weekly FVG being mitigated means the weekly sponsors the move, and weekly sponsorship means weekly targets. Without it, a 4H model sponsors a 4H move.
- **Q (21:30):** Is a trade invalid when price comes back to your FVG entry? What's your invalidation? **A:** [guest] FVGs can be revisited, especially if not fully filled; they are less sensitive than other PD arrays. Invalidation is candle closures beyond the defending side of the FVG.
- **Q (25:50):** Is aiming for 1:1 reasonable for someone who struggles to hold trades? **A:** [guest] He would aim higher; the only real answer is your own data from 20-50 trades at 1R/1.5R/2R. He likes 3R-3.5R so he doesn't need >50% accuracy. Journal every trade with before/after screenshots.
- **Q (28:20):** Do order blocks or FVGs hold more weight; which should we anticipate? **A:** [guest] Individual choice; he struggles to see order blocks and prefers breakers and FVGs. Don't marry a PD array; use what you see and study it via journaling. An order block is the last opposing candle before an explosive move that also creates an FVG.
- **Q (32:40):** Difference between a breaker and a mitigation block? **A:** [guest] A breaker forms after a new high (not a failure swing) and is a flipped order block; a mitigation block forms without the new swing. He doesn't study mitigation blocks.

- **Q (53:50):** Best way to backtest? **A:** [guest] Pick an instrument and session, use a kill zone indicator, map PDH/PDL, session highs/lows, weekly highs/lows, and watch how price delivers to them across timeframes.
