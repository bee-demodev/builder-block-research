## Mistakes and warnings
- Taking 1m 'inversion' longs inside a consolidation in a bearish daily profile (Instagram inversion traders); breakeven never hits and price runs the stop. Fix: Read the daily bias/profile first; a consolidation at the low in a bearish day is where continuation forms, not a long. (23:40)
- Fading breakouts / shorting a strongly trending gold. Fix: Trade the trend: wait for a swing low and trade higher. (08:00)
- Flipping bullish because price closed over the EQ. Fix: Treat it as a soft invalidation: keep bias, reduce targets to the open/liquidity around the open. (32:10)
- Shorting after the target has been hit (chasing). Fix: Enter before/into the target; if missed, let it go. (23:40)
- Trading the lagging (strongest) index short when bearish. Fix: Trade the weakest or the middle asset only. (50:40)
- Picking the V-shaped low as the target. Fix: Target the consolidation low, which is easier for price to reach. (1:01:10)
- Over-managing positions early on. Fix: Strict TP and SL; learn management later. (50:40)
- After a blown account, studying more ICT instead of fixing sizing and tilt. Fix: Stop learning new information; follow one strategy and stop repeating avoidable mistakes. (1:19:20)
