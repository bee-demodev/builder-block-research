# TTrades Fractal Model - Trading Candle 2

## TL;DR
How to trade the reversal candle (candle 2) of the fractal model. Core idea: a small wick supports expansion, a large wick does not. Frame the reversal by sweeping the previous HTF candle's high/low, then a CISD forming a protected swing, so you can assume the HTF wick has formed. If the wick is small (formed early), target previous-candle lows, standard-deviation projections, previous-day EQ and liquidity. If the wick is large (range already used), cut targets to the daily open, session lows or current candle low, or skip candle 2 and trade the continuation day (candle 3/4). Also covers session-dependent confirmation timeframes, a 4th-day limit and using the weaker correlated asset (SMT).

## Definitions
- **Expansion candle**: small wick both sides, large body (00:23).
- **Reversal candle (candle 2)**: opens, large opposing run forms the wick, returns toward the open before close (00:34).
- **Reversal to expansion candle**: a candle-2 whose wick stays small so it can trend in the new direction; the ideal case (02:06, 02:34).
- **Range (HTF)**: a candle whose range encompasses all later days' ranges; failure swings show as non-overlapping HTF candle extremes (08:34, 08:47).
- **Early CISD feature**: his indicator's mode that prints the CISD one candle earlier (04:41).
- **Low-resistance liquidity**: untaken session highs/lows (failure swings) that price can reach easily (11:27).

## Procedure / checklist
1. On the HTF, decide whether a new phase is due (after expansion) and mark the previous candle's high/low (03:04, 02:42).
2. On the LTF, wait for the sweep of that level, then a CISD forming a protected swing; assume the HTF wick is in (02:42, 04:37).
3. Judge the wick: small (early in the period, shallow sweep) supports expansion; large (range already used) does not (01:00, 05:30, 07:07).
4. Set targets: small wick: previous candle low, SD projections, previous-day EQ, PDH/PDL; large wick: daily/HTF open, session lows, current candle low (02:21, 04:58, 08:12).
5. Choose the confirmation timeframe for the session: Asia 1h/30m, London 1h/30m/15m, NY often lower (5m) (10:11-10:57).
6. Skip or downsize if overnight highs/lows are already taken or the day is mostly done; prefer the next day's continuation (11:16, 11:59).
7. Use the correlated asset; frame off the weaker/stronger one per SMT (13:58-14:52).
8. If C2 is missed, trade C3/C4 continuations (17:58).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: Gold daily, 1h for entry (03:04-05:59)
- After a large daily expansion, looks for the next candle to take the previous day's high and reverse. On the hourly: expansion into consolidation, expansion above the range met with expansion back = wick forming. Marks the series of up-close candles; CISD (early-CISD indicator) gives a protected high; small daily wick. Targets: previous-day EQ, PDH/PDL. Outcome: expansion lower (05:13). Counterfactual at 05:36: if the daily wick had already used most of the range, no expansion expected.

### Example 2: NQ (chart, Aug 2025), previous day high in NY (05:59-08:28)
- Two swing lows, no further swing low; only downside favored. NY open: Asia consolidation, London expanded higher, then consolidation inside the candle range, no FVG retrace. Large wick over PDH: targets adjusted to the daily open and session lows; both taken (07:51). Day closes as a reversal candle; next day (C3) gives the continuation into the lower FVG (07:58).

### Example 3: oil or gasoline, range (08:28-12:30)
- Previous candle's range contains the following days; failure swings both sides. Ignores CISDs with no level. Range low taken late in NY after oscillating all day: does not expect a run to the range high. An hourly CISD would only print at buyside liquidity (the target), so he drops to 5m: CISD then a new protected low at an important level. Tries for 2R (11:39); daily open gives resistance, then taken (11:43). Lesson: large-wick day, better to trade the next day, which formed a CISD and a small-wick continuation higher.

### Example 4: GBPUSD daily, 1h (12:30-15:10)
- Multiple days of expansion, maybe one more; no longs after day 4. Important level at the previous day high. Hourly: expansion into the high met with expansion back + CISD; target 2R or range EQ. Stopped out before target (13:49). Fix: EURUSD was weaker (shallow wick, never reached its previous day high; SMT). Framing the setup on EUR, with entry at midnight on GBP and the same stop, works back into the previous range.

### Example 5: NQ 5m (chart 7 Aug 2025), late-day reversal into range (15:10-18:06)
- Daily candle already expanded with a small lower wick; favor shorts back into range. Frame off the 9-10 am hourly candle closure / previous hourly candle high (mentions Kane's strategy as similar, 15:51). Sweep + CISD on 5m; hourly and new 4H candle both show small wicks. 4H-15m alternative: wait for closure below (17:02). Targets daily open, then session lows/current day low, both hit (17:26).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Expecting full expansion from a candle that already made a large opposing wick (01:33, 05:36).
- Taking setups late in the day when most of the daily range/time is used (09:23).
- Waiting for a 1h CISD in NY when it will only print at your target (10:38).
- Longing after the 4th day of expansion (12:44).
- Not checking the correlated asset; the stronger/weaker pair can give the cleaner frame (13:58).
