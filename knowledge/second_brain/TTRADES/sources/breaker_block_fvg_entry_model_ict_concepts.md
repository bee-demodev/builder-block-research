# Breaker Block & FVG Entry Model - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Explains the "unicorn" entry model: a breaker block with an overlapping fair value gap. FVG = 3-candle pattern where candle 1 and candle 3 wicks do not overlap. Bearish breaker structure = high, low, higher high (stop sweep), lower low (displacement through); the breaker is the last down-close candle(s) before the move that made the higher high. Bullish = low, high, lower low, higher high; breaker = the last up-close candle(s) before the move that made the lower low. Entry on the return into the breaker + overlapping FVG, stop on the swing extreme or beyond the breaker, target opposite liquidity (smooth/equal highs or lows), with SD projections as added confluence. Three NQ 2m/1m examples, one "left behind". Model credited to Tyler and Ash (Ash Trades).

## Definitions
- **Fair value gap**: 3-candle pattern; candle 1 and candle 3 wicks do not overlap, leaving an imbalance (00:37).
- **Breaker block**: structural pattern high-low-higher high-lower low (bearish) / low-high-lower low-higher high (bullish); marked on the last opposite-colour candle (or series) before the sweep leg (01:04-02:02).
- **Unicorn model**: breaker block + overlapping FVG from the displacement through it (02:02-02:31).
- **Mean threshold**: midpoint of the breaker's candle bodies/range (06:34).
- **Smooth highs / equal lows / equal highs**: liquidity pools used as draw (02:31, 06:03).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Mark the draw (smooth highs, equal highs, lows below) (02:31, 04:31, 06:03).
2. Wait for the structure: low, high, lower low (sweep), then displacement to a higher high through the up-close candle(s) (bullish) (02:55).
3. Mark the breaker = last up-close candle(s) of the move that swept the lows; confirm an overlapping FVG (03:33).
4. Enter on the return into the FVG/breaker; stop on the swing low or below the breaker/FVG; target the draw; check R (03:33-04:04).
5. Add SD projection of the manipulation leg (2-2.5) as confluence (04:31).
6. Invalidate if bodies close beyond the breaker's mean threshold; skip if the target is hit before entry (05:32, 06:34).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: NQ 2m, long to smooth highs (02:31-04:31)
- Draw: smooth highs; equal lows below wanted first. New low made (low, high, lower low), then higher high with displacement through the up-close candle. Breaker = highest up-close candle of the move that swept the lows; overlapping FVG. Entry in the FVG; stop on the low = about 2.3R, stop below breaker = about 5R; he keeps it on the low. Price drops into the FVG before taking the highs. SD projection 2-2.5 as confluence.

### Example 2: around New York open, bearish (04:31-06:03)
- Slow move up left lows; NY open marked with a vertical line. High, low, higher high, then displacement through the down-close series; breaker = lowest down-close candle/series; FVG present. Stop on high too large, so just above the breaker. Target lows reached before the entry is tagged: left behind, not interested.

### Example 3: NQ 1m, long to equal highs (06:03-07:07)
- Low, high, lower low with an extra sweep, then displacement up through the up-close series; next candle leaves an FVG. Entry in FVG, stop at the low. Price digs deeper into the FVG/breaker but bodies do not close below the mean threshold of the up-close candles. High hit for 2.14R.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Chasing after the target is reached without entry; just skip (05:32).
- Don't treat a deep wick into the breaker as invalidation; watch body closes vs the mean threshold (06:34).

## Conflicts and open questions
- Breaker candle wording: "lowest down close candle" (01:34) vs "last bearish candle" (02:02) vs "highest up close candle" (03:33 for bullish): lowest/highest vs last are not the same when a series exists.
- "Overlapping" FVG: how much overlap with the breaker is required is not defined.
- Stop choice (swing extreme vs beyond breaker) is discretionary; 2.3R vs 5R shown without a rule for choosing.
- Mean threshold of a series vs a single candle (body or wick range) is not specified.
- Example 1's final outcome (highs taken) is said in passing at 04:31, not shown in detail.
