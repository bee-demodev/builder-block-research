## Mistakes and warnings
- Shorting the strongest (lagging) asset, e.g. YM, in a bearish market Fix: Trade the weakest or middle asset; the laggard often stops you out (13:00)
- 200-point NQ stops on a 5m chart, or tightening a stop to cut risk Fix: Keep the structural stop and reduce position size; 'big' is relative to the day's range (13:00)
- Treating SMT as a signal on its own Fix: Wait for a reversal to form in the area; SMT is confluence (25:00)
- Waiting for a 15m closure on an opening-range move Fix: Drop to 5m/3m; the move completes within the 15m candle (37:00)
- Buying evaluations without a defined model Fix: Learn a model first, then trade it (1:12:30)
- Sitting on the chart all day searching for more trades after the window Fix: Trade the defined window and stop, whether or not you traded (1:12:30)
- Trusting the first lower-timeframe reversal off a POI Fix: Wait for a continuation (e.g. a sweep of the lows) before entering (1:17:20)
- Treating his streams as signals Fix: Make your own entry/stop/TP decisions from the analysis (29:00)
