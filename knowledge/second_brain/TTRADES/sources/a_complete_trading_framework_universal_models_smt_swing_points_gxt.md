# A Complete Trading Framework (Universal Models, SMT, Swing Points) | GXT

## TL;DR
A "TT Talks" guest lecture by Garrett (GXT); TTrades only introduces it (00:11). Garrett stacks three layers: (1) "universal models" (internal-to-external, external-to-internal, manipulation / "order pairing" ranges) say where price should reverse and what it targets; (2) swing formations (candle 2 closure, candle 2 reversal-to-expansion, candle 3 closure, with EQ of the prior candle as a wick-size filter) confirm the reversal mechanically; (3) SMT and PSP across a three-market "triad" confirm it again, ideally as a two-stage sequence. His highest-probability signature is SMT at the key level, then a PSP, then a crack in correlation between swing points (14:25). Examples on the oil, gold and index triads, plus three top-down trades (YM, RB, RTY) with a weekly/daily/4h narrative and LTF entries. The swing-formation part is the TTFM candle 2 / candle 3 logic; universal models and triads are Garrett's blend (credits TTrades, AM Trades, MMXM Trader, Trader Day, ICT at 00:55).

## Definitions
- **Universal model**: a framework that says, when price hits a key level, it should reverse and which opposing level it targets (01:37).
- **Internal to external**: key level = internal range liquidity (FVG); target = external range high/low that caused the retracement (01:37).
- **External to internal**: key level = external high / manipulated range high; target = back into the range, FVG first (01:58).
- **Order pairing range / manipulation range**: range high or low is the key level, the opposing side / swing is the target (02:18).
- **Candle 2 closure**: candle 2 closes back inside candle 1's range (02:48).
- **Candle 2 reversal to expansion**: same as a reversal candle but with a small wick, so it reverses and expands in the same candle; occurs when candle 1 hits the key level and closes as expansion (03:50).
- **Candle 3 closure**: candle 1 or 2 hits the key level without a candle 2 closure; candle 3 expands away from candle 2's extreme without taking it; "strong" = closes over candle 2's body (04:26).
- **EQ of range**: 0.5 of the previous candle's range, used as a wick-size gauge (02:57, 08:32).
- **Triad**: three correlated markets used for SMT (07:17).
- **SMT fill**: SMT at an FVG; one asset fills/enters the gap, the other leaves a breakaway gap open (08:00).
- **Crack in correlation** ("Ken correlation", "Kraken correlation", "cracking correlation"): the divergence that makes an SMT (07:53, 14:41).
- **Failure swing**: the swing on the asset that did not take the level in an SMT (09:06).
- **Two-stage SMT**: an SMT immediately confirmed by a second SMT (10:12).
- **PSP (precision swing point)**: correlated markets close a candle in opposite directions; from Trader Day (10:34).
- **Two-stage PSP**: SMT plus PSP confirming each other, in either order (10:59).
- **Continuation PSP**: a PSP inside expansion toward an open target; one asset's retracement close is a PSP, the other runs its extreme; no swing point forms (11:51).
- **Strength switch PSP**: named at 22:25, deferred to his PSP video; not defined here.
- **V-shaped reversal signature**: expansion into the key level, SMT, expansion away (26:35, 31:53).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Pick the universal model: key level (FVG, external high/low, range extreme) and its target (01:19).
2. At the key level, check the triad for SMT (high/low SMT or SMT fill) (07:43).
3. Confirm with a swing formation on the trading timeframe: candle 2 closure, candle 2 reversal-to-expansion (small wick), or candle 3 closure over candle 2's body (02:48-05:12).
4. If no SMT at the key level, require a swing-point SMT or PSP; best is a two-stage sequence (12:14-14:53).
5. Mark EQ of the reversal candle (or candle 3 on a C3 closure); the next candle should hold the upper half (bullish) / lower half (bearish) (05:42).
6. Prefer signals printed by a driver (9:00 close, 9:30, 10:00) (18:42, 30:12).
7. Drop to LTF (3m/1m) and enter on the CISD / FVG / order block in the direction of the HTF candle; target the opposing side of the range or HTF draw (24:08, 31:35).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Avoid trading reversal candles with large wicks; trade the small-wick expansion candle (03:17).
- Do not participate in candle 1 or candle 3 when neither shows reversal; wait for the candle 3 close (06:24).
- Treating a PSP that forms during expansion toward an open draw as a reversal gets you stopped out; it is continuation (17:14, 27:54-28:05).
- Equal highs / a PSP on a retracement inside expansion are a retracement, not a reversal (17:14).
- Gold triad needs the right data feed (Forex.com) so crosses open at 18:00 (15:58).
