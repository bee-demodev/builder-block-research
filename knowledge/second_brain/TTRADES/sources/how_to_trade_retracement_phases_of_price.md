# How To Trade Retracement - Phases Of Price

## TL;DR
Part 3 of TTrades' "phases of price" series (after consolidation and expansion; reversal and a blended fractal-model video are announced next). A retracement is the opposite of an expansion: a slow, shallow move against the trend, typically into a fair value gap, with failure swings left on the opposing side that become the target. He trades it either with a candle-2 closure / lower-timeframe entry model inside the FVG, or by waiting for a protected swing (close over the series of opposing candles) and then a continuation. Strong expansions retrace shallowly or not at all; a retracement that does not confirm quickly becomes a consolidation, and "expansion met with expansion" is a reversal that turns the protected swing into the draw. Examples: daily/hourly index (NQ likely), EURUSD 5m with hourly, monthly/daily, and an NQ lower-timeframe sequence with ES SMT.

## Definitions
- **Retracement**: slow, shallow, small-range move opposing the trend between expansions, usually into a PDA, generally an FVG (0:28-2:16, 4:49).
- **Expansion**: strong, fast, directional, large-range move (0:37, 4:49); covered in the prior video of the series.
- **Consolidation** (in this video): a retracement that fails to confirm and hangs around, or a sideways range whose low/high must be manipulated before continuation (7:24-7:46, 15:12-15:30).
- **Protected swing**: swing created when price closes over the series of opposing candles; trend stays valid until it is violated (1:48-1:56, 7:02, 13:19).
- **Expansion met with expansion**: an expansion through a high immediately answered by an opposing expansion = reversal (16:21).
- **T-spot / gray box**: zone his indicator marks where the higher-timeframe candle's wick should form (5:28, 6:42).
- **PDA**: price delivery array, here generally an FVG (2:07).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Have a bias from a higher-timeframe closure (candle 2 / CISD) expecting expansion (2:30-2:40).
2. After the expansion leg, mark FVGs it left and the failure swings on the opposite side (1:03-1:38).
3. Classify the pullback as it develops: slow/shallow into FVG = retracement; sideways and unconfirmed = consolidation; fast opposing = reversal (2:47, 7:20, 13:44).
4. Retracement: take a candle-2 closure / LTF model in the FVG, or wait for a close over the opposing series (protected swing) and enter on the close or retest (1:39-1:56, 9:16-9:30).
5. Consolidation: wait for the low (bull) / high (bear) to be swept, or SMT on the correlated asset, then a protected swing (8:16-8:46, 13:10, 15:23-15:50).
6. Stop on the swing wick (beyond the body if the candle lacks a wick); target 2R / failure swings; trail to new protected swings (9:34-9:42, 14:26).
7. If expansion meets opposing expansion, flip: the protected swing becomes the draw (16:21-16:46).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Calling a fast opposing move a retracement: it is a reversal (2:47).
- Expecting deep retracements after aggressive expansion; they are shallow or absent (6:27, 10:14).
- Waiting in a retracement that is not confirming: it has become a consolidation, so switch to waiting for a manipulation (7:20-7:46).
- Entering on the first FVG touch without a close-over (9:16).
- Trusting a shallow FVG tap or equal lows as the swing (8:16, 8:52).
- Holding onto a protected low after expansion met with expansion; it becomes the draw (16:32).

## Conflicts and open questions
- 9:34: for a long he says the stop goes "at least on the the wick or a little bit higher"; for a long the extra margin should be lower. Likely a slip; the chart was not checked.
- "Slow and shallow" has no numeric definition; the only measure given is the 9h-vs-3h time ratio in one example (4:10). How deep is "deep" (beyond the FVG? beyond 50%?) is undefined.
- "Retracements should be quick" (7:20) vs "slow shallow move" (0:44): he means the phase should resolve (confirm with a close) without lingering, while each candle is small. A rule engine needs a time limit before reclassifying a retracement as a consolidation; none is given.
- Instruments for examples 1, 3 and 4 are not named in speech; NQ is inferred from the ES SMT reference (13:10) and the price scales in the frames.
- Example 3 is described as "monthly and daily" while the frame at 12:09 shows a daily chart with monthly candles on the right; consistent.
- The indicator (failure swings, gray box / "T-spot") is his TTFM indicator, unnamed here.
