# Smart Money Divergences (SMT) - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Short early lesson on SMT divergence between the S&P 500 (ES) and NASDAQ (NQ). They normally track each other's market structure; a divergence is one making a higher high while the other makes a lower high (bearish) or one making a higher low while the other makes a lower low (bullish). Best with a liquidity grab at a point of interest; higher timeframes more reliable (he prefers 15m, uses 1m a lot). Trade the diverging index: the weaker one (lower high) for shorts, the stronger one (higher low) for longs. Two ways: enter immediately risking the divergence low/high (invalidated if taken), or the safer way: wait on 1-5m for a market structure shift and a return to an FVG.

## Definitions
- **SMT divergence**: ES and NQ disagree at a swing (higher high vs lower high, or higher low vs lower low) (00:36, 01:06).
- **Bearish divergence**: one index makes a higher high, the other a lower high; the lower-high index is weaker (02:09).
- **Bullish divergence**: one index makes a lower low, the other a higher low; the higher-low index is stronger (02:09).
- **Invalidation**: the diverging index takes its own swing low/high, so there is no divergence (03:09).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Compare ES and NQ structure at a point of interest with a liquidity grab (01:06).
2. Identify the divergence and the diverging index (weaker for shorts, stronger for longs) (01:37, 02:09).
3. Method 1: enter on it now, stop beyond its divergence swing, target (02:39).
4. Method 2: wait for a 1-5m structure shift and a return to an FVG (03:39).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: ES vs NQ, bearish SMT (00:36-01:06)
- Same structure until ES makes a lower high while NQ makes a higher high; short ES (weaker).
### Example 2: NQ vs ES, bullish SMT, "today" (01:06-04:08)
- NQ higher low, ES lower low; long NQ. Method 1: enter, risk the lows. Method 2: market structure shift with displacement, 2m FVG, multiple entries.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Keeping the trade after the divergence swing is taken: the divergence no longer exists (03:09).

## Conflicts and open questions
- At 01:37 he calls a bearish divergence "lower highs and higher lows", which contradicts his own definition (higher high vs lower high, 00:36); a slip of speech.
- At 02:09 "bullish divergence ... making lower lows and higher lows" is right (one each).
- Example 1 picks the "weaker" for shorts, which is the "diverging" one only if diverging means the one that failed; for longs he picks the one that held. The rule is: trade the index that did not take the swing (implied).
- POI and liquidity-grab conditions are "ideal", not required.
- "Two-minute fair value gap" (04:08) may be a 2m chart or a mishearing; listed as 2m.
