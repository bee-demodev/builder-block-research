# AFYZ | Trading The Daily OHLC - Protraction Profiles

## TL;DR
Guest lesson: AFYZ (an ICT index-futures trader, NQ/ES in New York) teaches three daily "protraction" (manipulation) templates that say which session makes the low (high) of the day: **classic protraction** (London makes the day's low at a key level, New York retraces and continues), **delayed protraction** (manipulation continues from ~3:00 to the New York open/8:30 into the key level, New York reverses London's move), and **Judas swing** (Asia and London both accumulate, New York runs their highs/lows then reverses in a V-shape). Each template is a power-of-three (accumulation, manipulation, distribution) read of the daily open-low-high-close, and the day's extreme must be made at a qualifying higher-timeframe key level (PD array) with SMT and a CISD. Each morning he studies the prior two sessions to pick the template, then executes with a market-maker-model retracement in New York toward the HTF draw on liquidity. TTrades only hosts (intro/outro); all teaching is the guest's, and the method sits beside TTFM daily profiles rather than inside them.

## Definitions
- **Protraction / protraction profile (manipulation template)**: the session in which the daily manipulation (wick) forms; used to infer when and where expansion happens (01:25-02:15).
- **Classic protraction (classic buy/sell day)**: Asia accumulates, London manipulates into a key level and makes the day's extreme (manipulation 2:00-5:00 a.m.), London expands, New York retraces then continues (03:19-04:20). Slide text: "If we manipulate / Protract into a >H4 PDA leading into London Open at 2 AM expect London to expand towards the DOL and New York to offer a continuation higher/lower" (frame 04:39).
- **Delayed protraction (New York reversal profile)**: Asia accumulates, London moves (higher/lower), manipulation delayed into ~3:00-8:30 a.m., often with 8:30 news, reversal in New York against London toward HTF DOL and London's extreme (05:00-06:07).
- **Judas swing (void profile)**: two sessions of accumulation (Asia + London) fail to manipulate the daily range; New York runs their high/low (with SMT), then distributes the other way in a V-shape (06:07-06:44, 11:49-13:42).
- **PD array**: premium/discount array; here swing lows/highs, FVGs, daily imbalance, >H4 levels used as the key level (04:24-04:40, 07:10).
- **CISD**: closure through the consecutive series of down-close (up-close) candles (07:52-08:00).
- **Market maker buy model**: the execution framework; "original consolidation" buy stops are the draw (09:12-09:20).
- **Internal range liquidity**: the 30m/1h FVG inside New York's retracement (08:55-09:05).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Before New York, look back at Asia and London to identify the likely template (classic, delayed, Judas) (14:19-14:35).
2. Establish the HTF draw on liquidity (bias) (14:38-14:48).
3. Identify the HTF key level (daily FVG, >H4 swing) where the day's extreme should form, with SMT vs ES and a CISD for confirmation (07:10-08:02, 10:21-10:36).
4. Classic: London already made the low; trade New York's retracement into an internal FVG for the second expansion leg (08:42-09:12).
5. Delayed: wait for the manipulation into the key level around the New York open / 8:30, smart money reversal + CISD, target London's highs and HTF DOL (10:00-10:47).
6. Judas: no qualified extreme yet; expect New York to run Asia/London highs or lows (with SMT), then fade the move; do not expect FVGs from the run to hold (12:15-13:35).

### Example 2: NQ, delayed protraction (09:54-11:46)

## Mistakes and warnings
- Assuming the day's extreme is in when no HTF key level was engaged; it must be qualified by a key level (12:15-12:36).
- Expecting an FVG created by a Judas run to hold; in a Judas swing you fade that move (13:09-13:35).
- Waiting for New York to return to the London low in a classic protraction; it does not have to (08:07-08:15).

## Conflicts and open questions
- Classic manipulation window is given as "2 a.m. to 5:00 a.m." (04:13) while the low is said to form "between the London open and the New York open" (03:40); the delayed template starts at 3:00 a.m. (05:44). Exact boundaries between classic and delayed (e.g. a low at 6:30 a.m.) are not defined.
- Timezone is not stated (presumably New York time).
- "Key level" qualification is qualitative: >H4 PDA on the slide, daily FVG/4H swing in examples; no rule on minimum timeframe beyond the slide text.
- Judas swing is called a "void profile of manipulation" (02:46); ICT's usual meaning of Judas swing (false move at the open) overlaps but he restricts it to failed Asia+London manipulation.
- Dates of the chart examples are not stated; frame shows the presenter's clock "Thu Sep 5" but not the chart date.
- Method is the guest's (market maker models, PD arrays), not TTFM C2/C3 language; mapping to TTFM daily profiles is left to the reader.
