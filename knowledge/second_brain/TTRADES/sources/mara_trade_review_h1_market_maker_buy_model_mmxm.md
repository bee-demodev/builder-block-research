# MARA Trade Review | H1 Market Maker Buy Model [MMXM]

## TL;DR
An options trade review on MARA (stock) from his ICT era, showing the same logic applies to stocks/options. Daily: an old low is taken, then a V-shaped aggressive expansion higher = reversal, bullish bias. A consolidation forms; being bullish, he waits for its low to be manipulated (run). Hourly (intermediate timeframe): close over the down-close candles that formed the low; the low may be run once more as long as price respects 0.5 of the opposing candle bodies; he went long below the low targeting failure swings (equal highs) above. He frames it as accumulation-manipulation-distribution; the title's "market maker buy model" is never named in speech.

## Definitions
- **Phases of price delivery**: expansion, retracement, reversal, consolidation; used to classify the move after a sweep (00:32, 03:22).
- **Opposing candle / order block**: the down-close candle(s) that formed the low; 0.5 of their bodies is the line not to lose (02:13).
- **Validated / protected low**: a low whose forming down-close candles have been closed over (03:12, 03:49).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Daily: old low taken, then classify the response with phases of price; V-shaped expansion = bullish reversal (00:25-00:57).
2. Mark the consolidation high and low; wait for the low (opposing side) to be run (01:02-01:21).
3. Hourly: wait for an aggressive close over the down-close candles that formed the low (02:00).
4. Allow one more run of the low if 0.5 of the opposing candle bodies holds; buy below the low (02:48-03:04).
5. Target failure swings / range high (01:49).

## Worked examples
