## Mistakes and warnings
- Shorting at perfectly equal highs with the stop just above them when there is no SMT Fix: Only use the high/opposing candles as invalidation when SMT confirms it; otherwise wait for the open. (30:50)
- Entering after the 9:30 move has already happened Fix: Enter pre-open on the 15m continuation, or wait for a new continuation at 10:00. (38:20)
- Chasing a short late in the hourly candle, far from its open or wick Fix: Enter early in the candle, around the open or in the wick. (57:20)
- Selling below the previous day low after the target is already hit (gold/silver) Fix: Sell around the daily open or in the wick, before the first target. (26:40)
- Shorting under the lows after an expansion into them Fix: Expect a new phase of price there; short up in the higher-timeframe candles. (1:38:20)
- Trading 5m swings in London and getting chopped Fix: Prefer the 15m, where consolidation is visible and the stop can go above it. (13:10)
- Judging a deep retest on a very low timeframe and placing stops too tight Fix: If the swing is protected and the trend holds, the trade is valid; size the stop at the protected swing. (1:36:40)
- Full-porting on a 5-second chart Fix: Don't; no sympathy for self-inflicted blowups. (1:37:30)
- Trading the lagging asset (NQ rebounding while YM/ES make the move) Fix: Trade the asset actually delivering, or ES when uncertain. (54:20)
- Sitting on the 1-minute trying to guess moves Fix: Work from higher-timeframe context. (38:10)
