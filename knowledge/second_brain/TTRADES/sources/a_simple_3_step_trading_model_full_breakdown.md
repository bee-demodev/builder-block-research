# A Simple 3-Step Trading Model (Full Breakdown)

## TL;DR
A joint playbook: guest AM Trades teaches step 1 (daily bias from "relevant swings" and "failure swings" on the daily chart) and step 2 (confirming the bias with three 7-hour daily profiles: 18:00 / 01:00 / 08:00 reversal), and TTrades teaches step 3 (Fractal Model entries in the New York session: 7h->15m, or lower-timeframe aligned 1h->5m and 30m->3m). Only two daily candle types are traded: a small-wick reversal at a relevant swing, or a daily continuation that respects a 50% invalidation of the previous candle. The same 50% invalidation is applied to the 01:00 (London) 7h candle when New York is expected to continue. The unifying entry rule is "let the wick of the 8:00 candle form, then trade the body", with stop on the protected swing and a 2R target in every replayed example. Four worked examples (ES/NQ, NQ) use SMT between ES and NQ both to reclassify apparent failure swings and to qualify entry swings.

## Definitions
- **Failure swing**: price reverses from a point, then a later day (or days) makes a deep run back into that point without taking it out; the reversal is "unestablished" (1:07-2:21, page).
- **Relevant swing**: daily high/low that is the current extreme of the range, identified as the original extreme a failure swing ran into, or any daily extreme with valid separation to the swings to its left (2:43-3:04, 10:30).
- **Valid separation**: enough distance between one candidate relevant swing and the next daily high/low that manipulating it does not create a new failure swing to the left (4:20-5:28). Not quantified.
- **Small-wick reversal**: daily candle that opens near a relevant swing, makes a shallow run into it, closes back into range and expands (11:59-13:47). Occurs either as a reversal of the trend or as a continuation after a consolidation (13:16).
- **Daily continuation**: day after a valid reversal at a relevant swing, with an opposing target open and no opposing signs (13:47-14:41, page).
- **Invalidation point (daily)**: 50% of previous daily candle from close to opposing extreme (14:41-16:55).
- **Invalidation point (profile)**: 50% of the 01:00 7h candle from close to opposing swing (27:27-29:05).
- **7h daily profiles**: 18:00 reversal (Asia makes HOD/LOD, London expands), 01:00 reversal (Asia fails/ranges, London runs the Asia range and reverses), 08:00 reversal (both fail; New York manipulates the intraday extreme) (21:18-22:11).
- **Intra-candle CISD (IC-CISD)**: CISD formed inside the higher-timeframe (7h) candle on the 15m or LTF, used to confirm the 8:00 wick (30:39, page).
- **Aligned swing point**: a lower-timeframe swing/closure formed inside the 7h expansion candle, not at its reversal, used for continuation entries (56:32).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Daily (AM Trades): mark relevant swings within ~30 daily candles; discard failure swings unless the correlated index shows SMT there (0:49-11:13, 45:58).
2. Classify today: small-wick reversal candidate (open near a relevant swing) or continuation (prior valid manipulation, opposing target open). If continuation, set 50% invalidation on the previous daily candle (11:48-16:55).
3. Let the 18:00 and 01:00 7h candles print; identify which profile aligns with the bias. For 18:00/01:00 profiles, set the 50% invalidation inside the 01:00 candle (20:07-29:26).
4. In New York (TTrades): choose 7h->15m (pre-9:30) or 1h->5m / 30m->3m (near/after 9:30); mark London EQ / invalidation and POIs (FVG, protected swing) (29:53-36:00).
5. Wait for a sweep into a POI and a close through the series of opposing candles (CISD / continuation) that implies the 8:00 wick is in; check SMT with the correlated index (30:39-31:10, 39:28-40:20).
6. Enter on the close-through or retest; stop on the protected swing (or covering >=50%, ideally 75%, of the opposing candle; on 3m "at least over the bodies, preferably on the high"); target 2R or liquidity to the left (40:20, 51:28, 57:29, 62:01).
7. If the first entry is missed and range remains, take a new continuation swing on the LTF (35:08, 44:24).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: NQ daily walk-through (conceptual bias sequence) (5:41-11:13)
- Monday gaps down into a relevant low (not a failure swing to the left); small wick then expansion into the opposing failure swings (5:41-6:24).
- Close of that day shows a manipulation at the relevant high; next day neutral with valid separation both sides; following day manipulates the high and closes back -> bearish continuation (6:24-7:46).
- Monday ranges without manipulation back into range at the previous-week low -> no long that day (7:46-8:01).
- Deep opposing run into a daily low with separation; NQ looked like failure swings at the lows but ES made a lower low while NQ made a higher low -> SMT, treated as valid bullish manipulation; target opposing relevant high (8:30-10:11).

### Example 2: ES (traded) / NQ (correlated), daily + 7h + 15m/5m/3m, date not shown (35:55-45:29)
- Daily (AM Trades): relevant low with separation to lower daily lows; ES lower low vs NQ higher low = SMT; expectation = another continuation day; invalidation 50% from close to prior-day low; day so far respects it (36:18-37:58).
- 7h: 18:00 candle formed the low, 01:00 expanded away -> 18:00 reversal profile; New York in continuation; invalidation inside the 01:00 candle (37:58-38:44).
- 15m (TTrades): after the 8:00 open price drifts lower; NQ swept a low and tapped an FVG, ES did neither -> SMT; ES closes over the down-close series -> protected low; long, stop on protected low, 2R hit before end of day (39:01-40:33).
- 1h/5m: no candle-2 closure on ES, but NQ printed one into the FVG -> treat as candle 2, trade stronger asset; 5m sweep into FVG then close-through; stop on low; 2R (40:53-42:04).
- 30m/3m: 30m candle-2 closure (engulfing) with two SMTs; 3m continuation closes over one down-close candle; entry on open with stop on low, or wait for a new continuation inside candle 3; both reach 2R (42:11-43:34).
- Late entry: 1h candle 2 then strong candle 3; a 5m continuation is still valid because the wick has formed (44:24-44:50).

### Example 3: NQ (ES correlated), long, London-reversal day, date not shown (44:59-52:56)
- Daily: relevant low engaged Monday (actionable small-wick reversal), then continuation day; no relevant swings near price within 30 candles, so focus on intraweek action; invalidation from close to opposing swing (44:59-46:47).

### Example 4: NQ (ES correlated), short, 01:00-reversal day, date not shown (52:58-58:26)

## Mistakes and warnings
- Trading away from a failure swing (it is a false reversal) (11:13).
- Taking a reversal day that had a large opposing run from the open; wait for the next continuation day instead (14:01-14:11).
- Jumping from bias directly to entry without profile confirmation (17:08-17:58).
- Treating an Asia low as established when London only ranged: New York will likely return to it (24:56).
- Using too-low timeframe stops pre-market: they get swept at the New York open (49:45, 61:55).
- Using a single small opposing candle for the CISD instead of the whole series (50:27).
- Marking relevant swings too far back (beyond ~30 daily candles) (45:58).
- Expecting every timeframe pairing to produce an entry; pick one and accept misses (52:42).

## Glossary additions
- `relevant swing` (relevant high/low): current daily extreme with valid separation, whose manipulation carries weight.
- `failure swing` (failure swing highs/lows, unestablished reversal): deep run back into an initial reversal without taking it out.
- `valid separation`: distance between candidate swings such that a manipulation does not create a new failure swing.
- `18 candle` / `18 reversal` (18:00 reversal, Asia reversal): first 7h candle of the day.
- `1:00 candle` (01:00 reversal, London reversal): second 7h candle.
- `8:00 candle` (08:00 reversal, New York reversal, 8:00 a.m. candle): third 7h candle; the traded one.
- `higher low of day` (caption): mishearing of "high or low of day".
- `candle to closure` (caption): "candle two closure".
- `IC-CISD` (intra candle change in the state of delivery): CISD inside the higher-timeframe candle.
- `small wick reversal`: see Definitions.
- `themarketlens.com`: watermark on AM Trades' diagram slides (frame at 31:17).
