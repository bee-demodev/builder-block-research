# TTrades Top-Down Chart Lessons #1

## TL;DR
First of a series reviewing "the current week's price action" with TTFM concepts (00:02); the chart is EURUSD daily around 1 Aug 2025 (frame at 01:36). He reads daily candles through the phases of price (expansion, retracement, consolidation, reversal; "expansion met with expansion" = reversal), then drops to the hourly to judge the daily candle's internal path (open-high-low-close vs open-low-high-close) and closure strength. The core lesson: a short needs a protected swing (a close below the level that forms opposing candles / CISD); "failure swings" (equal highs without a closing confirmation) are not shortable and tend to be revisited first. Once a protected high forms, the trend is ridden with 30m / 15m / 3m continuation entries (stop on the protected high, 2R or previous days' lows) until price stops respecting the candles.

## Definitions
- **Phases of price**: expansion, retracement, consolidation, reversal; read candle by candle (00:02-00:37).
- **Expansion met with expansion**: an opposite-direction expansion right after an expansion = reversal; on the higher timeframe it shows as a wick (00:37, 06:20, 13:52).
- **Failure swings**: equal highs / lows where price did not close beyond the level, so no protected swing formed; they attract a revisit (01:35, 04:22).
- **Protected swing / protected high**: a swing confirmed by a close beyond the level that forms opposing candles / a CISD; stop goes there (02:59, 05:20, 07:48).
- **Opposing candles**: a pair of candles where the second closes through the first, forming the CISD (02:59, 05:20).
- **T-spot**: the zone that should form the daily upper wick on a bearish day (03:27).
- **0.5 of the CISD**: midpoint of the change-in-state-of-delivery range; a daily close above it on a bearish read is a conflict (03:52).
- **Continuation entry**: expansion, retracement into a FVG, close below the level -> new protected swing -> entry (07:48, 12:20).

## Procedure / checklist
1. On the daily, label the last move (expansion) and wait for the next phase (00:02).
2. If the daily close is indecisive, drop to the hourly and read the candle path (OHLC vs OLHC) and closure strength (02:02-02:30).
3. Find protected swings: is there a close below the level? If not, expect failure swings to be revisited first (04:22-05:20).
4. Wait for a reversal (expansion met with expansion) and a close that forms a protected high (06:20).
5. Mark targets: previous day low and the prior day's low (06:50).
6. Pick an execution frame: 30m entry, 15m entry, or 30m-3m fractal with the TTFM indicator (bias bearish) (07:20, 09:23).
7. Stop on the protected high (or tighter if price should not trade above 50% of the opposing candle); aim 2R or the higher-timeframe lows (07:48, 10:20).
8. Keep taking continuation entries until the protected high is taken (14:25).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: EURUSD 1D, around 1 Aug 2025 (00:02-02:30)
- Expansion down met by bullish expansion (reversal). Consolidation with failure swings on the highs; sweep of the current day's low and a bullish close; expansion from 0.5 of the range higher, but it closes back inside the range -> not enough information on the daily. Date read from the frame at 01:36 ("Fri 01 Aug '25").

### Example 2: EURUSD 1h, the following days (02:30-06:50)
- His model printed a bullish setup then reversed. The daily candle was open-high-low-close (bearish) but closed bullish late -> less than ideal; needs more lower-timeframe confirmation. Next day: model prints a bearish setup but high in the range; opens above the T-spot, trades higher first forming the upper wick; daily close over 0.5 of the CISD = conflict. Expansion lower without a protected swing (failure swings) -> price revisits and takes the equal highs. Reacts at a FVG but fails to close below the needed level, runs the high, then expansion met with expansion and a close below -> protected high. Targets: previous day low and the prior day's low.

### Example 3: EURUSD 30m / 15m / 3m entries (07:20-10:50)
- 30m: expansion, retracement into FVG, protected swing forms; stop on the high, 2R (stop can be tightened since price should not exceed 50% of the opposing candle). A second continuation entry after a close below and reach into a FVG is still valid (2R to lows).
- 15m: same protected-swing level; stop on the high or above the bodies; 2R to the lows. Later consolidation, high swept, new protected swing -> another entry.
- 3m (30m-3m fractal, indicator bias set bearish): candle 2 reversal, CISD and FVG POI; first reach does not close below (not protected), second sweep then close below -> entry, stop on the protected high; cannot get 2R to the current low but targets the previous days' lows.

### Example 4: EURUSD lower timeframe trend management (10:50-15:20)
- Expansion, retracement, expansion; consolidation where he wants the high taken but also does not -> avoids. Breaks down out of the range; waits for a continuation entry instead of trading the breakout. Several protected-high continuation entries; eventually price takes the new protected high -> trend over, matched by a bullish reversal candle on the 30m.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Shorting off failure swings instead of a protected swing (04:22).
- Trading the breakout of a consolidation instead of waiting for the continuation entry (11:51).
- Trading when your ideas conflict (11:18).
- Treating a late-day bullish close as a clean bearish continuation setup (02:59-03:27).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Weak close" / "close into the lows" has no numeric threshold (02:30); 0.5 of the CISD is the only number given (03:52).
- "Failure to close below this level, usually the high gets taken out" (08:48) is immediately followed by a case where it is not taken out.
- T-spot is used without definition here.
- 50% of the opposing candle (07:48) as a tighter stop reference: which candle and whether body or range is not specified.
- He wants equal highs taken in the consolidation yet does not want the current high taken (11:18); no rule resolves that conflict except "avoid".

## Glossary additions
- `failure swings`: equal highs/lows without a confirming close; no protected swing.
- `to R` / `too hard` (08:17 "Can we get too hard to the lows?", 10:20): "2R".
- `retched` (09:50): "reached".
- `T-spot`: daily wick zone that should hold on a bearish/bullish day.
