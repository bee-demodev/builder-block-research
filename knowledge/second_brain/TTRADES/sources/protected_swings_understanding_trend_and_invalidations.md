# Protected Swings - Understanding Trend and Invalidations

## TL;DR
A protected swing is the high or low TTrades expects to hold while the trend continues; it is his invalidation and stop location. It is confirmed by a closure through the series of opposing candles (down-close for a low, up-close for a high) that made the swing, after either (a) a sweep of a short-term high/low, (b) SMT where only the correlated asset swept, or (c) a reach into an FVG. He tracks successive protected swings to stay with the trend (daily bias via closes outside the previous day's range) and enters when a new protected swing forms close to price, stop on the swing, targeting 2R / liquidity / standard deviation projections. This is the structural core under CISD and the fractal model's continuation entries.

## Definitions
- **Protected swing (protected high/low)**: a swing expected to hold while the trend continues; invalidation level (00:18, 14:05).
- **Series of down-close (up-close) candles**: the consecutive opposing candles that made the swing; may be a single candle or just a body (05:30, 12:20).
- **Failure swing**: a swing that did not get swept/protected; liquidity to trade towards (01:00, 14:45).
- **Turtle soup**: sweep of a previous high/low (here PDH) used to frame a reversal (03:50).
- **Anticipated vs formed protected swing**: HTF narrative anticipates where the swing will form; LTF closure confirms it (08:30).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Read daily narrative: sweep of previous day high/low and close back inside (bias), or closures outside the previous day's range (trend) (03:08, 07:43).
2. Drop to the pair timeframe (1h for daily, 15m for 4H) (03:30, 11:34).
3. Wait for a sweep of a short-term high (bearish) / low (bullish), SMT on the correlated asset, or a reach into an FVG (00:40, 00:55, 01:30).
4. Find the series of up-close (down-close) candles that made that swing; mark the level (body open of the series) (02:22).
5. Wait for a candle closure through that level = protected swing (02:22, 06:11).
6. Enter when the new protected swing is close to price; stop on the protected swing; target 2R, liquidity points or standard deviation projections (13:52).
7. Repeat on each new sweep to trail the trend; trend ends when the protected swing is violated (07:43).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Framing a reversal off a turtle soup without the closure; it fails (03:50).
- Using a far protected high as the stop: poor R:R; wait for a new one (13:05).
- Trying to catch the reversal; continuation entries are sufficient (16:00).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Series" boundary: which candles count when the series is interrupted ("ignore this small little candle" at 04:39, "use this level" at 10:10) is left to judgment; a rule engine needs a definition (e.g. ignore opposing candles below a size threshold).
- Close-through level: speech implies the open of the first candle in the series; not stated explicitly.
- "50% is retested" (14:20): 50% of what (the series, the FVG, the leg) is not stated.
- Instrument for Example 2 stated as "our same EU example" (07:43), so Example 1 is EURUSD by inference; Example 1 itself never names it.
- 2R vs liquidity target choice (15:25) is offered as either/or with no selection rule.

- `upclose candles` = up-close candles.
- `candle to closure` = candle 2 closure (03:20).
