# The Phases of Price Action: A Blueprint for Market Movement

## TL;DR
Final (fifth) video of the "phases of price" series. It blends the four phases (expansion, retracement, consolidation, reversal) into one flowchart that always starts at expansion: expansion→retracement→expansion is continuation; expansion→consolidation→expansion is either a breakout (continuation) or a manipulation that, when met by opposite expansion, is a reversal; expansion met with opposite expansion is a reversal. TTrades does not trade the phases alone: he filters them through the daily bias / daily candle closure, protected swings, CISD and EQ, then drops to the 1h, 4H and 15m (TTFM model + indicator) for entries with a 2R minimum. Several examples show him being wrong, skipping moves that never form a protected swing, and letting the next daily closure realign him. The same logic is fractal (1h as HTF, 5m as LTF).

## Definitions
- **Phases of price**: expansion, retracement, consolidation, reversal (00:27). This video blends them.
- **Expansion**: directional move; the starting state of every sequence (00:38). In a true expansion price should not retrace much (07:03).
- **Retracement**: slow, shallow counter-move followed by a close back through the counter-move's candles; continuation signature (03:52, 00:42).
- **Consolidation**: price stuck inside a range (e.g. inside a candle's range, 07:54); also any retracement that fails to form a protected swing (15:38) or that takes too long to close through (13:11); a daily inside bar (17:22).
- **Reversal**: "expansion met with expansion" in the opposite direction (01:13); forms a higher-timeframe wick (04:36, 06:56).
- **Manipulation**: expansion against the move out of a consolidation, which then is met with expansion (01:55, 05:16).
- **Breakout**: expansion out of a consolidation in the trend direction (02:06).
- **Continuation signature**: retracement or consolidation after expansion (00:45, 27:20).
- **TT Phases Flowchart**: slide (01:32) with Expansion at top branching to Consolidation and Retracement, Retracement leading to Expansion (green = "move in line with the current trend").

## Procedure / checklist
1. Set daily bias from the daily candle closure (and daily phases) (05:39, 16:46).
2. Identify the current phase on the daily / 1h: after any expansion ask "retracement, consolidation or reversal?" (03:43, 04:40).
3. Map the phase to the bias: bullish wants retracement to continue, or consolidation low run, or breakout + retracement; bearish mirrors (05:46).
4. Require confirmation: daily closure aligned + CISD + expansion met with expansion (for reversals) (10:46, 14:13).
5. Mark protected swings; no protected swing → no trade (13:33).
6. Use the 4H closure to profile the day (candle 2 closure) (18:14), then the 15m TTFM model/indicator for entry (18:40).
7. Stop on the protected swing/high; target 2R minimum, extend to previous day low(s), session lows, daily open (19:22, 25:33).
8. Invalidate if the opposite expansion-met-with-expansion takes the swing, or price trades over EQ in a retracement (11:14, 14:59).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Flipping bias intraday because a low is taken; keep the bias until expansion opposite forms a reversal (07:17).
- Taking a nice candle close as a reversal without a CISD (08:57).
- Trading an intraday reversal that is not paired with the daily closure; wait for alignment (14:13-14:28).
- Forcing a long in a messy move with no low run / no protected swing; accept missing it (13:33-13:51).
- Calling something a retracement when it forms no protected swing; it is a consolidation (15:38).

## Conflicts and open questions
