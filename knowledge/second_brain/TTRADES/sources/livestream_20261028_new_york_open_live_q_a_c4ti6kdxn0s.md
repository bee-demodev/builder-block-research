## Mistakes and warnings
- Gambling on news releases for dopamine. Fix: Don't trade the release; wait for the event (or the next day) and trade the model. (17:30)
- Building ideas from super low timeframes (e.g. a 15m continuation in Asia at 22:00) and asking without giving asset or context. Fix: Form the idea on the hourly or higher, execute on 15m/1m; state the asset and context when asking. (18:00)
- Taking a scalp late in a higher-timeframe expansion. Fix: Be in C3 or early C4, otherwise wait for a new phase of price. (21:05)
- Overusing SMT, including inside consolidations. Fix: Ignore SMT in consolidation; choose instruments by relative strength. (31:40)
- Pattern-trading any FVG / order block seen on the chart. Fix: Only take moves you can frame from bias and closures; use mechanical targets. (42:40)
- Expecting the indicator or model to have a fixed win rate. Fix: An indicator is not an algo; results depend on execution, R:R and sizing. (34:00)
- Scalping before FOMC / right after FOMC without a strong daily bias. Fix: Expect little volatility pre-event; prefer trading the day after FOMC. (15:00)
- Shorting the strong index (YM) when the idea is bearish indices. Fix: Short the weakest asset (NQ today) by relative strength. (16:30)
