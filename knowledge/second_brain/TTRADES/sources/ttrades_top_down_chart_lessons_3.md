# TTrades Top-Down Chart Lessons #3

## TL;DR
Trade review of a CAD futures (6C) long. TTrades starts from a bearish dollar read (monthly bearish candle 2 closure toward the previous month's low, daily candle 2 closure with an hourly CISD and V-shaped reversal), converts it into bullish foreign-currency bias, then picks the currency future with the cleanest daily structure: CAD, with an "ideal" daily candle 2 closure and protected swing. On the hourly he requires the next daily wick to form in the upper half of the opposing daily candle (the T-spot), takes a positional entry overnight off a new protected swing, and exits about 2.3R at a 30m consolidation high in New York. He shows the mirrored USDCAD short and admits GBPUSD was the stronger asset that day.

## Definitions
- **Ideal candle 2 closure**: candle 2 that takes the prior low, reverses V-shaped from an FVG, and closes back in while forming a protected swing (02:34).
- **Daily opposing candle respected**: the next daily candle's wick forms above 0.5 of the prior (opposing) daily candle (03:04).
- **Positional entry**: entry near an existing invalidation without waiting for intraday session confirmation (03:56).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Read the dollar (monthly candle 2, draw on liquidity, phases of price) (00:25).
2. Daily candle 2 closure plus hourly CISD and V-shape (01:31).
3. Invert bias for foreign currencies; scan futures; pick the cleanest daily structure (01:54).
4. Mark 0.5 of the opposing daily candle; the next day's wick should form in the upper half / T-spot (03:04).
5. On hourly, find protected swings (reach into FVG, close over) for invalidation (03:29).
6. Enter, stop on protected low, target the high; manage by phases of price (30m consolidation) (04:12-04:52).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: 6C (CAD futures) daily to 1h, long (00:25-05:37)
- Chart axis shows the replay bar at Tue 02 Dec '25 (frame 02:53, 04:00); the daily candle 2 is that day; the entry is overnight into the next day.
- Dollar: monthly bearish C2 toward previous month's low and equal lows; daily C2 closure; hourly CISD and V-shape.
- 6C daily: low taken, V-shape, opens into an FVG, ideal C2 closure with protected swing; targets previous month's high and equal highs above.
- Hourly: indicator marks the level; wick should hold the upper half; two protected swings from FVG reach and close over; entry where price respected the zone, stop on protected low, target the high.
- New York: over 2R; three expansion candles; 30m consolidation; TP at the consolidation high; exit about 2.3R. Price later expanded further (monthly expansion); he day-traded only.

### Example 2: USDCAD mirror (05:37-06:40)
- Bearish candle 2 closure; protected swings including one reaching into an FVG and closing below; price opens into an order block; same entry and stop give about the same 2R result; later daily/monthly expansion lower.

### Example 3: GBPUSD comparison (06:40-07:23)
- Same time as the CAD entry, GBPUSD made a much larger hourly expansion; CAD had better structure but was not the strongest asset that day.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Picking the cleanest structure can still miss the strongest asset (06:56).
- After three expansion candles, do not assume more; check for consolidation (04:31).

## Conflicts and open questions
- He says the CAD target is "previous month's high and then also our previous month's high" (02:45), a repeated phrase; second reference probably meant previous week's or another high, unclear.
- DXY is used for the dollar read but the symbol shown in frames is "USD"; the exact dollar ticker is not named in speech.
- The exact entry candle time is not stated; only "overnight" and exit in New York.

## Glossary additions
- `6C` (CAD futures, "Canadian futures"): Canadian dollar futures.
- Mishearings: "candle to closure" = candle 2 closure, "drawn liquidity" = draw on liquidity.
