# AP | Use Market Structure & Fibonacci Levels for Sniper Entries

> [!warning] Guest framework. Everything below except the 00:05 intro and 68:41 outro is AP ("AP Trades"), a guest on the "TT Talk" series, not TTrades. AP says he is not an ICT trader and credits DTFX for fractal structure; these are not TTFM rules.

## TL;DR
AP presents his "GB levels" (nickname "Yeet"), a custom Fibonacci retracement set (0.295, 0.41, 0.705, 0.7475, 0.79, anchored 0 at the new extreme and 1 at the protected/valid swing) used with strict body-close market structure. Technique 1: after a valid structure flip (body close through the last valid low/high), blind-limit the retrace at 0.7475 and exit at 0.41/0.295 (consolidating markets) or a new extreme (expanding markets). Technique 2: watch 0.41/0.295 from the far end; a body close back through 0.41 (he estimates ~80%) signals the deep retrace to 0.705-0.79, best after displacement away and a retest. He stacks confluence (unmitigated order block / "CSS", imbalance, wick 50%, peak-consolidation origin, "S&D structure") on the levels, shows it from daily to 5-second MNQ charts, uses SMT only at weak highs/lows, and recaps a 2025-03-13 long (~120 points). Relevance to TTFM: overlaps with protected swings, wick EQ (he praises TTrades' wick videos) and continuation order blocks, but the fib levels and percentages are AP's own.

## Definitions
- **GB levels / "Yeet" / GB puddle**: AP's fib set 0.295, 0.41, 0.705, 0.7475, 0.79; 0 = anchor (newest extreme), 1 = valid/protected swing (06:52, 14:12).
- **0.295 origin (his words)**: "the polar opposite on an opposite end scale of this 7475"; **0.41**: "a negative one deviation away from this 705" (07:02).
- **Valid flip / market structure shift**: body close through the last valid low/high (08:05).
- **Strong low / weak high**: the low that produced the break above a high is strong (should not be revisited); the opposite high is weak (draw) (28:18, 30:41).
- **Failure swing**: a retest that fails to make a new high/low; makes the lows that built it weak (16:39, 33:51).
- **Body close (AP)**: a true close of the body beyond the wick of the key level; a heavy wick without it means support holds (18:04).
- **Fractal (AP)**: a wick-high/wick-low pair whose body close through forms a lower-timeframe swing zone; also a 3-candle pivot of an imbalance (21:36, 40:24).
- **CSS**: order-block-like zone with stricter rules (a recent break-of-structure candle) (44:37, 52:01).
- **S&D structure**: the internal structure responsible for the body close through a low/high (25:02, 35:34).
- **Devil's mark**: his name for a wickless candle (23:00).
- **Peak consolidation area**: sideways high-volume pocket that retracements are drawn to (39:42).
- **Double tap**: return to a prominent swing area that liquidates the first-tap low before reversing (57:52).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Identify HTF context: POI (imbalance, order block), weak high/low, failure swings (09:45, 16:39).
2. Wait for a valid flip: body close through the last valid low/high; ideally the second continuation break (10:02, 36:13).
3. Anchor the fib: 0 at the new extreme, 1 at the protected swing (14:21).
4. Technique 1: blind limit at 0.7475 (or 0.705/0.79), confluence with order block/imbalance/peak consolidation; target 0.41/0.295 in consolidation or new extreme in expansion (10:42-11:57).
5. Technique 2: from the extreme, watch 0.295 then 0.41; on a body close beyond 0.41 with displacement and a retest (wick, 50% of a swing zone, order block), trade back to 0.705-0.79 (13:09, 20:18, 37:35).
6. Out-of-range continuation: after a body close beyond 0.295, look for the return to 0.41 (31:48).
7. Optional confirmation instead of blind limits: body close back through 0.705, then re-entry (53:18).
8. Manage: trail/scale; SMT only at weak extremes (32:48, 33:13).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Marking structure shifts from unvalidated right-edge lows; most are just retracements (08:12).
- Overfitting one strategy to one market condition (EMA crossover failed in consolidation) (02:42).
- Treating every first flip as a reversal; fake flips away from HTF areas (36:22).
- Executing at 50% of a whole range (30:25).
- Inner-range SMT is noise (33:35).
- Direct imbalances against you mean deeper drawdown (41:20).

## Conflicts and open questions
- His derivation of 0.295 ("polar opposite" of 0.7475) does not check arithmetically: 1-0.7475 = 0.2525, while 1-0.705 = 0.295; likewise 0.705-0.295 = 0.41, not a "-1 deviation" (07:02).
- Order of levels in speech is confusing: in the bearish mock the retrace goes "up" to 0.7475 and exits "down" at 0.41/0.295, so 0 is the new low and 1 the protected high (14:21); a rule engine must anchor 0 at the newest extreme.
- "80% of the time" after a close above 0.41 is an unmeasured estimate (13:21); "90 to 95% of my trades" revolve around GB levels (10:42).
- Some quoted prices are garbled: "21,09350" (16:06) = 21,093.50; "1895275" (22:47) on NQ from memory; "86550" (25:50) unclear; "20,76" (34:29) truncated; "21615"/"22040"/"21825" at 50:10-52:25 appear to be clock times or prices, ambiguous.

- No stop rule for technique 1 besides blind limits; stop "whatever you want to risk" (60:07).
