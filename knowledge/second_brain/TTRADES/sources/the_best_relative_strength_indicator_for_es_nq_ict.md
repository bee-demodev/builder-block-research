# The Best Relative Strength Indicator For ES & NQ - ICT

## TL;DR
TTrades explains relative strength/weakness between ES and NQ and how to pick the instrument per direction: shorts on the relatively weaker index, longs on the relatively stronger one. He judges strength from swing points (who takes / fails to take the shared swing), candle closures (who closes back inside vs outside a range), candle size, and how each respects order block mean thresholds and FVG/wick consequent encroachment; SMT is the explicit form of the same divergence. His tool: a TradingView chart of ES divided by NQ (ES/NQ): rising = ES stronger, falling = NQ stronger. Walked day-by-day on a daily week and a Friday 5m example, then a "theory": reversals on the ES/NQ chart mark a change in correlation and can lead the actual reversal on ES/NQ ("an SMT without an SMT"). Feeds TTFM's asset-strength / SMT selection step.

## Definitions
- **Relative strength / weakness**: one of two compared assets being more bullish / bearish than the other (00:03).
- **ES/NQ chart (ES divided by NQ)**: TradingView spread chart; up = ES relatively stronger, down = NQ relatively stronger (04:22-04:48). Cannot be bar-replayed (06:02).
- **SMT**: divergence at a swing between correlated assets, e.g. higher low on one vs lower low on the other (03:08).
- **Mean threshold**: 50% of an order block body (01:54, 07:52).
- **Consequent encroachment (CE)**: 50% of a wick or FVG (07:26, 09:45).
- **Change in correlation**: ES/NQ chart reversing, flipping which index leads (11:13-11:38).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Form the HTF expectation (here daily: move into a FVG, then expansion) (05:35).
2. Read ES/NQ direction to set which index should be stronger (06:02-06:57).
3. Each day, confirm with swings, closures in/out of ranges, OB mean threshold and CE reach (07:26-09:16).
4. Drop to 5m: short the weaker index into its HTF target; long the stronger index from its POI with SMT and displacement (09:45-10:45).
5. Optional: watch ES/NQ reversals near key times (9:30, 10:00) as an early sign of reversal on ES/NQ (11:38-13:03).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: ES vs NQ daily, Wed 19 July and Mon 10 (00:56-04:22)
- Swing high on Wednesday the 19th of July: next candle ES small down, NQ massive drop; on return ES takes the swing high, NQ does not get close → NQ weak (00:56-01:25, 03:33).
- Order block: NQ closes out of the OB open and respects its mean threshold, pushes lower; ES trades through to Monday's high, sweeps it (SMT) then closes back below (01:54-02:24).
- Monday the 10th swing: NQ trades down to it and makes a low, ES nowhere close → SMT, ES stronger (03:53-04:22).

### Example 2: daily week walk-through with ES/NQ chart (05:12-09:16)
- Expectation: move down into a daily FVG, then expansion; predicted ES stronger after Friday (05:35-06:02).
- Friday: ES/NQ moved down then up; NQ reached into the OB and closed back inside the range, ES reached the FVG but not the OB and closed below → NQ stronger that day (06:02-06:57).
- Monday: reaction out of the FVG; ES moves higher and almost reaches the wick CE, NQ nowhere close (06:57-07:26).
- Next day: both take lows; ES sweeps and returns almost to OB mean threshold, closes inside the OB body; NQ does not reach mean threshold, closes outside (07:52-08:20).
- Next day: ES takes the low but closes back inside; NQ respects OB mean threshold then closes below the low (08:20).
- Next: NQ reaches mean threshold, sweeps a low; ES reaches Monday's high into the wick CE; both take the previous day low, ES back inside, NQ outside (08:50-09:16).

### Example 3: Friday 5m, short NQ / long ES (09:16-10:45)
- HTF levels: NQ = last month's low / monthly FVG; ES = daily FVG with CE marked (09:45).
- NQ barely reaches into its FVG and displaces lower; ES reaches the CE first. In another FVG NQ bodies respect CE, up-close candle, displacement lower; ES reaches above the FVG first (09:45-10:15).
- Long ES: SMT at the low, displacement up, return to CE of the FVG, run to the morning highs; NQ fails to take the morning highs → SMT (10:15-10:45).

### Example 4: ES/NQ chart reversal at 9:30 (11:38-12:35)
- ES/NQ sweeps a high and falls back in range at 9:30; ES/NQ indices reverse at the open; falling ratio = NQ stronger: NQ closes over a high while ES struggles, plus SMT (11:38-12:35).

### Example 5: ES/NQ high at 10:00 leads 10:05 low (12:35-13:03)
- Low at 9:35 on ES/NQ marked; ES/NQ chart high at 10:00, while ES and NQ do not put in their low until 10:05 → ratio led the reversal (12:35-13:03).
