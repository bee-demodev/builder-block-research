# Draw on Liquidity (DOL) - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
How he finds the draw on liquidity each morning to set his daily bias. The draw timeframe depends on the entry timeframe: entering on 5m and lower, he looks for the draw on the daily, 4H and 1h; entering on the 1h he would look to the weekly and monthly. Draws are external liquidity (old highs/lows, equal highs/lows) and internal liquidity (FVGs, order blocks, occasionally volume imbalances / gaps). The deciding signal is displacement: follow where price displaces to, and where it fails to displace through a high or low, expect the opposite side. Long NQ 4H walk-through of alternating draws, then a live S&P example: nested daily / 4H / 1h FVGs, 15m gap respected, 8:00 sweep of internal lows with SMT vs NASDAQ, 1m order block long targeting 39.50 just before the draw.

## Definitions
- **Draw on liquidity (DOL)**: where the market is likely to reach next, external or internal liquidity on the HTF (00:35).
- **External liquidity**: old highs/lows, equal highs/lows (00:35).
- **Internal liquidity**: FVGs, order blocks, occasionally volume imbalances and gaps (01:02).
- **Failure to displace**: taking a level without a displacing close through it; points the other way (01:02, 04:53).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Pick the draw timeframes from the entry timeframe: 5m and lower -> daily/4H/1h; 1h -> weekly/monthly (00:06).
2. List external and internal liquidity above and below (00:35, 01:02).
3. Read the last displacement: follow it; at each taken level ask whether price displaced through or failed (01:31, 03:12).
4. Update the draw as each level is reached (02:37-05:22).
5. Check nesting of FVGs across timeframes and SMT at sweeps (05:56, 06:26).
6. Lower timeframe entry toward the draw; target just before it (07:29).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: NQ 4H walk-through (01:31-05:56)
- Gap up then aggressive move lower -> draw = lows below. Low taken, no bullish displacement, falls lower. Displacement up -> draw = high and FVG (reached with a volume imbalance). OTE-to-OTE draw back to FVG plus previous low. No displacement below -> reach up through the volume imbalance; equal highs left as later target. Failure to take a high, aggressive move lower; no displacement below a low; up without displacement; OB respected -> lows taken; no displacement back up -> lower; low not displaced through -> old highs / equal highs run. Displacement above a high -> next old high targeted (current price).
### Example 2: S&P futures, "today" (05:56-08:05)
- Daily OB mean threshold respected; daily FVG above with nested 4H and 1h FVGs. 15m before RTH: gap respected and displaced over; at 8:00 drop into an FVG running internal lows, SMT vs NASDAQ. 1m open: sloppy, highs then lows taken, SMT held, move higher; breaker present but he used the order block; long, stop on the low, target 39.50 just before the draw.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Switching bullish before displacement up (02:03).
- Being scared out by lower timeframe chop when the HTF draw is clear (07:29).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Displacement" vs "failure to displace" has no numeric test; he judges closes and candle size visually.
- Rule 15: rising without displacement means the high "isn't being targeted", yet elsewhere a lack of displacement below implies higher; the engine needs a symmetric definition.
- 39.50 is a partial price (S&P handle not said); date not said ("today").
- He says he would not have looked long without the draw (07:29), so the 1m entry depends on the HTF read.
