# Optimal Trade Entry (OTE) - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Early TTrades ICT lesson on the optimal trade entry (OTE): a Fibonacci retracement drawn swing high to swing low (bearish) or low to high (bullish) with levels 0, 0.5, 0.62, 0.705, 0.79, 1; the OTE zone is 0.62-0.79 with 0.705 as its midpoint. Price retracing into the OTE is where he looks for an entry or a lower-timeframe entry model, ideally where the OTE overlaps another PD array (FVG, up/down-close candles / order block mean threshold, breaker). New swings create new ranges, so OTEs are re-drawn fractally on lower timeframes. Worked on EURUSD daily→1h, gold 1h→5m→1m (3R, kill-zone filter) and ES 5m (TGIF setup, std-dev projection, 3.4R). In TTFM terms the OTE is the retracement-into-discount/premium step of a C3/C4 continuation entry.

## Definitions
- **OTE (optimal trade entry)**: the 0.62-0.79 retracement of a swing range, midpoint 0.705 (00:37-01:05, 02:49).
- **Premium / discount split**: the 0.5 level of the swing range (00:37).
- **Mean threshold**: 50% of an order block's body (05:19).
- **Displacement range**: the range of an aggressive move used to draw the OTE (05:49-06:14).
- **Failure swings**: repeated swing highs that failed; liquidity target (10:12).
- **TGIF setup**: Friday move expected to cap the weekly range after the weekly objective is hit (09:42). Named, not detailed.

## Procedure / checklist
1. Identify the swing range (swing high → swing low for shorts, low → high for longs) (01:05-02:08).
2. Draw fib 0 / 0.5 / 0.62 / 0.705 / 0.79 / 1 (00:37).
3. Look left for PD arrays inside 0.62-0.79 (FVG, up/down-close candles, OB mean threshold, breaker) (03:50-05:19).
4. If on a higher timeframe, drop a timeframe and watch the OTE retrace there (07:59).
5. Only act inside the kill zone (07:59-08:42).
6. Enter on the LTF FVG / order block after displacement out of the OTE; 0.705 preferred, 0.62 if eager (08:42, 11:17).
7. Stop at the swing extreme; target pre-session liquidity or a 2-2.5 std-dev projection lining up with old swings (09:14, 10:42).
8. After the move, re-draw the OTE on the new range (05:49).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: bearish and bullish fib draws, TradingView (01:37-05:19)
- Bearish: swing high → swing low, retrace hits the 0.705 then reaches for sell side liquidity below (03:18). OTE overlaps a FVG and up-close candles (04:49).
- Bullish: swing low → swing high; move out of OTE up into a FVG above (03:50). Order block (down-close candle that swept lows, closed over) has its open outside the OTE, so its mean threshold is used; a volume imbalance was also respected (04:49-05:19).

### Example 2: EURUSD daily → 1h (05:49-07:27)
- Daily displacement range; price retraces into the OTE (06:14).
- 1h: the retrace into the OTE fills an hourly FVG; displacement lower, fib tracked down with each new low; retrace into OTE then lower; takes a low and displaces up; new OTE lines up with a breaker, nice reaction (06:14-07:27).

### Example 3: Gold 1h → 5m → 1m short (07:27-09:42)
- 1h displacement down; fib adjusted on the new low; FVG inside the OTE (07:27-07:59).
- 5m: equal lows form (liquidity). First touch of the OTE is out of the kill zone → ignored (07:59).
- In kill zone: hits the .79, bodies respect the hourly FVG, displacement lower → 5m FVG (08:42).
- 1m: full-bodied 1m candle reaches into the 5m FVG then displaces lower = order block; entry there, stop on the high, target pre-session / London lows → 3R (08:42-09:14).

### Example 4: ES daily → 5m long, TGIF (09:42-12:29)
- Daily: Tuesday puts in the high of the week, expansion Wednesday and Thursday, weekly objective (daily swing low) taken → anticipating a TGIF setup to cap the weekly range (09:42).
- 5m: daily open marked; expects a New York reversal higher toward failure swings and the daily open (09:42-10:12).
- Sweep then displacement up; projection from that high to low: 2-2.5 sits just above the failure swings (10:12-10:42).
- Close over highs, OTE from the low to the current high, tracked until a retracement; entry at 0.62 (0.705 preferred), target the highs → 3.4R (10:42-11:17).
- Price swept into the OTE midpoint, displaced up (FVG entry also available), hit the daily open (resistance), eventually swept the failure swings (11:53).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Do not act on OTE touches outside the kill zone (07:59).
- Do not draw the fib before the swing is confirmed; keep dragging it as the range extends (02:08, 06:51).
