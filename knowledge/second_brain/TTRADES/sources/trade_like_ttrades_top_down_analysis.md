# Trade Like TTrades - Top Down Analysis

## TL;DR
TTrades explains why a lower-timeframe pattern (e.g. a bullish order block) fails when it fights the higher-timeframe candle, and how to stack three timeframes: bias (HTF candle direction), structure (intermediate timeframe: CISD confirms the HTF wick, provides POIs and protected lows/highs) and entry (lower timeframe: CISD / order block / propulsion block off the POI). Expansion happens only when all three are aligned. Four walk-throughs: 1D-1h-5m (unnamed symbol), silver 1W-4h-15m, USDJPY 1D-1h-5m, gold 1D-1h-5m. Stops go on the low/high the setup expects to hold; first target 2R (or the HTF objective), runners to HTF targets or the indicator's standard-deviation projections. This is the core TTFM timeframe-pairing logic in its earlier "order block / closure through the series" vocabulary.

## Definitions
- **Bias / structure / entry timeframes**: HTF gives direction, intermediate gives CISD confirmation and POIs, LTF gives the entry (02:02-02:21, 02:30-02:42).
- **Change in the state of delivery (CISD)**: a close through the series of down-close (bullish case) or up-close (bearish case) candles that made the extreme; may be a single candle (03:53-04:01, 07:08-07:17).
- **Order block / opposing candles**: the series of opposite-close candles at a swing; a close through them makes it valid (00:29-00:36, 05:33-05:40).
- **Propulsion block**: an order block formed on a retrace into a POI that price then closes over; used for continuation entries (05:37-05:43, 17:21-17:28).
- **High-resistance liquidity (as a low)**: a swing low that has already been swept; its wick 0.5 becomes support (02:44-02:59).
- **Expansion candle**: HTF candle with small wicks and a large body (07:49-07:55).
- **Failure swings**: lows that failed to make new extremes, marked as draw (07:21-07:25).
- **New phase of price delivery**: after expansion: consolidation, retracement or reversal (16:15-16:24).

## Procedure / checklist
1. HTF (daily or weekly): read the candle closure (swing point, close beyond previous bar or close back inside) to get directional bias and the HTF objective (01:48-01:58, 02:44-03:20).
2. Structure timeframe (1h or 4h): wait for price to reach a POI (FVG, opposing candle, sweep of a previous low/high) and for a CISD confirming the HTF wick (03:25-04:31, 07:41-08:51).
3. Mark targets on the structure timeframe (previous day high, equal lows/highs, standard deviations) (08:54-09:04, 12:04-12:08).
4. Entry timeframe (5m or 15m): wait for a CISD through the series of candles into the POI; enter on the close (preferred) or the retest (04:35-05:18, 09:15-10:01).
5. Stop on the low/high the setup expects to hold (at least beyond the bodies) (05:11, 14:49).
6. Take 2R at a short-term high/low; hold runners to HTF objectives or indicator projections (14:58-15:09, 18:54-19:13).
7. Prefer continuation entries off new protected lows/highs (05:21-05:54).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Trading a pattern without the HTF: stops on the "correct" LTF low get run (00:48-00:56).
- Lacking HTF analysis means looking the wrong way and getting caught offside (10:54-11:02).
- Entering too close to obvious opposing lows; wait for a close over the leg (14:30-14:46).

## Glossary additions
- `propulsion block`: continuation order block formed on a retrace into a POI.
- `high resistance liquidity` (as a low): a low already swept; its 0.5 wick acts as support.
- `failure swings`: lows/highs that failed to extend, used as draw.
