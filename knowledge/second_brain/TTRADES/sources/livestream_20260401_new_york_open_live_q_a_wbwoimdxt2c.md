## Mistakes and warnings
- Fading the daily candle (shorting a bullish daily on day three) because intraday looks bearish Fix: Treat it as a reversal that needs extra confirmation, or don't trade it (18:00)
- Using SMT to frame trades Fix: Use SMT only as confirmation after price displaces away (40:20)
- Watching every timeframe's closes while in a trade Fix: Define invalidation; only check the HTF candle your setup is based on (35:50)
- Trying to catch every move / force trades when confused Fix: Have one idea, wait for it to occur or invalidate; when confused, don't place a trade (1:00:20)
- Acting on emotions (tilt) and blowing accounts Fix: Feel the emotion but don't act on it; step away (e.g. move to paper) (1:04:20)
- Repeating the blow-up cycle for years without addressing the cause Fix: Find the root cause (overtrading, no system, oversize) and apply the matching fix, or quit (16:00)
- Trading the weaker instrument (e.g. GBP) when bullish Fix: Trade the strongest for longs, the weakest for shorts (22:00)
- Pairing a bullish currency with a consolidating one (GBPJPY) Fix: Pair bullish with bearish (GBPUSD) (1:08:20)
- CRT-style pattern trading that targets only the next candle's range Fix: Target real higher-timeframe objectives (35:50)
- Trading 15-second/30-second charts Fix: Use higher timeframes; very few sustain ultra-low timeframes (40:20)
- Using DXY direction to decide index trades Fix: Ignore dollar for indices; correlation is not reliable lately (52:20)
- Getting drawn in by big moves inside a range Fix: Recognise a range draws people in both ways; stay out (26:00)
