# My Favorite Reversal Pattern | Orderblocks - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
His favorite reversal / entry model: price reaches an important level (PD array or buy/sell-side liquidity), puts in a swing on the lower timeframe, then raids that swing; the up-close (or down-close) candle or series that made the raid becomes an order block once price closes beyond it, and its opening price is expected to support price in the new direction. Shown on DXY daily, USOIL daily, NQ 5m and gold weekly -> 4H, with standard-deviation projections of the manipulation leg (2-2.5, 4) as targets. This is the same mechanism as CISD (close through the series that swept) framed as order-block formation.

## Definitions
- **Important level**: PD array or buy-side / sell-side liquidity that price reaches (00:10).
- **Raid**: price runs a lower-timeframe swing at the important level (00:10).
- **Order block**: the up-close (bearish) or down-close (bullish) candle or series that made the raid, validated when price closes beyond it; reference = opening price (00:39, 01:36).
- **Market structure shift**: the close below/above the order block series (08:01).
- **Standard deviation projection**: projection of the manipulation leg; targets 2-2.5 then 4 (08:01).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Mark an important level (HTF FVG, previous month high, weekly FVG, daily swing low) (00:10, 02:39, 04:22).
2. Wait for a swing to form near it and get raided, ideally by a full-bodied candle (00:10, 04:50).
3. Identify the candle/series that made the raid; wait for a close beyond it (validation / MSS) (00:39, 05:21).
4. Mark its opening price; expect it to support price on the retest; bodies must respect it (01:36, 05:21).
5. Target opposite liquidity and/or 2-2.5 and 4 SD projections of the manipulation leg (03:44, 08:01).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: DXY daily (01:07-02:39)
- Daily FVG above is the POI; swing low forms, FVG filled, next day sweeps the lows into the POI. Close over the high of the down-close candle that swept -> order block at its opening price. FVG also present. Retest closes outside the opening price; next day moves higher toward the high.

### Example 2: USOIL daily (02:39-04:22)
- Previous month high above, weekly FVG below. Full-bodied up-close candle into the POI, closed below -> bearish OB; retest and displacement lower; sell-side swept, FVG created; reaches the weekly FVG. Low swept at the POI, close over the high -> bullish OB overlapping an FVG; displacement out to buy-side highs.

### Example 3: NQ 5m (prior frame read: ~8 Nov 2023) (04:22-06:29)
- Daily swing low (sell-side) hit; weak initial displacement, highs not broken. Full-bodied down-close candle sweeps (LTF sweep), then displacement and close over the high -> OB; FVG below; wicks into it, bodies respect the OB; price moves higher.

### Example 4: Gold weekly -> 4H (frames ~Jul-Aug 2023) (06:29-11:11)
- Weekly: swing high swept, no close above, close back below the lows -> up-close candles = OB POI (weekly FVG also there). 4H: swing high just below the POI swept; strong close below the up-close candles = OB / MSS. Projects the manipulation leg (2-2.5, 4). Hits the 1st SD, sweep with a down-close candle, closes over it, retraces up into the OB; bodies respect it. New LTF sweep + displacement lower -> new OB and MSS; projections from successive legs overlap; 1st SD retracement to the OB again, close below, supports lower; reaches the 2nd SD; another swept-high OB and FVG; new low (07:02-10:40).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Without a full-bodied candle into the level and real displacement, the setup is weaker (04:50).
- Judge the retest on bodies/closes, not wicks (02:08, 05:21).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Close below this up-close candle" (00:39) vs the opening-price reference (01:36): whether validation requires a close below the candle's low, its open, or the series' first open is not pinned down here (CISD video uses the first candle's open).
- "Ideally below this low" (07:02): whether the swing-low break is required or optional is left open.
- Projection signs (2-2.5 vs negative 2) and anchor (which leg is "manipulation") are discretionary.
- Instruments/dates for examples 3-4 come from an earlier frame read, not speech.
