# Order Blocks Simplified - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
ICT-era lesson on order blocks: an order block is the single candle or series of opposite-close candles into an important (higher-timeframe) level, validated when price sweeps liquidity, displaces and closes beyond that candle's (or series') opening price. Without a higher-timeframe level the pattern "doesn't mean anything" (01:23). He covers the order block at the smart money reversal, order blocks on the buy side / sell side of the curve for continuation (into a previous order block, an FVG, or a sweep), the mean threshold (50% of the bodies) as a better-RR entry and as the respect/invalidation line, and stops on the swing low/high rather than the order block's far end. The "close over the opening price of the down-close series" is the same closure later called CISD in TTFM (he says "change in the state of delivery" at 05:37).

## Definitions
- **Order block**: single or series of up-close (bearish) / down-close (bullish) candles into an important level, validated by displacement and a close beyond its opening price (00:38-01:05).
- **Important level**: higher-timeframe PD array: previous order block, FVG, or a sweep (01:23, 06:52).
- **Mean threshold**: 50% of the order block bodies, body low to body high (04:03).
- **Smart money reversal**: the first OB after a sweep that flips direction (06:52).
- **Buy side / sell side of the curve**: continuation OBs after the reversal, in the new trend (06:52).
- **Failure swings**: swing highs used as the target (05:37).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Mark the HTF level (1h FVG, OB, liquidity) and the draw on liquidity (02:56, 12:33).
2. Wait on the 5m for price to reach into the level (02:56).
3. Wait for a sweep of a swing low/high (03:28, 04:56).
4. Wait for displacement and a close beyond the opening price of the opposite-close candle / series (lowest body for a series) (03:28, 08:45).
5. Entry: retest of the OB opening price; if RR is poor (large series) use the mean threshold (04:29, 06:06).
6. Stop on the swing low/high; target liquidity / failure swings or 2R (01:54, 14:03).
7. After the reversal, take continuation OBs into OB / FVG / sweep (06:52).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: 5m, invalid OB (01:54-02:56)
- Buy stops swept, close below the up-close series; short on the retest, stop on the high, 2R target. Stopped out: no 1h level; the candles just made equal highs in a retracement higher.
### Example 2: same chart, valid long (02:56-03:28)
- 1h FVG below; price reaches into it, sweeps, closes over the down-close candle's open; long on the retest, stop on the low, target buy-side or 2R; take profit hit.
### Example 3: oil 5m, mean threshold (04:29-06:38)
- Into a 1h FVG, extra lower-timeframe sweep, close over the down-close series. OB-open entry with stop at the low and failure-swing target = 1.3R; mean-threshold entry ~2.6R; target reached after a while.
### Example 4: New York open after Asia/London consolidation (07:46-10:13)
- Sell stops under Asia lows swept, close over the down-close series. OB entry only 1.4R; FVG entry ~2R. Later a buy-side OB after a sweep: opening-price entry ~2R; mean threshold of the reversal OB ~2.6R vs FVG 1.95R.
### Example 5: bearish continuation off previous month's highs (10:34-12:05)
- Equal highs under previous month's high, up-close series closed below = OB; retrace into OB plus small FVG forms a new OB; repeated. A candle closed below without reaching an important level is not an OB.
### Example 6: bullish continuation, secondary entry (12:33-14:32)
- Draw = buy-side high. Down-close candles swept lows (OB with FVG). After open, down-close candles into OB/FVG sweep lows, close over = OB. Entry at open 1.5R to equal highs; mean threshold (lines up with an inversion) 2.5R; take profit hit. A stop at the OB low would have been wicked by one point.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Calling any opposite-close candle an OB without an HTF level (01:23, 02:28, 11:37).
- Stop at the OB end can be wicked while the idea is valid (12:05, 14:03).
- Moving the stop closer to the entry to improve RR; move the entry instead (14:03).
- Mean threshold entries may not fill (09:46).

## Conflicts and open questions
- Rule 3 says a sweep is optional, but he "always" waits for one; a rule engine must pick.
- "Important level" is not fixed by timeframe (1h FVG on 5m entries shown; pairing not stated).
- Mean threshold: measured "body low to body high" of the whole series; for a single candle it is open-to-close midpoint (implied).
- The respect test (no closes below mean threshold) gives no candle timeframe.
- Instrument names are not said except oil.

## Glossary additions
- `mean threshold` (MT): 50% of an order block's bodies.
- `buy side of the curve` / `sell side of the curve`: continuation leg after the smart money reversal.
- `smart money reversal` (SMR): the reversal OB after a sweep.
- `RTP` (mishearing): our TP / take profit.
- `serious candles` (mishearing): series of candles.
