## Mistakes and warnings
- Dropping to the 1-minute (or hunting 1m SMT) with no higher-timeframe reason Fix: Only go to a lower timeframe when the higher-timeframe setup tells you to (10:15)
- Shorting against a cluster of unswept highs Fix: Wait for a sweep / SMT (a correlated asset taking its high) before framing shorts off highs (11:40)
- Treating a POI plus displacement as a valid entry Fix: Require the full model: sweep, closure, CISD/continuation aligned with the HTF bias (23:50)
- Shorting a 'candle 3' on the H4 right after a bullish PDL reversal and C2 closure Fix: Read the candle formation; a bullish candle isn't telling you price is reversing (26:20)
- Shorting every SMT seen anywhere Fix: In-model SMT only after the C2/C3 closure; structural SMT at key levels is a different tool (36:20)
- Taking a breaker / continuation after the intraday low (the target) has already been run Fix: Ask what the target is; if it's already taken, price often just goes back into the range (38:00)
- Taking profit early ('locking in profit') while taking full stops Fix: Hold to the target. Early profit raises the break-even win rate (2R needs ~34%, 1R needs 50%) (46:50)
- Entering with no higher-timeframe logic (e.g. a random 9:44 long on the 1m) Fix: Every entry needs HTF candle logic, or at least a new continuation / invalidation of the opposing structure (51:50)
- Pattern-trading every C2/C3 formation Fix: Align the model with the higher-timeframe bias (55:20)
- Letting the indicator decide when auto bias 1 and 2 disagree Fix: Decide your own bias instead of strictly pattern-trading the indicator (1:04:40)
- Shorting after price has already trended down and hit the target Fix: No trade once the target is taken (1:11:40)
- Entering on 'macro' times at the end of a higher-timeframe candle after its range is already made Fix: Wait for the new HTF candle to open and form its wick (1:18:40)
- Waiting for a deep discount in a trending market Fix: When price trends you don't get the discount; buy below the yearly open after the wick forms (1:25:50)
