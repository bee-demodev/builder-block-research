# Higher Timeframe Analysis For Trading - Aligning Expansion Candles

## TL;DR
TTrades defines a trend as expansion candles aligned on several timeframes. A candle "supports expansion" when it has a small wick formed early and a large body; a large opposing run means it will tend to return to its open. He wants at least two aligned expansion candles (e.g. daily + 4H for a 15m entry), ideally three (daily, 4H, 1H/30m for a 5m/3m entry), with structure (protected swings, continuation order blocks) aligned inside them. The simplest application is trading candle 3 (continuation) on several timeframes at once; reversals are possible but harder. When the daily does not support expansion, targets shrink to the daily open / intraday highs. He shows his TradingView layout: an HTF power-of-three indicator plus two instances of his fractal model indicator. Examples from replay: USDCAD 15m (Tue 18 Nov 2025) and NQ 5m (Tue 14 Oct 2025).

## Definitions
- **Supports expansion**: candle with a small wick (formed early) and large body, so it can expand in one direction (00:25-01:10).
- **Large opposing run**: price opens and spends a long time moving against the candle's direction; the candle tends to return to its open or current high/low (00:53-01:04).
- **Aligning expansion candles / expansion alignment**: two or three timeframes' current candles all supporting expansion in the same direction, with structure aligned (01:10-01:53).
- **Candle 3 on multiple timeframes**: daily, 4H and 1H candles all in their continuation (C3) phase at once (03:34, 07:58).
- **Reversal candle (not quite a C2 closure)**: candle with a large wick that does not close back inside; usable to "frame" off its wick (10:21-10:29).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Set bias from the daily: does the daily candle support expansion (small wick) and in which direction (02:00, 05:14)?
2. Check 4H (and 1H): does the current candle support expansion in the same direction? If it has a large wick or opened against the bias, wait for the next candle open (02:16, 03:13, 10:06).
3. Prefer candle 3s: let C2 close, trade C3 once its small wick / protected swing forms (02:20, 03:53).
4. Align structure: protected swing or continuation order block on the entry timeframe (06:49).
5. Entry at the continuation (e.g. retest), stop beyond the protected swing, 2R target; if the daily does not support expansion, target the daily open / intraday highs; if the profile opposes the daily, be conservative (05:50, 09:30, 10:44).
6. Chart setup (04:01-04:44, 08:13-08:52): HTF power-of-three indicator for the daily (offset 15 on 4H/15m layout, 32 on the 5m layout); fractal model indicator for 4H & 15m (offset 17, most items off so only HTF EQ and opening price show); second fractal model instance for 5m & 1H with everything on.

## Worked examples
### Example 1: USDCAD 15m (fractal model 4H/15m), Tue 18 Nov 2025 replay, bearish (04:46-07:00)
- 4H C2 closure with a V-shaped reversal off a previous high; consolidation lows not yet run; the 4H reversal candle has a large wick, so no trade in it (04:46-05:12).
- Daily: opens, no relevant low, makes a high and a CISD, small wick -> can trend lower; daily and 4H both support expansion if a continuation forms (05:14-05:30).
- Price sweeps a high into an FVG and forms a continuation -> protected swing; entry, stop above, 2R; filled on a retest, then trends lower (05:34-06:05). Pullbacks are retracements; next candle opens into the protected swing, makes a small wick and forms a new continuation (06:15-06:43).

### Example 2: same move on 5m with 1H/4H/daily (07:03-08:13)
- Hourly reversal candle not set up for expansion -> wait for the next (C3); daily C3 + 4H C3 + 1H C3 is when the trend gets going (07:32-08:13).

### Example 3: NQ 5m, Tue 14 Oct 2025 replay, bullish bias (09:14-11:20)
- Daily has a large opposing run: no daily expansion expected; target the daily open (faint line) or intraday highs (09:22-09:52).
- 4H bearish candle and hourly do not support upside expansion -> wait for 10 AM new 4H and 1H candles (09:54-10:12).
- New candle: reversal candle with large wick (not a full C2 closure); hourly C2 closure + CISD -> long; C3 on several timeframes (10:21-10:44). Candle profile opposes the daily (open-high-low) -> conservative targets; price returns to the daily open and consolidates (10:44-11:20).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Entering inside a reversal candle with a large wick instead of waiting for the next candle (02:16, 05:05, 07:32).
- Expecting a big move when the daily has a large opposing run; target only the open/intraday highs (09:30).
- Trading good LTF structure against the HTF trend (11:38).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Small wick" vs "large wick" is not quantified in this video (he refers to a separate video on wick size, 00:38); GxTradez' EQ-of-prior-candle test is one candidate rule.
- Whether two aligned timeframes are sufficient for 5m entries, or only for 15m, is stated as preference, not a hard rule (01:21-01:45).
- Speech at 05:16 ("Price opens, it does not make a relevant low. It makes a high") describes a bearish small-wick daily; the wording could read as the reverse; interpreted from context (trend lower).
- Indicator offsets (15, 17, 32) are layout choices, not rules.
- The HTF power-of-three indicator source "from 2°" (04:23) is a mishearing; author unclear.
