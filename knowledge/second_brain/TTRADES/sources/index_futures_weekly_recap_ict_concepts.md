# Index Futures Weekly Recap - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Early (pre-TTFM, ICT-vocabulary) TTrades recap of one ES/NQ week. Frame overlays date it to the week of Monday 2022-05-23 (recording date itself not shown). Weekly plan: price had swept stops below the March low / last week's low, so he expected a move up into a daily FVG narrowed by an hourly order block, then a structure shift lower to turn bearish. Each day he marks liquidity at the 8:30 open, waits for a sweep, a market structure shift with displacement, and enters in the resulting FVG / order block (halving large FVGs with a 0.5 fib), targeting unfilled FVGs and relative equal highs/lows. Tuesday's long uses an ES/NQ SMT divergence; Thursday he admits his bearish bias was wrong and names the missed change in the state of delivery. Useful as an early record of his entry mechanics, not of the later C2/C3 fractal model.

## Definitions
- **Market structure shift (MSS)**: breaking the most recent swing low (for shorts) or swing high (for longs) on the entry timeframe, ideally with displacement (02:44-03:10, 10:17-10:42).
- **Displacement**: an energetic move through structure that leaves an imbalance; a break without it is not accepted (03:07, 10:27).
- **Imbalance**: FVG or order block left by displacement (03:12, 10:17).
- **Relative equal highs/lows**: highs/lows close together but not exactly equal (example: about "three dollars" apart on ES), treated as liquidity targets (04:16, 11:16-11:20).
- **8:30 open / news embargo lifts**: his intraday reference time for marking liquidity (00:58, 05:36, 16:29).
- **Broadening formation**: expanding-range pattern of higher highs and lower lows drawn on the hourly and 1m; its boundary is used as reversal confluence (03:52-04:10, 06:40).
- **Single order block**: one-candle order block he uses to narrow a POI or place an entry (00:32, 12:02).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Weekly: mark the liquidity taken last week and the HTF POI (daily FVG narrowed by hourly OB); write the plan (tweeted) (00:07-00:51).
2. Daily at the 8:30 open: mark swing highs/lows on the 15m; decide which side should be swept given the bias (00:58, 05:36, 14:14).
3. Wait for the sweep, then a market structure shift with displacement on 5m/1m (01:24, 10:17).
4. Enter in the FVG / single order block left by the displacement; if the FVG is large use its 0.5; sell in premium, buy in discount (02:10, 13:22).
5. Stop beyond the candle / imbalance that would invalidate; target unfilled FVGs and relative equal highs/lows (03:25-03:50, 04:16).
6. Optional: use SMT between ES and NQ to choose the asset and the stop (07:10).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: ES 1D/1h, week plan, Mon 2022-05-23 (00:07-00:51)
- Overlay: "Took out stops below march low / last weeks low. Daily FVG and Hourly OB above. Looking for price to reach toward those levels." Tweeted plan: move up into the 1H OB and daily FVG, then bearish after a structure shift (frame 00:47).

### Example 2: ES Monday 15m/5m/1m long then short (00:56-04:52)
- 8:30 open, liquidity at swing highs/lows; bullish into the daily FVG/OB, so wants lows taken (00:58-01:24).
- 5m: drop below and stop run, then up; 5m FVG entry, or 1m MSS + 1m FVG second entry; large FVG entered at 0.5 (01:24-02:22).
- At the daily OB / FVG + hourly OB: no swing lows taken = no short; then displacement after MSS, entry at the first imbalance, stop above the candle beyond the second imbalance; target an unfilled 5m FVG; confluence with an hourly broadening formation (02:34-04:10).
- Relative equal highs on 5m become a target: long from a 5m FVG with stop on the candle low; after the highs are taken, short again (04:11-04:52).

### Example 3: ES/NQ Tuesday 2022-05-24, SMT long (04:56-09:20)
- Plan intact (daily FVG and hourly OB filled), so expecting lower prices; overnight gap down; 15m FVG, the largest imbalance, aligns with the broadening formation (04:56-05:35).
- No false move up into the overnight highs; a clean top is left as a target (05:58-06:15). Overlay: "Price hits our broadening formation and touches the 15min FVG we highlighted earlier."
- 1m: NQ higher lows vs ES lower lows; long NQ risking the lows, about 20 points of risk, first target over 70 points; alternative entry on MSS + 2m FVG; move "nearly 250 points for about 20 points of risk" (06:57-07:51).
- ES alternative: sweep into the zone, MSS, 1m FVG with stop on the low; or 5m FVGs entered at 0.5 targeting relative equal highs (07:55-08:48).

### Example 4: ES Wednesday, Fed day (09:21-13:35)
- Short bias; wants a move above the Monday highs / relative equal highs first (09:37-10:11).
- 5m: first low taken without displacement (ignored); second with displacement -> short at the imbalance, or 0.5 / OB body; target a partially filled 5m FVG (10:17-10:59).
- Pre-Fed chop skipped; equal highs about three dollars apart are the afternoon target; 1m relative equal lows swept, MSS, single OB + FVG long, target the highs; a later entry skipped for poor R:R (11:02-12:31).
- Above the equal highs: looks for a reversal; first 1m break without displacement rejected; short from an order block, not the FVG above, because shorts must be above the swing's 0.5 (12:31-13:35).

### Example 5: ES/NQ Thursday, bias wrong (13:37-15:15)
- Still short-biased; missed higher lows after the Fed and NQ daily lagging (13:40-13:57). POI: broadening formation + FVG (14:03).
- At 8:30: ES does not take the liquidity, NQ does; 15m FVG; 1m entry risking about 10 points (14:38-15:04).

### Example 6: ES Friday long (15:17-17:05)
- Bullish; targets: order block, a high, a gap, the "4150 level" cluster of highs (15:17-15:40).
- Before 8:30 a slight move up took highs; wants a move down to run stops (15:43-15:57). At 8:30 after displacement and retracement, risk the lows; first target, second target the "4100 area" (16:29-16:39). Only one entry he liked: retrace into discount at a 15m FVG (16:47-17:05).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Shorting at the POI before any swing low is taken (02:57).
- Taking a structure break that has no displacement (10:27, 12:45).
- Holding a stale bias: ignoring higher lows / CISD after a news event and a lagging correlated asset (13:40-13:57).
- Chasing late entries with poor R:R (12:23).
- Trading the chop before a Fed release (11:02).
