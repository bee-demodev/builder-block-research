# Lower Time Frame Entries Using HTF POI - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Follow-up to his "Higher Time Frame Points of Interest" video: once price returns to the HTF POI (an FVG / BPR after a reversal), he drops to the 1m (or 15s / 5s) and waits for displacement or a lower-timeframe model before entering. He trades the stronger index (NQ held lows ES took). Repeated-entry method: after each displacement leg, draw an OTE from where the displacement started to where it ended, look for a PD array (OB, FVG) in the discount, often after internal liquidity is swept, and repeat until the HTF objective. Second half: a live morning where his short bias was wrong; a failure to displace below a low (stop raid, no FVG, no close below) flipped him long; small losses at break-even, then profit long. Failure-of-displacement is announced as next week's video.

## Definitions
- **BPR** (balanced price range): aggressive move one way and aggressive move back, overlapping gaps; he calls it "the box" (00:00, 06:19, 08:09).
- **OTE**: optimal trade entry retracement, drawn from displacement start to end (02:05).
- **Displacement range**: the leg from where displacement started to where it ended; re-anchored after each new leg (02:37).
- **Internal liquidity**: lows inside the displacement range swept before continuation (02:37).
- **Rating stops** ("raiding stops"): taking stops without closing/displacing through (05:27).
- **Failure to displace**: no close or displacement beyond the low after a raid (05:27, 09:04).
- **Broadening formation**: higher high, lower low expansion before continuation (04:26, 07:43).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Mark the HTF POI (FVG / BPR after a reversal) and the HTF objective (previous highs) (00:00, 03:55).
2. Compare NQ and ES at the POI; pick the stronger one (00:35).
3. On 1m, wait for displacement or a model; no blind entries (01:34, 04:26).
4. Draw OTE on the displacement range; find a PD array in the discount, ideally after internal liquidity is swept (02:05, 02:37).
5. Enter; if missed, re-anchor on the next displacement leg and repeat (02:37, 03:55).
6. If the opposite-bias model fails to displace (raid without close/FVG), flip bias (05:27, 06:19).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: NQ vs ES 1m at HTF FVG/BPR (00:00-01:34)
- NQ held lows ES took; long NQ on the 1m displacement candle, risk the low, target nearby highs; London highs later taken but not targeted.
### Example 2: 15m bullish bias, 1m repeated entries (01:34-03:55)
- Move into a 15m OTE/FVG; 1m displacement over structure; OTE on displacement range -> OB in discount; missed -> FVG after internal liquidity sweep; then again; up to the HTF previous highs.
### Example 3: live morning, wrong short bias (03:55-09:04)
- Wanted shorts above highs with equal lows below. 1m: up into the area; no displacement below the low, no FVG, still in OTE with a big FVG -> he went long. 15s: small OTE short to a low, partial, rest stopped at break-even. Aggressive down/up (BPR, visible on 5s) switched him bullish; long entries followed; price continued higher.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Shorting simply because price is above highs (04:26).
- Shorting a HTF pullback without a LTF break of structure (07:15).
- Holding a bias after a clear failure to displace; let LTF structure switch it (05:27, 07:15).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Displacement" is not quantified; the failure test is "can't close or displace below" plus "no FVGs" (05:27).
- OTE levels (fib numbers) are not stated.
- The second example's timeframe: "this will be on the 15 minute timeframe" (01:04) but entries are on the 1m; the 15m is the POI timeframe.
- Very low timeframes (15s, 5s) are used for a counter-bias scalp; no rule for when they are allowed.
