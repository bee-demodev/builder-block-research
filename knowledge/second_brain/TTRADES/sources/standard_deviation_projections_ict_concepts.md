# Standard Deviation Projections - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Standard deviation projections are Fibonacci extensions of the "manipulation leg" (the swing that swept liquidity before the change in state of delivery / structure shift), used for targets, areas to expect retracement or reversal, and as confluence. Fib levels: 1, 0, -1, -2, -2.5, -4. The -2 to -2.5 zone is the first area for a retracement/reversal; a displacement and close through it targets -4 (max expansion), after which he expects a retrace to the equilibrium of the leg. Projections gain weight when PD arrays (FVG, order block mean threshold, equal highs/lows, session lows) line up with them, or when multiple projections overlap. The final example runs top-down: daily FVG and weekly profile ("classic sell week", Tuesday high) to hourly projection, then 15m London and 2m New York entry.

## Definitions
- **Standard deviation projection**: Fibonacci extension of the manipulation leg using levels 1, 0, -1, -2, -2.5, -4 (00:38).
- **Manipulation leg**: the swing that swept liquidity before changing the state of delivery / shifting structure; in a high, low, higher high, lower low pattern it is the low to the higher high (01:08-01:37).
- **Smart money reversal**: reversal pattern of high, low, higher high, lower low (01:08).
- **Max expansion**: the -4 standard deviation (04:05).
- **Low hanging fruit**: liquidity resting just before the -2 to -2.5 zone (05:04).
- **Classic sell week**: weekly profile with a Tuesday high, expansion lower into Thursday (09:49).
- **Market maker sell model**: named for Example 3 (07:44), not defined.

## Procedure / checklist
1. Identify a reversal (sweep then CISD / structure shift) (01:08).
2. Find the manipulation leg (the leg that swept liquidity before the shift) (01:37).
3. Drag the Fib from the manipulation leg's extreme to its origin (high to low for bearish, low to high for bullish) (02:10, 02:39).
4. Mark -2 to -2.5 and -4 zones; optionally their CE (03:41, 10:19).
5. Look left for PD arrays near those zones (liquidity, FVG, OB mean threshold, equal highs/lows) (04:34).
6. Project newer manipulation legs; note overlapping zones (05:04, 09:23).
7. At -2 to -2.5 expect retracement/reversal; a close through targets -4; after -4 expect retrace to equilibrium of the leg (03:41-04:05).

## Worked examples
### Example 1-3: NQ intraday, continuous replay (04:34-09:23)
- Ex1: bearish; sell-side liquidity just above -2 to -2.5 (low hanging fruit) and within the zone; a second manipulation leg's -1 overlaps the first's -2 to -2.5, and its -2 to -2.5 overlaps the first's -4. Price hits the low hanging fruit, retraces, reaches the middle of the zone without taking the lows.
- Ex2: OB mean threshold near -2 SD; equal highs near -4; price reaches -4.
- Ex3: hits -1, retraces; FVG near side with equal lows and its CE; -4 near prior lows ("market maker sell model"); price reaches -2 to -2.5, retraces, then hits -4 below the low. Later, lows swept, a further -4 aligned with another projection's -2 to -2.5.

### Example 4: NQ 1D / 1h / 15m / 5m / 2m, top-down short (09:23-13:58)
- Daily: Tuesday reaches a daily FVG and reverses; next day expands lower below lows; classic sell week, expect Thursday lower.
- Hourly: manipulation leg = low to the high into the daily FVG; projected; Thursday target -2 to -2.5 (yellow box) and its CE.
- 15m: midnight open marked; Asia consolidation, London moves lower and back into range; London then sweeps a low and reaches equal highs (London retracing in the weekly profile).
- 5m/2m New York: big wick up and down; displacement below an order block; LTF manipulation leg projected; London low just above -2 SD is the target.
- Entry: retest of the order block, stop on the high, target London lows. Tagged in, distribution lower, equal lows left, retracement into up-close candles reacts, London lows hit. Hourly target box and its CE also reached (13:27).
