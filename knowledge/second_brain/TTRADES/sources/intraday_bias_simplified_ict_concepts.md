# Intraday Bias Simplified - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Early (ICT-era, credits "the MMXM trader") lesson that applies the daily-bias logic to intraday candles. Core tool: the previous candle's high and low. If a candle takes the previous high/low and closes outside, expect continuation to the next previous-candle extreme; if it takes it but cannot close outside ("fails to displace"), frame a reversal to the other side of the previous candle's range. Swing points and PD arrays (FVG, order block) are layered on: a failure to displace through a swing point, or a respected PD array, sets up the "next candle / next day model" (next candle moves back into the range toward the draw). He then pairs timeframes (4H bias with 15m/1m execution; 1h bias with 5m/30s or 1m) and states the logic is fractal from weekly to 5m. This is the precursor of the TTFM candle-2 / candle-3 closure logic.

## Definitions
- **Previous candle's high/low**: the extremes of the last closed candle on the bias timeframe; the default intraday draws (00:36).
- **Failure to displace**: price trades through a level (previous candle extreme or swing point) but cannot close beyond it; closes back in the range (01:04, 10:27).
- **Displacement / close outside**: candle closes beyond the previous candle's extreme; signals continuation (03:28).
- **Inside bar / inside candle**: candle within the previous range; "no direction", default to trend continuation (03:28, 05:44).
- **Swing points**: "just turning points in the market"; used as draws or to frame reversals (10:27).
- **Next candle model / next day model** (credited to the MMXM trader): after a failure to displace at a swing point, or a respected PD array, the next candle moves back into the range toward the draw (10:56, 11:23).
- **PD array**: FVG, order block, etc.; a level price can respect (11:23, 13:54).
- **Open-low-high-close (OLHC)**: bullish candle structure, down first then up; anything below a bullish candle's open is discount (16:35, 20:31).
- **Mean threshold**: midpoint of an order block (22:52).
- **Consequent encroachment**: midpoint of an FVG, an alternative entry to its start (23:50).

## Procedure / checklist
1. On the bias timeframe (4H or 1h here), read the last closed candle against the one before it (00:36-02:00).
2. Took previous high/low and closed outside -> draw is the next previous-candle extreme in the same direction (03:28).
3. Took it but closed back inside -> draw is the opposite previous-candle extreme (reversal) (01:04).
4. Inside bar -> continuation of trend; both sides swept -> no bias, wait a candle (03:28, 04:03).
5. Look left: swing points and PD arrays (FVG, OB) that are respected or not displaced through -> next candle model toward the draw; conflicting closure vs PD array = indecision (11:48-15:31).
6. Mark the HTF draw (e.g. previous 4H high) and the new HTF candle open; drop to 15m, then 1m/5m (16:06-17:29).
7. Wait for the LTF to move against the draw first (OLHC), find a failure to displace at a swing point, then displacement leaving an order block + FVG (17:29-18:39).
8. Enter at the FVG/OB, stop below the low / OB mean threshold, partial at a nearby high, target the HTF draw; require at least 2R (18:39-19:17, 22:52).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Expect to be wrong sometimes; the method supplies a draw, not certainty (07:30).
- Lower timeframes are noisier; prefer 4H/daily for the read, and switch to range high/low when range-bound (07:43, 08:52).
- Do not trade the closure blindly when a PD array sits in the path; that is indecision (14:26).
- An FVG entry with a stop that does not cover the OB mean threshold risks being stopped on a normal return to the OB (22:52).
