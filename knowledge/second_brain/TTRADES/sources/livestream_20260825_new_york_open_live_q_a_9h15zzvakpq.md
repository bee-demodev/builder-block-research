## Mistakes and warnings
- Calling the day bullish because of a 4H CISD and a weekly sweep. Fix: Check whether price has closed over the relevant daily level. Until it has, after an expansion, expect a retracement and don't treat the lower timeframe as aligned. (07:50)
- Trading the first 30-second reaction off the open. Fix: Give it a few minutes; strength often switches off the open. (10:40)
- Trading reversals in chop; on this day it would have meant two stop losses. Fix: Wait for a continuation; it keeps you out of the failed reversals. (33:20)
- Flipping to trade the invalidation off a 'random little SMT' when the planned setup fails. Fix: If price doesn't do what your model needs, don't trade. (23:20)
- Shorting away from a high/failure swings that the strongest asset hasn't swept. Fix: Require the strongest asset to sweep the high first; then use SMT and trade the weaker/middle asset. (52:40)
- Shorting after the daily candle has already hit its low and is far from the daily open. Fix: Recognise the move is done; don't chase. (1:15:00)
- Risking a large share of a trailing drawdown per trade (e.g. $700 of $1,500). Fix: Risk small (e.g. $100) and use more accounts for leverage. (1:07:20)
- Expecting 1-3R wins every time, or a 100% win rate. Fix: Accept losses; higher R:R means a lower win rate. (1:12:10)
- Learning many ICT concepts (FVG, OB, kill zones, fib, premium/discount) without a model. Fix: Learn one model (the fractal model) and swing points. (1:19:40)
- Holding a reversal idea while price makes a deep opposing run into the POI. Fix: Look for a shallow opposing run; a deep one makes the setup less ideal. (17:40)
