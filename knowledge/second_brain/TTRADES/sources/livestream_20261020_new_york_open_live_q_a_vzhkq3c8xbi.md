## Mistakes and warnings
- Blindly longing the weakest asset at its low because a correlated asset is rallying. Fix: Wait for the weak asset to actually reverse (sweep + CISD / intraday reversal), or long the strongest asset's continuation instead. (27:40)
- Trying to long the low of the day (reversal trading). Fix: Wait for 9:30 and a continuation to form, then trade the continuation. (33:20)
- Shorting in the middle of the range with no structure, which forces a stop far away. Fix: Wait for the open and a new swing to form so there is something to trade away from. (08:20)
- Calling gold 'bullish as hell' off one 15m candle and fading the daily candle. Fix: Align with the daily candle; one candle doesn't change the read; watch the phases of price. (26:40)
- Expecting a large NY move when Asia and London already expanded and price is consolidating. Fix: Lower expectations and targets on consolidation days. (15:20)
- New traders stacking every confluence (OB, FVG, breaker, MSS) or adding SMT/PSP before understanding the base model. Fix: Learn the foundation (free course, his model only) first, then add tools that actually help. (48:20)
- Believing that missing the first entry means the trade is over. Fix: Later continuation entries form; waiting for them also shows when the idea is wrong. (57:20)
- Expecting to understand what the market is doing every single day. Fix: Accept unclear days and weeks as normal; don't force trades. (52:50)
- Claiming you were 'right' on a direction call without having entered before the breakout. Fix: What counts is executing an entry before the move, not naming the direction. (59:40)
- Fixating on one index ('YM always moves further and faster'). Fix: The leader changes; trade whichever asset is stronger or weaker that day. (38:10)
