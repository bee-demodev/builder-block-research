## Mistakes and warnings
- Longing over range highs. Fix: At a range high only look to trade back into the range. (06:15)
- Trading in the middle of a range trying to break out of it. Fix: Trade from the range edges back into the range. (10:20)
- Shorting immediately after a low is taken out in a range, then getting stopped right out. Fix: Wait for a new continuation; better late than early. (29:40)
- Dropping to lower timeframes when the daily isn't clear. Fix: The daily is likely consolidating; wait for the open or skip the day. (38:40)
- Picking targets too far away. Fix: Focus on daily highs/lows and the typical daily range. (39:45)
- Pattern trading: acting on an LTF formation (V-shape) with no bias, no C2 closure, no CISD. Fix: Only trade LTF structure inside an HTF framework; don't go LTF until you have context. (52:30)
- Going to the 1m and overtrading (e.g. 30 trades) and blowing accounts. Fix: If you can't do it on the 1m, don't trade the 1m; most psychology problems are fixed by stopping the bad behaviour. (57:20)
- Expecting a reason for every price move. Fix: Accept you won't always know; recognise when you understand price and when you don't, and don't watch when confused. (26:00)
- Trading the lagging (weakest) asset for a move in the strong direction. Fix: Trade the leading or middle asset. (33:20)
- Rejecting a valid short because 'C2 didn't sweep C1', confusing a C3/C4 continuation with a C2. Fix: Learn the candle 2 closure and candle 3 closure separately. (1:10:30)
- One tilt day wiping out weeks of good trading. Fix: Max one trade a day. (1:23:20)
- Getting distracted (chat, giveaways) and missing the setup you planned (his own miss on gold). Fix: Stay with the idea at the planned time (10:00 4H candle). (1:08:30)
