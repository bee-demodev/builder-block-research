# EURGBP for Relative Strength (EU vs GU)

## TL;DR
A forex pair-selection tool, the FX analogue of choosing NQ vs ES. First get a dollar (DXY) bias from daily closures; trade only when the dollar is not consolidating. Then read EURGBP as a fraction (EUR over GBP): EURGBP bullish = euro stronger / pound weaker; bearish = euro weaker / pound stronger. With a bullish dollar, short the weaker foreign currency against USD; with a bearish dollar, long the stronger one. Confirm by comparing EURUSD vs GBPUSD daily closures and structure. Three examples, each with a bullish dollar, show the chosen pair expanding while the other lags. It is a selection filter, not an entry model; entries still come from the fractal model / phases of price.

## Definitions
- **Relative strength (FX)**: which of two foreign currencies is outperforming against the dollar, read from EURGBP direction and from EURUSD vs GBPUSD closures (0:17-1:47, page).
- **EURGBP as a fraction**: EUR / GBP; rising = numerator (EUR) stronger (1:04, page).
- **Ideal candle-2 closure**: candle-2 closure out of an FVG that simultaneously creates a protected swing (5:20).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Dollar (daily; hourly for detail): establish direction from closures (candle 2 / CISD); skip if the dollar is consolidating (0:39, 1:47).
2. EURGBP daily: direction via closures (candle-2 closure, engulfing, CISD, 0.5 respect); use the hourly if ambiguous (3:20-4:12, 8:27, 10:00).
3. Map: dollar bullish -> short the weaker (EURGBP up -> GBPUSD; down -> EURUSD); dollar bearish -> long the stronger (EURGBP up -> EURUSD; down -> GBPUSD) (1:04-1:47).
4. Confirm with EURUSD vs GBPUSD daily closures / structure (5:48-6:15, 8:49, 10:30).
5. Enter on the chosen pair with the fractal model / phases of price: wait for failure swings to be taken, use DXY SMT if the pair is consolidating, require a continuation (4:20-4:44, 6:18-6:41, 10:43-11:09).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: DXY bullish after taking a July low; EURGBP -> GBPUSD short, dates not readable (2:32-7:39)
- DXY took out a "pretty significant low from July"; Mon-Wed form the weekly low; expect candle 3/4 and up Thursday/Friday (2:32-2:58).
- EURGBP daily ambiguous (no candle-2 closure off the high); hourly: daily FVG + protected swing; reversal off the previous-day low with an hourly CISD -> EURGBP bullish -> GBP weaker (3:20-4:18).
- GBPUSD hourly: failure swings above; wait for them to be taken, then bearish structure, aggressive close and expansion lower; on the daily the pound expands while the euro is "not as weak" (4:20-5:03).
- Next day: DXY closed outside candle 2 (continuation); EURGBP daily ideal candle-2 closure out of an FVG with a protected swing -> still GBPUSD; GBPUSD closes through the series of up-close candles, EURUSD does not (5:03-6:15).
- GBPUSD hourly consolidation; DXY ran its consolidation low (SMT) -> trade the weaker asset once a continuation forms; he may have missed it without a retracement (6:18-6:49).

### Example 2: DXY candle-2 closure after multi-day expansion; EURGBP engulfing -> GBPUSD short (8:04-9:41)
- DXY: Mon-Thu expansion, Thursday forms a counter (candle-2 closure), expect Friday (candle 3) back into the range; dollar bullish (8:04-8:24).
- EURGBP: engulfing candle that took a high; hourly shows no reversal -> trust higher -> GBP weaker (8:27-8:46).
- Closures: EURUSD bearish, GBPUSD "very bearish"; result: GBPUSD expands lower, EURUSD does not (8:49-9:38).

### Example 3: DXY sweep + candle-2 closure; EURGBP bearish -> EURUSD short (9:41-11:37)
- DXY bullish (sweep with candle-2 closure); EURGBP bearish candle, continuation expected while it respects 0.5 of its range -> EUR weaker -> EURUSD (9:41-10:28).
- Closures: EURUSD bearish close, GBPUSD bullish close (10:30-10:43).
- Lower timeframe on EURUSD: CISD, indicator on; at the open price sweeps the consolidation high (daily wick) and continues through the lows (10:43-11:09).
- Side by side: EURUSD runs its low at 8:00, GBPUSD only at 9:00; GBPUSD almost runs its previous high, EURUSD keeps much more separation (11:11-11:35).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Right dollar idea, wrong pair: caught offside or missing the expansion (9:15).
- Trading EU/GU while the dollar is consolidating (0:39).
- Treating an engulfing candle that took a high as a reversal without checking the hourly (8:31).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Dollar" is never named as a symbol (DXY implied).
- No rule for when EURGBP itself is consolidating or conflicting with the EURUSD/GBPUSD closure comparison; he never shows that case.
- "0.5 of this range" (10:03) is not anchored (whole candle range vs close-to-extreme as in other videos).
- Example 1 intraday entry is described loosely ("Maybe we miss this move as it's traded too weak", 6:41); no entry, stop or target given in any example. Times "8:00 a.m." / "9:00 a.m." are presumably New York time.
- Page wording "pair it against yourself the weakest foreign currency" is a typo of "against the weakest"; meaning agrees with the video.
