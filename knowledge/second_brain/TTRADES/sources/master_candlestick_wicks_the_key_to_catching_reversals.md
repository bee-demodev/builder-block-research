# Master Candlestick Wicks - The Key To Catching Reversals

## TL;DR
A wick is a lower-timeframe reversal: an aggressive move one way and back inside one period. TTrades marks 0.5 of the wick (body to wick extreme). After a sweep-and-close-back-inside candle (his daily-bias closure), the next candle should respect the wick half: for bullish, open -> low formed in the upper half of the wick -> high -> close; for bearish, open -> high in the lower half -> low -> close. If the LTF closes through 50% of the wick, the wick is "disrespected" and price is more likely to run the wick extreme (the swept high/low) than expand. Shown 1D -> 1h and 1h -> 5m on several futures (ES, gold named). This is the wick-50% filter for the TTFM candle that follows a reversal closure.

## Definitions
- **Wick**: the part of a candle beyond the body; a LTF reversal within the candle's period (00:22).
- **Wick 0.5 / 50%**: midpoint between the body edge and the wick extreme (00:47).
- **Respected wick**: next candle forms its open-side extreme inside the wick half toward the body, then expands (01:09-01:40).
- **Disrespected wick**: LTF trades/closes through the wick 50%; price likely runs the wick extreme (01:59-02:13).
- **Open-low-high-close / open-high-low-close**: the expected internal sequence of a bullish / bearish expansion candle (02:55, 03:49).

## Procedure / checklist
1. HTF candle sweeps the previous low/high and closes back inside -> bias for next candle (02:40, 03:49).
2. Mark 0.5 of that candle's wick (body to extreme) (00:47).
3. Drop one timeframe (1D -> 1h, 1h -> 5m) (02:45, 05:22).
4. Expect the next candle's open-side extreme to form inside the wick half (upper half bullish, lower half bearish) and then expand (03:17).
5. If the LTF closes through the wick 50% against you, assume the wick extreme gets run; skip or wait for a re-close back through (06:26, 10:38).
6. Entry on a confirming LTF close, stop on the LTF swing, target 2R / previous day lows/highs (11:00).
7. Downgrade setups inside consolidation (08:18).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Fading after the LTF has closed through wick 50%; the wick extreme is now the likely draw (06:26, 07:29).
- Trusting wick setups inside a consolidation (08:18).
- Expecting a perfect rule: closes over 50% can still expand; respected wicks can be run late in the candle (11:09).
