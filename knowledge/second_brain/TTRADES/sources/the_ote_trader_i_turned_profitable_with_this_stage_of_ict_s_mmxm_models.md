# The OTE Trader | I Turned Profitable With This Stage Of ICT's MMXM Models

## TL;DR
Guest video: almost all content is from Tom ("The OTE Trader", forex trader, 5+ years), not TTrades; TTrades only introduces (00:11) and closes (27:06). Tom presents his "time frame alignment" model: a higher-timeframe (daily) key level with SMT marks a smart money reversal (SMR); he does NOT trade the SMR itself but waits for a new dealing range and trades the re-accumulation / distribution leg that follows. Framing is 4h -> 1h -> 15m (enter on 15m; or 4h-range -> 1h entry). The 1h confirms by violating an opposing PD array (bearish array closed through = buy side of the curve); the 15m entry needs no PD array, just discount (longs) / premium (shorts) of the new range, ideally after a short-term low/high is raided (turtle soup). Breakeven only once a new range forms. He then runs an FX Replay simulation on EURUSD vs DXY (June 2021) for a ~3.3% win. Relevance to TTFM: overlaps with CISD-style "opposing array violated" confirmation and HTF-level + SMT, but uses ICT MMXM / premium-discount vocabulary rather than candle-2/3 closures.

## Definitions
- **Time frame alignment (Tom's model)**: HTF key level + SMT -> new dealing range -> 4h raid into discount/premium -> 1h state-of-delivery change -> 15m entry in discount/premium (01:41-03:30).
- **Order pairing**: sell stops below an old low are run and then "paired" with buy stops above the range high (or the reverse); used to define the draw (04:09, 06:24, 11:05).
- **Offset re-accumulation**: relative equal lows from a short-term leg get run into the HTF key level before continuation (06:24).
- **Turtle soup**: a lower-timeframe range raided into a key level from a higher-timeframe range (06:57).
- **Buy side / sell side of the curve**: the halves of an ICT market maker model; buy side = the rally leg after the SMR (05:21, 12:37).
- **Smart money reversal (SMR)**: the turn at the HTF key level with SMT; not traded by Tom (02:14, 05:51).
- **"All selling into you buying"**: a bearish array violated and a bullish OB forming in the same area (08:28).
- **Low-risk sell**: the first lower-high sell point after the SMR at the key level in a sell model (13:00).

## Procedure / checklist
1. HTF (daily) key level, e.g. an IFVG / OB deep in discount of a bigger range (03:30).
2. SMT at that level with a correlated pair (AUDUSD vs NZDUSD; USOIL vs UKOIL; EURUSD vs DXY) (03:48, 12:05, 17:08).
3. After displacement, draw the new HTF dealing range; split into premium/discount (04:50, 12:07).
4. 4h: wait for a raid (sell-stop raid for longs) into the discount key level, ideally with SMT (06:57).
5. 1h: opposing PD array violated (close through) = state-of-delivery change; optional new same-direction OB in that area (07:26-08:28).
6. 15m: enter in discount (long) / premium (short) of the new range; prefer a turtle soup of a short-term low/high; FVG optional; NY kill zone for 15m (09:06, 15:13).
7. Stop below the raided low / above the dealing range high (09:31, 16:00).
8. Target HTF levels; breakeven only after a new range forms; partials when external range liquidity is raided (09:39, 25:59).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Passing a challenge too fast without a process; process builds confidence (01:10).
- Breakout trading has "no logic" (00:38).
- Going breakeven too early; wait for a new range (09:39, 25:11).
- Trading the SMR or the low-risk-sell leg itself instead of waiting for distribution (02:18, 13:09).
- Judging a trade by the in-range RR (<2R) when the target is an HTF level (16:00).
