# TTrades Fractal Model - Trading Candle 3

## TL;DR
Completes the candle-2 / candle-3 / candle-4 series. The deciding variable is candle 2's wick: a small wick supports expansion, so candle 2 itself can be traded with a lower-timeframe CISD; a large wick (opened and closed near the same price, closing back inside the prior candle's range) means candle 2 used its range reversing, so let it close and trade the one-sided continuation in candle 3. Candle 3 trades need lower-timeframe confirmation (CISD, then a protected swing / continuation order block, ideally after a reach into a POI). He avoids candle 3 after a candle 2 that already expanded (risk of a new phase: retracement, consolidation, reversal) unless liquidity is still open for it to reach, and then demands more lower-timeframe confirmation. How aggressive the lower-timeframe entry is (LTF reversal vs LTF continuation) depends on how strong the higher-timeframe bias is. The page (2025-09-06) summarizes the same rules.

## Definitions
- **Expansion candle**: opens near one extreme (small or no wick, "open low") and moves one-sided (00:34).
- **Reversal candle (large wick)**: opens, runs one way, comes back to close near its open, back inside the previous candle's range; price "isn't trending in one direction" (00:42, 01:33).
- **Candle 2 / 3 / 4**: candle 2 the reversal candle, candle 3 the continuation (or it can be a reversal candle), candle 4 always a continuation within the model (35:12).
- **Continuation order block / propulsion block**: within the new HTF candle, price opens, sweeps a high or reaches a POI, then an opposing candle formation shows the HTF wick is formed (02:04, 11:00).
- **Protected swing**: the swing whose series of opposing candles price closes through (CISD); stop goes on it (04:29, 04:38).
- **CISD**: close through the series of candles that made the extreme; a close through other candles does not count (23:22).
- **Three drives pattern**: third candle 2 closure attempt at the same area (22:17).

## Procedure / checklist
1. HTF: classify candle 2 by wick. Small wick → candle 2 tradable with LTF CISD. Large wick closing back inside prior range → let it close, plan candle 3 (00:58-01:24).
2. LTF: confirm CISD from the candle 2 extreme (01:39). No CISD on the closure → wait for it (20:31).
3. Candle 3: wait for price to open, reach a POI (FVG, high/low, HTF EQ/order block window), and form a protected swing / continuation order block (02:02, 29:57).
4. Entry on the open after the close through the series of opposite-close candles (or on the retest); stop on the protected swing; target 2R, projections, or the liquidity (04:38, 14:02).
5. If the first close-over fails, track the new low/high and wait for a new protected swing (04:21, 13:48).
6. Scale aggressiveness to HTF bias: strong bias → LTF reversal allowed; weaker → LTF continuation only (11:16-11:48).
7. Skip candle 3 when candle 2 already expanded to the draw; demand more confirmation when candle 2 expanded but liquidity remains (08:57, 20:00).

## Worked examples

- Daily: expansion down taking lows, met by expansion up (reversal); retrace into an FVG; daily candle is a reversal candle (Tuesday). Expect expansion higher next day.
- 1h: reach into FVG; first close-over fails → new low = new protected swing; close over the series of down-close candles → long on next candle's open, stop protected low, 2R or projections.
- Outcome: retest, new swing point, traded to the high. "Tuesday reversal", continuation Wednesday.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Trading a candle 2 that expanded a lot: you get caught in a retracement, consolidation or reversal (06:31).
- Trading a reversal instead of waiting for the continuation: in example 6 the reversal entry would have been stopped before the continuation formed (22:00).
- Counting a sweep of a consolidation high as a CISD; it must be the series of candles that made the high (23:22).
- Taking a slow CISD after long consolidation at face value; it is just consolidation lows being taken (23:45).
- Shorting into a candle that opened low and trades up with a larger wick: fighting the reversal candle (32:00).
- Chasing later continuations instead of the first (34:39).
