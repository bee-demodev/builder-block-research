# Next Day Model - Fractal Way To Get Bias For Trading

## TL;DR
The "next day / next candle model": read the previous candle's close relative to the candle before it to anticipate the next candle's direction. A sweep of the prior candle's high or low that closes back inside its range is a reversal (expect the opposite direction next candle); a close beyond the prior candle's high or low is a continuation (expect more of the same). He blends this with the phases of price delivery (expansion, retracement, consolidation, reversal) to filter conflicts, waits an extra candle when signals disagree, and confirms intraday with a CISD on 1h or 5m, entering with a stop at the invalidation swing and a 2R target. He calls it "the foundation to my bias" and a fractal concept usable on any timeframe; it is the precursor of the TTFM candle 2 closure logic.

## Definitions
- **Next day / next candle model**: using the previous candle (its close relative to the candle before it) to anticipate the next candle (00:13).
- **Reversal (candle)**: take out previous candle high/low, close back into its range (00:40).
- **Continuation (candle)**: take out previous candle high/low and close outside it (01:03).
- **Failure to close outside the range**: synonym he uses for the close-back-inside reversal read; after an expansion it can also mark a retracement (03:27, 12:39).
- **Opposing candle**: the down-close candle before an up move (or vice versa); he uses it interchangeably with order block and volume imbalance zone in this video (02:55, 04:53, 07:37).
- **Propulsion block**: series of candles reaching into an opposing candle and then closing beyond it (08:36, 11:18).
- **Phases of price delivery**: expansion, retracement, consolidation, reversal (02:28, 05:12).

## Procedure / checklist
1. On the daily, compare the last closed candle with the candle before it: sweep + close back inside = reversal; close beyond = continuation (00:40-01:22).
2. Check for conflicts (swing point swept vs. close beyond prior candle; bearish body without a close beyond): if conflicted or inside bar, wait one more candle (01:53, 02:44).
3. Check phase of price delivery: after ~3 expansion candles, expect retracement/consolidation/reversal; prefer trades with the trend (sweep of lows in a bull trend) (02:23, 06:24).
4. Check for POI contact (FVG, opposing candle / order block) on the reversal candle (02:49).
5. Drop to 1h (or 5m): mark the CISD from the series that made the high/low; wait for the close through it (08:21, 15:42).
6. Enter at the open / open high or on the close through CISD or on the propulsion block; stop at the invalidation high/low; target 2R or the prior lows (08:43, 11:21).
7. If a later daily candle fails to close outside the range in your direction (retracement), you can add to the position (13:01).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Mechanically fading every sweep of a high in a bull trend underperforms; use the phases of price delivery (05:10, 06:24).
- Don't force a bias on inside bars or bearish-bodied candles that don't close beyond the prior range (02:09, 02:44).
- Don't trade intraday volatility; wait for the higher-timeframe close (10:45).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Range" is used both for the previous candle's high-low and for a swing range; the rule for when a swing-point sweep overrides the prior-candle close is left to discretion ("if I had to choose") (01:55, 05:50).
- "3 days of expansion" is the only number given for phase change; no rule for 2 or 4 candles (02:25).
- Opposing candle, order block and "volume imbalance" are used loosely as the same zone (04:53, 07:37).
- 2R target is used throughout as a demonstration convention, not stated as a rule.
- The 13:44 segment says "daily time frame" while announcing an hourly example; the candle read there is daily, execution is 1h then 5m.
- Instrument for examples 2 and 4 is not identifiable from frames (price ~71-77).
