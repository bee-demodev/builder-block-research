## TL;DR
Sunday (no live market) 48-minute session from 2024: a review of the previous week (30 Sep - 4 Oct 2024) followed by viewer Q&A. He reads every market from the daily chart through the 'phases of price delivery' (expansion, then retracement, reversal or consolidation) judged by daily closures: gold failed to close over its down-close candles and fell back into its range, so it is a consolidation he will not touch until one side is taken; oil swept a low and displaced higher, and he explains why he did not short it on Friday (TGIF needs a weekly objective hit, which never happened); indices were choppy and the NQ weekly candle closed about where it opened; USDCAD is shown as a clean Wednesday reversal, Thursday continuation. He takes no trade; the only executions are hindsight replays (a USDCAD 5m order-block long that would have been stopped out, and an oil 'sell above the open' illustration that also failed). The Q&A gives many compact rules: he does not trade reversal days or Mondays (90-95% of the time), uses only order blocks and a change in state of delivery (built from a series of candles) as confirmation, enters at market on the candle close rather than waiting for a limit retest, uses SMT only as a CISD-confirmed confirmation never as bias, executes on the 5m for indices, manages only after 2R, and does not trade after three days of expansion.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Going long back into an order block after price already ran to and took the objective, then getting stopped out. Fix: Only reuse a mitigated order block while the draw on liquidity is still untaken. (09:10)
- Fading Friday (TGIF) just because the week had a Monday/Tuesday low and expansion through Thursday. Fix: Also require that a weekly objective has been hit. (07:00)
- Making Monday-to-Friday weekly outlooks that are invalid as soon as Monday does something else. Fix: Trade day by day and decide after each daily candle prints. (26:40)
- Taking a trade on SMT alone to catch the exact low; price keeps going and the easy trade after is missed. Fix: Wait for a CISD to confirm the SMT. (30:40)
- Wasting energy and money trading inside a consolidation range, then missing the real move. Fix: Stay out until one side of the range is taken. (31:50)
- Waiting for a limit fill on the order-block retest; the next candle often never retests. Fix: Enter at market on the close of the candle if R:R is still acceptable. (33:50)
- Repeatedly trying to pick the low of the week inside the range and getting stopped out several times. Fix: Let the (anticipated) low of the week form, then trade the expansion days. (33:50)
- Trading further continuation after three days of expansion. Fix: Stop; a new phase of price delivery is due and it is chasing. (47:40)
