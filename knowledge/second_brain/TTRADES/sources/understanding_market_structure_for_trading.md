# Understanding Market Structure For Trading

## TL;DR
Foundational (pre-TTFM) lesson on basic market structure: uptrend = higher highs and higher lows, downtrend = lower highs and lower lows, built on swing highs/lows (prerequisite: his liquidity video, 00:10). A trend change (market structure shift / break) needs a failure to displace at the extreme, then displacement and a close through the swing that made the last high/low, followed by the first opposite swing ("low risk buy" / lower high). The repeated warning: a lower-timeframe shift is often just a retracement into a higher-timeframe PD array, so trade with the higher-timeframe trend instead of catching tops and bottoms. Chart practice on NQ 5m (Jan 2024, from frames) and crude oil (MCL) 5m with an hourly FVG explaining a failed shift. In TTFM terms this is the ancestor of protected swings and CISD.

## Definitions
- **Higher high / higher low**: highs and lows relative to the prior swing; together they define an uptrend (00:37).
- **Market structure shift / break (shift in structure)**: change of trend confirmed by displacement and a close through the swing that made the last extreme (01:34-02:01, 10:55).
- **Low risk buy**: the first higher low after a bullish shift (01:34).
- **Displacement**: aggressive, decisive movement that closes through a level; lack of it at an extreme signals a possible shift (01:34, 10:22).
- **PD array**: premium/discount array such as an hourly FVG (13:08).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Identify the higher-timeframe trend first (02:01).
2. Mark swing highs and lows on the working timeframe.
3. For continuation, expect the next higher low (uptrend) / lower high (downtrend) relative to the prior swing, then a close beyond the prior extreme (03:27).
4. Watch for failure to displace at a new extreme (01:34).
5. Shift confirmed when price displaces and closes through the swing that made the last high/low (10:55, 11:24).
6. Prefer the new swing in discount (longs) / premium array (shorts) (08:17, 10:22).
7. Check the higher timeframe for a PD array that would make the shift just a retracement (13:08).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: NQ 5m bullish trend, Jan 2024 (03:27-04:47)
- Displacement out of a range makes a higher high; higher low relative to prior low; close over the high; repeats swing-by-swing. NQ 5m with "24/1/2024" read from frame at 07:03 (exact segment chart may differ).

### Example 2: bearish trend (04:47-06:40)
- Aggressive displacement lower; lower high, lower low repeated; one lower high in discount of the range; inner highs/lows swept but structure still lower highs relative to the defining high.

### Example 3: structure change practice (06:40-11:54)
- Higher-timeframe uptrend; short-term lower high / lower low (bearish). Lower high at a FVG, but no lower low; higher low and higher high without displacement = consolidation. Displacement and close over the previous high -> bullish shift; higher low ideally in discount. Later breaks the low that made the high -> possible bearish shift; lower high, close below the low -> lower low; lower high in a less preferred spot (not premium array) still leads lower. Review: the shift level is the low that made the new high; deciding point is whether a lower high forms or it is just a sweep in discount; short after displacement and close below toward old lows.

### Example 4: crude oil (MCL) 5m and 1h FVG (11:54-13:38)
- Higher highs and lows; price displaces and closes below a low, puts in a lower high, but never closes below the next low and instead takes the first high. Zooming to the hourly shows the 5m shift was a retracement into an hourly fair value gap; trading toward the high was the easier trade. Frame at 12:41 shows MCL1! 5m around 29/1/2024.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Shorting the first lower-timeframe lower low inside a higher-timeframe uptrend gets stopped out by a new higher high (02:31).
- Reading higher highs without displacement as a trend change; it is consolidation (07:11).
- Calling a shift on a higher high that broke no swing (10:55).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Displacement" has no size definition; "close below" vs "break of this low" are used interchangeably (09:37).
- Which swing qualifies as "the low that made the new high" when there are several internal lows (09:02 says no swing low inside)?
- Which higher timeframe to check is not fixed; the oil example uses 1h for 5m.
- Premium/discount is a preference, not a requirement: a lower high outside premium still works (10:22).

## Glossary additions
- `slow` (09:02, "any slow in here"): "swing low" / "low".
- `low risk buy`: first higher low after a bullish shift.
- `MSS` / `market structure break` / `shift in structure`: same event.
