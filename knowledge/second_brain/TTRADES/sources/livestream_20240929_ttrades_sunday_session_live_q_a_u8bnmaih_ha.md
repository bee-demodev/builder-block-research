## Mistakes and warnings
- Setting a limit order inside a fair value gap with the stop beyond it, then price rips straight through. Fix: Use the FVG to define an order block: wait for the reaction and a newly validated order block, then enter with the stop at that structure (limit or market). (48:20)
- Trading long on a Friday after Monday made the low of the week and price expanded all week, trying to catch the high. Fix: Expect Friday to cap the weekly range; look for the retracement/TGIF instead. (07:40)
- Shorting below lows that have already been swept. Fix: Wait for a swing point to form below the low and trade back into the range from discount of the last impulse leg. (12:00)
- Taking a New York reversal long into high resistance liquidity within the daily profile. Fix: Only trade toward the side with failure swings. (27:30)
