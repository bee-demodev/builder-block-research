# "Next Day Model" The MMXM Trader - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
TTrades walks through a model posted on Twitter by "MMXM Trader" (a third party, not TTrades' own model): skip Mondays, trade New York session only, execute on the 15m, use the economic calendar as the roadmap, and trade the day AFTER price reaches the anticipated higher-timeframe level (daily FVG / order block / PD array or a previous-day-high run that closes back in range). Top-down: daily level and draw on liquidity, hourly arrays (breaker, FVG, midnight open, SMT), then a 15m FVG / order block during New York AM that delivers toward the draw. He ties it to weekly profiles (open-low-high-close bullish week, "classic Tuesday low" week). Five examples: an unnamed daily FVG example, an order-block-plus-news example, NQ, EURUSD (twice) and GBPUSD.

## Definitions
- **Next day model (MMXM)**: trade the day after price reaches the anticipated HTF level; New York only; 15m execution; skip Monday (00:37, 02:01).
- **Draw on liquidity**: the daily target (FVG, sell stops, equal lows/highs) (00:37, 02:30).
- **Classic Tuesday low week**: bullish week whose low forms Tuesday, expansion from Wednesday (11:34).
- **Open-low-high-close**: bullish weekly profile order (03:00).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Daily: mark the HTF reaction level (FVG / OB / PD array) and the draw on liquidity; infer weekly profile (00:37, 03:00).
2. Skip Monday; wait for the day price reaches the HTF level or runs prior day/week high and closes back in range (01:07, 03:30, 05:48).
3. Next day, hourly: mark midnight open, hourly FVG / breaker / OB, SMT, sell/buy stops (01:35, 02:30, 06:17).
4. Before New York AM, drop to 15m: find a 15m FVG (inside hourly array) or OB (04:39).
5. Look for the reach into the 15m FVG/CE during the open, then delivery to the draw (01:35, 04:39).
6. If no setup, wait for the next day's continuation (07:20).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: unnamed, daily FVGs (00:37-02:01)
- Daily FVG draw below, daily FVG above. Monday skipped; Tuesday reaches the upper FVG and moves away hard; hourly breaker formed. Wednesday retraces into hourly breaker/FVG at midnight; New York 15m FVG CE, then delivery to the weekly open and the draw.
### Example 2: unnamed, daily OB (02:01-03:00)
- Thursday tests and respects a daily OB; Friday short setups. Hourly SMT at the highs in an hourly FVG; news wick, bodies respect; bearish OB entry to sell stops.
### Example 3: NQ (03:00-05:48)
- Daily equal highs, bullish structure; Monday takes Friday's high and closes back in range; draw = stacked lows / no-wick candle. Hourly FVG, then 15m FVG inside it; New York AM reaches the draw. Daily then closes back in range, expansion up expected.
### Example 4: EURUSD (05:48-08:59)
- Equal lows; previous week's high run, closed back into range. Tuesday: 15m FVGs, no reach, no setup. Wednesday: above midnight open, displacement down, 15m FVG respected; lows taken, daily equal lows taken later.
### Example 5: EURUSD, daily OB (08:59-10:39)
- Monday inside bar; Tuesday reaches the OB. Hourly daily open, sell-side below; 15m FVG with old high; old high used as resistance; sell-side taken, displaced through old lows.
### Example 6: GBPUSD (10:39-13:14)
- Smart money reversal, bullish order flow, highs above. Tuesday respects OB mean threshold; Wednesday: midnight down-close OB and hourly FVG, 15m FVG; no bodies below midnight; expansion takes Friday's high; week ends higher with Friday reaching higher.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Do not force Monday or a non-New-York trade (00:37).
- If the New York session has no setup, do not chase; next day (07:20).
- Inside bar before reaching the PD array is not the trigger (08:59).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Economic calendar as your roadmap" is listed but never applied in the examples beyond a news wick (02:30).
- "Skip Monday" vs Example 3: Monday's run of Friday's high is used as the trigger day, and he trades the next day; Monday is skipped for trading, not for analysis (implied).
- "Reaction" off the HTF level is not quantified.
- No stops or RR given; entries are only shown as FVG/OB touches.
