# Intro to the Inducement Model - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Very early three-minute intro comparing the inducement model with the ICT 2022 mentorship model. Sequence: liquidity resting beyond a high (or low) is run, structure breaks, then internal range liquidity (a swing that "induces" traders into the wrong side, plus their stops / break-even stops) is run into a fair value gap before the real move. He likes a FVG at the point where the inducement is swept. Examples: a 5m case with BOS, inducement and liquidity grab labelled, an hourly FVG example, and a 1m short where relative equal lows and stops are run into a FVG first. Summary: "internal liquidity in a range that is ran before the actual move."

## Definitions
- **Inducement**: a swing inside the range (often formed by a FVG entry) that lures traders in; its stops are run before the real move (00:34, 01:35, 02:34).
- **Liquidity grab**: initial sweep of external liquidity (01:05).
- **Break of structure (BOS)**: displacement through the swing after the grab (01:05, 01:35).
- **2022 mentorship model**: ICT's model (sweep, structure break, FVG entry); the inducement model adds the run of the internal liquidity before entry (00:06).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Liquidity grab of a high/low (00:06).
2. Break of structure with displacement (01:35).
3. Identify the inducement swing (internal range liquidity / traders' stops) (01:05).
4. Wait for it to be run, ideally into a FVG; enter there toward the real move (01:05, 02:04).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: unnamed symbol, 5m FVG (00:34-01:35)
- Low, lower high, low run (grab), high breaks structure, low that induces longs, run before the move; 5m FVG touched at the inducement.

### Example 2: unnamed symbol, hourly FVG (01:35-02:04)
- Hourly FVG; longs at the FVG put stops below = inducement; break of structure with displacement; deviation from the range = long area.

### Example 3: unnamed symbol, 1m short (02:04-02:34)
- Reach into a FVG runs shorts' stops; move down makes relative equal lows; entry into a FVG induces shorts; their stops are run into the FVG before the real move, with the equal lows still the draw.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Entering on the first FVG after the break (the 2022-model entry) can be the inducement whose stops are run (00:34).

## Conflicts and open questions
- Speech gives no rule for distinguishing the inducement entry from the real entry beyond "the internal liquidity has been run".
- Instruments are not named in speech; the frame at 02:10 shows ES1! (S&P 500 E-mini) 15m with a grey zone (FVG/range) and BOS label, so at least the higher-timeframe example is ES.
