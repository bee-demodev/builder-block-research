# How To Trade Consolidation - Phases Of Price

## TL;DR
Part one of a five-part "phases of price" series (consolidation, then expansion, retracement, reversal, and blending). A consolidation is price staying internal to a marked high and low for a period, typically after a large range or on reaching a HTF level. TTrades does not trade inside the range: he waits for a manipulation (one side run, or SMT in lieu of a run), then trades the expansion, ideally as a continuation once a protected swing is confirmed by a closure through the series of opposing candles (CISD). Consolidations resolve as a continuation (open draw on liquidity, no HTF level hit) or a reversal (HTF level hit). The PD array paired with consolidation is the range equilibrium.

## Definitions
- **Consolidation**: price remaining internal to a marked high and low for a period of time; a move from large range to small range (00:25, 00:52).
- **Manipulation**: price running one side of the consolidation (power of three: accumulation, manipulation, distribution) (01:39).
- **Continuation consolidation**: after expansion, open draw on liquidity, no HTF level hit; run of the opposite side then continuation (02:42-03:12).
- **Reversal consolidation**: consolidation at a HTF objective/level; run, CISD, reversal (03:57).
- **Breakout signature**: closing over the consolidation highs; should be followed by retracement or continuation (09:25).
- **Inducement**: a low taken into an FVG within a consolidation (10:11).
- **Propulsion block**: named as a continuation-entry location (14:01); not defined here.

## Procedure / checklist
1. Identify expansion (large range) or HTF level reached (00:52).
2. Mark the consolidation high and low; readjust if price fails to trade out (05:28).
3. Decide continuation (open DOL, no HTF level hit) vs reversal (HTF level hit) (02:42, 03:57).
4. Wait for the manipulation of the side against your bias, or SMT on the correlated asset (01:39, 02:05).
5. Wait for closure through the series of opposing candles into the important level (FVG, swept low, or 0.5 of the opposing candle) = protected swing / CISD (06:15, 06:35).
6. Enter (optionally via a fractal model on a lower timeframe, e.g. candle 2 closure then 1m) and target the draw (range high, swing high, PDL/PDH) (05:28).
7. If neither side is run, keep waiting; never trade inside the range (01:20, 17:54).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Trading inside the range means guessing the breakout direction; you get stopped out (01:20, 17:54).
- Catching the low at the manipulation without a strong bias: price may not form a low; wait for the closure (03:30, 09:25).
- After a breakout, an aggressive move back into the range signals the opposite direction (09:40).
