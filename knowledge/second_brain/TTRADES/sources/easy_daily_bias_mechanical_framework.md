# Easy Daily Bias - Mechanical Framework

## TL;DR
Every day is framed as either a reversal or a continuation of the prior daily candle. Reversals are framed off the previous day's high/low (PDH/PDL): price opens, trades into that level, forms a wick confirmed by a lower-timeframe CISD, then trades back into the range toward EQ or the opposite extreme; a shallow wick supports trading the reversal candle itself, a large wick means waiting for candle 3. Continuations are framed off the previous day's equilibrium: price opens, trades against the bias first, respects EQ (no LTF closes beyond it), then trades through the previous day's extreme. The framework is fractal (1D-1h, 4H-15m, 1h-5m) but he prefers to frame reversals from the 4H or higher, and it is always subordinate to a higher-timeframe bias. Prerequisites he names: his equilibrium, candle 2 / candle 3 closure, and wick-size videos. The companion article (page, published 2025-12-13) matches the video.

## Definitions
- **Reversal day**: open, trade into PDH (PDL), wick confirmed by LTF CISD, trade back into the range to EQ or the opposite extreme (00:37-00:52, 17:01-17:12).
- **Continuation day**: open, trade against the bias first into a POI around/before EQ, respect EQ, then through the previous day's extreme (00:52-01:02, 16:51-16:58).
- **EQ (equilibrium)**: midpoint of the previous day's (previous candle's) range (03:00-03:11).
- **Respect EQ**: no paired-LTF closures beyond EQ; touching is not required (03:38-03:53, 04:55-05:07).
- **Shallow vs large wick**: wick size at the reversal level; shallow supports expansion and trading the reversal candle, large means wait for the next candle (01:12-01:27). Wick size is taught in a separate video.
- **Open-low / open-high**: the first extreme made after the open; open-low = bullish, open-high = bearish (06:41-06:51).
- **T-spot**: in this video, where he looks for the continuation POI; in the NQ/USDCAD indicator examples he says it was "just the equilibrium" (06:07-06:12, 15:51-15:56).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Read the HTF bias first (trend, protected swings, candle 2/3 closure) (02:26-02:56, 09:00-09:15).
2. Mark previous day EQ, PDH, PDL (03:00-03:11).
3. Decide: continuation (bias candle closed strongly) or reversal (price trades into PDH/PDL against the prior day) (00:25-01:02).
4. Continuation: wait for open-against-bias into a POI before EQ; do not guess the FVG, wait for a new protected swing / CISD; target the previous day's extreme, extension depends on wick size (03:13-04:39, 05:07-05:57).
5. Reversal: wait for price to reach PDH/PDL, then CISD on 1h/30m (early) or 15m/5m (New York); prefer small wick and SMT; target EQ / FVG / opposite extreme (06:53-07:28).
6. Large wick on the reversal candle: skip it and trade the following candle (01:22-01:27, 15:13-15:25).
7. Execution example: entry short on the CISD, stop at the high, 2R (16:29-16:46).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: YM 1D -> 1h, bullish continuation (02:26-04:39)
- Daily trend higher with new protected swings: price reaches into an FVG and closes over the down-close candles into it, low protected, "ideal candle to closure" (02:30-02:56).
- Hourly: mark previous day EQ and PDH; price opens, trades lower into an FVG, respects EQ (no hourly closes below), then trades higher through PDH into previous highs on the left (03:23-04:15).

### Example 2: USDCAD 1h, bearish continuation (04:40-06:17)
- Daily candle-2 bearish close; mark EQ and PDL (04:43-04:55).
- Two FVGs below EQ; the first is not respected and removed; no C2, no C2, then a candle-3 closure forms a new protected swing -> HTF wick assumed formed (05:07-05:42).
- Move through PDL as first target and expansion lower. With the indicator: SMT with dollar at the reversal, CISD, T-spot = equilibrium (05:42-06:15).

### Example 3: reversal off PDL (06:17-08:38)
- Expected bearish continuation (open high, respect FVG/EQ, then PDL) fails: price opens and trades lower first (open-low = bullish) (06:17-06:51).
- At PDL: SMT with a CISD and a small wick; target EQ or the FVG; price goes well beyond EQ (07:12-07:40).
- Next day continuation: opens lower into an FVG, closes over, reaches PDH, messy retracement, then higher; protected swing swept after liquidity was hit (07:42-08:35). Frame 07:23 shows a YM-priced 1h chart (Dec 2025).

### Example 4: GBPUSD 1D -> 1h -> 5m, retracement inside a bullish bias (08:55-11:00)
- Daily candle-3 closure, new protected swing, bullish (09:07-09:15).
- Hourly: price first trades into PDH with a bearish CISD; treated as a retracement toward EQ/POIs, not a reversal (09:33-10:09).
- Consolidation, sweep, respect FVG, sweep again, then CISD with an "ideal candle 2 closure" on the hourly: entry on the open of candle 3 or a 5m continuation in candle 3 (10:14-10:38).

### Example 5: GBPUSD 1h -> 5m, intraday reversal at 11:00 (11:00-12:12)
- Previous candle's high = the 10 a.m. hourly high; wants small wick, high early in the candle (11:07-11:41).
- 5m CISD close below protects the high; counter-trend and messy; SMT: EUR and DXY took the high, GBP did not; runs the target lows (11:45-12:12).

### Example 6: 4H -> 15m, consolidation then reversal (12:48-15:25)
- Three bearish 4H candles, sweep then CISD closure; range low still an open target; consolidation forms (12:59-13:15). Frame 14:15 shows a CAD pair near 1.41 on a 15m chart.
- 15m: cannot close to form a protected swing = consolidation; wants another high run (13:37-13:58).
- Into the high, waits for closure through / CISD; entry, stop on the high, target the range low; works, with CISD and SMT (14:03-14:41).
- Next 4H candle continues with a large wick, so he would not trade it back into the range (14:46-15:25).

### Example 7: NQ 1D -> 1h, candle-3 continuation (15:27-16:48)
- Previous day: run into PDH, V-shaped reversal with CISD + SMT, bearish candle-2 closure (15:31-15:46).
- Candle 3: open higher first into T-spot / EQ; FVG gets no reaction, consolidates, takes a high, then CISD in Asia/early session; executes on the 1h (15:48-16:29).
- Entry short, stop at the high, 2R; R was 1.83 so he would use the body for the stop to make 2R; 2R hit (16:29-16:46). Frame 16:34 shows NQ 1h, 17 Nov '25, with a position tool reading R:R 1.51 at that moment.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Pattern-trading the reversal/continuation template without a HTF bias (09:00-09:07).
- Treating a bearish CISD at PDH as a reversal when the HTF is bullish; it can be just a retracement (09:39-09:57).
- Guessing which FVG will hold; wait for a new protected swing instead (05:11-05:26).
- Trading a reversal candle with a large wick; wait for the following candle (01:22-01:27, 15:13-15:25).
- Entering on a plain close at the level rather than a CISD (11:41-11:48, 14:05-14:14).
- Framing reversals below the 4H; it gets messy (12:24-12:45).
- Assuming a protected swing holds after liquidity is taken (08:21-08:35).
