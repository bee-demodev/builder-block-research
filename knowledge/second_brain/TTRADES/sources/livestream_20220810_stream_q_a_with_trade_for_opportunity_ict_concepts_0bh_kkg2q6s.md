## Mistakes and warnings
- Trading fair value gaps alone. Fix: Combine with a liquidity run, higher-timeframe reasoning and market structure. (10:40)
- Judging a full bearish turn from a 1-minute chart, especially on news/trend days. Fix: Use multiple timeframes; gauge trend on the 5-minute. (14:50)
- Treating a barely-taken high closed above (or a wick) as a structure shift. Fix: Require displacement and a close; otherwise expect a liquidity grab. (19:20)
- Playing breakouts. Fix: Trade the opposite side after liquidity is run; breakouts only with higher-timeframe structure agreeing. (39:00)
- Taking trades in the middle of a range. Fix: Trade the extremes using OTE/premium-discount. (43:00)
- Overthinking premium and discount. Fix: Find the current higher-timeframe range (low to high) and trade from its OTE area. (47:10)
- Getting too focused on 15-second charts. Fix: Keep the 1m/5m up to stay on the right side of higher-timeframe structure. (47:30)
- Waiting for full higher-timeframe confirmation before entering. Fix: You are late and miss the low-risk entry; aim for a middle ground (the intermediate timeframe shift). (53:50)
- [guest] Taking unlimited trades per day. Fix: Cap the number of trades so daily risk is bounded. (43:00)
