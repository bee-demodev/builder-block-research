# CPI Gold Trade Review | ICT Market Maker Sell Model [MMXM]

## TL;DR
Older ICT-vocabulary trade review (pre-TTFM naming) of a gold short after CPI. Daily: bearish order flow, price retraced into a daily bearish order block (FVG + closure below up-close candles) with sell-side liquidity below. He waited for the first red-folder news day (Thursday CPI, 8:30), when price swept the previous two days' highs (low-resistance failure swing) and closed lower on the hourly. On 5m he framed a market maker sell model with previous day low as the "original consolidation" target, entered at an FVG overlapping a mitigation block in the premium of the range, stop on an intermediate-term high, target 2R at previous day low (3R available to the full draw). He explicitly keeps the stop at the invalidation instead of moving to break-even. Date on the chart: Thu 11 Jan 2024.

## Definitions
- **Order block (bearish, as used here)**: up-close candle series that price closes below after reaching into an FVG and displacing (00:25).
- **Mitigation block**: a bullish order block from the buy side of the curve reused as resistance on the sell side (03:30, 07:03).
- **Original consolidation**: the MMXM starting range; here previous day low (02:23).
- **Intermediate-term high**: high with lower short-term highs on both sides (03:56).
- **Failure swing / low-resistance liquidity**: lower high beside a prior high (01:38).
- **Red folder news**: high-impact economic calendar event (01:00).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Daily: bearish order flow, identify order block and sell-side draw (00:04).
2. Calendar: wait for the first red-folder news day; trade after 8:30 (00:51).
3. Hourly: expect manipulation into previous highs (failure swings) then a close lower (01:38-02:00).
4. 5m: mark PDL as original consolidation / target; mark range high-to-low ignoring the news wick; wait for premium (02:23-02:50).
5. Mark a mitigation block; wait for a sweep of a short-term high and a short-term high on the other side (intermediate-term high) at an FVG overlapping the mitigation block (03:30-04:43).
6. Enter, stop on the intermediate-term high, target PDL (2R); hold without moving to break-even (04:50-06:16).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: XAUUSD 1D / 1h / 5m, Thu 11 Jan 2024 CPI (frame) (00:04-07:48)
- Daily: two swing highs, bearish order flow; Tuesday reached the order block, Wednesday drifted; equal lows below (00:04-00:51, 07:40).
- Hourly: consolidation; previous day high lower than the day prior (failure swing). CPI at 8:30 swept both highs and closed lower (01:38-02:00).
- 5m: range from post-CPI high (wick ignored) to low; premium; short-term highs; FVG + mitigation block entry; fill came a candle later; stop on intermediate-term high; target PDL 2R (02:23-05:05).
- Drawdown with another high swept and a new intermediate-term high formed; break-even stop would have been hit; price reached TP about half an hour later and then the full draw (3R) (05:30-07:03).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Moving the stop to break-even when price moves away; break-even is not the invalidation (06:10).
- Shorting into failure-swing highs without a sweep (04:10).
- Trading the quiet pre-news days of the week (00:51).

## Conflicts and open questions
- This review uses ICT vocabulary (order block, mitigation block, intermediate-term high, MMXM) rather than later TTFM terms (protected swing, CISD); mapping (implied): the order block close-below = CISD; the intermediate-term high plays the role of a protected high.
- Premium of the range is from the post-CPI high (wick excluded) to the low; how to choose which wick is "manipulation" is not defined.
- The chart frame shows XAUUSD on a lower timeframe than 5m at times; speech says 5-minute.
