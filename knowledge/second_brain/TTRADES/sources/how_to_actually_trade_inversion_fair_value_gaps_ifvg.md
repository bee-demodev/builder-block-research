# How To Actually Trade Inversion Fair Value Gaps (IFVG)

## TL;DR
TTrades places the IFVG inside the fractal model: an inversion is only valid when the HTF candle is a candle 2 that sweeps candle 1's high/low and has a small wick (supports expansion); then the LTF inversion acts as the CISD. Without that HTF context, a sweep plus inversion is "pattern trading" and gets stopped out. Practical rules: treat a cluster of FVGs as one big FVG and require a close through all of it; skip reversals without a V-shape (consolidation); with a large opposing run, expect expansion to wait for the next HTF candle (candle 3). Optional confluence: SMT between candle 1's and candle 2's extremes.

## Definitions
- **FVG**: 3-candle pattern; candle 1 and candle 3 wicks do not overlap (00:22).
- **IFVG / inversion**: FVG not respected and swiftly closed over; polarity flips (00:38).
- **Series of FVGs**: multiple adjacent FVGs in one leg treated as one zone for inversion (05:13, 09:57).
- **V-shaped reversal**: sharp reversal without consolidation; required for his inversion model (05:46).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Daily bias (e.g. reversal after 3 expansion days, previous day high) (08:20).
2. HTF candle (4h or 1h): candle 2 sweeps candle 1's extreme with a small wick (02:09). Optional SMT C1-C2 (02:16).
3. LTF (15m or 5m): mark the FVG / series of FVGs in the leg that made the sweep (05:13).
4. Wait for a close through the whole zone; accept a single rejection if it closes back quickly (10:37).
5. Enter; stop on swing / high; target 2R or other side of the range (10:52, 07:13).
6. If the HTF candle has a large opposing run, wait for the next HTF open (07:42).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: EURUSD 15m (02:24-04:47)
- First inversion ignored: no candle supporting expansion and no sweep of the previous high (consolidation). Next: high taken but no inversion. A candle with no wick is a draw on liquidity. New candle opens, takes previous high; no aggressive displacement; new short-term high above the previous 4h high, then inversion and CISD; failure swings below; continuation lower once the new candle opens. No SMT here.

### Example 2: sweep of previous low without V-shape (04:47-05:56)
- Reversal lower ran consolidation lows; new low swept; multiple FVGs treated as one; no close through; no V-shape, so avoided; want another run of the lows.

### Example 3: gold 3m with 30m candles (05:56-08:14)
- Bearish consolidation; consolidation lows taken (previous candle's low swept); HTF candle opened higher first then expanded lower: large opposing run, so only a reversal candle expected. Two FVGs combined; close through; option to enter at the next 30m open with stop allowed into the FVG, target other side of the range or 2R; continuation after reach into FVG and close over; both entries worked.

### Example 4: daily to 4h to 15m top-down (08:14-11:46)
- Daily: three expansion days into a POI; expect reversal off previous day high. 4h: candle 2 closure sweeping a previous high, shallow wick. 15m: initial inversion on a small FVG possible; then sell-off not trusted; new high; series of FVGs not closed through (loss avoided); new high with one FVG; one rejection, then closes through; entry with stop on high, 2R, nearly stopped out.
- Alternative: 1h/5m model shows an "ideal" candle 3 closure; inversion usable on 5m.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Pattern trading sweep + inversion with no HTF candle context (01:07).
- Inverting only one FVG when a series exists (10:06).
- Taking non-V-shaped reversals in consolidation (05:46).
- Expecting in-candle expansion after a large opposing run (07:42).
- Stop on the high was nearly hit; mind placement (11:03).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Swiftly closed over" (00:41) has no candle-count limit.
- "Quickly close back below it" after a rejection (10:40) is not quantified.
- "Small wick" is not tied to a numeric EQ rule in this video, though "around 50% of this higher time frame candle" is referenced (10:25).
- Example 1's first inversions are dismissed for lacking a sweep, but also no HTF timeframe is stated for "candle 1/2" there (15m chart with 4h references).

## Glossary additions
- `IFVG` (mishearings "IFGs"): inversion fair value gap.
- `series of FVGs`: adjacent gaps combined into one inversion zone.
- Mishearings: "candle to closure" = candle 2 closure, "drawn liquidity" = draw on liquidity, "fair value apps"/"fair value up" = fair value gap(s).
