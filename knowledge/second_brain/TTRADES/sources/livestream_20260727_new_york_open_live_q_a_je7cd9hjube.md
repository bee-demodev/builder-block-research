## Mistakes and warnings
- Entering at the low of an expansion candle, after the higher-timeframe open (chasing). Fix: Ask whether you are entering near the HTF open; wait for a new candle open / continuation and enter near its opening price. (11:40)
- Shorting below lows after the move has already run. Fix: Short higher, before the lows are taken; otherwise wait for the next HTF open or a new continuation. (30:00)
- Adding extra points beyond the protected swing to the stop. Fix: Stop exactly at the protected swing; its break is the invalidation. (14:00)
- Trading the lagging/strongest asset (YM) on a bearish idea. Fix: Trade the weakest asset in the direction of the move (NQ). (1:14:40)
- Longing into a gap just because price is in a gap (trading reversals). Fix: Don't try to pick reversals without a model reason; that produces many losses. (49:50)
- Trading consolidating, choppy markets (gold today). Fix: Trade something that is trending; learn when to avoid the market. (1:00:00)
- Using his old silver bullet / 9 a.m. high-low video. Fix: Learn the fractal model instead. (59:20)
- Assuming shorts are 'too late' after a pre-open reversal. Fix: Reversal then continuation: a valid continuation at the open can still run to the target (28,500 was hit). (05:00)
- Quitting your job to trade full time before having made money. Fix: Keep the job and put the work in alongside it. (1:04:20)
- Treating a funded account payout differently from the eval (fear). Fix: Trade it the same way; it is still a process. (50:40)
- Relying on signals from streams. Fix: You are responsible for your trading; use his bias to learn and execute yourself. (55:00)
