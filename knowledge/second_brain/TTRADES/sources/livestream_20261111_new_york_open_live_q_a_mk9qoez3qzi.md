## Mistakes and warnings
- Trading the weakest / lagging index when the others are trending. Fix: Trade the strongest or the middle asset; don't short the weakest when the strongest isn't reversing. (15:45)
- Closing trades early out of fear. Fix: Follow the plan; accept emotions but don't let them make decisions. (14:40)
- Going on tilt: flipping long/short after each stop-out. Fix: Risk management; stop gambling direction flips. (33:10)
- Stops repeatedly hit just before price goes to target. Fix: Check for a higher-timeframe FVG at the stop; wait for price to trade into it before entering. (1:15:00)
- Randomly looking for CISDs anywhere. Fix: Identify a swing point first, then the CISD. (1:15:20)
- Treating a swing as protected when price never closed through it. Fix: A protected swing requires a close through the series that formed it. (1:16:40)
- Taking profit at a near target that gives poor R:R for the entry timeframe. Fix: Hold longer on higher-timeframe entries, or refine to a lower timeframe so the near target is worth it. (11:40)
- Entering after a deep retracement for ~2R at the end of the HTF candle. Fix: Wait for closure through, a new continuation, and often the new HTF candle's open. (28:00)
- Using ICT macro windows (last minutes of a candle) for timing. Fix: Use the actual HTF candle open/close times (e.g. 10:00, 9:30). (48:20)
- Overcomplicated models needing many conditions to enter. Fix: Keep it simple; 27 conditions to take an entry probably won't work. (1:12:50)
- Forcing NQ trades on a low-quality, consolidating bank-holiday profile. Fix: Focus on the cleanest asset; skip markets that don't meet requirements. (34:10)
