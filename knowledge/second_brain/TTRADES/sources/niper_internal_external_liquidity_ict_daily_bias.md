# $niper | Internal & External Liquidity - ICT Daily Bias

## TL;DR
Guest lesson: TTrades hosts "Sniper" (Matt), an ICT trader mentored by the MMXM Trader, who does almost all the talking. Sniper reduces price to two moves: draw to external range liquidity (old highs/lows) and rebalance internal range liquidity (fair value gaps), alternating between them on every timeframe. Bias comes from watching a higher-timeframe key level (ERL or IRL) get tagged, then waiting for a market structure shift on the paired lower timeframe (M→D, W→4H, D→1H, 4H→M15, 1H→M5, M15→M1). SMT divergence at the key level is the main extra confluence. Entries are a retrace into a lower-timeframe FVG / breaker / order block in discount (longs), stop at the low that tagged the key level, target the next ERL or 2-2.5 standard deviations. Worked on NQ/ES 1h-5m, crude oil daily-1h, NQ weekly-4H-15m-1m, ES daily-1h-5m. This is ICT vocabulary, not TTFM; it lines up with TTFM's timeframe pairing and C2/C3 "key level then shift" logic but uses no candle-numbering.

## Definitions
- **External range liquidity (ERL)**: old highs and lows; buy side above highs, sell side below lows (01:56, 04:29).
- **Internal range liquidity (IRL)**: fair value gaps inside the range (01:56).
- **Higher timeframe key level**: an ERL or IRL on the higher timeframe of a pair, the place to watch for a shift (03:14).
- **Market maker buy / sell model (MMBM/MMSM)**: run of HTF ERL (with SMT), LTF close above a swing high, retrace into LTF IRL/breaker, reaccumulation via mitigation blocks, then delivery to the HTF IRL draw / "original consolidation" (04:29-07:41).
- **Original consolidation**: the HTF IRL pool the model finally returns to; on the 5m the hourly swing high draw is called this (07:41, 09:08).
- **Change in state of delivery**: close above the last down-close candle that took out liquidity (17:08).
- **Mitigation block**: up-close candle / order block on the "sell side of the curve" that price uses as support while reaccumulating (07:14, 17:38).
- **Advanced market structure**: long/short/intermediate-term highs and lows; intermediate-term = a short-term swing that violates the prior short-term swing and holds (12:04, 20:31-21:00).
- **Energetic swing**: the displacement leg used to measure premium/discount (07:14, 16:08).

## Procedure / checklist
1. On the HTF (M/W/D/4H) mark ERL (old highs/lows) and IRL (FVGs); decide which one price just tagged and which it should draw to next (02:17-02:45).
2. When price reaches the HTF key level, check SMT with a correlated index (NQ/ES/YM) (04:59, 13:14, 22:58).
3. Check the key-level candle bodies: wicks through are fine, bodies should not close beyond (23:31).
4. Drop to the paired LTF (M→D, W→4H, D→1H, 4H→M15, 1H→M5, M15→M1) (03:39-04:59).
5. Wait for a candle close above a LTF swing high (bullish) after the reaction (05:27, 10:36).
6. Measure premium/discount on the energetic swing; wait for the retrace into a discount LTF FVG / breaker / order block (16:08, 19:33).
7. Optional finer step: drop one more pair (e.g. 15m PD array → 1m MMBM) and use the CISD / order block there (16:08-17:08).
8. Indices: only after 9:30 equities open (15:39).
9. Stop at the low that tagged the key level; target next ERL or 2-2.5 std dev (10:36, 25:59).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: NQ (and ES alongside) 1h → 5m (08:09-10:36)
- 1h: price took an external range high, then drew into an hourly bullish FVG (IRL); SMT formed inside that hourly PD array (08:09-08:39).
- Price closed above a swing high after reaching IRL → assumes draw to the next hourly swing high (ERL) (08:39-09:08).
- 5m MMBM: price takes a 5m external swing high, retraces into a 5m internal FVG = entry; or wait for the close above the last down-close candle that tagged the gap, which formed a swing low and was re-tagged = 5m bullish order block (09:38-10:06).
- Draw on liquidity: 17,591.5 (10:06). Stop at the low that tagged the range (10:36). Outcome not stated.

### Example 2: Crude oil daily → 1h, short (11:05-13:14)
- Daily MMSM: price takes ERL, retraces into IRL tagging premium of the last energetic swing, then draws back to ERL (11:05-11:35).
- 1h: small purges of highs, then a candle closure below a low; retrace into the hourly gap = potential short (11:35-12:04).
- Advanced structure: long-term high, short-term high violated → intermediate-term high trading into hourly bearish FVG; short-term high to its right = entry to the external range low; stop above it or higher near the intermediate-term high (12:04-12:43).

### Example 3: NQ weekly → 4H → 15m → 1m (13:14-18:06)
- Weekly bullish FVG (IRL) reached; SMT at the lows inside it on the 4H (13:14-13:45).
- 4H: run of an external range high after the SMT, retrace into a discount 4H FVG (13:45).
- Viewed on Sunday: weekly draw is close, so expects Monday rather than Tuesday/Wednesday for the entry (14:11-14:40).
- Monday NY: reaccumulation; waits for 9:30; 15m bullish FVG in discount as reference; 1m MMBM off it (15:10-16:39).
- 1m: close above the last down-close candle = CISD and bullish order block; close above an inversion gap used as support; SMT and 1m FVG inside an up-close candle; later bullish order block supports the move to the draw (17:08-17:38).

### Example 4: ES daily → 1h → 5m (18:33-21:59)
- Daily: ES runs an external range low with SMT against NQ (18:33).
- 1h: energetic displacement closes above a minor swing high; with daily SMT, assumes CISD to higher prices (19:03-19:33).
- Premium/discount from that swing high to swing low; retrace into an hourly bullish FVG in discount; watches the 5m (19:33-20:03).
- 5m advanced structure: long-term low in the hourly IRL, failed close above a swing high, short-term low violated → intermediate-term low, then a close above a swing high; entry = short-term low to the right of the intermediate-term low (20:03-21:00).
- Price reached the external highs, rebalanced a 5m imbalance, failed to close back above and dropped (21:31).

### Example 5: daily IRL → ERL with YM SMT, 1h → 5m (22:29-26:27)
- Daily external range high, price trades into a daily IRL in discount (swing high to swing low); SMT vs YM at the lows; bodies not closing below the gap (22:58-23:31).
- 1h: the swing high to break is not closed through until end of London at 5 a.m.; no opportunity in NY AM; in the PM session price retraces into an hourly bullish breaker block = HTF key level for the 5m (24:01-24:29).
- He executed on a 5m tag of the breaker on his MetaTrader (CFD) feed; the futures chart did not show the tag (24:59-25:28).
- Stop below the breaker, TP at 2-2.5 std dev → 1:2-1:2.5 R (25:59).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Do not execute index trades before the 9:30 equities open (15:39).
- A wick through the HTF key level is not a failure; judge the bodies (23:31).
- Failing to close above a swing high on the LTF is "completely fine" while the structure builds; wait for the confirmed close (20:31).
- Feed differences: a tag visible on a CFD/MetaTrader chart may not show on futures; he still treats it as valid (24:59-25:28).

## Conflicts and open questions
- 02:17 misspeak: "if internal range liquidity is taken, that is an indication that price will draw to internal range" — the context (and "vice versa") means ERL taken → draw to IRL.
- 07:14: the HTF draw IRL "lying in premium of the range" in a buy model vs entries in discount (16:08); the range for each (which swing low/high) is loosely defined.
- 07:14 / 17:38: "sell side of the curve" in ICT is usually the left (down) side of the MMBM; he points to up-close candles on the rally as mitigations, so the side of the curve is unclear.
- "Energetic swing" and "very minor" swing high still counting as a closure (19:03) have no size threshold.
- Monthly → daily pairing (not monthly → weekly) differs from a strict one-step-down chain; a rule engine needs the exact pair table from rule 6.
- 2-2.5 std dev: which swing is projected is not stated.
- Guest material; ICT MMXM terms, not TTrades' own TTFM rules.
