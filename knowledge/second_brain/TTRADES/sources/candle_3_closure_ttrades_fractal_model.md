# Candle 3 Closure: TTrades Fractal Model

## TL;DR
Defines the candle 3 (C3) closure, the second of the only two closure formations in TTFM. When price reaches a HTF key level but candle 2 does not produce a reversal closure (it closes beyond candle 1's extreme instead), he waits: either a fresh C2 closure forms, or candle 3, without taking candle 2's extreme, closes over (bullish) or below (bearish) the body of candle 2, engulfing it; that C3 closure anticipates a candle 4 expansion. Both formations need a HTF POI and context; EQ (upper/lower half of C3's range) is where C4's wick should form. Execution is on the aligned timeframe (4H -> 15m) with the indicator, a protected swing for the stop and a 2R target.

## Definitions
- **Candle 3 closure (C3)**: at a HTF key level, no C2 reversal closure; candle 3 leaves candle 2's extreme untaken and closes over/below candle 2's body (engulfing) (01:36, 11:01).
- **Candle 2 closure (C2)**: candle 2 sweeps candle 1's high/low and closes back inside (10:45).
- **Continuation / reversal closure**: as in the C2 video (00:33-01:04).
- **New week opening gap**: used as a POI that price takes before a C3 closure (05:22).
- **Failure swings**: equal lows/highs already reached; trading away from them is avoided (06:53).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Confirm a HTF key level / POI (FVG, equal lows, new week opening gap, swing high/low) (10:38).
2. Did candle 2 sweep and close back inside? Yes -> C2 closure, trade C3/C4 (01:11).
3. No -> let candle 3 print: does it close over/below candle 2's body without taking candle 2's extreme? Yes -> C3 closure, trade C4 (01:24-01:50).
4. Neither -> no trade; optionally wait for a close through the series of opposing candles to form a new protected swing (09:42-10:07).
5. Mark C3's full range; C4 should hold the half in the trade direction (02:16, 08:06).
6. Drop to aligned timeframe (4H -> 15m); use the CISD / protected swing; enter at the open or in the favourable half of the previous HTF candle; stop at the protected swing; target 2R or prior highs (08:13-09:14).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Expecting a reversal just because a key level was hit, without a closure (01:20, 03:24).
- Choosing random swing points or pattern-trading the model (10:19).
- Taking a C2 that leaves a nearby high or that sits after equal lows were already reached (06:26, 06:49).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Closes over the body of candle 2" is not defined as over C2's open vs close vs its high; the page/diagram wording "engulfing" suggests body only, while the bearish diagram says candle 2 "closes over the high of candle 1" (01:56).
- Whether C3 must leave candle 2's extreme strictly untaken (1 tick) is not specified.
- "So close" (a nearby high left behind) has no distance threshold (06:28).
- C3 is called a reversal "reasonable to say" (04:24), i.e. lower certainty than C2, but no different sizing is given.
- 2R is a demo target, not a stated rule.
