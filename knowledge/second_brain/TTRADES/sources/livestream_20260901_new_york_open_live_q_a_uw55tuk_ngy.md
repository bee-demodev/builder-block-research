## Mistakes and warnings
- Taking a 'perfect' structural pattern (FVG continuation) late in the daily range with no higher-timeframe framework. Fix: Check HTF framework and where price is in the daily range; enter reversal days early, before ADR is made. (17:20)
- Using SMT to force a setup on garbage structure ('SMT here and here and here'). Fix: Have the framework first; SMT is only an added confirmation. (51:20)
- Pattern trading: 'but there's a 4H bullish closure / an OB' while ignoring that it's inside a consolidation. Fix: Need framework, bias and actual structure; read the consolidation context. (52:30)
- Missing the trend then dropping to the 1m to trade late into it and getting chopped. Fix: Don't go lower timeframe in chop; short before the expansion, not after. (27:20)
- Trying to predict tomorrow's (or weeks-ahead) bias before the daily candle closes. Fix: Wait for the daily closure. (33:10)
- Treating a weekly C2 as a guaranteed rip. Fix: Need lower-timeframe confirmation (intra-candle CISD). (25:00)
- Deciding on unclosed candles and moving stops live (why backtests look better than live). Fix: Wait for closures; leave the trade alone. (1:08:20)
- Revenge trading, oversizing, tilt, random trades, then asking what's wrong. Fix: Stop doing it; change or quit. (1:16:00)
- Loading charts with many lines/monthly EQs that can't be used in real time. Fix: Just mark the FVG; drop everything you can't execute live. (1:14:00)
- Buying far-OTM or short-dated options for swings. Fix: Near/slightly OTM, about 4x the expected time; avoid theta decay. (15:00)
- Assuming a bullish CISD = a low has formed. Fix: Confirm it. (10:00)
- Not knowing whether lack of setups is analysis or self-doubt. Fix: Define your model so you can tell if it is present. (1:17:30)
