# Trade Continuations Using Order Blocks

## TL;DR
TTrades shows how to enter after missing (or deliberately skipping) the reversal entry: once the reversal sequence (purge, CISD) has formed, wait for structure, i.e. a new "opposing candle / order block". A valid continuation order block requires price to first either retrace into a FVG (important level) or sweep a short-term low/high, then close through the series of opposing candles; the only exception is SMT with a correlated asset. Stop goes on the new swing (tighter than the reversal low), targets are the draw (PDL/PDH, standard deviation projections, 2R). If price trends away without touching a FVG or sweeping a swing, there is mechanically no continuation entry and the move is missed. Shown on GC 5m (1h pairing), 4h-15m, oil 1D-1h, 1h-5m and a monthly/daily GBPUSD case; fractal.

## Definitions
- **Continuation entry**: entry after a confirmed reversal, off a new opposing candle / order block that forms the next swing (00:47-01:39).
- **Opposing candle / order block (continuation)**: the series of opposing-colour candles that reached into a FVG or swept a swing; once closed through, it forms a new invalidation (01:20-02:10).
- **Reversal sequence**: purge (turtle soup) -> inversion -> CISD (00:30, 15:41).
- **Invalidation**: the swing (low/high) that must not be taken for the idea to hold; stop location (01:07).
- **Stages of distribution**: low-risk sell (reversal) vs 1st/2nd stage distribution (continuations) (06:03).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Confirm HTF bias and a reversal sequence (sweep + CISD) on the execution timeframe (00:30, 03:13).
2. Do not chase; wait for price to retrace into a FVG or sweep a short-term swing (03:43).
3. Wait for the close through the series of opposing candles into that level (04:33).
4. Enter; stop on the new swing (high for shorts, low for longs) (05:14).
5. Target PDL/PDH, standard deviation projections, or 2R (03:33, 08:06).
6. Look for further continuations on retests of the new order block while downside/upside objectives remain (08:06, 14:31).
7. If no FVG tag / sweep / SMT, no trade (11:54).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Chasing after the reversal leaves; wait for a new order block (03:43, 12:54).
- Calling any close through an opposing candle a continuation; it must first tag a FVG or sweep a swing (14:04).
- Market entry after an aggressive displacement with stop on the high = bad RR; use retest (10:12).
- Trusting the first lower high in a fresh move (09:39).
