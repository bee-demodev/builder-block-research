## Mistakes and warnings
- Fading a strong expansion (shorting gold after it already moved) because it looks extended. Fix: Sit out if you missed it; nothing is bearish while the 4H is aligned until that candle closes. (14:20)
- Trading seconds charts for dopamine. Fix: Not part of his approach; most who do it are gambling. (20:20)
- Repeatedly getting stopped out on a 5m continuation entry before price runs to target. Fix: Move up one timeframe and enter on the 15m (the stop is usually inside a 15m FVG). (27:20)
- Dropping to the 5m on every asset you watch. Fix: Wait for an hourly or 4H idea, then go lower. (33:40)
- Risking a percentage of the nominal prop account size (1% of 25k or of a 1M account). Fix: Risk from the drawdown: drawdown / number of losses in a row you want to survive (e.g. $1,500 / 10 = $150). (44:10)
- Tilt and revenge trading, going too low on timeframes without reasoning. Fix: Implied: reason from the daily chart and HTF first. (47:30)
- Thinking a wick far from the HTF open means more risk. Fix: Keep risk fixed and adjust position size to the stop width. (1:03:50)
- Going long at 10:00 after ~2,800 points of range and a C2-C3-C4 expansion (entering candle 5). Fix: Do not look for a candle-5 continuation; expect a new phase of price. (1:08:30)
- Expecting a continuation when the candle has closed in a way that does not support the move. Fix: Wait for a reversal or a long-wick failure and recovery, then trade the continuation. (42:40)
