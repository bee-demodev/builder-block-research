## Mistakes and warnings
- Using ETF charts (or SPX) for futures analysis Fix: Use the futures chart; ETFs have data gaps. Without real-time ES data, US500 CFD is a free substitute (t=3500). (02:10)
- Reading trend from 1-2 minute displacement alone Fix: Check the 15m and higher: the 'downtrend' may be a wick into a 15m FVG. (43:30)
- Overwhelming yourself with Judas-swing projections Fix: Start with projections from the origin of the reversal; study AM's trade reviews. (43:30)
- Getting stuck on the 1-minute all day Fix: Use it only while actively seeking an entry toward a known higher-timeframe target. (55:00)
- Switching between bullish and bearish bias frequently Fix: Trade toward the higher-timeframe draw and accept missing moves. (51:00)
- Predicting Monday before Asia/London have printed Fix: Wait for London and the open before forming the day's view. (32:50)
- Using the VIX for direction Fix: VIX measures volatility, which can come in both directions. (34:40)
