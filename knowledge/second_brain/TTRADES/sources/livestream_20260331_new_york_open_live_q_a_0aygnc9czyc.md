## Mistakes and warnings
- Having the right bias but chasing price, then getting stopped out. Fix: Enter around the timeframe opens, before the targets are hit and before the daily range is made. (07:30)
- Treating SMT as a standalone strategy and shorting every SMT while price is trending up. Fix: Use SMT only as a confluence inside a model/framework with a V-shape reversal at a relevant level. (58:20)
- Trading the lagging asset to its target after the others have already moved. Fix: Long the strongest or short the weakest; if unclear, trade ES. (27:00)
- Asking where to enter after an expansion has already happened. Fix: Trade the expansion itself. Afterward, only a sweep/reversal scenario is tradable. (1:14:20)
- Looking for a downside continuation at the lows after the move (or a 4H C3 after a monster candle). Fix: The continuation was earlier; don't short the lows or trade after a huge candle, except the 06:00-10:00 into 10:00 continuation. (1:37:20)
- Taking four trades on NQ on a choppy day. Fix: Daily max loss of one (or two) trades; don't trade chop. (1:02:10)
- Copying his short without knowing where to take profit. Fix: Have your own strategy and TP plan before entering (e.g. London lows, session lows, 0.5 EQ). (1:26:20)
- Failing prop payout phases without knowing why. Fix: Identify the root cause (tilt, oversizing, overtrading) and apply a specific limit, e.g. one trade a day or proper size. (1:26:20)
- Treating paper trading casually. Fix: Trade paper like it's real to avoid building bad habits. (1:02:10)
- Pattern-trading 4H power of three (closures only) without context. Fix: Anchor reversals to context like the previous-day high. (1:37:20)
- Flipping a bullish bias because of relative equal lows. Fix: Keep the bias while all timeframes are bullish; only consider the lows if price is actually near them or a reversal forms. (12:00)
