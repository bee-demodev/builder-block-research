# Top Down Analysis - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
His top-down process depends on the entry timeframe: for 5m / 1m / sub-1m entries he takes POIs from the daily, 4h, 1h and sometimes 15m, not monthly/weekly (those are for 4h entries). He marks daily POIs (FVG fill area, blocks marked from wick to open), a 4h block (body to wick of the up-close series), then uses 15m / 5m / 1m / 15s setups to trade from one POI to the next during the New York AM session only. The single walkthrough (instrument not named) shows a bullish opening drive into the 4h block, then a reversal toward the daily block's open. This is an early-style video using ICT vocabulary (blocks, 2022 model, OTE, mitigation blocks) rather than TTFM terms.

## Definitions
- **Point of interest (POI)**: HTF area marked for reactions: FVG fill area, blocks (01:29).
- **Block (daily)**: order block marked from wick to open (01:56).
- **4h block**: body to wick of the up-close candle series (02:27).
- **Opening drive**: the move right after the New York open (03:54).
- **2022 model**: liquidity run, break of structure, clean FVG (05:28).
- **Mitigation block / OTE**: named as LTF entry types, not defined (06:03, 06:54).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Choose the entry timeframe; for 5m/1m/sub-1m, take POIs from daily, 4h, 1h, sometimes 15m (00:06).
2. Daily: note previous highs/lows, order blocks, FVGs; mark POIs (wick to open) for either range scenario (01:01-01:56).
3. 4h: mark the block (body to wick) (02:27).
4. 1h: check for anything additional (02:27).
5. 15m/5m/1m: decide the draw (which POI is more likely) from the New York open behavior (03:25-03:54).
6. Enter on LTF FVGs / previous lows / 15s setups toward the POI; stop small (04:22-06:03).
7. At the POI or target, read displacement vs failure; trade to the next POI (06:27-06:54).
8. Stop at the end of the AM session (06:54).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: unnamed instrument, 1D to 15s, New York AM (00:34-07:21)
- Daily: above previous highs, an order block respected (no close above); move lower, then news sends price higher, takes the high, closes back into the range, retests the previous high, displaces lower through internal liquidity. POIs: the FVG-fill area and a lower block (next downside target).
- 4h: previous low may be retested; block marked body-to-wick of the up-close series.
- 15m: after open, run below previous lows into an FVG, then aggressive move up; he chooses the 4h block as the draw (bullish off opening drive). He says he missed this move live.
- 5m/1m/LTF: 5m FVG lines up with 1m; lower-timeframe entry inside the 1m FVG near previous lows; news event fails to close below; new highs reached.
- At the POI: 2022 model not cleanly present; after a drop, 15s FVGs offer entries toward lows. At the lows, a block fails to displace higher; low invalidated; 15s short at OTE toward the daily block's open; price drops. Afternoon not traded.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Using monthly/weekly POIs for 1m entries (00:06).
- Waiting for a 1m entry that only comes late; go lower for a better entry (04:22).
- Expecting the 2022 model every time (05:28).

## Conflicts and open questions
- Instrument and date are not stated; prices not given.
- Daily block marked "wick to the open" (01:56) vs 4h block "body to the wick" (02:27): marking convention differs by timeframe or speech is loose.
- How he decides "this point of interest makes more sense" (03:54) is discretionary.
- ICT-era vocabulary (2022 model, OTE, mitigation block) not used in later TTFM videos; relation to TTFM rules is unclear.
