# Intraday Bias - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Short early ICT-era follow-up to his daily bias video: use the 4h and 1h chart as your "daily" timeframe for intraday bias. Three inputs: displacement, structure (e.g. relative equal lows as the draw) and imbalances (FVGs). Once direction is known, go to lower timeframes for an entry (retrace into OTE, run liquidity) and target the obvious liquidity or an FVG in premium. A second framing uses pre-London / Asia and London price action: a previous high run, displacement down with an FVG, then London running highs into that FVG to form an intermediate-term high = bearish toward resting lows; after the lows are run and displacement up appears, flip bullish. ## Definitions
- **Intraday bias**: directional view taken from the 4h/1h charts used as the "daily" (00:00).
- **Structure**: here, relative equal lows/highs and old lows/highs as draws (00:35, 01:05).
- **Intermediate-term high**: the high formed when London runs highs into a bearish FVG; its holding keeps bias bearish (02:05-02:35).
- **OTE**: optimal trade entry retracement zone (not defined numerically) (00:35, 01:38).

## Procedure / checklist
1. On 4h/1h, find displacement, structure (the liquidity draw) and imbalances (00:00-00:35).
2. Decide direction toward the draw (00:35).
3. Drop to a lower timeframe; wait for a retracement into OTE that runs nearby liquidity; enter, risk the swing, target the draw (00:35-01:05).
4. Alternatively, read pre-London / Asia then London: previous high run, displacement down plus FVG, London runs highs into the FVG; if that high holds, bearish to resting lows (02:05-02:35).
5. When the target lows are run and displacement up follows, flip bullish (02:35-03:08).

## Glossary additions
- `OTE` (optimal trade entry): ICT retracement zone in the displacement leg.
- `intermediate term high` (ITH): significant swing high, here the London high into a bearish FVG.
- `trendline liquidity`: stops resting along a sequence of lows/highs forming a trendline.
- Mishearings: "when we were a trace back up" = "when we retrace back up" (00:35); "old lows to be ran" = old lows to be run (01:05).
