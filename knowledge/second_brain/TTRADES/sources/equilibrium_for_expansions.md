# You Are Using Discount and Premium Wrong! - EQ For Expansions

## TL;DR
TTrades uses equilibrium (0.5 of a candle or range, wick high to wick low) "opposite" to ICT's discount/premium: in an expansion phase price rarely retraces into discount/premium, so for continuation he wants the upper half of the previous (bullish) candle respected to trade higher and the lower half respected to trade lower. If that half fails, he flips toward the other side of the range. Each day he sets an if-then from the daily closure + previous day EQ, then matches PD arrays (FVG, opposing candle/order block, protected swing) inside the respected half and waits for a CISD / close through the down- or up-close series on the hourly or 15m. Large-wick candles use 0.5 of the wick instead. After about 3 expansion candles into liquidity he expects a new phase and steps aside. The concept is fractal (daily→1h, 4H→15m, etc.).

## Definitions
- **Equilibrium (EQ)**: 0.5 of a candle or range, wick high to wick low (00:41); used as a bias divider in expansions (01:24).
- **Discount / premium (ICT)**: lower/upper half as buy/sell zones; TTrades uses the opposite half for continuation (00:17-00:24).
- **Protected low**: low formed out of an FVG after closing through the down-close candles (01:58, 03:43).
- **Failure swings**: swing highs/lows left resting at the range extremes; used as targets (03:19, 06:28).
- **Propulsion block**: order block that forms after closing through the down-close candle into an FVG + order block POI (18:19-18:23).
- **T-spot**: the 0.5 range of the new candle that price taps before expanding (18:37) ("that would be the t-spot").
- **Page** (`using_equilibrium_in_continuations.md`): same content; adds pairing list daily EQ→1h, 4H EQ→15m, 1h EQ→5m, 15m EQ→1m, and target with standard deviation projections.

## Procedure / checklist
1. On the daily (or 4H) close, mark 0.5 of the previous candle/range, wick to wick (00:41, 01:46); use 0.5 of the wick if the wick is large (10:29).
2. Write the if-then: respected half → continuation to PDH/PDL or range extreme; not respected → other side/failure swings (06:14).
3. Mark invalidation (protected swing) and PD arrays (FVG, opposing candle/OB) in the respected half (05:38, 01:53).
4. On the 1h/15m, wait for price to reach the PD array during the NY session and confirm with CISD / close through the down- or up-close series / order block formation (03:55, 09:24).
5. Entry with stop over the protected swing; target opposite side of range or 2R (12:35).
6. After ~3 expansion candles into liquidity or a gap that already expanded, stand aside until new daily closures (16:04, 08:23).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Waiting for deep retracements into discount/premium during expansions; they rarely come (01:04, 14:00).
- Measuring EQ with bodies; always wick to wick (00:48).
- Using the full candle's EQ when the wick is very large (10:29).
- Trusting an order block inside a consolidation (13:28).
- Taking a long without the close through the down-close candles that made the low (10:08).
- Continuing to trade after ~3 expansion days into lows (16:04).

## Glossary additions
- `EQ` (equilibrium, 0.5, "05", "0 five"): midpoint of a candle/range, wick to wick.
- `PDR a` / `PD aray` / `PDR` (11:30, 12:28, 07:39) = PD array.
- `wretch` (03:45) = reached.
- `propulsion block`: order block re-formed after closing through the down-close candle into an FVG/OB.
- `failure swings`: resting swing points at range extremes used as targets.
