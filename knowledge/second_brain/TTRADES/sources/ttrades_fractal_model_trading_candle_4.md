# TTrades Fractal Model - Trading Candle 4

## TL;DR
How to trade day 4 of a daily swing (the "daily swing model"). The model rests on "no reversal without a swing high/low". Once a daily swing low forms with a strong candle-3 close (or a daily CISD), the 4th day should open, form its wick in the upper half of candle 3's range (above C3's EQ, 0.5) and continue higher; mirror for bearish. Top-down: daily for bias, a middle timeframe (30m/1h) for structure and session reversals (London reversal -> NY continuation), a lower timeframe (3m/5m) for the entry CISD, often paired with 8:30/9:30 drivers and SMT. Entries: stop at the low or body low, target 2R. The concept is fractal but he prefers it on daily candles.

## Definitions
- **Daily swing model / day 4**: the day after a confirmed daily swing (C1-C2-C3), expected to continue out of C3's upper (bull) / lower (bear) half (00:11-01:14).
- **Swing low / swing high**: low with a higher low on each side; high with a lower high on each side (02:10, 14:46).
- **Middle timeframe**: the structure timeframe between daily and entry, here 30m or 1h (01:46, 03:14).
- **Driver**: a time catalyst such as the 8:30 news or 9:30 open (05:07).
- **Body to body**: C3's range measured on bodies only, whose upper half should also support (08:31).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Daily: identify a swing low/high (higher low each side) and a strong C3 close or daily CISD; check SMT with the correlated index (02:04-02:59).
2. Mark C3's EQ (0.5); day 4 must make its wick above it (bull) / below it (bear); invalidation if it trades through (03:01-03:36).
3. Middle TF (30m/1h): read phases of price; look for a London reversal (sweep + SMT + CISD) and expect a NY continuation (03:39-04:23).
4. LTF (3m/5m): around 8:30/9:30 drivers, wait for a sweep into HTF FVGs and a close through the down-close candles (05:03-05:42).
5. Entry on the CISD close; stop at the low or body low; target 2R (05:49, 10:19).
6. Missed it? Take a continuation from an FVG with a fresh close-through (09:36-10:38).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: index futures (ES used as SMT pair), daily -> 30m -> 3m (02:04-06:20)
- Daily swing low; a competing swing high is ignored because ES swept its low (SMT) and the close is a valid daily CISD. Plan: low in upper half of C3, targets PDH, left swing point and beyond. 30m: move lower is a retracement/consolidation, not a reversal. London: fails to take the low while ES does (SMT); close through the down-close candles -> London reversal expected to hold. NY: FVGs forming; 3m consolidation; 8:30 runs the lows; single down-close candle closed through = CISD. 3m/30m/daily aligned; stop at low, 2R hit (05:56).

### Example 2: daily -> 1h -> 5m, bullish (06:20-11:06)
- Consolidation, highs taken, aggressive move through lows: expansion in, expansion out = HTF wick/reversal. Strong daily close but the low has no low on each side yet: it is C3, so let one day pass; swing confirms; HTF range equal highs as target. Day 4: expansion away, low expected to hold. London retraces into the area; close through the down-close candles at an important level; upper half body-to-body should support. 5m: waits for failure swings to be run, then close through the down-close candles that made the low; entry, 2R (09:43). Missed-entry continuation: FVG retracement with close over, stop at body low, 2R hit (10:38).

### Example 3: EURUSD daily -> 1h -> 5m, bullish (11:06-14:18)
- Equal highs (failure swings) as draw; retracement then failed continuation = consolidation; run of consolidation low then bullish close; swing low confirmed. Mark 0.5 of the range; 1h: expects a low to be taken since there was no aggressive close; SMT removes that need; close through down-close candles. 1h entry option with stop on low and 2R, or wait for retracements (FVG / swing point). NY: aggressive up, then a reversal sweep of a low; 5m confirmation; stop at low or below bodies; 2R hit (13:53).

### Example 4: bearish, daily -> 1h -> 5m (14:18-16:46)
- Expansion met with expansion (reversal), lows to the left as draw on liquidity. Swing high with a close through the up-close candle. Day 4 should respect the lower half of C3, at most reaching into an FVG there. Overnight: small swing, then falls; protected high from sweeping a high and closing through the level. 0.5 of the hourly candle used fractally; 5m entry on the close; stop above the FVG; almost stopped, then 2R (16:33-16:46).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Treating a low without a higher low on both sides as a confirmed swing; that is still C3 (07:16).
- Entering on a sweep alone without the close through the opposing candles (09:21).
- Expecting continuation after a deep retrace into C3's discount (01:16).
- Stops too tight: leave room for a retest of the FVG inside the HTF candle's 0.5 (16:38).

## Conflicts and open questions
- Bias anchor alternates between "strong closure in day three" (00:43) and a daily CISD plus SMT (02:48); which is required is not fixed.
- Rule 19: in example 2 he waits a day for the swing to confirm, which makes that "day 4" really the day after the confirming close; the day count is relative to swing confirmation.
- The upper half is measured on wicks (02:23) and also "body to body" (08:31); which wins in conflict is not said.
- 8:30/9:30 drivers are given for index futures; no FX driver times are given in the EURUSD example.
- Instrument for examples 1, 2 and 4 is not named in speech (example 1 uses ES as the SMT pair, so it is probably NQ or YM); chart symbols unreadable in frames.
