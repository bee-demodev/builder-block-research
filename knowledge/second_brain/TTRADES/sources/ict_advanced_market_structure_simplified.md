# ICT Advanced Market Structure Simplified

## TL;DR
ICT-era lesson (credits "the MMXM trader") on the short-term / intermediate-term / long-term swing hierarchy. Short-term high/low = a 3-candle swing; intermediate-term = a short-term swing with a short-term swing on each side that is less extreme (in practice: a short-term swing gets swept, then a new short-term swing forms on the right, validating it); a "rebalanced" intermediate-term swing forms when price trades into an FVG and away. Long-term high/low = the swing formed off the reaction to an HTF level / PD array, validated by a short-term swing on its right. Once formed, the long-term and intermediate-term swings are expected not to be violated while price delivers to the draw; he prefers the intermediate-term high being taken to signal a structure shift. Used for entries when price is choppy and there are no clean FVGs. Precursor of TTFM protected swings.

## Definitions
- **Short-term high / low (STH/STL)**: 3-candle swing; high with a lower high each side / low with a higher low each side (00:30).
- **Intermediate-term high / low (ITH/ITL)**: short-term swing flanked by less-extreme short-term swings on both sides (01:00); on the chart, a short-term swing that sweeps a prior short-term swing and is then confirmed by a short-term swing on its right (03:27, 09:04).
- **Rebalanced ITH/ITL**: swing created when price rebalances an FVG and trades away (01:25, 09:44).
- **Long-term high / low (LTH/LTL)**: swing formed by the reaction off an HTF level / PD array; validated by a short-term swing on the right and price trading away (01:25, 06:27).
- **Market structure shift**: break of a short-term high (acceptable) or, preferred, of the intermediate-term high (04:41).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Identify the HTF level / PD array (15m buy-side, daily discount FVG, London order block) (02:20, 07:19, 10:48).
2. Drop to the LTF (1m, 1h, 5m) as price reaches it; label short-term swings (02:47).
3. Mark the long-term swing at the reaction; validate it when a short-term swing forms on its right (02:47, 07:51).
4. Wait for a sweep of a short-term swing and a new short-term swing on its right -> intermediate-term swing (03:27, 08:24). FVG rebalances also create intermediate-term swings (09:44).
5. Treat the long-term and intermediate-term swings as not to be violated while price delivers to the draw; violation = wrong (08:24, 10:19).
6. Enter off a short-term swing formed in premium (shorts) / discount (longs) after the intermediate-term swing, targeting the draw (12:56, 13:28).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: 15m buy-side sweep -> 1m (02:20-07:19)
- 15m high swept with failure swings below. 1m: short-term high, lows broken, short-term high on the right -> long-term high anticipated (02:47-03:27). Sweep of a short-term high then a lower high on its right -> intermediate-term high (04:06). 15m lows (draw) hit. Reverse: short-term high broken (possible MSS; he prefers ITH taken), long-term low forms (04:41). Equal lows swept, short-term low on right -> intermediate-term low (05:14-05:53). That low gets swept to retrace into discount, new intermediate-term low, reaching for highs (05:53-06:27).

### Example 2: daily discount FVG -> 1h (07:19-10:48)
- Short-term low swept, then short-term low on its right -> long-term low (07:51). Sweep of short-term low into the FVG, new short-term low -> intermediate-term low, not expected to be violated (08:24). Further sweep-and-confirm intermediate-term lows; consolidation then expansion; a rebalance of an FVG creates another intermediate-term low (09:04-09:44). Price trades higher.

### Example 3: NQ 5m (frame ~12:38 per prior frame read: 31 Jan 2024) London order block -> NY (10:48-13:28)
- Gap reached during London, Asia highs swept, up-close London candle = order block. Into NY price returns to it; short-term highs form; at 8:30 another short-term high, then a short-term high on its right -> long-term high (11:15-11:48). Short-term highs swept, then a lower short-term high on the right -> intermediate-term high (12:21-12:56). Retrace into premium / up-close candles forms a short-term high; price drops to the sell-side liquidity (lows) (12:56-13:28). Choppy price, no clean FVGs; structure used for the entry.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Do not label a swing intermediate-term before the confirming short-term swing forms on its right (04:06, 09:04).
- Equal-lows "sweeps" are weak; wait for a clear sweep (05:14).
- If the confirmed intermediate-term swing is reached, the directional assumption is wrong (10:19).

## Conflicts and open questions
- PDF definition (ITL = STL with higher STLs both sides, 01:00) vs chart usage (STL that sweeps a prior STL, then confirmed by an STL on its right). The chart version is a stricter/different test; a rule engine must pick one.
- Long-term swing validation: 02:47 needs a short-term swing on the right; 09:44 says "short term low on either side of it". Left-side requirement is unclear.
- "Reaction off a higher timeframe level" is not quantified (how far must price trade away?).
- Example 3 date comes from an earlier frame read, not speech.
- How a "rebalance" of an FVG is measured (touch, CE, full fill) is not defined.

## Glossary additions
- `short term high/low`, `intermediate term high/low`, `long term high/low`: ICT swing hierarchy, see Definitions.
- `failure swings`: swing points resting below/above that price failed to break (02:20).
- `drawn liquidity` (Whisper at 04:06): draw on liquidity.
