## Mistakes and warnings
- Forcing a trade every day, including ideas the daily profile doesn't support. Fix: Only trade when the profile supports the idea (e.g. open-high then trend for shorts); otherwise pass. (14:30)
- Taking a bias off a random lower-timeframe FVG. Fix: Anchor every idea to a higher-timeframe draw on liquidity. (28:20)
- Selling GC at 2 a.m. expecting a reversal, then a continuation, when gold was not bearish. Fix: Trade with the higher-timeframe read; gold's daily favoured upside. (35:10)
- Trading EURUSD from the middle of its range and taking a stop. Fix: In the middle of a range there is a lack of bias; skip it. (49:50)
- Watching every tick of a live candle, predicting its close and entering early, then ending up in a doji. Fix: Wait for the candle to close, as you do in backtesting; use the 15m if lower timeframes are too noisy. (58:20)
- Coming to streams for signals (entries/stops) instead of learning to execute. Fix: Use his stated bias and draw, and learn to execute on your own. (26:40)
- Expecting a trader to always know where price is going. Fix: Accept 'I don't know' days; work in if-then statements and do nothing when there is no draw. (45:00)
