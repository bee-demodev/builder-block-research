# A+ ICT Entry Checklist - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Early TTrades ICT video: the checklist he runs after he has a daily bias, before taking an entry. Order: (1) stop raid (a swing low/high taken), (2) market structure shift / displacement over the opposite swing, (3) return to discount (longs) / premium (shorts) of the displacement range, ideally the OTE, (4) a PD array there (FVG or order block preferred; OTE, breaker, box setup also used, often blended), then (5) risk management for solid R. Entry choice inside the zone balances R:R vs fill probability and "what catches my eye". Examples on NQ 15m, a 5m short, ES 1m/5m, NQ 1m box setup (rejected as not A+ because no raid before displacement), and ES daily VI → 1h → 15m → 5m. This is the ICT ancestor of the TTFM C2-sweep / CISD / retrace-into-PD-array entry.

## Definitions
- **Stop raid**: a swing low/high that is put in and then raided (taken) (00:10).
- **Market structure shift / displacement**: previous opposite swing broken by an aggressive move (00:39).
- **Box setup**: deviation below/above a range then back in ("aggressive down, aggressive up"), read as accumulation-manipulation-distribution; entry on a retest of the manipulation (07:50-08:23).
- **Breaker block (bullish)**: up-close candles before the raid on liquidity (02:35).
- **Order block (bullish)**: down-close candle before the displacement (02:35).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Have a daily bias (00:10).
2. Stop raid: a lower-timeframe low (high for shorts) taken; one tick does not count (00:10, 07:19).
3. Market structure shift: aggressive displacement over the prior high (low) (00:39, 02:05).
4. Fib the displacement range; wait for discount / OTE (premium for shorts) (02:05-02:35).
5. Find a PD array there: FVG, order block, breaker, box setup; blend what catches the eye (02:35-03:32).
6. Place the entry balancing R:R and fill odds; stop at the swing / OB extreme (03:04, 03:32).
7. Partial at the first target, optional break-even, final target opposite liquidity / morning highs, runners (04:02, 09:58).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: NQ 15m long into daily FVG fill (01:35-04:02)
- Price reaches a daily FVG below with sell side liquidity resting; reaction, but no shift until the next candle (01:35-02:05).
- Discount below 0.5 of the displacement range, narrowed to the OTE; small FVG, down-close order block, up-close breaker before the raid, box setup area (02:05-03:04).
- Chosen: FVG nested in the order block inside the OTE, aligned with box setup; stop on the low; partial, then buy side liquidity hit (break-even optional) (03:32-04:02).

### Example 2: 5m short to equal lows (04:02-05:40)
- Range in premium of a larger displacement range, equal lows below (04:02).
- 5m: buy side liquidity taken, aggressive move off; high-low-higher high; breaker with a FVG inside (04:33).
- Entry at the breaker or narrowed to the FVG; partial and reach for the lows, which were hit (05:04).
- Recap: stop raid, MSS, return to premium with PD arrays (05:40). Instrument not stated.

### Example 3: ES 1m and 5m short (05:40-07:19)
- 1m: high broken, close back in, body respecting the level = stop raid, then shift (05:40).
- Fib from the new low; breaker + FVG in the OTE; price fills the FVG into the OTE and continues lower (06:15).
- 5m view of the same setup: high raided, displacement down, OTE retrace into an order block; entry on the OB, stop on the OB high, target new low (06:47-07:19).

### Example 4: NQ 1m box setup, not A+ (07:19-08:58)
- One tick below a low → not counted; displacement up without a raid → not counted (07:19).
- Later move below the range and back in = deviation (AMD); retest of manipulation, candles not closing below the old low, move higher to liquidity above (07:50-08:23).
- Worked, but no LTF liquidity raid before the move, so he is not interested (08:23).

### Example 5: ES daily VI → 1h → 15m → 5m long (08:58-10:28)
- Daily POI: a volume imbalance on ES (08:58).
- 1h: stop raid, displacement up; fib shows price back at equilibrium (08:58).
- 15m: aggressive move below sell side into the POI, displacement back up; a FVG exists but is in premium → rejected (09:27).
- 5m: discount part has an order block and FVG; entry there, stop on OB low, partial/break-even, target morning initial highs, runners; entry respected, displaced higher (09:27-09:58).

## Mistakes and warnings
- A one-tick break is not a stop raid (07:19).
- A displacement with no raid before it is not an MSS for this checklist (07:19, 08:23).
- An FVG in premium is not a long entry even if price reacts from it (09:27).
- Entries placed too deep for R may never fill (03:04).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Discount" step is called flexible (00:39) but later used as a hard reject (09:27).
- Minimum raid size beyond "a tick" is not given.
- Box setup is used as a confluence in Example 1 but rejected as standalone in Example 4; only the missing raid decides.
- Partial level and break-even rule are discretionary ("depending on if I move my stop").
