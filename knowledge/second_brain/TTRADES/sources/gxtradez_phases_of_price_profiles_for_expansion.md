# GxTradez | Phases of Price & Profiles For Expansion

## TL;DR
Guest lesson on the TTrades channel: Garrett ("GxTradez", runs "The Market Lens" community and a "GXT" model/indicator) teaches his narrative framework. TTrades only gives the 0:11 intro and the 1:03:35 outro; every rule below is the **guest's**, not TTrades'. Core ideas: (1) phases of price (consolidation always leads to expansion; never consolidation to reversal/retracement), each with a lower- and higher-timeframe "signature"; (2) only trade expansion, triggered by manipulation paired with SMT; (3) candle profiles (bullish = open low first, small wick) and an EQ (50%) filter on the prior candle / wick that decides whether a candle "supports expansion"; (4) a 4H session profile for futures (2 AM / 6 AM / 10 AM candles) with if-then rules and "driver" (8:30 news, 9:30 open) pairing; (5) the goal is aligning daily, 4H and 1H expansion candles toward one draw on liquidity. He explicitly departs from TTrades' C2-closure rule (12:47, 13:33). Six worked examples (GC, CL, SI, YM/NQ, ES, a bearish NFP-week day) from April 2025.

## Definitions
- **Phases of price delivery** (learned from "the MMXM trader"): expansion, consolidation, retracement, reversal; allowed transitions in rules 1-2 (02:04-02:40).
- **Expansion signature**: trading inside an expansion candle: small wick, opposing candles supporting price toward ERL/IRL, lows rejected, highs displaced, shallow retracements; the LTF expansion makes up the HTF body (02:58-03:40).
- **Consolidation signature**: LTF = range after expansion; HTF = inside bar (candle that does not take out the previous candle's high or low) (05:25-06:04).
- **Retracement signature**: deep, slow, choppy pullback after expansion into an objective that leaves failure swings and no manipulation; target EQ of the range or IRL. HTF = three consecutive candles or one large candle into an objective; on LTF it shows as stacked near-equal highs (06:14-08:20).
- **Reversal signature**: expansion into a level met by expansion away (V shape), confirmed by CISD; HTF = swing point / candle close (08:43-09:55).
- **Failure swing**: a swing left by a retracement without manipulation (near-equal stacked highs/lows); not a reversal point, a target (06:20, 08:15).
- **Manipulation**: a sweep of a high/low (or protected swing) that triggers expansion; "AMD" = accumulation (consolidation), manipulation, distribution (04:52, 06:49).
- **AMD continuation / AMD reversal**: trading out of a consolidation by failed manipulation of its far side (continuation, 04:52) or by using the consolidation's high/low as the reversal point (09:30).
- **Expansion candle / supports expansion**: candle with a small wick and large body; a candle with a large opposing wick ("reversal candle") does not support expansion (03:06, 16:21-17:15).
- **Reversal into expansion candle**: a candle that opens into a key level, reverses and expands, keeping a small wick; a type of swing formation (19:41, 26:20).
- **Candle profile (OHLC / OLHC)**: bullish = open, low, high, close (opens low first); bearish = open, high, low, close (18:21-19:36).
- **Drivers**: the key times that make the daily body: 8:30 news ("red folder"), 9:30 index open, a 10 AM news release; 10 AM is also a 4H candle open (23:43-25:10).
- **Model (reversal point -> target)**: internal -> external, external -> internal, swing/range low -> swing/range high (his favorite) (14:19-14:41).
- **Key levels he uses**: FVG / internal range liquidity, swing points, order blocks, plus "order paying ranges" [order-pairing? unclear], external-to-internal and opposing candles (11:17, 27:06).
- **Strength switch**: the weaker asset of an SMT pair temporarily prints stronger price action than the stronger one so it can catch up to the level (36:58-39:23).
- **Fake SMT**: divergence where the stronger asset fails to reverse; the lagging asset will still take the level (36:30-36:43, 55:12).
- **Propulsion block**: opposing candles then a close through them, referenced from TTrades' channel (44:15).

## Procedure / checklist
1. HTF narrative: identify the phase of price on daily/weekly (expansion, consolidation, retracement, reversal) and the draw on liquidity (14:19, 59:12).
2. Choose a model (internal -> external, external -> internal, or range low -> range high) and the key level that should form the low (bullish) or high of the day (14:19, 21:24).
3. Require manipulation at that level paired with SMT; check strength / strength switch across correlated assets (07:49, 36:30).
4. Candle profile: the day should open on the correct side (bullish = open low first) and form its low from a key level, ideally before the drivers and not after ~10:30 (18:29-18:57).
5. EQ filter: the next candle's low must hold the upper half of the prior expansion/C2 candle or wick; otherwise it does not support expansion (16:32-17:15).
6. 4H session profile (futures 2 AM / 6 AM / 10 AM): apply the if-then chain and driver pairing; if the current 4H candle has a large wick, wait for the next open (22:30-25:10).
7. Entry: CISD (or small opposing candle / FVG) on the LTF inside a 4H expansion candle inside a daily expansion candle (30:40, 1:02:39).
8. Targets: daily swing points / opposing liquidity; if the day has a large opposing wick, target only back to the open or current-day session highs/lows (20:25, 58:23).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Trading away from failure swings instead of manipulation; failure swings are targets (07:30, 1:00:00).
- Pattern trading: taking every SMT, key level or C2 closure; C2 must contain a LTF reversal signature, not a consolidation (44:37, 1:01:51).
- Treating a large 6 AM wick as a reversal when it is only a LTF retracement (42:14).
- Reading every SMT as a reversal; check whether the strongest asset fails to reverse and whether strength is switching (36:30, 55:12).
- After manipulation price should expand; a deep retracement after manipulation is an invalidation (34:55-35:05).
- Expecting continuation past the open after a large opposing wick; cut targets (20:25, 58:23).
- Early-week NFP trading expected to be low probability (59:38).
