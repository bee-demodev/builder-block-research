# ICT Son's Model - Higher Time Frames

## TL;DR
Follow-up to his earlier "ICT Son's model" video, scaling the model to higher-timeframe draws. Two frameworks: (a) 1m entry: 4H or 1h draw on liquidity (old high/low or imbalance), a 15m stop raid opposite the draw plus a candle close back in, then a 1m FVG entry toward the draw; (b) 5m entry: daily or 4H draw, an hourly stop raid opposite the draw (he does not wait for the close back in), then a 5m entry model toward the draw. SMT between ES and NQ at the swept/unswept extreme is used as confluence. Targets: draw on liquidity, partials at equal highs / failure swings, standard deviation projections when the draw gives too little RR. This is an early form of the TTFM timeframe-pairing idea (HTF draw, mid-TF sweep, LTF entry).

## Definitions
- **Draw on liquidity**: target old high/low or imbalance on the HTF (00:34).
- **Stop raid** ("stop rate"): sweep of an old high/low on the middle timeframe opposite the draw (00:34, 05:07).
- **SMT**: ES and NQ disagree at a swing (one takes it, the other does not) (01:29, 04:01, 05:58).
- **External to internal**: after external liquidity is taken, the next draw is an internal imbalance (02:57).
- **BISI**: buy-side imbalance sell-side inefficiency (bullish FVG), here closed below (03:32).
- **Low resistance liquidity**: stacked higher lows (04:01).
- **Failure swings**: swing points used to take profit early (06:33).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Pick the entry timeframe; pair the draw: 1m entry with 4H/1h draw; 5m entry with daily/4H draw (00:34, 05:07).
2. Mark the draw (old high/low, imbalance, wick CE) (00:34, 09:32).
3. On the middle timeframe (15m or 1h), wait for a raid of liquidity opposite the draw; on 15m wait for the close back in (01:58); on 1h he does not wait (05:07).
4. Check SMT between ES and NQ at the raided swing (01:29, 05:58).
5. On the entry timeframe, enter on an FVG / inversion gap / OB at a discount (premium for shorts), stop on the swing or the day's extreme (01:58, 04:29, 08:04).
6. Target the draw; partials at equal highs / failure swings; SD projection if RR < ~2 (02:27, 06:33, 10:58).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: NQ 1h / 15m / 1m, long to equal highs (01:29-02:57)
- ES took an hourly low, NQ did not (SMT); draw = equal highs (London highs). 15m old low swept and closed back in; 1m displacement up, equal highs before New York open; entry at range discount, 1:2R; partials at equal highs. Judas off the open, then higher; London highs taken.
### Example 2: NQ, short to hourly FVG (02:57-05:07)
- After London highs taken, draw = hourly FVG (external to internal). 15m swept London highs, bearish close below a BISI; ES did not take London highs (SMT). 1m: higher lows as low-resistance liquidity; FVG entry, stop on the high; hourly FVG reached.
### Example 3: ES 4H / 1h / 5m, long (05:32-07:04)
- 4H external to internal, draw = 4H inefficiency above. Hourly low swept with SMT vs NQ. 5m inversion gap closed over; entry toward the draw. Failure-swing exit would have lost half a point on ES but avoided drawdown.
### Example 4: S&P daily / 1h / 5m, short (07:04-09:21)
- Daily FVG with an old low above it = draw. Midnight open marked; London sweeps an hourly high. 5m: accumulation, manipulation, distribution into New York; displacement, order block, FVG; short above open, stop ideally at the high of the day; second entry added; draw hit. Profile: open, high, low, close.
### Example 5: NQ daily / 1h / 5m, short (09:32-11:54)
- Daily up-close candle closed below and retested; large-wick CE marked; hourly lows = draw. Price reaches wick CE (no bodies over), displaces, New York sweeps an hourly swing high. 5m FVG short, stop on the high; draw only 1.14R, so target the 2nd SD (hit); hourly draw lines up with the 4th SD; old low taken.

## Mistakes and warnings
- A draw that gives only ~1R: do not force it; project standard deviations or pick a better target (02:27, 10:58).
- Holding to the full draw can mean sitting through drawdown; failure swings as partial exits (06:33).
- Local stop vs day's high: if trying to catch the daily range, use the high of day (08:04).

## Conflicts and open questions
- Title says "Higher Time Frames", but the intro says the model is used "on the five minute and one minute chart" (00:10): "higher" refers to the draw timeframe.
- 15m raid requires a close back in (00:34, 01:58); the 1h raid in the 5m framework explicitly does not (05:07). Unclear if this is a deliberate rule difference or personal preference.
- "Discount of this range" (01:58): which range (post-sweep swing low to high) is not defined.
