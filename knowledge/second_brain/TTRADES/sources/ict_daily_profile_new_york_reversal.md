# ICT Daily Profile - New York Reversal

## TL;DR
Part 2 of his ICT-era daily profile series (part 1 = London reversal). A New York reversal day is a daily candle that forms OHLC/OLHC with its wick made in New York: London trades toward but never reaches a relevant HTF PD array, price keeps retracing into the NY session, hits the HTF key level, prints a CISD (an order block close-through), then expands. A London "reversal" that did not touch an HTF level is a fake CISD that sets up the NY one. Timing anchors are 8:30, 9:30, 10:00 and midnight; targets are failure swings / low-resistance liquidity (session highs/lows, previous day levels). Focus sessions: London and NY AM, for indices.

## Definitions
- **New York reversal (daily profile)**: OHLC/OLHC daily candle whose wick is completed in the NY session after London failed to reach a relevant HTF PD array (00:44-01:37).
- **Fake CISD**: a London CISD that formed without an HTF level being reached; it builds equal lows/highs for NY to run (03:43).
- **Order block (his usage here)**: the series of opposing candles whose close-through is the CISD (06:16).
- **Propulsion block**: an order block built off an order block (07:03).
- **Low-resistance liquidity / failure swings**: lined-up session highs/lows and previous-day lows that price runs through easily (03:12, 09:01).
- **Higher time frame imbalance**: a gap between HTF candles (11:14).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Daily: set the bias from the reversal candle / wick context and mark the HTF key level and draw on liquidity (05:16-05:37, 09:01).
2. London (2-5 am): did it reach the HTF level and form the high/low? If not, or if it only built equal highs/lows, expect NY (03:26, 05:45, 09:29).
3. NY: watch 8:30, 9:30, 10:00; wait for the sweep into the HTF level and a CISD (order block close-through), ideally at an important time (02:31, 09:41).
4. Entry on the close; stop at the swing or beyond the order block; target failure swings / session highs-lows, partials above 2R (02:42, 04:39, 06:23).
5. Re-entries: new order block from an 8:30 opposing run; propulsion block (06:38-07:05).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: bullish NY reversal off previous day low (01:41-03:22)
- PDL = sellside liquidity. London moves lower and higher without reaching an HTF level. NY reaches the level; first CISD fails, new low; new series of down-close candles lines up with the NY open; close over; stop at the bottom of the order block; runs all failure swings above.

### Example 2: fake London reversal (03:22-05:12)
- London forms a low, but no HTF level was reached, only more equal lows. NY purges the lows; failure swings stacked above. M15 CISD would print far away, so he drops lower. At the NY open: sweep, cannot close below, close over = order block; stop on low; targets London and Asia highs; partials; runs through the high by midnight.

### Example 3: bearish reversal candle, 30m/15m (05:12-07:32)
- Daily respecting the lower half / CE of a wick, closing back inside the previous candle's high: bearish reversal candle. London trades higher (no London high). NY displaces down through up-close candles (30m order block); failure swings from Asia/London below; stop on high or above OB; target 2.65R; midnight support; hits TP after getting tagged in. 8:30 opposing run forms a new order block (also midnight confluence) = propulsion block; continues lower.

### Example 4: external liquidity and the continuation day, 15m (07:32-10:45)
- Range high and most recent swing low. Day 1: London sweeps the low, NY open makes an opposing run and takes the previous day's high, then aggressive move out and back in = CISD; stop above OB, 2R into session lows; not tagged in. Daily: swept one side of the range, PDLs lined up, bearish reversal candle closing inside the previous day high: expect OHLC. Next day: London's high swept, NY: 8:30 manipulation up then displacement and close lower; 9:30 opposing run (LTF order block), then takes PDL and lows; OHLC forms.

### Example 5: ES 1h then 15m, London and NY combined (10:45-12:44)
- ES hourly: reversal with SMT at the highs, low-resistance liquidity below, order block that swept lows into a gap; HTF imbalance from high to low. 15m: London takes the previous high, close below an up-close candle = London CISD; continuation lower reaches the HTF objective; 8:30 manipulation up, NY manipulation down into the objective then close over: OB entry targeting equal highs; works.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Calling a London low a reversal when no HTF PD array was reached (03:34, 05:02).
- Using the wick low as stop on a far target when the order block gives better risk-to-reward (02:45).
- Expecting the first CISD at the level to work; a failed one means a deeper probe first (02:11).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Order block" and "CISD" are treated as the same object here (06:16); later TTFM material prefers CISD language. The definition of the series (how many candles) is not given.
- "Relevant HTF PD array" is never listed exhaustively (PDL, wick CE, previous range, HTF imbalance appear in examples).
- Session times: London "2 to 5" (05:48); NY times 8:30/9:30/10:00 (09:41); NY AM end time not stated. Time zone not stated (ET implied).
