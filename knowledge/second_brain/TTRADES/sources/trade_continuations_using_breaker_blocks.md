# Trade Continuations Using Breaker Blocks

## TL;DR
Most traders use breakers at the reversal; TTrades shows how to use them for continuations, as an alternative to his usual order-block continuation entries. After the HTF reversal (daily closure + CISD), wait for a short-term swing in the new trend to be swept, then for a new higher high (bullish) / lower low (bearish): the up-close (bullish) or down-close (bearish) candles of that mini-swing form the continuation breaker. Enter on the close or the retest, stop on the swing or the far side of the breaker, target 2R or the range/HTF liquidity. Exceptions: SMT can stand in for the sweep, and a reach into an FVG can stand in (he admits that one "really isn't" a breaker). He still prefers order blocks as simpler.

## Definitions
- **Breaker block (bearish)**: high, low, higher high, lower low; down-close candles from the high to the low (00:31-00:49).
- **Breaker block (bullish)**: low, high, lower low, higher high; up-close candles before the lower low (00:49-01:04).
- **Continuation breaker**: a breaker formed after the reversal, from a sweep of a short-term swing in the new trend plus a new higher high / lower low (01:19-01:43).
- **SMT breaker**: no sweep on this asset, but SMT with the correlated asset; the up-close candles are treated as the breaker once broken (01:56, 08:39-08:54).
- **Intermediate-term low** (advanced market structure): forms by sweeping a short-term low or reaching into an FVG (02:22-02:31, 04:16).
- **Propulsion block**: order block at the mean threshold that has been closed through (12:46-12:58).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Daily: bias from closure (sweep + close back in / engulf) toward liquidity to the left (05:25, 06:56).
2. 1h: confirm CISD; do not trust a sweep low until bullish structure / CISD (03:03, 07:22).
3. Find a short-term swing in the new direction, wait for it to be swept (or SMT, or reach into FVG) (04:05, 01:56).
4. Wait for the new higher high / lower low to complete the breaker; mark the up/down-close candles (bodies if wicks are messy) (04:30-04:42).
5. Enter on the close or retest; stop on the swing or other side of the breaker (01:43, 04:45).
6. Target 2R or range/HTF liquidity; optionally refine on 5m (or 1m for 15m) (06:24, 12:29).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: RB (as heard) 1h → 5m, range low swept (02:52-05:22)
- Range; range low taken, failure swings on the high. Close over down-close candles → bullish structure; wait for a swing low (02:52-03:35).
- 5m: series of down-close candles that formed the low closed through → CISD (03:45-03:58). Low swept, nice reaction, higher high; breaker by bodies; entry at its start, stop on other side of the breaker, target range high; hit after a while (04:28-05:05).
### Example 2: daily → 1h bearish (05:22-06:46)
- Daily: high taken, bearish closure; target lows to the left. 1h with indicator: sweep, close back in, CISD (05:22-05:48).
- High swept → high, low, higher high, lower low; down-close candles = breaker; entry on retest, stop on other side of breaker; target 2R / lows to the left; hit (05:54-06:35).
### Example 3: daily → 1h → 5m bullish with SMT breaker (06:46-09:29)
- Daily: aggressive sell-off sweeps a low, closes back higher (06:46-07:01). 1h: consolidation low swept into FVG; wait for CISD (07:05-07:30).
- 5m: expansion into consolidation; lows purged; messy low, high, lower low, higher high; entry on retest, stop below, ~2R; tag-in and hit (07:36-08:33).
- Second continuation: low not swept but SMT → up-close candles as breaker; close over, entry on retest, stop on low; 2R hit (08:36-09:15).
### Example 4: daily → 1h → 5m bearish (09:29-12:08)
- Daily: expansion, retracement, expansion, swing high → bearish day expected (09:32-09:50). 1h: CISD; high to the left swept → down-close candles = breaker (09:55-10:23). Blind short could work, but he prefers bearish closures (sweep + engulf) (10:23-10:48).
- 5m: highs swept, lower low breaks; entry on retest of down-close candle, stop there or on the high, 2R hit (10:53-11:38). Hourly-only entry would also have hit 2R, slower (11:52-12:08).
### Example 5: 15m → 1m, bullish with SMT vs NQ (12:11-14:25)
- Equal highs above; SMT off the lows with NQ; bullish 15m closure (12:14-12:29). 1m: CISD; retrace to 50% (mean threshold) respected; propulsion block respected (12:36-12:58).
- Consolidation, reach into FVG, close higher (indicator shows continuation) (13:10-13:28). Lows swept, low/high/lower low/higher high → up-close candles = breaker; entry on close; stop on low or body; 2R hit after some time (13:39-14:21).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Trusting a swept low before bullish structure / CISD (03:03, 07:22).
- Trading the reversal breaker instead of waiting for the continuation breaker (06:38).
- Blindly shorting a breaker without bearish closures (10:23).
- Getting caught in consolidation chop instead of waiting for the sweep (09:17).
- Picking tops when late to the trend instead of waiting for a continuation (14:25).

## Conflicts and open questions
- Breaker definition here (high, low, higher high, lower low → down-close candles from the high to the low) is the classic structure; which exact candles (whole series vs last candles) are included varies by example ("this whole series from this low to this high", 01:37).
- The FVG-reach variant is explicitly "not a breaker" (02:39), so a rule engine must treat it separately.
- SMT exception has no defined correlated asset or SMT test.
- Retest entry vs close entry: no rule which to choose.
- "RB" (03:42) instrument is unclear from audio and frames (possibly RBOB gasoline futures); example 5's main instrument is not named (SMT "with NQ", likely ES).
