Sunday guest stream (2023) in which Day Trading Rauf, an ICT-style trader, walks through his weekly preparation for the week of 25-29 Sep 2023 while TTrades hosts and relays chat questions. No live trading; Rauf recounts EURUSD shorts from the prior week.
