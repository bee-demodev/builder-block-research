# Reversal or Pullback? Using Market Structure To Determine Trend - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Very early, short lesson: tell a pullback from a reversal with market structure shifts. In an uptrend, mark short-term lows; if none is broken with displacement it is just a pullback; a break with displacement (energetic move leaving a fair value gap / imbalance) is a market structure shift. Use the 5m as the trend read but check the 15m (less noise, recommended for most people): a 5m shift against an intact 15m trend is a counter-trend trade that should target the imbalance where longs reload, then a 5m or 1m bullish MSS inside that POI re-enters with the trend. A break without displacement does not count. Chart is ES (S&P 500 E-mini) 5m/1m/15m with on-chart annotations.

## Definitions
- **Pullback**: retracement within a trend that breaks no structure (or breaks lower-timeframe structure only) (01:03, 01:34).
- **Market structure shift (MSS)**: displacement through a short-term swing that leaves an imbalance (FVG) (00:34, 03:41).
- **Imbalance**: fair value gap created by the displacement (00:34, 03:41).
- **Energetic move**: displacement (02:05, 03:41).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Read trend on 5m; confirm on 15m (00:34).
2. In an uptrend, mark short-term lows (01:03).
3. Low broken with displacement and FVG = MSS; without displacement = ignore (03:08).
4. If 5m breaks but 15m does not: counter-trend only to the 15m imbalance; otherwise wait (01:34).
5. In the POI, wait for a 5m or 1m bullish MSS and enter on the return to its imbalance (02:05).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: ES 5m / 1m (00:34-03:08)
- Bullish MSS leaves an imbalance -> uptrend. First short-term lows never broken (pullbacks). Next low broken on 5m, but 15m still bullish with a FVG below -> counter-trend short only to that imbalance. 5m bullish MSS there gave little; 1m MSS with displacement, imbalance, return -> long near the bottom. Repeats: 5m low broken but 15m intact; imbalance fills and price goes long. On-chart annotation at 02:15: "Since we are now down trending on the 5min you would want to look for a MSS back bullish or on the 1min chart inside that FVG."

### Example 2: ES 15m (03:08-04:11)
- Short-term highs not taken; one high broken without displacement or FVG -> not a shift. Later an energetic break with a FVG = MSS -> bullish trend; marks lows, none taken with displacement before the overnight session.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Treating a 5m break as a reversal while the 15m trend is intact (01:34).
- Counting a structure break without displacement / FVG (03:08).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Short-term low" is not defined (which swing size).
- Uses 5m as the trend frame but recommends 15m; no rule on which wins beyond "15m bigger picture".
- The 5m MSS in the POI "didn't really give us a whole lot" yet is called takeable (02:05); entry criteria between 5m and 1m are discretionary.

## Glossary additions
- `energetic move` / `energetically`: displacement.
- `reload longs`: re-enter longs at the higher-timeframe imbalance.
