## Mistakes and warnings
- Seeing an SMT and framing a whole trade around it without a higher-timeframe bias. Fix: Set the daily bias first; use SMT only to time trades in that direction. (29:25)
- Taking a valid-looking fractal pattern against the day's bias (e.g. NQ 15m shorts on a bullish day). Fix: Only frame trades in the bias direction; accept being wrong. (15:00)
- Trading the lagging/weakest asset in the bias direction. Fix: Trade the strongest asset (or ES, the middle asset, if relative strength is unclear). (21:40)
- Trading after the expansion, inside the following consolidation. Fix: Trade the expansion itself; skip post-expansion ranges. (26:00)
- Taking a technically valid continuation whose target was already taken. Fix: Require an additional continuation for confirmation, or ignore it. (51:40)
- Entering right at 09:30 with no trusted low and getting run out at the open. Fix: Wait 3-5 minutes for a closure / SMT; missing a move is acceptable. (14:30)
- Treating an inside-day close as bearish (or neutral) without looking inside it. Fix: Read the lower-timeframe path of the daily candle and check for a CISD. (44:00)
- Waiting for deep OTE retracements to enter fractal-model trades. Fix: Expanding price does not retrace much; a full retrace to OTE is not an entry. (1:01:40)
- Trying to capture every move in the market. Fix: Only trade what aligns with the bias and the model; sit on hands otherwise. (52:10)
