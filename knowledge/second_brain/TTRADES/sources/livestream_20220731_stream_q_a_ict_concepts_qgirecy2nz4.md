## Mistakes and warnings
- Hunting equal highs/lows and liquidity on the 1-minute chart, which gets confusing. Fix: Zoom out; mark liquidity on the 15-minute. (21:40)
- New ICT traders get sucked into the lower timeframes and watch the 1-minute all the time. Fix: Only drop to lower timeframes once there is a higher-timeframe reason (e.g. price retracing into a 5-minute FVG); keep referencing the 5m and 15m. (30:10)
- Shorting in a discount (e.g. after a wrong bias). Fix: Only short from premium; counter-trend targets are imbalances in discount or the expected higher low. (16:00)
- Chasing a move that left without retracing. Fix: No retracement, no risk-to-reward: skip it. (35:10)
- Entering on a weak structure break without displacement leads to a quick stop-out. Fix: Prefer a break where the candle closes through with displacement. (15:00)
