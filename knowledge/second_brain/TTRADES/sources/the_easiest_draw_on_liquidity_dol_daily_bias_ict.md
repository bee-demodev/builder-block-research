# The Easiest Draw On Liquidity (DOL) - Daily Bias - ICT

## TL;DR
Early (pre-TTFM) lesson, credited to the MMXM trader (00:10): the easiest draw on liquidity, present almost every day, is the previous day high or low. Unless price consolidates, it takes PDH, PDL or both; in a bullish trend it takes PDH over and over, in a bearish trend PDL. First question on the daily: is price more likely to reach PDH or PDL? That answer is the draw and the target; then drop to a lower timeframe for a POI entry. Bias refinements: failure to displace beyond PDH/PDL, closes through up-close candles (order block validation), and a three-day lookback of highs/lows. NQ daily walk-through then New York examples with AMD (accumulation, manipulation, distribution) into PDH; days where Asia already took PDH are skipped.

## Definitions
- **Draw on liquidity (DOL)**: the liquidity (here PDH/PDL) price is expected to reach; doubles as the target (02:54).
- **Failure to displace**: price trades beyond PDH/PDL but does not displace/close beyond it (03:14).
- **Consequent encroachment (CE)**: midpoint of a fair value gap or gap; a down-close candle respecting it signals continuation (01:54, 08:12).
- **Order block validation**: a close below an up-close candle validates it as a bearish order block (06:43, 07:14).
- **New week opening gap (NWOG)**: gap between Friday close and Sunday open; he marks it and its CE on the Monday example (08:12).
- **AMD**: accumulation (Asia/London consolidation), manipulation (move against the bias, e.g. on 8:30 news), distribution (move toward the draw) (08:12, 10:45).
- **Box setup**: his separate setup: deviation beyond a range/level, back inside, retest of the deviation before the move (04:58, 08:45).

## Procedure / checklist
1. On the daily, mark PDH/PDL (and the last three days' highs/lows) (04:29).
2. Decide which is more likely: trend, failure to displace, displacement through lows/highs, PD-array reactions (02:28, 03:14).
3. That level is the draw and target; extensions beyond it are possible (05:13).
4. If the target was already taken in Asia, skip the day (09:49).
5. In New York, look for accumulation (Asia/London) and a manipulation against the bias (8:30 news or open) (08:12, 10:45).
6. Drop to 5m; enter a FVG or the retest of the manipulation after a close through the 8:30 open; stop at the manipulation low; target PDH (08:45, 11:11).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: unnamed symbol 1D, PDH trend and PDL trend (00:53-02:28)
- Uptrend with supporting PD arrays: PDH reached day after day. Downtrend: PDL and other lows taken; one day PDL not taken -> retracement into a FVG, down-close candle respecting its CE, then PDL reached.

### Example 2: NQ 1D bias walk-through (05:13-07:44)
- Aggressive displacement higher -> PDH repeatedly; then both PDH and PDL taken with a bearish close and a reach into a daily FVG -> PDL. Up-close candle does not change the idea; targets PDL and equal lows. Sweep below with a close back inside: not long because structure is bearish; wants a close below the up-close candle (order block). Next day closes below it -> PDL again, twice; finally displaces below a low and closes back inside -> PDH or consolidation.

### Example 3: NQ Monday, New York long into PDH (07:44-09:22)
- Marks the new week opening gap and its CE plus PDH (the draw). Asia and London consolidate, New York open manipulates down -> expects distribution into PDH. 5m FVG entry, stop at the low, about 2R to the high. Price dips into the range deviation, rejects off the NWOG CE, then expands into PDH.

### Example 4: NQ next day, skipped (09:22-10:14)
- Asia session already took PDH -> no interest in New York; price continues up in the AM session anyway.

### Example 5: NQ next day, 8:30 news AMD (10:14-11:40)
- London consolidates, takes a high, moves lower; 8:30 news manipulates down. Wants a close below the 8:30 opening price and the down-close candles, then a retest of the manipulation (box setup) before distribution. Price reaches PDH, completing AMD.

### Example 6: NQ following day, skipped (11:40-12:37)
- PDH taken at the start of Asia -> not watching later sessions; choppy session before reaching a daily FVG.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Going long just because PDL was swept and closed back inside while structure is bearish (06:43).
- Expecting an entry every day because there is a bias every day (07:44, 12:37).
- Trading New York for a target already hit in Asia (09:49, 12:15).

## Conflicts and open questions
- His daily bias video treats a sweep of PDL with a close back inside as bullish; here he overrides it with bearish structure and order-block validation (06:43). The rule for which wins is "structure", not stated precisely.
- "Three days" lookback (04:29): unclear whether the third-back day's high/low is a target equal to PDH/PDL or only confluence.
- Extensions beyond PDH/PDL (05:13) are not quantified.
- Close below the 8:30 opening price is the trigger (11:11) on a bullish day; direction of that close vs bias is not explained (likely the manipulation leg closing below the open).
