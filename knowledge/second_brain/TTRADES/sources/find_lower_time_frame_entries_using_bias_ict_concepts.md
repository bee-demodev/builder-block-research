# Find Lower Time Frame Entries Using Bias - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Early TTrades ICT video, follow-up to his intraday-bias video and intro to his "build a model" series. Process: on the higher timeframe (here 15m) mark points of interest (old/equal highs and lows, FVGs, order blocks); bias is just waiting for price to reach those POIs. At a POI drop to a lower timeframe (1m) and wait for a setup: displacement with real FVGs against the move, then a return to a FVG / order block / OTE for entry, stop on the high, targets at internal range liquidity / OTE or the session and equal lows. Do not auto-enter at the POI and do not chase; take secondary entries on later premium PD arrays while no displacement breaks the bearish structure. Replay of one session on an unnamed instrument. In TTFM terms: HTF level (C2 area) → LTF confirmation (CISD-like displacement) → entry in the PD array.

## Definitions
- **Point of interest (POI)**: old highs/lows, equal highs/lows, imbalances, order blocks — areas to watch price (05:02-05:25).
- **Judas swing**: a false move up before the intended drop (01:05).
- **Order block (bearish, here)**: opening of a series of up-close (green) candles before they move down (03:30).
- **Sell side of the curve**: the declining leg, where premium PD arrays should resist price (04:30).

## Procedure / checklist
1. HTF (15m): mark POIs (05:02-05:25).
2. Wait for price to reach a POI; do not enter there blindly (01:05, 01:58).
3. LTF (1m): wait for displacement with big FVGs through structure (02:25).
4. Enter on the FVG / order block, check OTE; stop on the high (02:57-03:58).
5. Targets: internal range liquidity / OTE, then lows / equal lows marked earlier (02:57).
6. Add or re-enter on later premium PD arrays until an aggressive opposing displacement appears (04:30, 05:54-06:23).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: unnamed instrument 15m → 1m short session (00:06-06:47)
- 15m POIs: relative equal lows, an old low, highs already taken, a 15m FVG and a high above (00:06-00:36).
- FVG just filled; not biased (aggressive up met with aggressive down, at the low of the range / "basically low of the year"); wants a Judas swing up first (01:05).
- Price runs the highs; 1m: no structure break, obvious liquidity grab, no closes below; brief drop reversed more aggressively up (01:34-01:58).
- Next POI higher; waits for displacement; tiny FVG = possible but unconvincing; then aggressive drop with an FVG → short on the FVG or order block, risk on the high, targets IRL/OTE or lows/equal lows (02:25-02:57).
- Second entry: FVG retrace refined to the order block, inside the OTE, same stop and target (03:30-03:58).
- Run of new highs then short; bearish OB on the sell side of the curve as an add (04:30). Highs hold; drop into the lows (05:02).
- Lunchtime secondary entry: aggressive up, aggressive down, small high raided, FVG sell (06:23).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Entering at the POI without an LTF setup — price can keep going (01:58).
- Chasing a missed entry; wait for the next PD array (03:30).
- Treating a high broken without displacement as a reversal (06:23).

## Conflicts and open questions
- Instrument, date and session times are not stated; "lunchtime" is not given in clock time.
- "Big" FVG / "aggressive" displacement have no size rule; he contrasts displacement ranges visually (02:25).
