## Mistakes and warnings
- Trading reversals directly with the stop on the high and no confirmation; this is how he took most early losses. Fix: Wait for a lower high / higher low continuation. (23:20)
- Looking for deep retracements for entry during expansion. Fix: Expect shallow retracements (lower half of the gap); a deep one is a reversal. (29:10)
- Chasing a move that already ran (gold down 7%) or trying to catch its reversal at every gap/PDL. Fix: Accept the missed move; wait for the next clean candle 2 closure / candle 3. (41:20)
- Forcing trades on EURUSD when the dollar has no clean framework. Fix: Trade only when there is a clean candle 2 closure out of a POI. (38:20)
- Getting stressed/slow on entries on M1-M3. Fix: Move up a timeframe; identify root cause and solution. (49:10)
- Entering trades based on a streamer's idea and then asking for targets. Fix: Decide targets yourself before entering. (1:16:40)
- Flipping bias every time price moves against you. Fix: Only change view if there is a reason (relevant level hit). (1:28:20)
- Taking a closure out of a consolidation as a valid continuation. Fix: Treat it as a likely liquidity sweep; demand a cleaner framework when bias is weak. (1:13:20)
- Using OTE for retracement levels. Fix: He uses 0.5 of the wick instead. (57:30)
