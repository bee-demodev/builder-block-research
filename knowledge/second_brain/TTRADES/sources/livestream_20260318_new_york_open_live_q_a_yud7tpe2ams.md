## Mistakes and warnings
- Shorting after the lows (the target) are already taken. Fix: Short inside the range toward the lows; after the target is hit it's a chase. (14:50)
- Entering continuations late, after the short-term lows were already swept (why continuations fail). Fix: Enter only in the early part of the continuation, then hold with the stop above the new protected swing. (24:10)
- Pattern trading: seeing bullish and bearish T-spots at once and getting confused. Fix: Have a higher-timeframe idea first; only drop to the low timeframe because HTF analysis sent you there. (30:30)
- Shorting the strongest asset (NQ) on a bearish day. Fix: Short the weakest asset; the strongest will run you out while it forms SMT. (1:06:20)
- Bias right but stops swept before TP on M1/M3 entries. Fix: Stay on a higher timeframe for entry (15m, or 5m) and learn HTF opening prices. (1:10:30)
