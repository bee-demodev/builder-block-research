# Change In State Of Delivery [CISD] - Orderblock Formation - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Defines CISD: price moving into a higher-timeframe PD array or important level, then closing beyond the opening price of the up-close (or down-close) candle or series of candles that made the extreme; that close flips delivery from bullish to bearish (or the reverse) and validates the series as an order block. With a series, use the opening price of the first candle. He then builds an entry model: manipulation (sweep / failure to displace) -> CISD -> entry on the order block / FVG with stop on the manipulation extreme -> targets from standard-deviation projections of the manipulation leg (-2 to -2.5, then -4) and nearby liquidity. Last section adds CISD as confirmation to the AMD "box setup". This is the CISD building block later central to the TTFM.

## Definitions
- **Change in state of delivery (CISD)**: price flips from bullish to bearish (or reverse) when a close occurs beyond the opening price of the single candle or series of opposite-direction candles that delivered price into an HTF PD array / important level (00:10-01:35).
- **CISD level with a series**: the opening price of the first candle in the series (01:06, 01:35).
- **Order block (here)**: the up-close (bearish OB) or down-close (bullish OB) candle(s) whose opening price was closed through; "validated" by that close (03:40, 10:50, 13:30).
- **Manipulation**: the sweep or failed displacement out of a range before the CISD; projected for targets (07:56, 11:29).
- **Standard deviation projection**: projecting the manipulation leg (high to low) to -2/-2.5 and -4/-4.5 for targets; refers to his previous projection video (03:11, 05:20, 06:23).
- **Box setup (AMD)**: accumulation range, highest body (bearish) / lowest body (bullish) as the box line, manipulation deviation, entry on a retest of the line, stop on the manipulation, targets from projections (12:25-12:54, 14:04).
- **Next day model**: daily candle respects a daily PD array and closes away; next day takes the previous day's extreme (04:21).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Identify HTF PD array / important level or liquidity (old highs, daily FVG) (01:35, 04:21).
2. Wait for manipulation: sweep of the level or failed displacement out of the range; prefer an LTF sweep (02:06, 07:56).
3. Mark the opening price of the first candle of the series that made the extreme; wait for a candle CLOSE beyond it = CISD (01:06, 08:27).
4. Project the manipulation leg; note -2/-2.5 and -4/-4.5, and liquidity sitting there (03:11, 10:18).
5. Enter at the CISD or on a retest of the order block / FVG, within premium (shorts) of the dealing range; stop on the manipulation extreme (08:27, 09:35).
6. Target liquidity (London lows, equal lows, old high) and/or projections; require about 2R (03:11, 06:23).
7. If missed, re-enter on later order blocks formed by new closed-through series (08:59).

## Mistakes and warnings
- A sweep plus displacement back into the range is not a CISD until a candle closes beyond the opening price of the series that made the high (02:06).
- Do not take a sub-2R trade at a liquidity target; extend the target with projections or skip (03:11).
- Shorts only from the premium of the dealing range (09:35).
- In the box setup, entering immediately on the retest without an order-block formation is the unconfirmed version (12:54).

## Conflicts and open questions
- PDF rule says use the opening price of the FIRST candle in a series (01:06), but at 08:27 he says "close below this last up close candle"; whether a single-candle CISD at the last candle suffices when it is part of a series is unclear.
- "Series" boundary undefined: does an interleaved down-close candle end the series?
- At 07:31 the projections are said to be "from the high of the week", while at 04:51 the projected leg is the hourly manipulation leg from high to low; anchor points for projections need defining.
- Which close counts (body close on the execution timeframe vs any timeframe) is implied, not stated: he uses the execution chart's candle close.
- Stop choice in box + CISD (swept CISD high vs manipulation high) is discretionary ("I can decide", 13:30).
- Example 1 symbol is not said or visible in frames.
