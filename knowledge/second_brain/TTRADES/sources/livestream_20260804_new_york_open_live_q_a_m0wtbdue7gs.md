## Mistakes and warnings
- Entering after the candle (or day) has already made its range, i.e. at the highs. Fix: Enter mid-candle or near the opening price; if the range is made, pass. (11:30)
- Predicting the 9:45/10:00 candle or tomorrow's daily before it closes. Fix: Wait for the closure; base anticipation on closures. (18:10)
- Flipping bias because price didn't give your preferred entry. Fix: No entry, same bias and target. (15:20)
- Trading a lower-timeframe reversal formation that fades the 4H and all higher timeframes. Fix: Use it as a clue for correlated assets, not a trade. (28:40)
- Dropping to the 1-minute to call every wiggle while waiting on a level. Fix: Sit ~30 minutes and watch the reaction on the working timeframe. (35:10)
- Widening the stop after a string of losses. Fix: Keep the mechanical stop; move up one timeframe for entry timing. (58:20)
- Adding intraday risk in the same direction as an open swing (doubling exposure). Fix: Trade against the swing intraday as a hedge, or not at all. (34:20)
- Shorting a distant HTF gap (SIBI) just because it exists. Fix: Trade what price is doing now; reference POIs, don't trade them blindly. (1:05:45)
- Seeking more strategy when backtests work and live results don't. Fix: Find the self-sabotage (fear, ego, tilt, oversizing) and fix it, or quit. (53:20)
- Moving the stop to avoid green-to-red, getting stopped, then revenge trading. Fix: Stop doing what you know doesn't help; accept the discomfort of following rules. (1:01:20)
- Trading after several massive candles (e.g. GBPJPY after three big candles). Fix: Skip it or use the cleaner pair (USDJPY). (47:00)
- Watching micros and minis both and getting confused by their differing structure. Fix: Chart the minis only. (04:30)
