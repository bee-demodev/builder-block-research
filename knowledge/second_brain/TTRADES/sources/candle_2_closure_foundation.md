# Candle 2 Closure: The Foundation of TTrades Fractal Model

## TL;DR
Defines the candle 2 (C2) closure, one of the two TTFM closure types (C2 and C3). A C2 closure is a reversal closure: the candle sweeps the previous candle's high/low and closes back inside the previous candle's range; it anticipates a C3 continuation, and a C3 close beyond C2's extreme anticipates a C4 expansion. A C2 closure only counts at a higher-timeframe POI (swing high/low, FVG; per the page also an opposing candle). He drills identification by marking every swing point on a daily chart and filtering for real C2 closures at a POI, then confirms on the aligned timeframe (1D->1h, 1h->5m, 4H->15m) with a CISD from the series that made the extreme, using EQ (0.5 of the C2 wick / C3 range) as the hold area.

## Definitions
- **Candle 2 closure (C2)**: reversal closure at a HTF POI; sweep of previous candle's high/low with a close back inside its range (00:57). Page: "A Candle 2 closure is always a reversal closure."
- **Candle 3 closure**: the other TTFM closure type; mentioned only (a 4H example at 11:53 is labelled C3, "a future video").
- **Continuation closure**: close beyond the previous candle's high/low (00:36).
- **POI (for framing a closure)**: swing high/low, FVG (01:39); opposing candles after a protected swing (06:22); page adds "an opposing candle" when neither is present.
- **CISD (here)**: close through the series of candles that made the high/low (07:44, 10:09).
- **T-spot**: the zone (boxes on chart) where he looks to enter the continuation after CISD, plotted by his indicator (10:55).

## Procedure / checklist
1. On a HTF chart (daily), mark every swing point (03:45).
2. Remove swings with no close back inside the previous candle's range (04:43).
3. Keep only those where the sweep took a POI: high/low or FVG (05:01-06:11).
4. Prefer C2s that are reversals, not already-expanded candles (05:30).
5. Drop to the aligned timeframe (1D->1h, 1h->5m, 4H->15m); mark the CISD from the series that made the extreme (07:20, 09:10).
6. Watch EQ: 0.5 of C2's wick and of the previous candle range; bias holds while price respects it (03:08, 08:10).
7. Look for lower-timeframe C2 closures at POIs inside the move for continuation entries; T-spot boxes (08:40, 10:53).
8. If C3 closes beyond C2's extreme, expect C4; be careful from C5-C6 (02:12, 11:38).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Treating every sweep-and-close-back as a C2 without a HTF POI (01:30, 02:53).
- Counting swings where price never closed back inside the previous range (04:43).
- Trusting a C2 that is really an expansion candle with a shallow wick (05:30).
- Overstaying past candle 5-6 (11:38).

## Conflicts and open questions
- POI list in speech is FVGs and highs/lows (01:39); the page adds opposing candle as fallback; the video only allows opposing candles after a protected swing, called "more advanced" (06:26).
- "Respect the 0.5" for C3 relative to C2's wick is not given a closing vs wick rule (03:16).
- How far back a swept high may be (monthly high "from quite long ago") is unbounded (05:20).
