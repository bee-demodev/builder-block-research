## Mistakes and warnings
- Overthinking all-time highs because there is nothing to the left Fix: Keep trading the candles in front of you; use a swing point or ADR as the target (15:00)
- Entering after the day has already expanded through the highs (late) Fix: Be in before the expansion; otherwise wait for the next day (18:00)
- Fading a big daily candle back across the range Fix: Wait for the daily close and look for a small wick on the next daily candle (36:40)
- Shorting a 1H C2 aggressively before it closes, counter-trend with no POI Fix: Acknowledge it is counter-trend; use ADR as a POI or wait for the closure (14:30)
- Entering trades too early Fix: Wait for a continuation (37:20)
- Revenge trading / tilt / overtrading blows funded accounts Fix: One trade a day max for at least a month (48:20)
- Getting stopped out on the 5m before price goes to target Fix: Go up one timeframe for the entry (15m) (54:30)
- Pattern-trading lower timeframes without a higher-timeframe context Fix: Start from a higher-timeframe bias/closure (1:12:00)
- Chasing a profit target within a time limit in prop evaluations Fix: Just follow your rules; size by drawdown (1:14:00)
- Trading the volume-profile POC Fix: Trade the edges of the range or HTF low-volume nodes (41:40)
- Using CFDs and expecting his 4H candles and levels Fix: CFDs have different opens/closes and levels; use futures for matching candles (51:20)
