# Which Swing Highs and Lows Matter in Trading?

## TL;DR
TTrades defines which swing points are "relevant" (worth expecting a reversal from) versus "failure swings" (stacked or too-close highs/lows that price is expected to run through). A relevant swing is the extreme of a cluster and has "valid separation" from the previous swing; valid separation is judged by eye, not by a number. Relevance is limited by a fractal lookback of three higher-timeframe candles including the current one (daily chart: 3 months; hourly chart: 3 days). Relevant swings are the POIs where he waits for a TTFM candle 2 / candle 3 closure; failure swings (stacked liquidity) are targets, and he wants none of them on the stop-loss side. The video assumes his separate failure-swings video.

## Definitions
- **Swing point (bullish)**: a candle whose low has a higher low to its left and to its right (00:35). 3-candle fractal low.
- **Swing point (bearish)**: a candle whose high has a lower high to its left and to its right (00:45).
- **Relevant swing high/low**: a swing point where a reaction or reversal can be expected. It is the extreme of nearby swings, has valid separation from the previous swing, and falls within the 3-HTF-candle lookback (00:52-03:34).
- **Failure swing**: a swing that is not expected to hold because older/stacked highs or lows sit beyond it or very close to it; "failure swings are those stacked highs, right? That stacked liquidity" (08:17). Covered in a separate prior video (00:17).
- **Valid separation**: enough range between a swing and the previous swing for a reversal there to be trusted; discretionary (01:54, 12:21).
- **Lookback period**: the current higher-timeframe candle plus the two before it (3 HTF candles); swings outside it are no longer treated as relevant (02:56-03:34, 10:06-10:43).
- **EQ**: equilibrium (50%) of the candle; with the wick, the level candle 3 should respect (02:51).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Mark previous highs and lows on the chart (04:24).
2. Take the current higher-timeframe candle and the two before it; only swings inside that window are candidates (03:10). Daily chart → monthly candles (3 months), hourly → daily candles (3 days) (10:06-10:43).
3. For each side, discard failure swings: highs/lows that are equal, stacked, or too close to an older swing beyond them. The extreme with valid separation is the relevant swing (01:02-02:20, 04:31-05:12).
4. Prefer relevant swings that are also higher-timeframe highs/lows (monthly high/low on the daily) (09:46-10:04).
5. When price trades into a relevant swing, classify the reaction (retracement, reversal, consolidation) and look for a candle 2 / candle 3 closure, with candle 3 respecting the wick or EQ (02:42, 03:36, 05:42).
6. Target the other side of the range or the failure swings (stacked liquidity); stop side should have high-resistance liquidity, with no failure swings (05:49, 08:17).
7. If a relevant swing is close to an older one ("sketchy"), wait for a close through and confirmation (08:49). Between relevant swings, treat moves as retracements unless there is SMT (09:00).
8. Re-mark relevant swings as new retracements form (06:21).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Trading a reversal off a swing that has highs/lows still to its left or very close to it; such swings are expected to be run (01:02-01:36, 05:05).
- Trading away from a low that has equal lows right below (04:53-05:12).
- Expecting a reversal from a fair value gap or minor high while a nearby high is left untaken (06:43, 08:36).
- Anticipating a reversal before price reaches the relevant swing without SMT (09:07).
- Using random highs/lows instead of relevant ones within the lookback (10:43).

## Conflicts and open questions

## Glossary additions
- `relevant swing` (relevant high/low): extreme swing with valid separation inside the 3-HTF-candle lookback; reversal candidate.
- `failure swing` (stacked highs/lows, stacked liquidity): swing expected to be run; a target, not a reversal point.
- `valid separation`: discretionary distance between a swing and the previous swing that makes it trustworthy.
- `look back period` (lookback): current HTF candle + 2 prior HTF candles.
- `higher time frame higher low` (mishearing): "higher time frame high or low".
