# ICT Daily Profile - London Reversal / New York Continuation

## TL;DR
Part 1 of a 4-part daily profiles series (older ICT-concepts era video). The London reversal profile is a "classic expansion" daily candle: London (2:00-5:00 a.m.) makes the daily wick (high in a bearish day, low in a bullish day) and shows a CISD / market structure shift; New York AM (8:30-12:00, he extends to 12) continues in the daily direction, completing open-high-low-close (or open-low-high-close). He trades the NY continuation: wait for an 8:30 opposing run / manipulation or a retracement into London's range, enter at the order block formed by closing through the series of candles, stop on the opposing run, target 2R; watch 8:30, 9:30 and 10:00 time levels. Bias comes from the daily candle (e.g. bullish reversal candle).

## Definitions
- **Daily profile**: the intraday sequence that builds the daily candle's OHLC across sessions (00:23, 01:54).
- **London reversal / NY continuation**: London (2-5 a.m.) makes the daily wick and reverses with a CISD; New York continues in the daily direction (01:10-01:57).
- **Classic expansion candle**: open, manipulation creating the wick, expansion, close (00:54-01:08).
- **Opposing run**: NY move against the expected direction (e.g. 8:30 moving higher in a bearish day) used as manipulation and stop location (05:25, 07:00-07:09).
- **Propulsion block**: order block formed by down-close candles into an order block, which gets retested (03:52, 05:46).
- **Kill zones**: Asia, London, New York AM, New York PM session windows (00:30).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Daily: determine direction from the daily candle (reversal candle, sweep of PDL/PDH + close back inside) and targets (internal liquidity, range EQ) (02:01, 09:52).
2. 15m/30m: during London 2-5 a.m., watch for the move into the previous range / sweep, then CISD (close through the series) (02:46, 05:00).
3. Do not trade London; wait for NY (06:41).
4. NY: at 8:30, expect an opposing run (manipulation) or a retracement into London's range (07:00, 08:04).
5. 5m: enter at the order block formed by the close through the opposing run's candles; stop on the opposing run; target 2R (03:43, 07:11).
6. Watch 9:30 and 10:00 for further turns/lows (07:28, 08:28, 09:37).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: Oil (CL) daily → 15m → 5m, bullish (02:01-04:41)
- Daily: bullish reversal candle, continuation higher; target internal liquidity, then range EQ; daily FVG marked (02:01-02:31).
- 15m: London 2-5 moves lower into the previous range, closes over the down-close candle = CISD (02:46-03:06).
- 5m NY: 8:30 consolidation, manipulation (AMD), close over down-close series = order block; entry, stop on the low, 2R; propulsion block retested; 2R hit (03:24-04:06). London made the daily wick, NY continued (04:12-04:36).
### Example 2: 15m/30m bearish (04:41-06:12)
- London sweeps a high; 30m up-close series used for CISD (05:00-05:21). NY: move higher into opposing candles / order block; 30m propulsion block retested around 10:30, volume imbalance at 8:30, continues lower (05:30-06:03).
### Example 3: bearish, 8:30 opposing run (06:12-07:37)
- London consolidates then reaches for a high at the end of London; CISD (06:20-06:43). NY: 8:30 moves higher = manipulation; close below = order block; entry, stop on the opposing run, 2R hit; low around 10:00 (06:53-07:34).
### Example 4: bearish, retracement into London range (07:39-08:57)
- London runs low resistance liquidity higher; close below up-close candles (07:44-07:58). 8:30 manipulates down instead of up → retracement into London's range → short; continuation lower at 10:00 (4H OHLC) (08:02-08:41). Daily closes open-high-low-close (08:50).
### Example 5: ES 15m (08:57-09:46)
- London sweeps one side of the range; single up-close candle = CISD (09:00-09:21). 8:30 news manipulates up into London's order block, continues lower; 9:30 move into an important level; left for next video (09:26-09:50).
### Example 6: daily bullish reversal → 30m (09:50-10:56)
- Daily: sweep below PDL, close back inside = bullish reversal candle (09:52-10:04). 30m: London 2-5 moves lower then closes over down-close series = order block; NY retests and trades to a high, cannot displace over it, back into range; mean threshold and midnight open hold; next day London forms a low and trades higher, finishing the TP (10:06-10:56).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Using a messy LTF series for the CISD; go up one timeframe (05:06).
- Expecting a direct continuation when 8:30 already manipulates in your direction; expect a retracement into London's range first (08:04).

## Conflicts and open questions
- This is an older ICT-concepts video: it says "change the state of delivery or get a market structure shift inversion however you define it" (01:43), so CISD and MSS are used interchangeably here, unlike later TTFM videos.
- No rule for how far London must move to count as the daily wick, or what to do if London's CISD comes after 5:00 (example 3 reaches the high "right at the end of London", 06:25).
- NY window end is personal ("I kind of extend this out to 12", 00:47); time zone assumed New York.
- Stop/target given only as 2R; no higher-timeframe target rule beyond internal liquidity / EQ in example 1.
- Instrument for examples 2-4 and 6 not named.

## Glossary additions
- `London reversal` (LR): daily profile where London makes the daily wick and reverses.
- `New York continuation`: NY AM session continuing London's reversal.
- `opposing run`: move against the expected direction used as manipulation / stop anchor.
- `ons side` (06:44) = on side.
- `inbalance` (05:57) = imbalance (volume imbalance).
