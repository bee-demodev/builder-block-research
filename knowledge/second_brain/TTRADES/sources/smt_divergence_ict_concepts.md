# SMT Divergence - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
SMT ("smart money technique/tool") is a divergence between correlated assets (ES/NQ, EU/GU): one makes a higher high (or lower low) while the other fails to. TTrades treats SMT only as a confluence that confirms a reversal at a pre-existing higher-timeframe idea or level, never as a stand-alone entry. He looks for it at session highs/lows, previous-day highs/lows and HTF POIs, then still waits for displacement and an entry model (breaker, unicorn, CISD, FVG). The video also covers inversely correlated pairs (EURUSD vs DXY, structure mirrored), picking which asset to trade after an SMT, and "SMT inside a fair value gap" as a way to read a lower-timeframe SMT from the higher-timeframe chart.

## Definitions
- **SMT (smart money technique / tool)**: divergence between correlated assets where one makes a new extreme (higher high / lower low) and the other does not (00:10, 01:05).
- **Correlated assets**: assets expected to have similar structure and direction, e.g. ES/NQ, EU/GU (both against the dollar) (00:38).
- **Inversely correlated assets**: assets with mirrored structure, e.g. EURUSD vs the dollar; a high on one pairs with a low on the other (12:18).
- **Lower-timeframe SMT**: divergence visible only as one asset's wick taking a level the other's does not on the HTF chart (03:38-04:34).
- **SMT within an FVG**: one asset trades into an FVG (above the LTF swing high forming it) while the other does not reach it (15:30-16:25).
- **High-resistance liquidity**: a high that ran another high; he puts stops there in volatile conditions (09:44).
- **Breaker block**: in a high, low, higher high, lower low sequence, the down-close candle (bearish case) (09:44).
- **Unicorn**: entry on a breaker block overlapping an FVG (07:35, used without separate definition).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Have a pre-existing HTF idea / draw on liquidity (e.g. reversal to sell-side liquidity) or HTF POI (02:37, 06:12).
2. Mark the same reference levels on both assets: session highs/lows, previous-day highs/lows, HTF POI (04:34, 05:03).
3. Watch for one asset to take the level while the other fails: that is the SMT (02:04).
4. Check the SMT is not cancelled: the lagging asset must not later take the same level (05:38).
5. Wait for displacement and structure (e.g. breaker, CISD, FVG) before entering (05:03, 08:40).
6. Choose the asset to trade by HTF setup / relative strength; on news days prefer the one that swept (10:12).
7. Stop: below/above breaker+FVG, or on high-resistance liquidity when volatile (07:35, 09:44).
8. Target failure swings / equal lows / original draw on liquidity; aim for at least 2R (08:07, 10:41, 11:50).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: ES vs NQ, intraday (01:34-03:38)
- Correlated structure shown on both (highs/lows at the same times). ES makes a higher high; NQ does not: SMT (02:04).
- ES eventually makes a higher high, but NQ still has not; then displacement down (02:37).
- Context: expected reversal to sell-side liquidity; Judas up after the midnight open, London puts in the high of day with an SMT; price reaches for the lows (03:09). He says SMT was "not the main reason" for the reversal, only confluence (03:38).

### Example 2: ES vs NQ 11 o'clock low, lower-timeframe SMT (03:38-04:34)
- HTF chart: ES wick takes the 11 o'clock low, NQ does not. On 5m: ES lower low, NQ higher low; confluence to the reversal.

### Example 3: ES vs NQ London high (05:03-06:12)
- London high marked on both. ES takes London high, NQ not yet. He does not short immediately. NQ fails to take it, a bullish PD array is not respected, price continues lower and runs the low.

### Example 4: EU vs GU forex SMT, long (06:35-08:40)
- EU lower low while GU higher low: SMT, but no displacement, so no trade. Aggressive move lower: GU takes a low, EU does not (second SMT).
- Displacement up; low, high, lower low, higher high. Entry: unicorn on the breaker block; stop below breaker and FVG; target failure swings, 3.2R. Down-close candles support price higher; take profit hit.

### Example 5: NQ vs ES, CPI day, short (08:40-12:18)
- NQ reaches a daily FVG and displaces lower; no short before 8:30 CPI. After CPI: NQ takes the high into the FVG, ES does not: SMT.
- Entry on the breaker block (high, low, higher high, lower low); stop on the high that ran another high (high-resistance liquidity) because the breaker is not clean and CPI adds volatility. Traded NQ (the one that swept). Target equal lows below.
- Body did not close over the breaker; price displaces lower; retrace into up-close candles / order block pushes lower.
- Alternate entry: 15m PD array, 1m chart, CISD close below up-close candles; stop on the high; 1.65R to the low, so he would move entry to the FVG for 2R.

### Example 6: EURUSD vs DXY, inverse correlation (13:14-15:30)
- Same highs/lows marked on both. EURUSD higher low while DXY makes a higher low instead of a lower high: SMT, reversal follows. DXY does not take a high that EURUSD does: SMT on the low. Next reference: the 10 a.m. level; Euro catches up then fails and DXY continues lower.

### Example 7: ES vs NQ, SMT inside a 15m FVG (15:30-16:53)
- Same 15m FVG on both. ES trades back into it, NQ does not: SMT; reaction lower. On 1m: ES reaches above the swing high forming the FVG, NQ cannot.

## Q&A
- **Q (10:12):** After an SMT, trade the asset that made the higher high or the one that failed? **A:** Depends on the HTF setup and relative strength, partly personal opinion; on a high-news day he prefers the one that swept liquidity (high-resistance liquidity for the stop).
- **Q (09:44):** Why put the stop on the high instead of just above the breaker? **A:** Breaker not clean, and CPI volatility; a high that ran another high is high-resistance liquidity.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Hunting SMTs everywhere: they appear constantly; only those at HTF levels/ideas matter (04:34).
- Entering immediately on an SMT: it is only confluence; wait for structure and displacement (05:03, 06:35, 08:40).
- Trading before high-impact news (CPI) even with an FVG reaction (08:40).
- Forgetting the SMT can be invalidated if the lagging asset takes the level (05:38).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Same time" matching between assets is visual (vertical lines); no tolerance (bars/minutes) is defined for pairing swings.
- Asset choice after an SMT is explicitly "personal opinion"; no rule a rule engine can encode except the news-day preference.
- No swing-point definition is given (how many bars make the high/low being compared).
- Example 6 narration is hard to follow (calls a DXY swing a "higher low" where a mirror of EURUSD is expected); the general inverse rule at 12:48 is clearer.
- He uses "PD array", "order block" and "unicorn" without defining them in this video.

## Glossary additions
- `SMT` (smart money technique, smart money tools): correlated-asset divergence used as reversal confluence.
- `high resistance liquidity`: a high that ran another high; stop anchor in volatile conditions.
- `PD array` (premium/discount array): generic term for POIs such as FVGs, order blocks, breakers.
- `GU` / `EU`: GBPUSD / EURUSD.
- `FX Replay`: replay tool used to demonstrate.
