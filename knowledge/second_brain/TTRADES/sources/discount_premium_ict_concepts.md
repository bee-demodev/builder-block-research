# Discount & Premium - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Early TTrades ICT lesson: mark a range swing high to swing low (the most prominent range) with a Gann box or fib; 0.5 is equilibrium, above = premium, below = discount. Ranges nest and expand: track the range extreme as price extends until a swing forms, and each new swing creates a new range. Why: entering at equilibrium with stop and target at the range extremes is 1R; deeper into discount (longs) / premium (shorts) improves R. Rule: longs from PD arrays in discount, shorts from PD arrays in premium. Examples: NQ daily FVG in discount, DXY weekly→daily nested ranges with an order block, GBPUSD daily → 15m with Asia sweep and an OTE entry (3.43R vs 1.6R at equilibrium). This is the dealing-range / equilibrium filter TTFM uses for C3/C4 retracement entries.

## Definitions
- **Range**: swing high to swing low, the most prominent one in the area (00:09).
- **Equilibrium**: 0.5 of the range (00:38).
- **Premium / discount**: above / below equilibrium (00:38).
- **Ranges within ranges**: each new swing makes a new range nested in the larger one (01:06).
- **Next day model**: after a sweep of Monday/Friday high and a close back in, expect the next day to drop to the discount FVG (06:08). Named only.

## Procedure / checklist
1. Pick the most prominent swing range; draw Gann box / fib (00:09-00:38).
2. If price is extending, keep moving the extreme until a swing forms (02:33-03:00).
3. Longs: find a PD array (FVG, order block) in discount; shorts: in premium (02:03, 03:00).
4. Wait for a raid / setup at that level (PDL raid, Asia low sweep) (06:38-07:09).
5. On the lower timeframe, draw the displacement range and enter in its discount / OTE; target the HTF PD array (07:09-07:42).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: NQ daily (02:33-03:36)
- Swing low, leg up, range tracked higher until a swing high; discount FVG for longs; price reaches it and moves higher.

### Example 2: DXY weekly → daily (03:36-06:08)
- Weekly up move marked (high to the low that made the range), then daily: discount FVG reached (04:06).
- New range from high down to new low; want price to reach premium of the previous range (04:39).
- Reversal model: LTF low swept, structure broken up; displacement range; discount order block to the left; price respects the OB opening price and expands higher (04:39-05:07).
- New range again; new day makes a new high, range tracked, price returns into discount (05:38).

### Example 3: GBPUSD daily → 15m long (06:08-08:14)
- Daily: FVG filled, new high, aggressive displacement range; small FVG in discount (06:08).
- Next day sweeps Monday and Friday's high and closes back in; next day expected to reach for the FVG (06:08).
- Raid of previous day low, close back in, FVG above → want price back up (06:38).
- 15m: midnight and Asia range marked; Asia low and older lows swept into the FVG, aggressive move higher → new range + OTE (07:09).
- OTE entry targeting the daily FVG: 3.43R vs 1.6R at equilibrium; no closes below the OTE; daily FVG reached and exceeded (07:42-08:14).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Many nested ranges make it confusing; pick the most prominent one and re-anchor on each new swing (01:06).
- Entering at equilibrium gives only 1R with extreme-to-extreme stop/target (01:34).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Most prominent range" is not defined numerically; which range applies when several are nested is shown case by case.
- 04:06 "looking for long positions. Or a draw on liquidity for short positions" — speech mixes directions; the chart logic is longs from discount.
- Raid "or some sort of setup" (06:38) leaves the trigger open.

## Glossary additions
- `gone box` (Whisper, 00:38): Gann box.
- `next day model`: sweep-and-close-back-in day followed by a move to the opposite PD array.
