# Simple Options Day Trading Strategy - Auto Plotted Levels and ICT entries!

## TL;DR
An early, rule-based 2-minute strategy shown on the AMD stock chart (options are only in the title). Rules: only play reversals off an auto-plotted level; after the reversal, a short-term high or low must be taken with displacement; enter on the return to the resulting imbalance (FVG or order block); stop at the low of the candle that made the imbalance; target the next highs/lows, imbalances or levels. If the imbalance is too large, enter at its 0.5 Fib or skip the trade. He moves the stop to breakeven after the first target. Pre-TTFM material.

## Definitions
- **Level**: auto-plotted level (indicator not named) that the reversal must come off (00:07, 01:40).
- **Imbalance**: FVG or order block created by the displacement (02:48, 03:50).
- **Short-term high/low**: minor swing taken after the reversal (00:07).

## Procedure / checklist
1. 2-minute chart (00:07).
2. Price reverses off an auto-plotted level (00:07).
3. A short-term high/low is taken with displacement (00:07, 01:05).
4. Mark the imbalance; enter on the return (00:33).
5. Stop at the low (long) of the candle that made the imbalance (00:33).
6. If the imbalance is too large, enter at its 0.5 or skip (00:33).
7. Targets: next highs/lows, imbalances, auto-plotted levels; after first TP move stop to breakeven (01:40, 02:11).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: AMD 2m long (00:33-02:48)
- Price hits a level and reverses. Two short-term high breaks without displacement are ignored; the third breaks with displacement up. Imbalance (FVG) marked; entry on return; stop at the low of the imbalance candle.
- Risk about 40 cents for about 80 to the first target ("almost two to one"). Targets: three successive highs then the auto-plotted level. Stop to breakeven after first TP; all targets hit.

### Example 2: hypothetical short, large imbalance (02:48-03:50)
- Reversal, short-term low taken with displacement; entering at the start of the imbalance risks about 60 cents; 0.5 Fib entry risks about 40 cents.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Taking a short-term high break without displacement (01:05).
- Entering at the start of a large imbalance; risk too big (02:48-03:18).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Too large" imbalance has no threshold.
- The auto-plotted levels indicator is not named; the title mentions options but the video trades the underlying chart only.
- Stop at "the low of the candle that made the imbalance" is for longs; short equivalent (high) implied.
- Breakeven after first TP here vs other videos where he warns breakeven on runners can stop you out early.
