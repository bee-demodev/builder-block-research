# Important Liquidity Levels - Draw On Liquidity - ICT

## TL;DR
Lesson on which liquidity levels to mark and why. A candle can do only three things relative to the previous candle: consolidate (inside), take one side (directional), or take both sides; unless it consolidates, expect price to reach for the previous candle's high or low. The same logic is applied to previous month, previous week and previous day highs/lows (each frames a draw on liquidity or a reversal), then to session highs/lows (Asia 20:00-00:00, London 02:00-05:00, NY AM 08:30-11:00, indices kill zones). Displacement or lack of displacement beyond the Asia range in London sets up the NY AM move. Final example aligns monthly FVG (NQ), daily SMT with ES and a daily CISD, the next day model, and a NY AM London-high sweep with an OTE entry to the previous day low. Foundation for TTFM candle-2/3 reading across timeframes.

## Definitions
- **Three candle outcomes** (from "the strat"): consolidation (inside), one-sided / directional (one side taken), both sides taken (00:38-01:04).
- **Buy-side / sell-side liquidity**: the previous candle's high / low (00:38).
- **Previous month / week / day high and low**: previous candle extremes on those timeframes; draws or reversal frames (01:30, 02:41, 05:02).
- **Kill zones (indices)**: Asia 20-0, London 2-5, NY AM 8:30-11 (06:33).
- **Inversion level**: a level displaced back through that then supports continuation (08:44).
- **SMT**: correlated indices diverging at a swing (NQ lower high, ES higher high) (09:53).
- **OTE**: optimal trade entry retracement zone of a range (11:41).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Mark previous month, week and day highs/lows; read each closure: inside, one side, both sides, close outside or back in (01:30-06:09).
2. Mark HTF PD arrays (monthly FVG and its CE), check SMT between NQ and ES, wait for a daily shift (close below up-close candles) and retrace (09:13-10:28).
3. Apply the next day model for the previous day low as draw (10:28).
4. Mark the Asia range; in London watch displacement vs failure to displace at Asia high/low (07:32, 11:06).
5. Project the London manipulation leg for 2-2.5 and 4-4.5 targets (08:15).
6. In NY AM look for a stop run of a session extreme plus displacement toward the draw, enter in the OTE, stop on the swept extreme, target the draw (11:41).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Small doji / indecision candles do not give a reliable draw; mark both sides and wait (03:11, 05:32).
- Do not trade Asia/London price for this setup; observe until NY (11:06).

## Conflicts and open questions
- Outside-bar handling: at 05:32 he leans to continuation in the close direction; in other videos an outside bar means no bias. Needs a rule.
- Kill zone times are stated for indices only; time zone (NY) is implied, not stated.
- The OTE retracement levels are not given numerically.
- Projection levels stated as "2 to 2.5 and 4 to 4.5" without sign; elsewhere he says negative 2 / negative 2.5.
