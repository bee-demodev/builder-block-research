## Mistakes and warnings
- Market-entering short (or long) because the forming candle 'looks bearish' before it closes. Fix: Wait for the candle to close; the closure often reverses the impression. (20:10)
- Revenge trading, tilt, oversizing, not following a strategy, all while knowing better. Fix: Follow a strategy and do what you know you should; limit to one trade per day. (11:30)
- Taking a second trade per day, which doubles exposure to your weaknesses. Fix: One trade per day; use slower (15m) entries if you revenge trade on 1m. (12:50)
- Shorting at the lows / trading continuation beyond a daily target that was already hit after a good range. Fix: Stand aside once the target is hit; don't fade the move either. (07:10)
- Trading gold long against a bearish daily (counter-trend) because the 1H/4H look supportive. Fix: Require the daily, 4H and 1H aligned in one direction. (33:50)
- Repeating a losing setup type (e.g. reversals) without reviewing why it loses. Fix: Review the journal and drop the setup type that keeps failing. (28:00)
- Chasing an entry after price has already broken the level. Fix: Enter before the break, from the POI/protected swing. (41:00)
- Full-porting accounts and expecting outsized returns. Fix: A steady ~6% a month compounds enormously; size sensibly. (1:06:30)
- Reading the indicator's 'Bias: Neutral' table entry as a market bias when it reflects the user's bias setting. Fix: It shows neutral because the indicator is set to neutral bias; set or derive bias yourself. (1:19:40)
