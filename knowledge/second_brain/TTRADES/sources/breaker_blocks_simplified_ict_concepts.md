# Breaker Blocks Simplified - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
A breaker block is the single or series of opposite-close candles between the first swing and the swing that is later broken in a low, high, lower low, higher high pattern (bullish: the up-close candles) or high, low, higher high, lower low (bearish: the down-close candles). He wants displacement with the breaker, ideally an FVG overlapping it, which forms the "unicorn model". The video covers entry choices (breaker vs FVG), two stop placements (beyond breaker+FVG vs swing), breaker vs order block marking, projecting the manipulation leg for targets, and three top-down examples (one a loss). Recorded around New Year (he says "Happy New Year").

## Definitions
- **Breaker block (bullish)**: in low, high, lower low, higher high, the single/series of up-close candles between the low and the high, marked including wicks (00:39, 07:09).
- **Breaker block (bearish)**: in high, low, higher high, lower low, the single/series of down-close candles (02:43).
- **Unicorn model**: breaker block overlapping a same-direction FVG (03:53).
- **Order block (vs breaker)**: series of opposite candles marked only at the opening price (07:09). See conflicts.
- **Manipulation leg (in a breaker)**: the leg that made the lower low (bullish case) (08:02).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Identify the four-swing pattern (L, H, LL, HH for bullish; H, L, HH, LL for bearish), each swing confirmed (00:39, 01:04).
2. Mark the up-close (bullish) / down-close (bearish) candle series between the first low/high and the next swing, wicks included (00:39).
3. Require displacement through the breaker, ideally an FVG overlapping it (unicorn) (03:53).
4. Entry: start of the FVG or the breaker inside the FVG (04:49).
5. Stop: beyond breaker + FVG, or at the swing (04:21).
6. Target: 2R, opposing highs/lows, or SD projection of the manipulation leg (08:24, 10:19).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: GBPUSD 5m, bullish breaker (01:04-02:15)
- Low, high, lower low (confirmed swing), higher high; breaker = up-close candles. Retest reacts higher; a later sweep into the breaker keeps bodies respecting; continues higher.

### Example 2: EURUSD 5m, bearish breaker / unicorn (02:43-05:53)
- First reading fails (no lower low). Second reading: high, low, higher high, lower low; breaker = down-close candles; retest then displacement lower, FVG created (unicorn).
- Entries: start of FVG or breaker within FVG. Stops: swing high (2R further) vs just above breaker. Breaker stop hit 2R quickly; swing-stop 2R just missed and then stopped out.

### Example 3: 5m unicorn, long (05:53-07:09)
- Low, high, lower low, higher high; up-close breaker with FVG overlap. Entry on the breaker, stop below breaker low, 2R; filled and TP hit; never returned to the FVG.

### Example 4: Breaker vs order block marking (07:09-08:02)
- Rectangle = breaker; blue line = order block open. Both entries worked; the order block caught almost the very low.

### Example 5: Breaker with projection (08:02-08:55)
- Manipulation leg (the lower-low leg) projected. Entry on breaker, stop below breaker low; 2R or projections for 4R; projections hit, retracement, then reached "4 to negative 4.5".

### Example 6: GBPUSD 1h / 5m, top-down long (08:55-10:52)
- Hourly: sweep of low, displacement up, L/H/LL/HH, breaker overlapping FVG (hourly unicorn); London kill zone.
- 5m: lower low into the important level, displacement, higher high, breaker and FVG. Entry at start of FVG, stop at the low, target 2R, highs, or -2 SD (3.62R). TP hit. Hourly context: internal to external liquidity.

### Example 7: 1h unicorn, short (10:52-11:53)
- Previous-day low with equal lows; swing high run; H/L/HH/LL; down-close breaker overlapping FVG. Entry on hourly, stop on the high, target equal lows; TP hit.

### Example 8: ES 60m / 5m, long, loss (11:53-13:00)
- HTF FVG respecting, sell-side liquidity below; sweep and close back inside. 5m: L/H/LL/HH breaker aligned with FVG. Entry at FVG, stop below breaker, 2R target: stopped out.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Marking a breaker before the final swing forms (no lower low = no breaker) (03:17).
- A swing-based stop may require a far 2R that is missed; consider the breaker stop (05:21, 06:43).
- Losses happen even with a full unicorn setup (13:00).
