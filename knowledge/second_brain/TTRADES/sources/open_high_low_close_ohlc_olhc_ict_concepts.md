# Open High Low Close (OHLC / OLHC) - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Short early TTrades ICT lesson on candle anatomy as a trading plan. A bullish candle forms open → low → high → close (OLHC): unless the low equals the open, price must trade below the open first, so buy below or near the open and expect expansion up to the high, then the close. A bearish candle forms OHLC: sell between the high and the open. Whether you expect bullish or bearish comes from your draw on liquidity. Examples: ES weekly candle (Tuesday low, then expansion), NQ daily candle using midnight open (15m), a 4H candle OLHC traded on the 5m with the open as support, and the same sequence read as accumulation-manipulation-distribution (AMD). This is the root of TTFM's "candle 3 opens, manipulates, then expands" logic for HTF candles.

## Definitions
- **OLHC**: open, low, high, close — the bullish candle sequence (00:03-00:37).
- **OHLC**: open, high, low, close — the bearish candle sequence (00:37-01:05).
- **Midnight opening price**: used as the daily candle's open for index intraday work (02:01).
- **AMD**: accumulation (consolidation around the open), manipulation (move beyond the open against the bias), distribution (expansion to the other extreme) (04:43).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Decide the draw on liquidity for the candle (weekly, daily, 4H) → bullish or bearish (01:05).
2. Mark the candle's open (weekly open, midnight open, 4H open) (02:01, 03:38).
3. Bullish: wait for the low below the open, then buy below/near the open; bearish: wait for the high above the open, sell between the high and the open (00:37-01:05).
4. Confirm: no closes back through the open after the manipulation (03:38).
5. Hold for expansion toward the draw; the opposite extreme and close follow (04:11).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: ES weekly candle from the daily (01:32-02:01)
- Monday, then Tuesday puts in a low; expansion candles up; the week forms open-low-high-close.

### Example 2: NQ 15m, daily candle with midnight open (02:01-03:10)
- Open, then a high, then expansion down to a low and the close: OHLC daily candle; sell above/around midnight open.

### Example 3: 4H OLHC on the 5m (03:10-04:43)
- Draw on liquidity identified on the 4H; after a down-close 4H candle expects the next to be OLHC (03:10).
- 5m: new 4H open, low put in, price back above, open supports with no closes below, expansion to the draw, then the high and the close (03:38-04:11). Instrument not stated.

### Example 4: AMD view (04:43-05:13)
- Consolidation around the open (accumulation), run above the open = manipulation (the open-high), then sell-off to the low and close (distribution).
