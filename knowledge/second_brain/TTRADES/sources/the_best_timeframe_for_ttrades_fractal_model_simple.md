# The Best Timeframe for TTrades Fractal Model (Simple)

## TL;DR
TTrades' "favorite fractal model": daily for bias, 4h for the swing point (and daily profile), 15m for entry. Daily bias comes most easily from a daily candle 2 closure inside a POI (low, high or FVG) or a reversal candle closing back inside the prior range; the next day is candle 3. On the 4h he waits for the daily wick to form via a 4h candle 2 / candle 3 closure (e.g. London candle 2 -> 6am and 10am expansion), confirmed by a lower-timeframe CISD; on the 15m he prefers a continuation (new protected swing) over trading the reversal itself, entering at market on the new candle open with the stop at the protected swing, 2R or HTF objectives. Core message: the easiest expansions come from trading candle 3 on several timeframes at once (daily + 4h). Three examples: unnamed symbol, gold (bearish), oil (advanced, bullish with a stop-out first). Ends with a promo for the "Lens Path" mentorship with AM Trades.

## Definitions
- **Favorite fractal model**: 1D bias -> 4h swing point -> 15m entry (00:11-00:22).
- **Candle 3 across timeframes**: daily candle 3 traded via a 4h candle 2/3 and a 15m continuation inside the 4h candle 3 (02:54-03:06, 06:28-07:01).
- **Low we can trust**: a low formed out of a POI with a sweep, V-shaped reversal and CISD inside the candle (02:29-02:52).
- **Continuation**: close through the series of candles that went into the POI after a reaction, creating a protected swing (05:08-05:33, 09:14-09:30).
- **T-spot area**: area on the 4h/15m where the POI sits (09:03-09:16); not defined further here.
- **Lens Path**: TTrades + AM Trades mentorship program (15:44-16:30).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Daily: POI reached; candle 2 closure inside it or reversal candle closing back in the previous range -> bias for candle 3 (00:28-01:01).
2. 4h: wait for a candle 2 (or 3) closure in the bias direction that marks the daily wick; check CISD on the lower timeframe (01:09-02:08, 03:57-04:20).
3. 15m: inside the 4h candle 3 wait for a sweep into a POI, V-shape, and a continuation (close through the candles into the POI) (04:31-05:33).
4. Enter at market on the new candle open; stop at the protected swing (or the bodies) (05:44-05:52, 09:31-09:40).
5. Target 2R, or HTF objectives (failure swings, previous day low/high, equal highs) (05:52-06:05, 14:08-14:28).
6. If the low is taken before the CISD, keep the bias while the key 50% area holds and wait for a new candle 2 (12:41-12:56).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: unnamed symbol, 1D-4h-15m, bullish (03:31-07:07)
- Daily: price in an FVG, bodies respecting, candle 2 closure; target the FVG above (03:31-03:50).
- 4h: candle 2 right off the open engulfing the prior down-close candle; target failure swings left (03:57-04:20).
- 15m: down-close candles into an FVG make a new low, V-shaped recovery, close over = continuation; entry at market on the new open, stop at the protected low; 2R or failure swings ~4R; target hit; another continuation in candle 4 takes all targets (04:31-06:26).

### Example 2: gold, 1D-4h-15m, bearish (07:08-10:14)
- Daily: failure swings run into an FVG after 7 days without a swing; bearish candle 2 closure; target FVG below (07:08-07:47).
- 4h: sweep of a short-term high, aggressive V, CISD; first candle not a candle 2, wait for a new high and closure; then candle 2 sweeping the high with an aggressive bearish candle (07:47-08:23).
- 15m: respect the lower half of the 4h candle; target previous day low; POI in the T-spot / FVG; continuation through the candles into the POI; stop on the high or the bodies; 2R at the low; trends lower (08:24-09:53).

### Example 3: oil, 1W-1D-4h-15m, bullish, advanced (10:14-15:41)
- Weekly: equal highs above, reversal Monday of last week, Friday closed higher = expansion candle; respect 50% of it (10:40-11:04).
- Daily: down-close swing candle then close over; not much below 50% of it; day opens ranging/bearish; want PDL taken then highs (11:04-11:35).
- 4h: price opens into the PDL; not bullish, no reversal yet; then a reversal candle (11:47-12:03).
- 15m: first continuation off the new 4h open fails (below 50% -> positional long stopped) (12:06-12:30); CISD does not form before the low is taken; still bullish; new candle 2; continuation off an FVG; deep retracement into the lows; new protected swing in candle 3; entry on the open of candle 4, stop on the low; 2R hit at once, potential over 8R to equal highs; trend continuing (12:33-14:42).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Trading the first reversal with a limit: either it reacts or falls through; wait for the continuation and save the loss (04:46-05:08).
- Trading a third expansion candle when the draw is already met (01:51-01:57).
- Treating a non-bullish 4h candle as a reversal (11:57-12:01).
- Abandoning the bias after a single stop-out while the key area still holds (12:44-12:53).
