# How To Trade Expansion - Phases Of Price

## TL;DR
Part 2 of TTrades' 5-part "phases of price" series (consolidation, expansion, retracement, reversal, combined). Expansion is a one-sided trending move with shallow counter-moves; it follows a consolidation or retracement (or a reversal on the HTF) because price cycles large range -> small range -> large range. You do not wait for a deep discount retrace in expansion; you track protected swings: price reaches into a PD array (e.g. FVG) in the upper half of the HTF candle, closes over the opposing (down-close) candles, and that low should hold. After roughly 3 days of expansion, especially into an objective (previous day high/low), expect a new phase (small range / retracement / reversal) and avoid entering right after expansion. Mainly shown daily -> hourly, said to be fractal (1h-5m, 1M-1D).

## Definitions
- **Expansion**: one-sided trending move, large ranges, shallow counter-moves (00:27, 08:00).
- **Large range / small range**: expansion leg vs consolidation or retracement leg; market alternates them (01:03, 08:28).
- **Protected swing (in expansion)**: low (bullish) formed when price reaches a PD array and then closes over the down-close candle(s); must hold for the trend to continue (01:40, 03:06).
- **Breakout signature**: consolidation resolved by closing over the previous high without first manipulating the low (04:19).
- **Expansion met with expansion**: overlapping opposing FVGs (BPR) forming a CISD; a reversal type covered in the reversal video (06:21, 09:08, 12:10).

## Procedure / checklist
1. On the daily, establish the phase: reversal (sweep + close back inside + CISD) or continuation after a retracement/consolidation (02:23, 12:10).
2. Drop to the hourly; look in the upper half of the HTF candle (bullish) (02:03).
3. Wait for price to reach into a PD array (FVG / important level) and close over the opposing candles -> new protected swing (03:11).
4. Trade in the trend direction; trail by each new protected swing; invalidated if it is taken (03:06, 04:46).
5. Count expansion days; after ~3 days or after hitting an objective (PDH/PDL), stand aside and let the next day print (05:22, 10:12).
6. In the following small range, mark its high and low; continuation needs manipulation of the counter-trend side, reversal the trend side (07:16, 11:17).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: Gold (GC) 1h with daily candles (02:23-08:49)
- Daily: sweep of a low, close back inside, CISD -> expect reversal and expansion higher (02:23-02:41).
- Hourly: sweep low, close over down-close candles -> protected swing; repeat on each sweep into FVG; two aggressive candles then six candles of retrace = expansion into retracement (02:53-04:14).
- Breakout through previous high instead of manipulating the low; new opposing candle intraday after retesting the opening price (04:14).
- After 3 days of expansion, sweep of the PDH plus CISD -> reversal; expansion lower with shallow wicks, new protected swings; consolidation fails to manipulate its low -> continues lower into the FVG and next day (05:18-08:28).

- Consolidation: high swept, then low swept, then breakout (11:04-11:17). Hourly: failure swings on the high, reversal, shallow retracements, consolidation before breakout (11:34-12:06).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Waiting for a deep retrace into discount during expansion; it rarely comes (00:36, 01:32).
- Entering right after an expansion or after 3 days into an objective (08:49, 10:12).
- Getting long inside a consolidation before its low is swept (14:47).
- Calling a protected swing before a close over the opposing candle (04:46).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "3 days of expansion" is a heuristic ("generally"); no rule for what counts as an expansion day (range size, close location).
