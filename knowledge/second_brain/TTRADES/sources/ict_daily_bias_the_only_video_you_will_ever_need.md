# ICT Daily Bias - The Only Video You Will Ever Need!

## TL;DR
Early, pre-TTFM daily bias lesson that credits "the MMXM trader" for the concepts and his next day model (00:10). Bias is framed as "which side of liquidity is price more likely to reach next": first previous day high / low (PDH/PDL), then previous week high / low, then swing points. A close outside the previous day's range keeps the draw on that side; a reach beyond PDH/PDL that cannot displace / close outside frames a reversal toward the opposite side. The next day model: after price respects a PD array or fails to displace beyond a swing, anticipate the next daily candle (open-low-high-close or open-high-low-close) and hold that bias until the draw on liquidity is hit. Ends with two hourly -> 5m executions (London, New York) with FVG entries, stop on the swing high, 2R or liquidity targets. This is the seed of the later TTFM candle 2 / candle 3 closure logic.

## Definitions
- **Draw on liquidity / daily bias**: the side of liquidity (PDH/PDL, previous week high/low, swing point) price is more likely to reach next (00:10, 00:38).
- **Failure to displace**: price trades beyond a level but cannot close / displace beyond it and closes back in the range (01:06, 07:53).
- **Next day model**: credited to MMXM Trader; after a failure to displace or respect of a PD array, anticipate the next daily candle's shape (open-low-high-close bullish, open-high-low-close bearish) and keep that bias until the draw is met (08:21-09:09, 18:01).
- **Open low high close / open high low close**: bullish / bearish daily candle templates used to anticipate the next day (08:21).
- **PD array**: premium/discount array (FVG, order block) that price "respects" (08:47, 11:09).
- **Look left**: search older swing points for a draw when nearby ones are exhausted (12:44, 13:18).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Mark previous day high and low (00:10).
2. If the last daily candle closed outside the previous range, the draw is the next same-side level (01:59).
3. If it reached beyond PDH/PDL but closed back inside, the draw is the opposite side (01:32, 02:29).
4. If inside bar / no bias, mark its high and low, lean with the trend, and wait for which is taken (12:09).
5. Add previous week high/low and swing points as draws and reversal points (05:01, 07:28); look left if needed (12:44).
6. Apply the next day model after a PD-array respect or failure to displace (08:21, 11:09).
7. Drop to 1h, mark midnight opening price, look for a stop hunt into a PD array in London or the New York kill zone (14:22, 16:27).
8. Drop to 5m, enter the FVG, stop on the swing high, target 2R or the PDL / swing liquidity (15:22, 17:00).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Do not expect PDH/PDL framing to work every day (04:36, 13:18).
- A small sweep may not be the real high; wait for confirmation before entering (14:51).
- In an uptrend, a bearish shift that goes too far means price is likely to go lower; do not hold the bullish bias blindly (11:38).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Displace" vs "close": he uses "can't displace", "can't close over" and "close back in the range" interchangeably; a rule engine needs a definition (body close beyond the level is the closest reading).
- The large-candle -> consolidation expectation (04:01, 10:37) has no size threshold.
- The next-day-model bias is held "until we reach our draw on liquidity" (09:09); no rule for invalidation other than an opposite close.
- Example 4 and 5 instruments are not named in speech; the GBPUSD reading is from one frame only.
- Midnight opening price is marked (14:22) but never used in a stated rule in this video.
