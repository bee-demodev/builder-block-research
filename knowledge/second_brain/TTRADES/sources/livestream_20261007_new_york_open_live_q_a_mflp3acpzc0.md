## Mistakes and warnings
- Pattern trading the model: reading a lower-timeframe bullish structure as bullish when the higher-timeframe bias is bearish. Fix: Start from the bias (daily/hourly) and only take lower-timeframe setups that align with it. (07:00)
- Expecting the indicator to stop pattern trading. Fix: Set the bias first; then use the indicator on the lower timeframe to mark the model. (31:30)
- Taking a valid continuation with almost no range to the target (stop cannot be positioned). Fix: Require room to the target from where the setup forms; skip otherwise. (19:10)
- Using an indicator ADR that is skewed by outlier days to decide how much range is left. Fix: Eyeball the last few daily candles. (25:50)
- Treating FOMC minutes like the FOMC rate decision. Fix: Only the rate decision matters to him. (24:10)
- Trading against a daily candle that already made a large opposing move. Fix: Align with the daily candle; only fade the daily bias when the wick is small. (38:20)
- Expecting every wick entry to work / trying to be right all the time. Fix: Accept losses; the stop loss exists to stop the loss. (38:40)
- Trading back into the range after the overnight move already hit its target (fading your own bias). Fix: Stay away and wait for the next day's new range. (57:40)
- Entering a setup candle whose wick formed late in the candle. Fix: Wait for the next candle and trade the continuation. (53:10)
