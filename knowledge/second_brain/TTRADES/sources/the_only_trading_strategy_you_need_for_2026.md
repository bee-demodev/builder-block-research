# The Only Trading Strategy You Need For 2026

## TL;DR
A compact statement of the whole fractal model as one system. Premise: price cannot reverse without a swing point. Step 1: daily bias from the daily close vs the previous day's range (close beyond PDH/PDL = continuation; sweep and close back inside = reversal). Step 2: on the structure timeframe (hourly, or 4h/30m), find a POI inside the previous day's range (FVG or low for longs, FVG or high for shorts) and wait for a candle 2 or candle 3 closure there. Step 3: drop to the paired lower timeframe (1h->5m, 30m->3m), confirm CISD but do not enter on it; wait for a continuation order block (sweep of a short-term extreme then close through the opposing candles), enter on close or retest, stop at the protected swing, 2R (HTF objectives optional). Examples: unnamed symbol bearish (1D-1h-5m), USDJPY (1M-1D-1h), oil (failed continuations = consolidation, do not trade). The page (published 2026-01-03) matches the video step for step.

## Definitions
- **Swing point**: the bullish or bearish turn that any reversal must form (00:28-00:40).
- **Continuation daily bias**: close above PDH (bullish) or below PDL (bearish) (01:01-01:15).
- **Reversal daily bias**: sweep of PDL/PDH and close back inside (01:15-01:37).
- **POI**: FVG or low (bullish), FVG or high (bearish), inside the previous day's range (01:51-02:09).
- **Continuation order block**: after the CISD, a sweep of a short-term low/high into a POI, then a close through the series of opposing candles (02:57-03:08). Page: "price sweeps a short term high or low and then closes through a series of opposing candles."
- **Structure timeframe**: the timeframe where the swing point and POI are read (hourly; some use 4h or 30m) (03:20-03:32).
- **In-candle CISD**: CISD formed inside the current daily candle on the structure timeframe (08:41-08:48).
- **EQ**: equilibrium of the range, lining up with the level price should not trade below (10:46-10:50).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Daily bias from the daily close vs PDH/PDL (00:50-01:37).
2. Structure timeframe: POI inside the previous day's range; wait for a candle 2/3 closure there; note the draw (PDH/PDL, daily FVG) (01:37-02:30).
3. Drop to the paired timeframe; confirm CISD; do not enter yet (02:37-02:56).
4. Wait for a continuation order block: sweep into a POI, close through the opposing candles (02:57-03:08).
5. Enter on the close or the retest; stop at the protected swing (body or wick high/low); 2R, optional HTF objectives (03:11-03:18, 07:17-07:30, 09:06-09:27).
6. If continuations keep failing, call it a range and stand aside until one side is taken (12:54-13:40).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: unnamed symbol, 1D-1h-5m, bearish (03:46-09:29)
- Daily: highs taken, failure swings and an FVG, multiple days without a swing; reversal closure -> bearish; target daily FVG below and PDL (03:46-04:13).
- 1h: sweep of highs, close through up-close candles = CISD (04:15-04:28). Next day: doji-like reversal closure; 5m trades high into equal highs, no short (04:43-05:15). Too close to the high to trust; then aggressive V-shaped displacement = continuation order block on the hourly; bullish reversal fails; bearish candle 2 inside a small FVG retesting the continuation OB (05:15-06:26).
- 5m: FVG, short-term high swept, close below = continuation; entry on close or retest, stop body high or actual high, 2R; filled on the retest; continuation waits until candle 5, then moves lower into the daily FVG (06:26-09:06).

### Example 2: USDJPY, 1M-1D-1h, bullish, late October (09:29-12:04)
- Monthly candle 2 closure bullish for October; equal highs above; do not fade (09:29-09:48).
- Daily: reached into an FVG, bodies respect it, close back above (10:10-10:19).
- 1h entry: don't want price below an existing continuation level (lines up with EQ); FVG reach without close over -> consolidation and new low; then close over = continuation; entry on retest (highs just taken), stop on full bodies, 2R hit (10:33-11:41). Could refine on 30m/15m (11:49-12:04).

### Example 3: oil, 1D-1h-15m, bearish, not a trade (12:04-14:15)
- Daily bearish closure with CISD; hourly messy with failure swings; no close below the level repeatedly = consolidation (12:04-13:01).
- Sweep of the consolidation high / PDH gives a protected swing; 15m sweep and close through = breakdown; possible but he would not trade it (13:12-13:57).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Model hopping (00:17-00:25).
- Entering on the CISD itself instead of the next continuation (02:50-02:56).
- Shorting into equal highs or trusting a reversal right at the high (05:05-05:45).
- Forcing trades in messy ranges where continuations keep failing (13:57-14:15).

## Conflicts and open questions
- 04:30: "the lower half of this previous day's range" for the bearish day's wick means the high should form without retracing above 0.5 of the prior (reversal) day; whether a trade above 0.5 invalidates the bias, and by close or wick, is not stated.
- Rule 9 pairs 1h->5m and 30m->3m; in Example 2 he enters on the hourly from a daily swing, so the pairing is a default, not a requirement.
- Rule 18: body high vs wick high stop left to preference.
- "Multiple days of expansion" (03:56) gives no count; the gold example in another video used 7 days.
- Example 1 symbol is not named.

## Glossary additions
- `continuation order block`: sweep into a POI then close through the opposing candles after the CISD; the entry trigger.
- `structure time frame`: timeframe for swing point and POI (usually 1h).
- `downclo candles` = down-close candles; `5m minute` = 5-minute; `wretched into` = reached into; `dogee` = doji.
