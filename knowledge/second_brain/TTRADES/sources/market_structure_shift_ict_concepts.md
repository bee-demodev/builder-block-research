# Market Structure Shift - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Defines displacement (aggressive move with full-bodied candles, easiest spotted by the fair value gaps it leaves) and the market structure shift (MSS): displacement through a previous low (bearish) or high (bullish), ideally after a stop raid that creates a breaker and high resistance liquidity for the stop. The most important filter: price must engage a higher-timeframe level before the shift, otherwise he does not look for a setup. After the MSS he frames the displacement range's premium/discount and enters a PD array (FVG, breaker, OTE, order block). Examples: ES lower timeframe off PDH with 10:00 news (14 Jul 2023 from frame), ES 1h off previous week low, DXY daily off a daily FVG. Pre-TTFM precursor of the CISD-after-POI rule.

## Definitions
- **Displacement**: aggressive, full-bodied candle(s), usually leaving FVGs (00:10-00:39).
- **Market structure shift (MSS)**: displacement and close through a previous low/high that changes trend (02:07, 04:15).
- **Stop raid**: sweep of the swing before the shift; forms the breaker and high resistance liquidity (02:07).
- **Breaker block**: formed by the raid-and-shift sequence; used with the FVG as the entry zone (02:34, 04:15).
- **OTE**: optimal trade entry from a fib of the displacement leg (04:44).
- **Displacement range**: the MSS leg; its premium/discount decides where to look for the PD array (05:52).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Mark the higher-timeframe level (PDH, previous week low, daily FVG) and any news time (02:34, 03:01).
2. Wait for price to engage it (03:01).
3. Wait for a sweep of a nearby high/low, ideally without a close beyond it (03:33).
4. MSS: aggressive full-bodied closes through the opposite swing (04:15).
5. Frame the displacement range; find a PD array in discount/premium (FVG, breaker, OTE, order block) (04:15, 05:52).
6. Enter on the PD array; target session lows/highs or the next liquidity (05:18).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: ES lower timeframe short off PDH, 10:00 news (02:34-05:18)
- Frame at 04:03 shows ES around 4550, "Fri 14 Jul '23". PDH and 10:00 (news) marked. Price engages PDH. First attempts do not break the low or are not aggressive -> no MSS. Sweep of the high without a close over it (better sweep), then full-bodied candles below the low, touches midnight and returns. MSS -> FVG inside a breaker with OTE confluence; price touches it, displaces lower; a second FVG, sweep of the current low, then London / overnight lows taken.

### Example 2: ES 1h long off previous week low (05:18-06:23)
- Aggressive move into the previous week low; not long until displacement closes over the high; close over the high = MSS. Displacement range premium/discount: order block in discount; reaction off the order block and the midnight opening price; continues higher through the week.

### Example 3: DXY 1D long off daily FVG (06:23-07:23)
- Price draws to a daily FVG, raids a low into it, then closes over the high = MSS. Order block in discount of the range; price reaches into it, closes outside it, and moves higher.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Taking an MSS that did not engage a higher-timeframe level first (02:34).
- Counting a non-aggressive break of the low as a break of structure (03:33).
- Going long at the level before the displacement close over the high (05:18).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Aggressive" / "full-bodied" have no size thresholds.
- Which swing counts as "this low" for the MSS (internal vs swing that formed the high) is shown, not specified.
- The 10:00 news mark is set up but never tied to a rule (03:01).
- He mentions "touch midnight" (04:15) and midnight opening price reaction (05:52) without stating a rule.
