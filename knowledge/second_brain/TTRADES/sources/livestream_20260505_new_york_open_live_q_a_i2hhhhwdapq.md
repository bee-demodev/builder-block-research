## Mistakes and warnings
- Doing SMT first and building the trade around it (e.g. ES not taking a high while NQ has = short). Fix: Have a model first; use SMT only to confirm it. (10:50)
- Fading the daily candle, e.g. trying to short a small-wicked bullish daily or reversing against the daily range. Fix: Trade in the direction of the daily; counter-daily reversals are harder, need more confirmation and less aggressive entries. (08:30)
- Shorting the strongest asset, or chasing the asset that already moved (YM). Fix: Trade the middle asset that is still in the upper half of the range toward the target (ES here). (29:20)
- Going long right at the highs after highs are taken and the daily range is already decent. Fix: Enter around the opening price early in the candle; at the highs expect a retracement or reversal. (34:10)
- Taking a long without the close over the level, with a stop far below. Fix: Require the closure; if the stop must be far away, skip. (45:00)
