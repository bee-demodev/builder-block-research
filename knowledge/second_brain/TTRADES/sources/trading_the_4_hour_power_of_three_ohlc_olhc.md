# Trading The 4 Hour Power Of Three - OHLC / OLHC

## TL;DR
TTrades maps power of three (AMD) onto 4h candles with candle 2 / candle 3 logic: candle 1 is always accumulation; a candle 2 with a large opposing run is manipulation only (wait for candle 3 to be distribution); a candle 2 with a shallow wick is manipulation and distribution in the same candle (reversal to expansion). The wick of the expansion candle is "formed" once the LTF prints a protected swing via CISD; then trade the body. Bias comes from the daily, the 4h candle closures say whether price should expand now or reverse first. Examples: NQ/futures with 4h-15m pairing, USDJPY with forex 4h timings, then lower pairings (gold 3-5m, NQ 1h-5m) referred to as "GXT" (Garrett's 4h profiling + TTFM).

## Definitions
- **Power of three / AMD**: accumulation, manipulation (opposing run), distribution (real move), mapped onto candle 1 / candle 2 / candle 3 (00:30).
- **OHLC / OLHC**: open, high, low, close of the 4h candle (bearish: open, make high/wick, trade lower; bullish: open, low, high, close) (00:17, 09:00).
- **Opposing run**: the move against intended direction after the HTF candle opens; becomes the wick (01:25).
- **Reversal to expansion candle**: candle 2 makes a small shallow run below candle 1's low and trades higher in the same candle (02:08).
- **Continuation expansion candle**: candle 3 after a reversal candle 2 with a large opposing run (02:35).
- **Positional entry**: entry without a precise POI, often at the HTF open, stop where invalidated (13:35, 16:22).
- **GXT**: "Garrett's 4hour profiling with my fractal model" (11:21).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Daily bias and objective (e.g. previous day high, closes outside prior range, EQ respected) (04:28).
2. Classify the latest 4h candles: reversal with large opposing run (trade next candle as candle 3) or shallow wick (trade within same candle) (03:41).
3. Drop to the paired TF (15m, or 5m/3m/1h model) at the next 4h open (04:48).
4. Wait for the opposing run, a sweep, and a CISD that creates a protected swing; this is the formed wick (06:01).
5. Enter anywhere after; stop on the protected swing (or beyond 50% of the CISD / EQ); target daily objective, 2R, or -1 SD (06:22, 12:50, 15:46).
6. Stop trading the candle once the target is hit; for more, wait for the next 4h open (10:03).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: futures, daily to 4h to 15m (04:12-07:06)
- Daily: Wednesday reversal, Thursday continuation closing outside Wednesday's range, so Friday should continue to Thursday's high; EQ of previous day respected.
- 15m: 2:00 4h candle is a reversal with a large opposing run below the 22:00 candle's low (shallow run first, no CISD, continued lower), closes strong: candle 3 = 6:00.
- 6:00: sweep of a low, close over into an FVG, protected swing; stop on that low; target previous day high; reached (06:41). London reversal, NY continuation. Instrument not named in speech.

### Example 2: USDJPY weekly reversal, forex 4h timings (07:06-11:02)
- Weekly reversal candle: expect shallow opposing run and expansion higher this week; daily shows slow shallow retracement into an FVG leaving failure swings on the high.
- 15m with forex 4h candles 17:00, 21:00, 1:00; want reversal off the 21:00 low. Price went up first, so no early shallow run; later expansion met with expansion and CISD, model prints; the lower wick is already formed.
- Continuation: short-term target hit, retracement into an FVG, close over the down-close candle = protected swing; stop there, 2.45R to the highs; reached. No holding past the 9:00 candle; new 4h candle gives another continuation off a protected low after a sweep (he dislikes trading over old highs).

### Example 3: gold, 4h power of three with 3m/5m fractal model (11:02-14:58)
- 2:00 London reversal, so 6:00 continuation lower; consolidation highs run, CISD; entry with stop beyond protected swing or 50% of CISD and EQ, 2R.
- Before the new 4h open, 30m candle 3 closure forms a protected swing; positional entry at the 4h open, stop on protected swing, 2R; falls just short of 2R into an FVG, returns to entry, second confirmation, continuation lower. 6:00 candle: small wick, large body.

### Example 4: NQ 5m with 1h model (14:58-16:37)
- Previous day high swept, expansion met with expansion, reversal shown as a 6:00 4h wick; look for 10:00 continuation lower; hourly and 5m models print the T-spot.
- Stop on the opposing candle or FVG; target 2R or -1 SD (large opposing run, so -1 only); target hit. Positional entry (no clear POI).

### Example 5: 5m / 1h / 4h walk-through (16:37-21:03)

Summary: 6:00 reversal, 10:00 continuation; 14:00 = manipulation and expansion in the same 4h candle.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Trying to catch the wick: "you're going to get continuously stopped out" (04:02).
- Taking an in-candle reversal after price has already made too large a range in the opposite direction (08:35).
- Chasing a missed entry instead of waiting for a new protected swing (09:17).
- Holding beyond the expansion candle after the target is hit (10:03).
- Fighting an expansion candle back into the range before it closes (18:54).
- Expecting deep SD targets after a large opposing run (15:58).
