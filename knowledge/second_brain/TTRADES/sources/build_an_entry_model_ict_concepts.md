# Build An Entry Model - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Early lesson on building a lower-timeframe entry model from ICT concepts. Choose your entry timeframe (he uses 1m and 15s) and kill zone (he trades the New York open), pick a small concept set (displacement/FVGs, order blocks, OTE / premium-discount, SMT, inducement, BPR), draw and label a diagram, and stick to it. His four Twitter models: model 1 = liquidity grab, structure break, retrace to FVG; model 3 = model 1 plus OTE for RR; models 2 and 4 = models 1 and 3 with internal range liquidity taken before the move. Fifth model: BPR (aggressive move met by an aggressive opposite move, overlapping FVGs), short risking the high (or the bodies), targeting new lows. HTF POIs are explicitly out of scope (covered in the "Higher Time Frame Points of Interest" video).

## Definitions
- **Entry model**: a labelled, fixed diagram of the conditions for an entry (01:47).
- **Model 1**: liquidity grab, structure break, retrace into FVG (03:15).
- **Model 2 / 4**: models 1 / 3 with internal range liquidity taken before the move (03:44).
- **Model 3**: model 1 plus OTE on the displacement leg (03:15).
- **BPR (balanced price range)**: overlap of opposing FVGs after an aggressive move met by an aggressive opposite move (04:11, 04:43).
- **Kill zone**: the session window where the model is searched (00:32).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Choose entry timeframe (1m / 15s) (00:32).
2. Choose kill zone (New York open) (00:50).
3. Pick concepts; draw and label a diagram with each requirement (01:47).
4. Model check: liquidity grab -> displacement / structure break -> FVG retrace; optionally in OTE; optionally internal range liquidity taken (03:15-04:11).
5. Or BPR: overlap of opposing FVGs; short/long there, stop at the high/low (or bodies) (04:43).
6. Stick to the model (02:16).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: model 1 / 3 long (02:46-03:44)
- Liquidity grab, no closes below, aggressive move up breaking structure, retracement into FVG; OTE from displacement leg low to high contains the entry.
### Example 2: model 2 / 4 long (03:44-04:11)
- Lows run, aggressive move up breaking structure; OTE entry with internal range liquidity and an FVG to the left.
### Example 3: bearish BPR (04:11-04:43)
- Aggressive buy met with aggressive sell; overlap = BPR; short risking the high (or its bodies), targeting new lows; lower timeframe shows a liquidity run too.
### Example 4: bullish BPR (04:43-05:13)
- Aggressive sell met with aggressive buy; smaller overlap = entry; worked.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Entering without a defined, labelled model (01:47).
- Changing the model instead of sticking to it (02:16).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Breaker structure" in the classic setup (02:46) vs "breaking structure" in model 1 (03:15): likely the same idea (structure break), not a breaker block (implied).
- OTE levels, "aggressive" displacement and which BPR gap is "smaller" are not defined.
- Inducement and order blocks are listed but not used in any model.
- Diagrams are on screen; frames not checked.

## Glossary additions
- `worth bodies` (mishearing, 04:43): "or the bodies".
- `kill zone`: session time window for entries.
