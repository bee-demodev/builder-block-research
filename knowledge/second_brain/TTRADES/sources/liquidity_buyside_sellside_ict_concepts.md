# Liquidity: Buyside & Sellside - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Beginner lesson. Liquidity rests at swing points: a swing low (low with a higher low each side) holds sell stops = sell-side liquidity; a swing high (high with a lower high each side) holds buy stops = buy-side liquidity. Two types: old highs/lows (a single standout swing) and relatively equal highs/lows (clustered swings). Levels he always marks: previous week high/low, previous day high/low, and session highs/lows (Asia, London); each frames a draw or a reversal. Two ES 5m days show NY trading from one set of session levels to the other with 5m FVG entries. Foundation for TTFM's liquidity / draw logic.

## Definitions
- **Swing low / swing high**: low with higher lows each side / high with lower highs each side (00:10-00:39).
- **Sell-side liquidity (SSL)**: sell stops resting below swing lows (01:07).
- **Buy-side liquidity (BSL)**: buy stops resting above swing highs (01:07).
- **Relatively equal highs/lows**: two or more swings very close together or clustered (04:30).
- **Old high/low**: single standout previous swing (05:00).
- **Session highs/lows**: Asia and London session extremes (05:57).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Mark obvious swing highs/lows on the outside of the range; update as swings are taken (01:34-04:30).
2. Classify as old highs/lows or relatively equal highs/lows (04:30-05:00).
3. Always mark previous week H/L, previous day H/L, Asia and London session H/L (05:28-05:57).
4. Into NY, note which session levels are untouched; expect NY to take one set and draw to the other, with 5m FVG entries after displacement (06:24-07:33).
5. If session levels are exhausted, look to previous day H/L (08:34).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: swing-point labelling walkthrough (prior frame read: EURUSD 1h/4H) (01:34-04:30)
- Labels SSL/BSL on each swing; taken highs no longer matter; failing to take a previous low creates relatively equal lows; focus on outside-of-range swings.

### Example 2: previous week high/low (frame: EURUSD 4H) (05:00-05:57)
- Previous week: Tuesday low, Thursday high. Price takes previous week high back into an FVG, then expands higher. Previous day high used as a draw and taken before expansion.

### Example 3: ES 5m day 1, sessions (06:24-07:33)
- Asia low already taken; Asia high, London high and London low untouched. NY open: London high taken, reach for midnight (open), Asia high taken. Displacement down; 5m FVG retested with no body closes inside; target London equal lows; second 5m FVG; London low reached.

### Example 4: ES 5m day 2, sessions + previous day (07:33-09:03)
- Previous day H/L marked. ## Mistakes and warnings
- Once a swing is taken, stop treating it as liquidity; move to the next swing (02:03).
- Don't mark every small swing; use the obvious outside-of-range ones (03:10).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Relatively equal" has no tolerance (ticks / % of ADR); a rule engine needs one.
- "Reach for midnight" (06:24) refers to the midnight open level without defining it here.
- Session times are not given in this video (see important_liquidity_levels_draw_on_liquidity_ict: Asia 20-0, London 2-5).
