## Mistakes and warnings
- Pattern trading the model: picking any HTF FVG and calling a candle 2 closure. Fix: Start from a higher-timeframe idea of what price should do, then look for the C2 closure at a relevant POI. (28:40)
- Seeing an inversion after a sweep and automatically turning bearish. Fix: Treat it with suspicion; he often expects it to be ripped through (he predicts stop-outs for 'inversion traders' today). (13:50)
- Shorting the weakest index at the low of the day after the lows were already taken. Fix: Favour the side with sensible risk-to-reward, or wait for the stronger asset to reverse. (15:40)
- Trading the following candles right after a run of the previous day low and another low. Fix: Expect consolidation/chop there; wait for a clean framework. (21:10)
- Forcing a trade on a day with no framework. Fix: Let the open play out; sit on hands if it says nothing. (11:30)
- Scalping all day to get reps. Fix: Fine while learning, but avoid building bad habits; he finds it exhausting and stopped after 2022. (41:40)
- Entering without knowing where to exit / waiting for him to give exits. Fix: Define the exit before entry; he does not give signals. (23:50)
- Taking a model setup when the invalidation leaves only 1:1. Fix: Skip it; he does not trade 1:1. (50:10)
