## Mistakes and warnings
- Trading lower-timeframe setups through news with tight stops Fix: Expect slippage (a -1R can become -3R); only hold through news on higher timeframes with distant stops, or don't trade right before red-folder news. (16:30)
- Waiting for discipline to appear (tilt, revenge trading) Fix: Max one trade a day, follow a model; make the active decision to change, or quit trading. (17:30)
- Forcing a trade when you don't know what's going on Fix: Sit and wait; many traders fail because they feel they must trade. (35:00)
- Making the market fit your idea before news Fix: Let the news release, then trade what forms. (37:25)
- Trading every potential swing low (supply/demand style) and taking repeated losses Fix: Wait for the higher low to form its wick, then trade it. (42:40)
- Pattern trading (first presented FVG, shorting because price hit an FVG) Fix: Frame trades from HTF context, targets and SMT, not patterns. (16:15)
- Adding volume and extra tools Fix: Simplify; extra inputs create confusion in execution. (32:10)
- Predicting many candles ahead Fix: Predict only the next candle. (52:20)
- Taking a reversal-type entry against the current bearish candle Fix: Wait for a candle that supports the direction (reversal on reversal is not ideal). (55:30)
- Trading deep retracement continuations Fix: Skip them; a deep retracement means the read has flipped. (1:03:10)
- Going long away from a failure swing inside a bearish, already-expanded daily candle Fix: Align with the HTF candle; only trade the retracement after the low is taken. (56:20)
- Trading currencies on 5m or lower Fix: Stay on 15m or higher for FX; it cleans up structure. (1:13:50)
