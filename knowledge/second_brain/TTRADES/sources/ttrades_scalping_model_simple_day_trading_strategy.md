# TTrades Scalping Model - Simple Day Trading Strategy

## TL;DR
The scalping model shifts the TTFM down one level: the hourly candle replaces the daily as the bias candle, a 15-minute swing point forms the hourly wick, and a 1-minute fractal model / protected swing is the execution. The bias comes from an hourly candle 2 or candle 3 closure that anticipates an expansion candle; the trade is the body of that hourly candle. Entries are continuation entries off protected swings with stop on the swing and 2R (or HTF objective) targets. An advanced variant trades the hourly reversal candle (candle 2) itself, aligned with the 9:30 open, which he says most people should not focus on.

## Definitions
- **Scalping model**: hourly bias (C2/C3 closure) → 15m swing point forming the hourly wick → 1m fractal model entry; trade = body of the hourly candle (00:10-01:42; page "What You Are Actually Trading").
- **Hourly internal to external**: trading from an hourly FVG (internal liquidity) to external liquidity (00:45, 09:05).
- **Continuation signature**: retracement or consolidation after expansion (01:50, 08:55).
- **A+ entry**: candle digs its wick into the POI then continues (07:09).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Daily context sets direction (page: "Daily chart sets the broader directional context").
2. Hourly: identify candle 2 or candle 3 closure after liquidity interaction / FVG; that sets the expected expansion candle (bias) (00:38).
3. 15m: find the swing point forming the hourly wick; require a 15m candle 2 closure (ideally 15m C3 aligned with hourly C3) (00:56, 06:22).
4. 1m: look for FVG interaction, CISD, protected swing formation (page "Entry"; 04:00).
5. Enter on the closure through (or the retest, or at the HTF candle open if a protected swing already exists); stop on the protected swing (04:20, 10:14, 10:59).
6. Target 2R, HTF objectives, or the other side of the range (04:20, 13:18).
7. Skip if price leaves without the POI; consider continuation only if a retracement (not reversal) and HTF targets remain (06:50, 07:40).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: Gold (XAUUSD) 1h / 15m / 1m, bullish daily trend (01:42-05:20)
- Expansion into consolidation, gap overlapping as POI; hourly candle 2 closure off the low after an hourly sweep (02:28).
- No valid 15m swing; highs taken first. Aggressive move back in; wait one candle; 15m swing fills two FVGs; small hourly wick (03:14-03:30).
- 15m candle 2 closure back inside previous candle low; 1m: confirmation, reach into FVG, closure over, entry, stop on low, 2R hit after consolidation (04:00-05:05).

### Example 2: YM 1h / 15m / 1m, bearish (05:20-08:41)
- Expansion down, shallow move into FVG, equal lows below; hourly candle 2 closure → candle 3 lower (05:35).
- 15m: small FVG holds, candle with no wick, candle 2 closure; 15m C3 aligned with hourly C3 (06:00-06:22).
- 1m: price does not reach the POI before dropping; he would miss it; continuation after retracement possible because HTF target lows remain; it works slowly (06:50-07:55).

### Example 3: Instrument not named, 1h / 15m / 1m, bullish (08:41-11:45)
- Consolidation range low taken, expanded through the high; hourly gap (internal to external); hourly candle 3 opens at noon (08:41-09:20).
- 15m: weak C2, then a bearish candle 2 closure trusted only once candle 3 engulfs and EQ holds (09:27).
- 1m: protected swing already present before new hourly open, below EQ; position at open for 2R, or wait for continuation and enter on close through / retest; both TPs hit (10:14-11:30).

### Example 4 (advanced): Instrument not named, 1h / 15m, bearish at 9:30 (11:45-14:10)
- Expansion down, long consolidation, range high taken so the range low is the draw; approaching 9:30 (11:52).
- 15m CISD at the open; hourly supports downside; bearish C3 closure; LTF continuations off FVGs; protected swing at candle 3 open; entry at open, stop on the protected swing, 2R hit; alternative target consolidation lows (12:20-14:04).
- He labels this as trading candle 2 / the hourly reversal candle, which is more difficult (13:30, 14:04).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Taking a technically valid but out-of-model candle 4 entry; wait for a valid swing (02:40).
- Chasing after price leaves the POI; below short-term lows a new phase can start (06:50).
- Trusting a bearish-bodied candle 2 closure for a bullish trade before candle 3 confirms (09:35).
- Trading the hourly reversal candle is advanced; most should avoid (11:52).
