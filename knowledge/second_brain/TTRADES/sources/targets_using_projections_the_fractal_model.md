# Targets Using Projections - The Fractal Model

## TL;DR
Projections (standard deviations) are mechanical targets he uses as confluence with existing liquidity targets, or alone where no liquidity rests (all-time highs). They are always drawn in candle 2 at the reversal: after the CISD, take the manipulation leg (the extreme and the opposing swing that made it), anchor a Fibonacci tool at the extreme and drag to that swing, with levels 1, 0, -1, -2, -2.5, -4, -4.5. A normal manipulation leg targets -2 to -2.5 (expansion: -4 to -4.5); a large manipulation leg targets only -1. He likes this on the hourly chart for the daily range, and the TTrades indicator can draw the projections automatically. Examples: BTC weekly/4H, gold hourly, YM 5m, two unnamed charts.

## Definitions
- **Projection / standard deviation**: Fibonacci extension of the manipulation leg at levels -1, -2, -2.5, -4, -4.5 (00:28).
- **Manipulation leg**: in candle 2 after the CISD, from the extreme (low for bullish) to the opposing swing that made it (the high that made the low); i.e. the CISD's opposing run (01:01, 01:58).
- **Large manipulation leg**: very large opposing run; example: almost half of the range to the target (02:01, 07:11).
- **Max target**: -4 to -4.5 (05:04).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Identify candle 2 (closure / reversal / swing point) and confirm its CISD (05:07).
2. Find the extreme and the swing that made it (the series of candles / swing high that made the low) = manipulation leg (04:17).
3. Fib: anchor at the extreme, drag to that swing; levels 1, 0, -1, -2, -2.5, -4, -4.5 (04:25).
4. Judge leg size: normal → -2/-2.5 (expansion -4/-4.5); large → -1 (01:38, 02:01).
5. Look left for liquidity matching the levels; take profit at liquidity if it sits before the projection (05:12, 09:43).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: BTC 1W → 4H (02:27-03:59)
- Weekly candle 2 closure: previous week's low swept, closed back inside; CISD. Manipulation leg low → high that made it. Projections align with equal highs and a previous high.
- Next week hit the first projection and liquidity; good weekly close → candle 4 with a shallow wick; hit targets and projections.

### Example 2: Gold 1h (Thursday daily candle 2) (04:02-05:28)
- Thursday candle 2 closure with large wick and CISD; projection from the low to the swing high; -2/-2.5 align with previous day high and liquidity left. Hit, went through; then -4/-4.5 reached after a retracement (open low, high close).

### Example 3: YM 5m, late New York session (05:28-06:52)
- Unclean reversal, small range → low expectations; very large manipulation leg; -2 would mean 500-600 points → use -1; a low on the left lines up with -1. Hit -1, took the low, reversed back into the range.

### Example 4: unnamed, 1h with daily candles; frame label Wed 24 Sep '25 (06:52-09:03)
- Candle 2 closure, CISD, failure swings left; manipulation leg ~half the range to target → -1. Open low, high close through -1; then expansion into -2/-2.5.
- New daily candle 2 closure; waited for CISD; manipulation leg from high to the low that made it; indicator projections; large leg → -1 within current daily range; hit; failure swings line up with -2/-2.5 → all projections hit.

### Example 5: unnamed, 5m (frame price ~2,47x) (09:05-11:00)
- Large manipulation leg → -1 hit, consolidation, small reversal. Next reversal with closure: -2/-2.5 just before a low → take profit at the low; price hit it and then the final target on a new candle ("most of the time I'm going to take my profit here").
- Opposite reversal: -1 aligns with equal highs, -2/-2.5 with failure swing highs; in consolidation watch -1 (internal liquidity). Did not reverse off the high; looking higher; "where we actually are currently" (live at recording).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Expecting -2/-4 from a large manipulation leg ("an absurd move") (07:24).
- Being greedy for the final projection when liquidity sits just before it (10:06).
- Expecting a big move late in the New York session (05:58).

## Conflicts and open questions
- "Large" manipulation leg is not numerically defined; one example uses "almost half" of the range to the target (07:11). A rule engine needs a ratio threshold.
- Fib settings list says "1, 0, 1" (00:32); -1 is meant (used throughout).
- Whether -2 vs -2.5 vs -4 is chosen beyond "if price is going to expand" is left open.
- Anchor direction: bullish = anchor low, drag to the high that made the low (01:12); bearish = the low that made the high (01:30); the 08:05 example says "from this high to the low that made that high".
- Recording date unknown; example 4 frame shows Wed 24 Sep '25 and example 5 is described as current, so recorded around late September 2025 (inferred).
