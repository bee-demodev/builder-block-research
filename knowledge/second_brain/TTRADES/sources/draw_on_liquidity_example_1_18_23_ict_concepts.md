## Definitions
- **Draw on liquidity**: where price is expected to reach (staged PD arrays and liquidity) (00:07, 02:06).
- **BPR (balanced price range)**: used as a PD array target, not defined (01:04).
- **Low-resistance liquidity**: lows taken easily in the trend (02:38).
- **Volume imbalance** (Whisper "volume in Don's"): daily PD array used as the short POI (00:07).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Daily: choose the POI that prior bodies/wicks respected (00:07-00:36).
2. Check the correlated index for its own POI fill and SMT on 4h (00:36-01:04).
3. 1h: mark staged targets (BPR, equal lows) (01:04).
4. 15m: refine (15m BPR, body respect) (01:38).
5. Track reactions at each target; if sliced, look to the next liquidity / PD array (02:06).
6. Re-check the daily for the final target (02:38).
