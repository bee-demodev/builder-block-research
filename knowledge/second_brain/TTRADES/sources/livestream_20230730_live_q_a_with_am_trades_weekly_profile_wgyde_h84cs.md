Sunday-evening (17:00-18:00 NY) guest Q&A in which TTrades hosts AM Trades about AM's weekly-profile PDF; no live market and no live trades. AM explains his weekly-profile process: filter the USD red-folder calendar for the first high-impact driver of the week, never trade Monday, wait for price to engage a daily/4H PD array on the right day of the week, confirm with a 1H change in state of delivery (or a 30m breaker), then trade the following days' expansion only in the New York session, executing on M1-M5.
