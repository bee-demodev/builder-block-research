# OTE Optimal Trade Entry - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Very early, three-minute lesson (prerequisite: his premium and discount video). OTE = the fib levels a retracement is likely to reach: draw the displacement range that broke structure, use 0.62 and 0.79 (with 0.5 as the premium/discount line); a retracement into the 0.62-0.79 zone is likely, better with an imbalance or POI inside. Uses: confluence with a FVG after a breaker structure, nested OTEs ("OTE inside an OTE"), a higher-timeframe POI (S&P 1h touches 0.62) for a lower-timeframe entry, the same fib levels as retracement targets after a short, and on lower-timeframe displacement legs to choose between many small FVGs. Pre-TTFM ICT content.

## Definitions
- **OTE (optimal trade entry)**: retracement zone between the 0.62 and 0.79 fib of the displacement range that broke structure (00:34).
- **0.5 fib**: premium / discount line of the range (00:34).
- **Breaker structure**: the structure break setup he anchors the OTE to (01:05, 01:39).
- **Smart money reversal**: reversal sequence on the S&P hourly preceding the displacement leg (01:39).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Identify the displacement leg that broke structure (00:34).
2. Draw the fib from its low to high (bullish); mark 0.5, 0.62, 0.79 (00:34).
3. Look for a FVG / imbalance / POI inside 0.62-0.79 (00:34, 01:05).
4. Use a higher-timeframe OTE as POI, then enter on a lower timeframe; if missed, use a nested OTE on a lower-timeframe breaker (01:39, 02:09).
