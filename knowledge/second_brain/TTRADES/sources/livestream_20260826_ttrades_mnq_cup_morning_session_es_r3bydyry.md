## TL;DR
Morning stream (09:14-11:35 NY, Wednesday) of TTrades' one-day MNQ Cup, a free NinjaTrader sim competition (MNQ only, 5 contracts max, $10k, no drawdown rule). TTrades co-hosts with his friend Nick (Nick Does Futures): Nick is the face cam and the right-hand MNQ 3m/15m/30m chart; TTrades stays off camera on the left NQ1!/ES1!/GC1! chart. Before the open TTrades leaned bearish: NQ failed to close over the level from the day before, NQ was the weakest index, ES took its high with SMT and closed through the up-close candles, so he wanted the 7 a.m. high to hold, price to pull into the 15m imbalance (~29,180-29,190) and run toward the previous day's low. The open was a choppy, divergent move (NQ up, YM down, ES chop). YM, the weakest index, reversed with a 5m closure. He used that to time NQ through its equal highs into the previous highs, and his bearish idea was invalidated by a close over the daily open (W bottom). At 10:00 the 4H printed a C2 closure. NQ and YM did not sweep the previous 4H high, but ES did (a small SMT). He would not short it because he trades continuations, not reversals, though he admitted a 1m SMT/CISD framing existed. Price then fell back about 150 points to the 9:30 lows and chopped around the range EQ. Nick lost on two early shorts into the equal highs, then caught the post-10:00 drop with a short on the retest under 29,260. TTrades took no trades. The rest is chat Q&A (defining consolidation, SMT only as confluence, failure swings on gold, early-C2 setting, no-sweep days) and giveaways.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Shorting into equal highs right after the weakest asset has reversed (Nick's first short) Fix: When the weakest index reverses, expect the stronger one to run the equal highs (failure swings) into the previous high; don't fade it and don't place stops just above the equal highs. (26:50)
- Looking for SMT randomly at highs and lows to support an idea Fix: First have a valid model or reason for a direction, then use SMT as confluence. (1:59:10)
- Pattern-trading the indicator's signals without alignment Fix: Every pattern must align with something (the HTF bias / draw). (2:17:30)
- Framing shorts when price failed to sweep the important level Fix: Without the sweep the trend stays intact; wait for a sweep or for bearish price action. (53:00)
- Trusting a reversal that forms off a failure swing Fix: Wait for the relevant swing / previous day low to be taken (watch the relevant swings video). (1:22:10)
- Oversizing: more than one or two minis on a 50K funded account Fix: Size down; that sizing is a sign of an unprofitable trader. (44:10)
- Stacking too many confluences until confused Fix: Watch one model video (e.g. the fractal model) and keep it simple. (1:49:50)
- Going all-in at the open with no setup (many cup players went full long at 9:30) Fix: It worked on this day for some, but it is luck; the early leaders were replaced by those who took profit at the highs and flipped short. (Speaker partly Nick.) (1:16:40)
