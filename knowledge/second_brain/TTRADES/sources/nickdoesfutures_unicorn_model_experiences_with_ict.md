# NickDoesFutures | Unicorn Model & Experiences With ICT

## TL;DR
Guest presentation: TTrades only introduces (00:11) and closes (33:46); everything else is NickDoesFutures (ES/NQ futures, NY AM killzone trader) describing his ICT-derived "Unicorn" (breaker + FVG) model and his routine. The model is a three-layer filter: (1) a written weekly plan built from the economic calendar, higher-timeframe draw on liquidity and DXY/bond/index correlation, with each weekday graded by probability; (2) a written pre-session plan (bias, liquidity targets, news, low-probability-day check); (3) execution only after price taps a 1h/4h POI, a lower-timeframe stop run, displacement and a retest of the breaker, using paired timeframes 1h->5m, 4h->15m->1m. Risk: limit at the breaker edge, stop at the FVG candle or breaker (rarely the swing low), 80% off at 2R, rest at 3R. Most of the video is about psychology (waiting, strategy hopping, social-media FOMO, journaling). It sits next to TTFM as a guest's ICT model; it is not taught as TTFM, though the HTF POI -> LTF entry pairing mirrors TTFM timeframe pairing.

## Definitions
- **Unicorn strategy / model**: the breaker entry model he trades; the transcript names it while showing a 1h FVG, a 5m stop hunt and a breaker, entry on the breaker retest, stop at the FVG candle or breaker (21:27-21:38). Breaker + FVG overlap is the ICT-community meaning; he never states it in one sentence (implied).
- **Trapped order flow**: price sitting between a bullish and a bearish HTF FVG (weekly FVG above and below, or daily FVG + weekly FVG + daily breaker) without closing through either; an "indecisive" state = low probability (12:49-13:42).
- **Indecisive candle**: HTF candle that closed neither above the upper FVG nor below the lower one (13:00-13:05).
- **Nested PD**: price trading inside a daily FVG while the setup forms (20:51-20:56). "PD" = PD array.
- **Pre-session game plan**: four written items each morning: bias, liquidity targets (e.g. Asia highs, London relative equal highs), news, low-probability-day check (18:42-19:00).
- **Low-probability day/session**: see rule 16 (17:44-18:42).
- **Properly aligned time frame**: 4h->15m, 1h->5m, 15m->1m pairs (17:05-17:15); on-chart text at 21:21: "4H PD Array->15M MMXM, 1H PD Array->5M MMXM, 15M PD Array->1M MMXM".

## Procedure / checklist
1. Sunday/Monday night: read the economic calendar, mark news days; grade each weekday (no trades / low / medium probability) (07:30-11:58).
2. Weekly/daily analysis of ES, NQ, YM, DXY, bonds: HTF draw on liquidity, expected weekly candle, trapped order flow, SMT only with displacement (12:49-15:06).
3. Each morning write the pre-session plan: bias, liquidity targets, news, low-probability check (18:42-19:20).
4. 9:00-11:30 ET: watch 1h/4h for price to tag one of 1-2 marked POIs aligned with bias (15:13-16:30).
5. No tag -> no lower-timeframe chart (16:30-16:38).
6. Tag -> drop to paired timeframe (1h->5m, 4h->15m, or 4h+15m->1m), wait for a stop run of a consolidation / Asia high-low, displacement, breaker (17:05-17:44, 21:08-22:12).
7. Enter on the stronger/weaker pair with a limit at the breaker edge; stop at FVG candle or breaker; 80% at 2R, rest at 3R (19:20-19:46, 22:20-23:24).
8. Journal the trade and write post-session thoughts (24:17-26:28).

## Q&A
- **Q (30:05):** Do you ever trade opposite your weekly plan? **A:** Yes, when the daily clearly shifts and the draw is clear, e.g. a bearish FVG invalidated (week of August 12) so now looking for longs.
- **Q (30:41):** Trades per week? **A:** 0-3; after two winners midweek, stop for the week.
- **Q (31:14):** How do you set daily bias? **A:** Obvious structure shifts, indices vs dollar vs bonds, clear displacement; otherwise wait.
- **Q (31:47):** Same bias for a long time? **A:** Fine, but wait for price to confirm before trading; he has been long-only near all-time highs for months.
- **Q (32:40):** What keeps people from profitability? **A:** Impatience, overtrading, strategy hopping, FOMO, mainly social media (Twitter).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Learning too many ICT concepts (rejection blocks, multiple courses) is noise; remove parts you don't need (02:39-03:24).
- Expecting ICT to never lose; it is "just another strategy" (03:00-03:13).
- Seeing others' trades on social media then forcing a trade next day (10:55-11:16, 33:10-33:38).
- Dropping straight to 1m and chasing displacement without a session bias or draw on liquidity (16:41-17:01).
- Anxiety during a trade usually means oversizing or not enough backtesting of the entry (24:39-25:01).
- Strategy hopping after losses instead of testing the model over 3-12 months (26:49-27:21).
- Prop-firm time limits and rules that force trading (07:02-07:12).
