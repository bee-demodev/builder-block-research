# Timeframe Alignment: How To Align Timeframes For Expansion

## TL;DR
Top-down analysis uses three timeframes: higher for bias, middle for structure, lower for entry. The structure timeframe must first shift (CISD or MSS) to match the higher-timeframe bias, then retrace into a key level (FVG, high/low, order block); then the entry timeframe must shift the same way at that level (order block formation / CISD). When all three point the same direction, expansion occurs. Fixed pairings: W-4H, D-1h, 4H-15m, 1h-5m, 30m-3m, 15m-1m; bias for a structure timeframe comes from that timeframe's own pair (5m entry → 1h structure → 1D bias; 1m → 15m → 4H). Four examples: ES weekly/4H/15m, gold daily/1h/5m, Dow 30m/3m with SMT, EURUSD daily/1h/5m with hourly runners.

## Definitions
- **Bias / structure / entry timeframe**: the three roles in top-down analysis (00:31).
- **Key level**: FVG, a high or low, or an order block on the structure timeframe (01:31).
- **Order block formation (entry)**: entry-timeframe price moves from bullish to bearish (close through the series) at the key level (01:55).
- **Change in the state of delivery**: close through the series of up-close (down-close) candles into the important level (04:00).
- **Propulsion block**: an order block formed off another order block (09:02).
- **Expansion condition**: all timeframes aligned in one direction (10:27).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Bias on the higher timeframe (e.g. swing point formed, old high reached) (00:57).
2. Drop to the paired structure timeframe; wait for CISD/MSS in the bias direction (01:10).
3. Wait for a key level on the structure timeframe (FVG retesting the CISD, high/low, order block) (01:27).
4. Drop to the paired entry timeframe; wait for its CISD / order block formation at the key level (01:47).
5. Enter (on the next candle open or close), stop on the high/low, target 2R; runners to higher-timeframe liquidity (06:00, 15:26).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: ES weekly → 4H → 15m, bottom-right label 4H ES1!, week of 28 May (2024 by price) (03:28-07:30)
- Weekly reaches into an old high; 4H: no close below the up-close series yet; consolidation; highs to the left swept → new high → close below its series = CISD.
- Retracement into the range, new 4H order block. 15m at the 4H order block: first close fails, new high, new series, close below → short, 2R on entry timeframe; hit.
- Alternative: 4H entry with 4H invalidation; hit 2R, or wait for swing low taken; also hit TP. Weekly formed a swing point; trading an expansion candle short.

### Example 2: Gold 1D → 1h → 5m (07:30-10:29)
- Daily: three days of expansion into an old high, expanding away from the weekly open; reversal expected off previous day high.
- 1h: previous day high swept; series of up-close candles closed below = CISD; retrace into small FVG retesting the CISD; first close fails, second close below = propulsion block.
- 5m: retest of hourly order block, close below up-close candles → short at the open of the new candle; consolidation, then expansion to TP.

### Example 3: Dow (YM) 30m → 3m, New York session (10:29-12:28)
- No sweep of the low but SMT; failure swings on the highs; daily profile = New York reversal. Close over → low should hold.
- 30m bullish FVG = POI; 3m: bearish price action into the 30m FVG, then close through the down-close candles while sweeping LTF lows → long, stop on low, 2R; hit.

### Example 4: EURUSD 1D → 1h → 5m (12:28-15:58)
- Daily: downtrend, retrace into FVG sweeping a high = consolidation manipulation → target consolidation low (AMD).
- 1h: up-close series into the level closed below = aligned bearish; mean threshold of the CISD should hold; London retrace into hourly POI (CISD, FVG, high to the left swept) with an hourly candle closure.
- 5m: wait for close through up-close series → short, stop on high, or wait for retest; consolidation respecting 0.5 of the CISD → propulsion block; close below → short, stop over swing high, 2R; runners to hourly short-term low, then daily consolidation low; low taken.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Entering before the structure timeframe shifts in the bias direction (10:06).
- Getting caught up on the lower timeframe: entering on the structure timeframe is an alternative (06:49).
- Expecting a reversal out of a consolidation that still respects 0.5 of the CISD (14:44).
