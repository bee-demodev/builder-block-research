# TTrades Swing Trading Model - Simple Strategy

## TL;DR
TTrades scales the fractal model up for swing trades: weekly candle bias (candle 2 closure, previous week high/low, failure swings, FVGs) -> daily swing point (daily candle 2 / candle 3 with CISD) -> hourly entry (protected swing, V-shaped reversal, continuation), with alternatives monthly -> weekly -> 4h (crypto) or weekly -> 4h directly. If the hourly is messy, move up one timeframe. Entries need a reasonable stop (he skips continuations whose stop is too large) and about 2R, with the HTF objective as the full target. Four examples, including a valid loss (second entry stopped, first entry +3.74R). The education page (published 2026-02-21) restates the same three steps.

## Definitions
- **Weekly bias**: anticipated direction of the current weekly candle, from liquidity, highs/lows, FVGs and candle closures; minimal form: which previous-week extreme price reaches for (00:30-00:46, 02:28-02:38).
- **Daily swing point**: daily candle 2 or candle 3 in the bias direction that forms the wick of the daily candle (01:02-01:12).
- **Failure swings**: untaken highs/lows left behind, used as full expansion targets (03:11-03:15, 03:27-03:32).
- **V-shaped reversal**: sharp reversal off a sweep into a consolidation, creating a protected swing (07:06-07:18). Page: "a sweep of liquidity followed by a sharp rejection and a strong closure away from that level".
- **Intracandle CISD**: CISD seen inside the forming HTF candle on the lower timeframe (07:58-08:01).
- **BPR**: named as an alternative description of the daily V-shaped reversal (09:32-09:35); balanced price range (glossary, not defined here).
- **T-spot**: area on the daily and hourly to be respected for the move higher (13:19-13:24); not defined in this video.

## Procedure / checklist
1. Weekly: decide the expected weekly candle direction / which previous-week extreme is the draw (00:28-00:56, 02:28-02:46).
2. Daily: wait for a candle 2 (or 3) closure with CISD in that direction; no swing point = no trade (01:02-01:44, 02:51-03:32).
3. Hourly (or 4h): find a protected swing / V-shaped reversal / continuation; reject entries with too-large stops (03:32-03:52, 06:55-07:36).
4. If messy, move up one timeframe (11:18-11:37).
5. Stop on the protected swing; first target about 2R, full target the HTF objective (failure swings, previous week extreme, equal highs) (04:47-04:53, 07:22-07:36).
6. Hold through the candle 3 day(s) into the draw (01:45-01:58).

## Mistakes and warnings
- Taking continuations with oversized stops (03:44-03:52).
- Trusting a continuation that did not reach into the POI without SMT (04:55-05:04).
- Expecting every valid setup to win; losses with context, bias and structure are valid (08:28-08:54).
- Forcing a rigid timeframe alignment on messy charts (11:18-11:37).
- Entering a no-wick protected candle before the continuation confirms (16:31-16:51).

## Conflicts and open questions
- Rule 11 (entry without a continuation) is explicitly discretionary; a rule engine has no condition for when it applies.
- "Stop loss is just too big" has no numeric threshold (03:44).
- "Upper half" / "0.5 of this wick" thresholds: close-based or wick-based violation is not stated.
- Example symbols are not named except the ETH comparison; no frames for this video.
- Page says weekly bias uses "changes in the state of delivery"; video says liquidity, FVGs and candle closures (CISD only checked at the daily level).
- "T-spot" (13:21) and "BPR" (09:34) are used without definition.
