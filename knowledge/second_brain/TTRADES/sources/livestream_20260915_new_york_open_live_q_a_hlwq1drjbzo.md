## TL;DR
Tuesday, the day before FOMC (he notes FOMC is 'tomorrow'). He came in bullish on the indices because ES swept a 1H low (~7,652.5) overnight while NQ held above Monday's low (SMT), with an hourly CISD off the ES low, but the daily had not respected the EQ (larger opposing run), so he only expected modest targets; his single idea was NQ to the overnight/Asia highs (~29,496 line). YM never reversed (it kept making new 1m lows), NQ chopped in failure swings between ~29,400 and ~29,450, the 4H candle sat at its EQ, the last attempt (consolidation run into the NQ 1m FVG ~29,398-29,402) failed after 10:00 and NQ dropped to ~29,350 by 10:12; he called his bias wrong and took no trade. The lessons he wrote on screen: if bullish, use the weakest asset reversing to frame longs on the strongest; if bearish, use the strongest asset reversing to frame shorts on the weakest; and waiting for clean continuations saves you from reversal losses when the bias is wrong. Side reads: gold consolidation (possible bearish turn if silver reverses off the high it took), oil cool-off/bearish with large-wick targets, dollar bullish to the range highs, plus B-ADJ/settlement settings and loss-streak psychology.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Putting on a long before the open when no swings are expected to hold after a high was taken. Fix: Let the open go; wait for the run of the lows, a reversal and the 15m closure. (15:40)
- Blindly longing the reversal on the weakest asset (YM) after a massive run down. Fix: Don't trade the reversal; use the weak asset only to time a continuation on the strongest. (17:10)
- Entering on a 15m C2 that has a large wick. Fix: Large wick = limited target (daily open / intra-candle highs); expect a retracement and let the next candle expand. (23:00)
- Trading right after an expansion and getting chopped. Fix: Price needs to range after expanding; wait for the range, then look to trade. (36:10)
- Trusting lows after a deep run that left failure swings. Fix: Only trade from swings that hold; skip setups built on failure swings. (42:30)
- Always trying to trade reversals; when the bias is wrong you keep getting stopped out. Fix: Wait for clean continuations; they keep you out on days your bias is wrong. (58:20)
- Expecting a CISD or any strategy to work 100% of the time. Fix: Accept losses as part of the edge; anyone selling 100% is lying. (1:01:30)
- Charting futures without back-adjust and reading the contract rollover jump as a real gap. Fix: Turn B-ADJ on and SET off; check against QQQ/NDX. (1:02:50)
- Judging yourself after three losses in a row / one profitable month. Fix: Look at stats over ~100 trades and 3-6 months. (1:06:40)
