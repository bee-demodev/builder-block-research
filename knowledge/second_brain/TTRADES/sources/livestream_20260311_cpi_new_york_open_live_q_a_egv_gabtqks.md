## Mistakes and warnings
- Waiting for a CISD close when the CISD level is at your short-term target, so you enter at the target. Fix: Use the V-shape reversal into a gap plus SMT as the trigger instead. (19:10)
- Copying social-media turtle-soup entries that look tight because the stop was moved after the fact. Fix: Enter at the sweep with the stop actually there, only with all timeframes aligned; most people should avoid it. (28:20)
- Moving the stop to breakeven too early, getting retested out before the move. Fix: Wait for a new protected swing and for price to trade away, then move the stop. (32:20)
- Predicting multiple legs/days ahead. Fix: Anticipate one leg, see what happens, then predict the next. (32:20)
- Taking blind retest entries into a POI. Fix: Wait for a swing point / candle 2 close in the POI showing it wants to go lower. (38:00)
- Switching bias on one 15m candle. Fix: Only switch when a relevant level is hit or relative strength changes. (1:04:30)
- Believing 90-100% win rate claims or expecting a mechanical model to catch every move. Fix: Accept missed moves; nobody has 100% win rate. (49:00)
- Comparing yourself to others' 10R posts. Fix: Ask where the other trades are; high-R chasers usually have low win rates. (1:46:30)
- Repeating what doesn't work (entitlement to the market's money). Fix: Learn from it and stop doing it. (1:04:30)
- Holding for an extra few points near the target and giving back much of the move. Fix: Trail to the 1m bar as price approaches the target. (1:22:50)
