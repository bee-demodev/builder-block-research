## Mistakes and warnings
- Chasing: buying after the HTF candle has already expanded or made its average range, and after highs are already taken. Fix: Enter around the HTF candle open after the wick forms. If you weren't in before the expansion, do nothing pre-open. (13:00)
- Calling a daily FVG reaction bearish just because one candle mechanically closed back below it. Fix: Require a closure plus a change in state of delivery with aggressive price action; read the phases of price and use discernment. (29:40)
- Pattern-trading the model on choppy, consolidating assets (gold, silver). Fix: Only trade a clean framework: a continuation inside a larger continuation, with a protected swing confirming the wick. (29:40)
- Overtrading: most trades in an overtrader's journal (~80%) have no higher-timeframe bias or reason. Fix: Step one: have an HTF bias or reason before any trade. (35:00)
- Trying to predict whether 10:00 will continue or reverse (Judas swing narratives). Fix: Trade the continuation you have; use the 4H candle close as a time-based exit. (33:40)
- Expecting a strategy never to lose. Fix: Accept losses; size so you never risk everything on one trade. (22:40)
- Using his stream as a signal service. Fix: Learn the framework; he does not call live trades. (39:00)
- Believing you need many more technical concepts. Fix: Highs/lows, FVG and opposing candles (CISD) were enough for this whole read. (52:30)
