# Price Targets - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Short early lesson: he chooses targets from two things, liquidity (lows/highs where stops rest, especially equal highs/lows) and imbalances (FVGs on the execution and higher timeframes, small order blocks). After a stop run and lower-timeframe structure shift, list the targets in order: nearby swing lows, FVGs, equal lows, then further liquidity; add to the position on retracements; counter-trend scalps can go "imbalance to imbalance" toward the final liquidity target. Imbalance targets exit earlier than pure liquidity targets but give more targets. Chart is ES 5m (on-screen "S&P 500 E-mini Futures").

## Definitions
- **Liquidity (target)**: lows/highs where stops rest; equal lows/highs are the obvious pools (00:39, 01:46).
- **Imbalance**: fair value gap on the execution or higher timeframe (00:39).

## Procedure / checklist
1. After entry (stop run + LTF structure shift), list liquidity in the path: nearest lows, equal lows, further pools (00:06-01:46).
2. Add imbalances in the path: 5m and 15m FVGs, small order blocks (00:39, 01:11).
3. Scale / add on retracements, risking the high; continue to the next liquidity (02:16).
4. For counter-trend scalps, go imbalance to imbalance toward the final liquidity target (02:16-02:48).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: ES 5m short after a stop run (00:06-01:11)
- Buy stops run above highs, LTF structure shift; targets: three lows below (liquidity), a 5m FVG, and a 15m FVG coinciding with the liquidity run.

### Example 2: long after a run of a high and shift (01:11-01:46)
- (Direction as spoken: "got long"; the targets listed are below: order block, lower FVG, liquidity, equal lows.)

### Example 3: short from an FVG, then adds (01:46-02:16)
- Structure shift, short in one of two FVGs; obvious target equal lows, taken; next liquidity pools; adds on retracements risking the highs; small imbalance expected to fill.

### Example 4: counter-trend scalp to HTF imbalances (02:16-03:20)
- Expect a return up into an HTF imbalance; long, target imbalance, then the next imbalance, final target the highs. A short from a small order block would be weaker than waiting for the move into the FVG.
