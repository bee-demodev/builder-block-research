# Inversion Fair Value Gaps (IFVG) - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Defines an inversion: a fair value gap that gets closed through (disrespected), after which price either retraces into the old gap from the other side or simply continues. Consequent encroachment (CE, 50% of the FVG) is the decision line: respect = continuation of the gap's direction, violation hints at an inversion, but the inversion needs a full close through the gap. Examples: EURUSD 1h sweep to 5m inversion shorts and longs (external -> internal liquidity), NQ 2m consolidation inversion long and in-trend inversions as continuation entries, and gold daily using an old SIBI from the sell side of the curve as support (reaccumulation). Pre-TTFM ICT lesson; IFVG later reappears in TTFM entry models.

## Definitions
- **Fair value gap**: three-candle pattern, candle 1 low not overlapping candle 3 high (bearish) or candle 1 high not overlapping candle 3 low (bullish) (00:28).
- **SIBI**: sell-side imbalance buy-side inefficiency = bearish FVG (00:57).
- **BISI**: buy-side imbalance sell-side inefficiency = bullish FVG (01:47).
- **Inversion (IFVG)**: a FVG closed through, which then acts from the other side (00:57, 05:34).
- **Consequent encroachment (CE)**: 50% of a FVG (01:22).
- **External / internal liquidity**: stops beyond the range's old highs/lows vs FVGs inside the range (03:20, 05:04).
- **Propulsion block**: order block formed off an earlier order block (06:38).
- **Smart money reversal**: his term in the PDF for the reversal sequence: CISD, inversion, market structure shift (08:13).
- **Sell side / buy side of the curve**: the decline leg vs the rally leg of a reversal (08:13).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Higher timeframe: identify the sweep of external liquidity (EURUSD 1h) (01:47).
2. Lower timeframe: mark the opposing FVG; wait for a close through the whole gap (02:16, 05:34).
3. Entry: market on the close, a retest of the inversion, or the CE of the old gap if R:R is short (02:16, 03:48).
4. Stop above the opposing candle / on the sweep low (02:16, 06:03).
5. Target the opposite liquidity (lows, the other side of the range, hourly FVG) (02:47, 04:17, 06:03).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: EURUSD 1h -> 5m short (01:47-03:20)
- Hourly sweep of buy stops above old highs; 5m bullish FVG closed below = inversion; entry options market or retest, stop above an opposing candle, target the lows; FVGs support price lower; lows hit. Hourly: price ran to the other side of the range sweeping sell-side liquidity.

### Example 2: EURUSD 5m long, external -> internal (03:20-05:04)
- After the low sweep, consolidation; a new low sweeps sell stops and forms a FVG; close over it = inversion. Entry with stop on the low targeting the FVG midpoint is about 1R -> refine to the CE of the old SIBI. Entry fills, displacement, FVG respected, reaches the hourly SIBI.

### Example 3: NQ 2m consolidation long (05:04-06:38)
- Aggressive move down and back up; no follow-through, sweeps highs but cannot close outside the range -> consolidation; wait for external range liquidity. Sell-side liquidity swept twice; close over the CE (hint) then over the SIBI (inversion). Return to the inversion, stop on the low, target the other side of the range; CE respected; sweep then displacement over -> order block, new high.

### Example 4: NQ 2m in-trend inversions (06:38-08:13)
- Uptrend: order block and propulsion block; retrace creates a SIBI, close over it -> entry into the trend; choppy then new high. Repeats: higher-low area has a SIBI, close over -> higher high; another inversion he "just missed".

### Example 5: GC 1D old SIBI as support (08:36-09:40)
- Equal highs above; SIBI marked; close over the SIBI and the down-close candles (smart money reversal); short-term low forms. Old sell-side FVG dragged out; price closes over it, reaches back into it, then moves up to sweep the buy-side liquidity.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Treating a close over the CE as an inversion; require a close over the whole gap (05:34).
- Taking an inversion entry with about 1R; refine to the CE instead (03:48).

## Conflicts and open questions
- His minimum R:R requirement is implied (> ~1R) but not stated as a number.
- "Close over" = body close beyond the far edge of the gap, implied by the CE vs full-gap distinction (05:34).
- Smart money reversal is a PDF term not defined in speech.
- He says "external range liquidity" and "internal liquidity" referencing a separate video; no definition here.
