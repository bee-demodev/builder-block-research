# Choosing Dealing Ranges & Displacement Ranges - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Short early lesson on picking the range for premium/discount. Dealing range: the nearest high and low that contain current price (zoom in, find a high and a low, Fib it); ranges nest, and "external" liquidity on a zoomed-in view becomes internal when you zoom out. A dealing range is only valid if price is inside it. Displacement range: the aggressive leg that broke structure; anchor the Fib at the low that started it and trail the high as it extends. For longs he wants a retracement into a discount of both the displacement range and the dealing range, ideally OTE (0.62) with a PD array (order block) there. Context: New York morning session (8:30, 9:30). Instrument not named.

## Definitions
- **Dealing range**: nearest swing high and low containing current price; Fib high-to-low gives premium/discount (00:05-00:33).
- **Displacement range**: aggressive leg over structure; Fib anchored at the starting low, trailed to the latest high (01:37, 03:13).
- **OTE**: retracement zone in discount; 0.62 cited (03:43, 04:46).
- **PD array**: premium/discount array, e.g. order block (04:16).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Zoom to current price; pick the nearest high and low that contain price (00:05, 01:37).
2. Fib high to low: dealing range premium/discount (00:33).
3. Identify the displacement leg that broke structure; anchor at its origin low, trail the high (03:13-03:43).
4. Long only on retracement into discount of both ranges, ideally OTE (03:43-04:16).
5. Find a PD array (order block) in that zone; enter when candles respect it (04:16).
6. Target the high (05:25).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: New York morning session, dealing vs displacement range (01:03-02:40)
- 8:30 start, 9:30 open. Lower dealing ranges invalid (price above); zoom out to the containing high/low; price in premium of the dealing range. Displacement up to a high; retracement into discount of the displacement leg, then higher; new displacement range; reach for highs.

### Example 2: OTE and order block long, two entries (02:40-05:25)
- Displacement leg starts; dealing range marked; Fib from the structure-breaking low, trailed up; price reaches premium of the dealing range (short zone, but he wants longs).
- Retracement into discount / OTE with an order block; candles do not close through it; move higher (entry 1).
- New displacement range trailed; price reaches over a high without displacing, comes back to 0.62 with an order block, also below the dealing range midpoint (entry 2, targeting the high). Price ran up, broke the high, then moved lower.

## Mistakes and warnings
- Confusion over which highs/lows to use; labels like external/internal depend on zoom (00:05, 00:33).
- Using a dealing range price has already left (01:37).

## Conflicts and open questions
- Speech at 03:13 says "we are in a discount down here and a premium down here" (likely misspoken "premium up here").
- OTE bounds are not given beyond "0.62".
- How far to zoom for the "nearest" high/low is discretionary.
- No timeframe or instrument named.
