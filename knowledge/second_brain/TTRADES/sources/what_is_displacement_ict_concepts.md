# What is Displacement? - ICT Concepts

## TL;DR
Very short early ICT-era answer to viewer questions. When price breaks a short-term high you want displacement: an "energetic" move. He judges it by comparing two breaks of short-term highs over the same number of candles (four): the displacing break has a larger candle range and covers more distance beyond the short-term high. After displacement, look for the FVG setup and buy in the discount of the displacement range (swing low to displacement high). Pre-TTFM vocabulary; the closest it gets to a quantitative displacement test.

## Definitions
- **Displacement**: an energetic break of a short-term swing, recognised by larger candle range and more distance covered past the swing than a weak break over the same number of candles (00:39-01:49).
- **Displacement range**: from the swing low before the move to the displacement high; used for premium/discount (01:49).

## Procedure / checklist
1. Mark short-term highs (00:07).
2. When one breaks, take the breaking candles (he uses four) and note their range and the distance beyond the high (00:39).
3. Compare with a weak break over the same number of candles; larger range and distance = displacement (01:12).
4. Find the FVG in the displacement; enter only if it is in the discount of the displacement range (01:49).

## Worked examples
### Example 1: ES 1m, morning session, date not readable (00:39-01:49)
- Weak break: four candles break a short-term high with a small range and little distance beyond it (00:39).
- Strong break: four candles break a later short-term high with a larger range and more distance = displacement (01:12).
- FVG in the displacement; buying at a discount of the low-to-displacement-high range (01:49). No stop, target or outcome stated.

## Mistakes and warnings
- A break of a short-term high that is "not very energetic" is not displacement (00:39).

## Conflicts and open questions
- Comparison is relative (one break vs another), with no absolute threshold (ATR multiple, body %).
- "Four candles" is the count he happened to use; not stated as a rule.
- Discount boundary (50% of the range?) not stated.
- Contrast with structure_shift_vs_liquidity_grab_ict_concepts, which defines displacement by large candles closing at their extremes over structure; neither video requires a specific number of closes.

## Glossary additions
- `energetic move`: his plain-language synonym for displacement.
- `displacement range`: swing low to displacement high (bullish), used for the discount check.
