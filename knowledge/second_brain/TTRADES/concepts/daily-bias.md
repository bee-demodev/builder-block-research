# Daily bias, bias invalidation and the next-day model

## Definition

**Latest (2025-2026).** Daily bias is the one direction TTrades will trade for the current daily candle, read **only from the previous daily closure** against the candle before it: a close beyond the previous day high/low (PDH/PDL) is a continuation bias, a sweep of PDH/PDL with a close back inside is a reversal bias ([[the_only_trading_strategy_you_need_for_2026]] 00:57-01:37; [[fractal_model_fundamentals]] 01:20). His "easiest" mechanical version is a daily candle 2 or candle 3 closure confirmed by an H1 CISD, the daily-hourly fractal model ([[livestream_20260617_new_york_open_live_q_a_with_nickdoesfutures_tdiwwmrwf2k]] 1:02:10; [[livestream_20260401_new_york_open_live_q_a_wbwoimdxt2c]] 26:00; [[livestream_20260610_new_york_open_live_q_a_w7fu3rx5ims]] 49:10), but it is not the only way: he also blends closures, phases of price, the previous day EQ and OHLC/OLHC ([[livestream_20261027_new_york_open_live_q_a_bjzpiunp8gc]] 20:20; [[livestream_20260727_new_york_open_live_q_a_je7cd9hjube]] 50:50). The bias holds until it is invalidated ([[livestream_20260722_new_york_open_live_q_a_g1iidq3raky]] 10:20), and he names two invalidations: price invalidating the EQ, or a reversal off the previous day high/low ([[livestream_20261110_new_york_open_live_q_a_cp1nyqa_t5c]] 1:14:40). The bias must then be confirmed by the daily profile (open, make the low, trade higher) before any entry ([[livestream_20260401_new_york_open_live_q_a_wbwoimdxt2c]] 1:36:40). No bias means no trade and no dropping to lower timeframes to find one ([[livestream_20260923_new_york_open_live_q_a_kbjaggkxdei]] 23:30; [[livestream_20261103_new_york_open_live_q_a_tqokgrazwky]] 1:10:50).

**2023-2024 (ICT draw-on-liquidity framing).** Bias was "the draw on liquidity": is price more likely to reach PDH or PDL? Trending up, the draw is PDH; a PDH taken without a close over it flips the draw to PDL ([[ict_daily_bias_the_only_video_you_will_ever_need]] 00:10-02:29; [[the_easiest_draw_on_liquidity_dol_daily_bias_ict]] 02:28). The **next day model** (credited to the MMXM Trader) anticipated the next daily candle's shape (OLHC bullish, OHLC bearish) after a failure to displace or a respected daily PD array, held until the draw is met ([[ict_daily_bias_the_only_video_you_will_ever_need]] 08:21-09:09; [[next_day_model_the_mmxm_trader_ict_concepts]] 00:37). From late 2024 the same read is re-cast as candle closures plus phases of price ([[next_day_model_fractal_way_to_get_bias_for_trading]] 01:53-05:50; [[livestream_20241006_ttrades_sunday_session_live_q_a_r7yw6ou1lds]] 05:50).

**2022 (ICT-concepts streams).** Bias came from displacement, structure and imbalances on the 4h/1h used as "the daily", plus where liquidity rests (equal highs/lows) ([[intraday_bias_ict_concepts]] 00:00; [[livestream_20220923_stream_q_a_giveaway_winners_announced_am_traders_0yzqr8hpyeu]] 07:30; [[ict_daily_bias]] 04:14). He was relaxed about it: "don't focus on determining bias", trade LTF structure without one ([[livestream_20220807_stream_q_a_ict_concepts_h_xtsfk8np4]] 28:10; [[livestream_20220828_ict_orderflow_q_a_with_holyangelbruv_8z2abzljunu]] 22:10).

## Engine notes

- **Closure read.** `rules.md` 3.4 and 5.1-5.2 classify `B[-1]` against `B[-2]` as reversal / continuation / inside / outside-closing-inside, and cases A-I map them to `Verdict.bias`. This matches rules 2-4 and 17. Inside bar (E), outside bar (F), reversal without POI (G) and conflicting reads (I) are `neutral`; F and I are proposals (open questions 7, 8). The teacher's 2023 rule 73 and his 2026 rule 19 (read an inside day's path) are not encoded (inference; `companion/styles/ttrades/rules.md` 5.2).
- **POI and CISD gates.** Bias needs `bias_poi_present` (G) and `bias_cisd_confirmed` (S, case H neutral until the S-timeframe CISD prints). That follows rules 5-6. The teacher also says closures alone can be enough and a daily POI is ideal, not required (rule 7; [[livestream_20260729_new_york_open_live_q_a_xraklbjhw5k]] 24:30), so the engine is stricter than him on non-POI days (inference; rules.md 6 stage c1).
- **EQ respect is a zero-tolerance gate** (`cont_eq_respected`, `eq_tolerance_frac = 0`, rules.md 3.9 and 9.2); the teacher treats one H1 close beyond EQ as a soft invalidation that cuts targets (rule 33), and only a close without reclaim as failure (rule 32). Open question 5 is still open.
- **No invalidation path.** The engine has no "EQ invalidated -> trade to the opposite previous-day extreme" path (rule 39), and `Verdict.bias` keeps the closure value on a reversal, so a reversal short can show `bias = bullish` (rules.md 7.1, open question 32). The teacher flips the target to PDL once a bullish C2 fails ([[livestream_20260923_new_york_open_live_q_a_kbjaggkxdei]] 27:30 note) but does not trade the reversal leg himself (rule 43).
- **Daily profile is not a criterion.** Rule 50 (bias without a confirming daily profile is not traded) has no counterpart; daily profiles are listed as unused in open question 26. The reversal path's `rev_opposing_run_small` and `rev_formed_early` (deadline 11:00 NY) approximate the small-wick idea of rule 48 (inference; rules.md 7 stage c1).
- **Expansion count and target hit.** Rules.md 5.4 stands aside after three expansion candles (rule 59 says three to four) and reports `cont_objective_open` as advisory after the trader's answer to question 16; the teacher stops at the target (rule 57). Exits are fixed R (`target_rr` 2.0), not PDH/PDL (rules.md 8.3).
- **Opens and ADR.** The engine builds the trading day from the 18:00 open and draws the midnight and 09:30 opens only as information (rules.md 10.6, "Intraday reference levels"), consistent with rule 64. Average range is a 20-bar mechanical average; the teacher eyeballs daily range (rule 56).
- **Not modelled:** SMT (open question 23) and relative strength (rules 35, 49, 69), weekly/monthly bias (rule 67), the dollar inverse for FX (rule 69).
