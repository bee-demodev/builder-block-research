# Sessions, opens and time of day

All clock times are New York time. "Guest" marks a rule said by a guest on the TTrades channel, not by TTrades.

## Definition

**2026 (current).** Time in TTFM comes from the opens and closes of higher-timeframe candles, not from session windows. "I don't really care about kill zones. I'm just caring about the higher timeframe candles that I'm trading within" ([[livestream_20260310_new_york_open_live_q_a_wm1s7uivq08]] 18:40); time of day does not matter "as long as I'm inside this higher timeframe candle and it's formed a wick" ([[livestream_20260616_new_york_open_live_q_a_tjuolmjncmy]] 1:01:00). The clock still matters through a few fixed events. The daily futures candle opens at 18:00 ([[livestream_20251007_candle_2_confirmations_live_lesson_q_a_uzpgxvypvgc]] 08:30). 09:30 is the NYSE equity open that injects volatility into index futures ([[livestream_20260311_cpi_new_york_open_live_q_a_egv_gabtqks]] 19:10). 10:00 opens new 4H, 1H and 30m candles, so any new idea forms there ([[livestream_20260311_cpi_new_york_open_live_q_a_egv_gabtqks]] 1:32:40; [[livestream_20260722_new_york_open_live_q_a_g1iidq3raky]] 41:20). CPI, NFP and FOMC are the only news releases he times around ([[livestream_20260910_new_york_open_live_q_a_wa9m30uhrm0]] 18:40). He trades New York only, roughly 08:00-10:30 ([[livestream_20260728_new_york_open_live_q_a_yngx9oaefui]] 37:20). Entries belong early in the higher-timeframe candle, near its opening price, after its wick has formed ([[livestream_20260819_new_york_open_live_q_a_rvrk4mltjss]] 1:18:40). Exits can be timed by the close of the expansion candle (09:45 for the 15m, 10:00 for the 30m/1H/4H) ([[livestream_20260728_new_york_open_live_q_a_yngx9oaefui]] 50:30). He rejects ICT macros as "fake": a macro is only the minutes around a higher-timeframe open or close ([[livestream_20260401_new_york_open_live_q_a_wbwoimdxt2c]] 40:20).

**2022-2024 (ICT-concepts era).** Time meant kill zones. For indices these were Asia 20:00-00:00, London 02:00-05:00 and New York AM 08:30-11:00 ([[important_liquidity_levels_draw_on_liquidity_ict]] 06:33), and the model was searched for only inside them ([[build_an_entry_model_ict_concepts]] 00:32). In 2022 "time matters more than price for entries" ([[livestream_20220724_stream_q_a_ict_concepts__ekue_pwime]] 33:10). The daily reference was the **midnight open** ([[open_high_low_close_ohlc_olhc_ict_concepts]] 02:01), with the 8:30 open as a second premium/discount pivot ([[livestream_20220821_8_14_22_mmxm_live_with_niper_ict_concepts_umlwrlxd_v8]] 38:20). By October 2024 he used the 18:00 open and kept midnight only as support/resistance ([[livestream_20241006_ttrades_sunday_session_live_q_a_r7yw6ou1lds]] 27:10). He gave no explicit "I used to" for this change (see SES-13).

**Session roles (all eras).** Asia accumulates, London manipulates and New York expands. A London reversal that holds leads to a New York continuation; a London that keeps running into a key level sets up a New York reversal ([[ict_daily_profile_new_york_reversal]] 01:16; [[ttrades_fractal_model_trading_candle_4]] 04:18). How much of the daily range and time the earlier sessions used decides what New York can still do ([[livestream_20261020_new_york_open_live_q_a_vzhkq3c8xbi]] 15:20).

## Engine notes

- Kill zones: `session_filter_ok` is computed from `kill_zones = [02:00-05:00, 08:30-11:00]`, only when the entry timeframe is below 1h, and is **advisory** by default (trader answer to Q18). The teacher's 2026 stance (rules 17, 28) matches advisory; the windows themselves are still disputed (SES-1). Inference: an 08:00-10:30 New York window would match his 2026 practice better than 08:30-11:00. (companion `rules.md` 9.1; [[livestream_20260728_new_york_open_live_q_a_yngx9oaefui]] 37:20)
- The engine treats 8:30 / 9:30 / 10:00 as advisory time levels only ("never gating"). The teacher uses them as gates: no reversal after 10:00 (rule 40), wait 3-5 min after 9:30 (rule 43), wait for 10:00 when the 4H has made its range (rule 62). None of these is encoded (teacher-vs-engine, SES-3, SES-6).
- `daily_wick_deadline = 11:00` (a proposal) decides "formed early" for a 1D bias, and `wick_time_frac_max = 0.50` does so for other bias timeframes. Teacher: "early New York or before" (rule 60), reversal "early, not midway" (rule 59). Inference: 11:00 is later than his 08:00-10:30 window; 10:00 or 10:30 would fit rule 40 better (SES-2).
- Entry inside the candle: `cont_entry_window_open` keeps the entry window open while setup candle 3 (or an allowed candle 4) is forming. Rules 57, 58 and 61 (near the HTF open, not in the last minutes, the 3-criteria score) have no engine check. Inference: a "distance from the HTF open / fraction of ADR used" criterion would encode rule 58.
- Session-dependent confirmation timeframe: the engine reads the reversal CISD on `E` instead of `S` only for a 1D bias inside the New York window (rules.md 4.3, Q19). The teacher's map (rules 69-71) covers Asia, London and pre/post-9:30, so the engine covers only part of it (SES-9).
- Reference levels: the engine draws the Asia (**18:00**-00:00) and London (02:00-05:00) high/low plus the midnight and 09:30 opens as information. The teacher's Asia kill zone is 20:00-00:00 (rule 23); the 18:00 start matches the 18:00 daily open and the 7h Asia candle (rule 12), not his kill-zone table (SES-10).
- Not in the engine: time-based exits (rules 67-68; SES-5), the news filter (rules 73-76), day-of-week rules (79-80), and "the earlier session already did the job" (rule 36). All are teacher rules a reviewer may ask about.
