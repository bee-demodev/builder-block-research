# Stops, targets, trade management and risk

## Definition

In the 2026 streams TTrades runs one fixed scheme. Risk a fixed dollar amount per trade, sized from the account's drawdown and the number of losses in a row you want to survive, never from the nominal account size ([[livestream_20261104_new_york_open_live_q_a_j8u9ch8gkdc]] 44:10; [[livestream_20260803_new_york_open_live_q_a_nesscpmzwr0]] 1:00:40). The stop goes exactly on the protected swing the trade moves away from, with no extra buffer: "if it gets taken out, that's my invalidation" ([[livestream_20260727_new_york_open_live_q_a_je7cd9hjube]] 13:24). The minimum target is 2R, and a 2R system breaks even at about a 34% win rate (about 40% after fees) ([[livestream_20261103_new_york_open_live_q_a_tqokgrazwky]] 30:00). Targets are higher-timeframe liquidity: the previous day's high or low, failure swings, the other side of the range, and the daily open on large-wick days ([[livestream_20261020_new_york_open_live_q_a_vzhkq3c8xbi]] 21:00). He scales those targets down when the wick is large or the daily range is already made ([[wick_size_matters_the_key_to_understanding_reversal_and_expansion_candles]] 02:29; [[ttrades_fractal_model_inversion_intraday_strategy]] 02:52). Managing the trade is optional. Beginners should use a strict TP and SL with no management ([[livestream_20260821_new_york_open_live_q_a_4rnc3qxxc20]] 50:40). Break even is allowed only when a return to the entry would itself invalidate the trade ([[livestream_20260610_new_york_open_live_q_a_w7fu3rx5ims]] 50:35; [[livestream_20260902_new_york_open_live_q_a_grc5fvb5tdg]] 42:00). Trailing goes to new protected swings ([[ttrades_top_down_chart_lessons_4]] 6:08). He does not enter once the target has been hit or the daily range has been made ([[livestream_20260331_new_york_open_live_q_a_0aygnc9czyc]] 24:00).

**2025 (TTFM playbook videos).** The same core in a more mechanical form. The stop goes on the protected swing, or on the candle bodies when price "should not trade above 50% of the opposing candles" ([[fractal_model_fundamentals]] 18:01). The minimum target is 2R ([[fractal_model_fundamentals]] 19:08). The best R comes from a lower-timeframe invalidation with a higher-timeframe target ([[fractal_model_fundamentals]] 13:57). Projections become indicator-driven (-1, -2, -2.5, -4, -4.5), and leg size picks the level ([[targets_using_projections_the_fractal_model]] 00:28, 01:38, 02:01). Time-based exits at the HTF candle close appear ([[ttrades_fractal_model_inversion_intraday_strategy]] 05:18).

**2023-2024 (TTFM forming, AM Trades lessons).** The 2R floor is stated many times ([[intraday_bias_simplified_ict_concepts]] 22:52; [[change_in_state_of_delivery_cisd_orderblock_formation_ict_concepts]] 03:11). Targets come from AM Trades' standard-deviation projections of the manipulation leg, -2/-2.5 then -4 ([[targets_with_standard_deviation_projections_ict_concepts]] 00:07, 00:36). Low-resistance liquidity and failure swings are preferred targets ([[low_resistance_liquidity_run_ict_concepts]] 08:28). Stops sit on the sweep extreme or beyond the order block or breaker, and the choice is a trade-off between R and survival ([[breaker_blocks_simplified_ict_concepts]] 05:21). Break even is shown both ways: at 3R ([[ict_silver_bullet_strategy_no_daily_bias_with_backtest]] 06:09) and "do not move the stop to break-even" ([[cpi_gold_trade_review_ict_market_maker_sell_model_mmxm]] 06:16).

**2022 (ICT-concepts Q&A).** Classic ICT risk. The stop sits on the candle that created the FVG or on the short-term swing ([[livestream_20220810_stream_q_a_with_trade_for_opportunity_ict_concepts_0bh_kkg2q6s]] 1:01:50). Targets are "liquidity and imbalances" ([[price_targets_ict_concepts]] 00:06). He takes the first partial at nearby liquidity and then moves to break even ([[livestream_20220814_stream_q_a_with_am_trades_ict_concepts_cruftskoveo]] 21:10). Fixed dollar risk is already the core habit: 4% of a $2,500 trailing drawdown = $100 a trade on micros ([[livestream_20220810_stream_q_a_with_trade_for_opportunity_ict_concepts_0bh_kkg2q6s]] 43:00).

## Engine notes

- Stop: the companion puts the stop on the protected swing of the trigger that fired (rules.md 8.2) with `stop_buffer = 0`, which matches rule 2 (2026). The 2025 "little room" in rule 3 is not modelled. Inference: the two sources agree on the default, and the buffer is the trader's discretion.
- Body stop: the engine allows a body stop only when every candle in the trigger series has a body of at least 60% of its range (`body_stop_min_body_frac = 0.6`, a proposal), and only when the reward check is `quality`. The teacher's test is different: price "should not trade above 50% of the opposing candles" (rule 4, [[fractal_model_fundamentals]] 18:01). See RISK-3.
- Exit: `Verdict.targets[0] = entry ± target_rr × risk`, with `target_rr = 2.0` and no second target. That matches the teacher's default fixed 2R (rule 43). Partials, runners, time exits, break even and trailing (rules 44-62) are not modelled (rules.md 8.3, 10.6).
- The 2R floor: `*_reward_at_least_2r` is advisory by default, so a trigger whose first structural level is under 2R away still says `long`/`short`. The teacher skips such trades or refines the entry (rules 18-19). When the check is `quality`, rules.md gives the limit price that would yield `min_rr` (8.1), which is the teacher's refinement in rule 19.
- Chase rules: `cont_objective_open_at_trigger` (quality) blocks a continuation trigger formed after the HTF target was taken (rule 64), and `entry_latest_candle_frac = 0.75` blocks late-candle entries (rules.md 11). Rule 63 ("before the daily range is made, around the daily open") has no engine check apart from the advisory `stop_size_ok`, which uses a 14-day mean ADR (rules.md 8.2). The teacher estimates range from recent expansion candles instead (RISK-10).
- Played-out verdicts: once the plan's outcome is `target_reached` or `stopped`, the decision drops to `watch` (rules.md 10.3). That matches rule 53 ("the move is over for you") at the level of the engine's 2R exit.
- Projections: listed in rules.md 8.3 as confluence only and not computed, because the leg's start is chosen by eye. The teacher also calls them confluence (rule 38). Rule 40's size test (about half the range) is the only number for "large".
- Sizing and frequency: the engine does no position sizing, applies no trade-per-day cap and keeps no exposure state. `PositionPlan.cost_r_mini`/`cost_r_micro` and `risk_adr` are information only (rules.md 10.6). Rules 70-77 are outside the engine's scope (inference).

## Conflicts and open questions

- RISK-1: Break even is variously never used, used at 3R, used after a new protected swing, or used only when a return to entry invalidates; the engine has no BE.
- RISK-2: Stop buffer: "no extra room" (2026) versus "a little room to sweep" (2025), with no amount given; the engine uses 0.
- RISK-3: Body-stop test: the teacher uses 50% of the opposing candles, the engine a body of at least 60% of range.
- RISK-4: Fixed 2R versus holding for HTF targets, runners and time exits; the engine has a single 2R exit.
- RISK-5: Is 1:1 acceptable? He says "a 1:1 fractal model works" and also "I don't take 1:1 trades".
- RISK-6: "Large" manipulation leg: the only number is about half the range to the target.
- RISK-7: Projection anchor: the manipulation leg (2023), the CISD bodies (indicator 2024), or the Candle 2 leg.
- RISK-8: The risk-per-trade number ranges from 4% of drawdown to drawdown/15, with 1% and under-1% from guests.
- RISK-9: Where to trail: each protected swing, the 1m bar near the target, daily swings, or not at all.
- RISK-10: "Daily range made": recent expansion candles, ADR (range only), or by eye; the engine uses a 14-day mean.
- RISK-11: Skip after target hit versus "HTF target open overrides ADR".
- RISK-12: To reach 2R, should he move the entry, the stop or the target?
- RISK-13: Under 2R to the near level is fine for HTF targets, yet a continuation without 2R is skipped.
- RISK-14: Time exit: hold to the HTF close, or exit at the target and not beyond the candle?
- RISK-15: Stop hit by a wick trade-through or an idea killed by a close (H1 BPR close).
- RISK-16: Large-wick reversal target order: EQ first, or the daily open first.
- RISK-17: Stop must cover the EQ / 50% of the FVG / the order block's mean threshold; the engine has no such check.
- RISK-18: Consolidation highs cannot be trusted as stops; the engine has no such check.
- RISK-19: Partials: take them at 2R versus only if the remainder still meets the model versus no trims.
- RISK-20: Reversals with "very small stops" versus "he doesn't normally trade reversals" (speaker possibly Nick).
- RISK-21: The 2025 Timeframe-alignment chart shows R:R 1.50 while speech says 2R.
- RISK-22: The seed playbook's 50%-of-opposing-run body test and "let the wick form" come from 2026 pages not in this corpus.
- RISK-23: The trade count, one a day versus 0-4 a week, has no engine counterpart.
- RISK-24: "Take profit off structure, not fib extensions" (2022) versus projections as mechanical targets (2023 on).
- RISK-25: The "useful without structure aligning" remark in the 2023 projections video is unclear.
