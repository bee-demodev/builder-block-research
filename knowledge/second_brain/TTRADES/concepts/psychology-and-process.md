# Psychology, discipline, backtesting and daily process

"Guest" marks a rule said by a guest on the TTrades channel (Ash Trades, NickDoesFutures, Sniper, Ben, AP, Alex, Holyangelbruv, TFO, AM Trades), not by TTrades. This page is about how the method is run: when not to trade, how many trades, how to behave in a trade, how to learn and test the model, and the daily routine. Sizing and the exits themselves are in [[risk-and-management]].

## Definition

**2026 (current).** TTrades reduces psychology to one instruction: "Don't do dumb stuff". Most problems are behaviours the trader already knows are wrong (tilt, revenge trading, oversizing, not following the model, over-managing), and the fix is to stop them, "change or quit" ([[livestream_20260729_new_york_open_live_q_a_xraklbjhw5k]] 1:19:00; [[livestream_20261110_new_york_open_live_q_a_cp1nyqa_t5c]] 17:30). The process that enforces this:
- Max one trade a day, 2R or -1R, proper size and the model's rules, for at least a month ([[livestream_20260505_new_york_open_live_q_a_i2hhhhwdapq]] 47:20).
- Closure-only decisions, made live exactly as in backtesting ([[livestream_20260616_new_york_open_live_q_a_tjuolmjncmy]] 58:20).
- One idea per session, traded only through the fractal model ([[livestream_20260902_new_york_open_live_q_a_grc5fvb5tdg]] 48:30).
- Set and forget once in ([[livestream_20260803_new_york_open_live_q_a_nesscpmzwr0]] 26:40).
- Analysis always starts on the daily chart ([[livestream_20260610_new_york_open_live_q_a_w7fu3rx5ims]] 56:35).

He learns the model by **reverse engineering** moves in hindsight rather than bar-replay guessing, and no longer backtests or journals himself ([[livestream_20260916_new_york_open_live_q_a_9yvbe_20hdg]] 1:07:50; [[livestream_20260821_new_york_open_live_q_a_4rnc3qxxc20]] 50:40). Results are judged over 100+ trades, and a loss within the rules is not a bad trade ([[livestream_20260728_new_york_open_live_q_a_yngx9oaefui]] 10:30; [[livestream_20260721_new_york_open_live_q_a_m8xcjkoubhu]] 42:40).

**2022-2023 (ICT Q&A era).** Process meant one repeatable setup ([[livestream_20220807_stream_q_a_ict_concepts_h_xtsfk8np4]] 22:10), two to three good trades a day or none ([[livestream_20220731_stream_q_a_ict_concepts_qgirecy2nz4]] 34:50), demo before going live ([[livestream_20220810_stream_q_a_with_trade_for_opportunity_ict_concepts_0bh_kkg2q6s]] 1:06:00), and top-down replay backtesting with a journal ([[livestream_20230112_back_testing_live_session_fx_replay_8r6pxpeldiu]] 19:00). Guests pushed detailed journaling of mechanics and feelings ([[livestream_20220911_fractalizing_entries_q_a_with_ben_ict_concepts_yrmkkr4coju]] 26:20). Losses were thought of in series of about 15 trades ([[livestream_20230730_live_q_a_with_am_trades_weekly_profile_wgyde_h84cs]] 30:30).

**2024-2025 (guest lessons, TTFM).** The guest interviews (Ash Trades, NickDoesFutures) made one model, written plans and journals the core of profitability ([[ash_trades_the_journey_to_profitability_the_unicorn_model]] 27:57; [[nickdoesfutures_unicorn_model_experiences_with_ict]] 24:17). TTrades' own line was "pick one system and follow it; hopping between models destroys consistency" ([[the_only_trading_strategy_you_need_for_2026]] 00:17).

## Engine notes

- Closure discipline is built into the engine: `companion/types.py` forbids reading a bar that is not closed, and the forming bias/setup candles are synthesised from closed entry bars (companion `rules.md` 2.1). This matches rule 27. Inference: the engine reads the forming HTF candle's running extreme, which the teacher also does when he waits for the wick, so this is not a live-candle prediction.
- "No bias, no trade" is rule 3 of the engine's model page (companion `rules.md` 1). It matches rules 8 and 10. The engine has no "I don't know what's going on" state beyond `watch`, so chop and confusion (rules 11-12) map only to blocks such as "in a range".
- Set and forget: the exit is a fixed multiple of risk (2R default, 1R selectable; Q15) with no break-even. `cont_newer_protected_low` only reports, as advisory, that the stop could trail. Both fit rule 43 and stay neutral on rule 45 (PSY-5).
- Played out: after `target_reached` / `stopped` the decision becomes `watch` (Q33, 2026-11-13). This matches "once the target is hit, the idea is done" and "don't chase" (rules 25, 33).
- No daily trade cap, no "one idea per day", no daily-loss stop: the verdict is recomputed at every check and can propose the other side the same day. Rules 17, 21 and 23 are not encoded (teacher-vs-engine, PSY-1, PSY-6). Inference: a per-day "first trigger only" switch would encode rule 21 for backtests.
- The engine is mechanical: it reports every trigger that passes its gates, which is the "take every trade the model prints" reading (rule 14), not the "A+ setup on the cleanest asset only" reading (rule 15) (PSY-2).
- Data hygiene (rule 68): whether the companion's feeds are back-adjusted with settlement-as-close off was **not checked** here (inference: worth verifying, because daily C2/C3 closures depend on the true close).

## Conflicts and open questions

- PSY-1: Daily trade cap: one a day (2026) vs 2-3 a day (2022) vs none in the engine.
- PSY-2: Take every trade the model prints vs only the A+ setup on the cleanest asset.
- PSY-3: "Choppy" / "don't know what's going on" days have no definition the engine can test.
- PSY-4: How long to wait at a level: ~30 minutes vs "if it's going to go it goes right away".
- PSY-5: Set and forget (no trail, no BE) vs trail on pullbacks vs 2022 partials and break-even.
- PSY-6: One idea and one direction per day vs an engine that re-evaluates and can flip sides.
- PSY-7: Never flip the bias vs switch when price does not confirm / follow displacement.
- PSY-8: Daily loss cap: one or two losers vs one trade a day vs "a max daily loss" (no number).
- PSY-9: "Better late than early" vs "enter early in the HTF candle".
- PSY-10: Minimum sample to judge a model: 15 trades vs 100+ trades / 3-6 months.
- PSY-11: Replay backtesting (2022-2023) vs reverse engineering and "no longer backtests" (2026).
- PSY-12: Journal every trade vs "no longer needs it".
- PSY-13: Weekly outlook: none (day by day) vs weekly plan / Tuesday weekly read / monthly read.
- PSY-14: Start on the daily (2026) vs 1H/4H used as daily charts (2022).
- PSY-15: Stop after a win (guest Ash) / one trade after a streak vs take every model trade.
- PSY-16: Data settings (back-adjusted, settlement off, minis) not verified in the companion.
- PSY-17: Scalping useful for reps vs "don't trade the 1m" and "move up a timeframe".
- PSY-18: "Use what you see best / any ICT model works" vs "learn this one model".
