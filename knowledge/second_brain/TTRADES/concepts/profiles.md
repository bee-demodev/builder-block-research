# Weekly, daily and session profiles

## Definition

A profile is the order in which a higher-timeframe candle builds its open, high, low and close, read live on lower timeframes. A bullish candle is open-low-high-close (OLHC): it opens, makes its low (the wick) early, then trends up. A bearish candle is open-high-low-close (OHLC) ([[open_high_low_close_ohlc_olhc_ict_concepts]] 01:32; [[easy_daily_bias_mechanical_framework]] 06:41). In 2026 TTrades uses the **daily profile** as the second filter between the daily bias and the entry: bias, then profile, then entry ([[livestream_20260722_new_york_open_live_q_a_g1iidq3raky]] 36:30). He calls the daily profile "middle-timeframe structure" (the structure of the daily candle) and reads it on the 4H and 1H ([[livestream_20260401_new_york_open_live_q_a_wbwoimdxt2c]] 32:00; [[livestream_20261110_new_york_open_live_q_a_cp1nyqa_t5c]] 20:20). For a bullish day he wants price to "open, make some sort of low, then trade higher". A day that gaps up and expands without an established low is not bullish to him, even when the daily chart is ([[livestream_20260506_new_york_open_live_q_a_eipm_d8rrsq]] 24:30; [[livestream_20261028_new_york_open_live_q_a_c4ti6kdxn0s]] 24:10). He names a handful of daily profiles: London reversal into New York continuation (his A+), New York reversal, Asia reversal, the intraday reversal, and consolidation days he does not trade ([[livestream_20260317_new_york_open_live_q_a_00izxpadr5a]] 22:40; [[livestream_20260721_new_york_open_live_q_a_m8xcjkoubhu]] 11:40). The **weekly profile** is the same idea one level up. He calls it "a series of daily biases", not a weekly bias: a Tuesday reversal sets up a Wednesday continuation, and so on until an objective is hit ([[livestream_20260325_new_york_open_live_q_a_aqny1yzvfys]] 1:18:20). He reads it on the daily chart ([[livestream_20260803_new_york_open_live_q_a_nesscpmzwr0]] 07:50) and calls it an advanced add-on: the daily-hourly fractal model works without it ([[livestream_20261110_new_york_open_live_q_a_cp1nyqa_t5c]] 47:50). "Weekly profile applied to lower timeframes is my fractal model" ([[livestream_20260331_new_york_open_live_q_a_0aygnc9czyc]] 1:26:20).

**2024-2025.** The daily profile is taught as session templates. A London reversal is a classic expansion candle: London puts in the wick during 2-5 a.m., a CISD forms, and New York continues to complete the OHLC ([[ict_daily_profile_london_reversal_new_york_continuation]] 00:50, 01:34). A New York reversal is a day whose wick forms in New York because London never reached a relevant HTF PD array ([[ict_daily_profile_new_york_reversal]] 00:44, 00:57). The indicator video ties profiles to candle numbering: after the HTF closure and CISD, trade the next HTF candle's OHLC ([[intro_to_ttrades_fractal_model_indicator_settings]] 01:32). The 2025 lessons turn the profile into wick rules: a small wick early, then trend ([[higher_timeframe_analysis_for_trading_aligning_expansion_candles]] 00:43). Guests add their own profile systems: Jokerszn (expansion/retracement/consolidation), AFYZ (protraction), GxTradez (4H session chain) and DTR (7-hour candles).

**2022-2023.** ICT power of three (accumulation, manipulation, distribution) applied to the week and the day ([[open_high_low_close_ohlc_olhc_ict_concepts]] 04:43). Named ICT weekly templates appear: the classic Tuesday-low week and the classic sell week with a Tuesday high ([[next_day_model_the_mmxm_trader_ict_concepts]] 11:34; [[standard_deviation_projections_ict_concepts]] 09:49). Midnight-open daily profiles: short above the midnight open on a bearish day ([[ict_son_s_model_higher_time_frames]] 08:42). The narrative is framed off Asia and London session highs and lows ([[liquidity_buyside_sellside_ict_concepts]] 05:57). The guest AM Trades teaches the weekly profile as HTF PD array + day of week + 1H CISD ([[livestream_20230730_live_q_a_with_am_trades_weekly_profile_wgyde_h84cs]] 23:20).

## Engine notes

- The companion encodes no daily, weekly or session profile. rules.md 4.3 says the weekly profile is "not encoded; it is the same Candle 2 / Candle 3 / Candle 4 logic with a weekly bias", and Q26 lists daily profiles (London reversal, New York reversal, seek and destroy) and the weekly profile as defined in the source but not used. TTrades' own 2026 framing supports that reduction (rules 49, 62), but nothing in the engine does the second filter of rule 9.
- `Verdict.bias` is closure-based (rules.md 5, Q32). The teacher's 2026 bias on a reversal day follows the profile, not the daily trend (rule 25). On those days the engine and the teacher can disagree about direction (inference; see PROF-1).
- The engine reads the candle's wick size and timing, which is the core of the candle profile in rules 3-6. Small/large wick and "early in the candle" are proposals (`wick_small_frac`, `wick_time_frac_max`, `daily_wick_deadline = 11:00`, rules.md 11). Rule 11 ("open, a low, then a rally"; a gap and expansion without a low is not bullish) has no engine check.
- Session references are information only. The engine draws the Asia (18:00-00:00) and London (02:00-05:00) highs/lows and the midnight and 09:30 opens on intraday stacks (rules.md 10.6), and kill zones are advisory (9.1). The 7h, 4H-session and protraction templates (rules 64-73) are not modelled.
- The engine has no day-of-week logic and no expansion-day count (Monday rule, TGIF, Friday cap: rules 55-57). The nearest checks are `bias_candle_index_ok` (Candle 5+ invalidates) and the Candle 4 permission after a strong Candle 3 (rules.md 3.7, 9.2), which mirror rule 48 and the guest's "avoid the fourth day" (rule 69) on any bias timeframe.
- Rule 18 (the daily profile replaces the POI on a continuation) clashes with the engine's required `cont_poi_tagged` step (rules.md 6, c2). A POI-free continuation would stay at `watch` (PROF-5).
