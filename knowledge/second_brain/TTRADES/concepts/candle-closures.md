# Candle numbering (C1-C4), candle 2 and candle 3 closures, expansion and wick size

## Definition

**Latest (2025-2026).** Candles are numbered around a swing point: candle 2 is always the swing high or low, candle 1 is simply the candle before it (arbitrary, "don't spend effort identifying C1"), candle 3 follows, candle 4 follows candle 3 and is always a continuation if the idea is right ([[fractal_model_fundamentals]] 00:46; [[livestream_20260923_new_york_open_live_q_a_kbjaggkxdei]] 49:10). Only two closures define a swing he trades ([[livestream_20260505_new_york_open_live_q_a_i2hhhhwdapq]] 35:20; [[candle_3_closure_ttrades_fractal_model]] 10:31). A **candle 2 closure (C2)** is a reversal closure at a point of interest: candle 2 sweeps candle 1's low (high) and closes back inside candle 1's range ([[candle_2_closure_foundation]] 00:57-01:12). A **candle 3 closure (C3)** is used when price reached the level without a C2: candle 3 leaves candle 2's extreme untaken and closes over (below) candle 2's body, an engulf, and candle 4 is expected to expand ([[candle_3_closure_ttrades_fractal_model]] 01:36-01:55); the 2026 wording puts the line at candle 2's opening price ([[cisd_validates_swing_points]] 02:22). A closure is only valid with a lower-timeframe CISD inside that candle ([[cisd_validates_swing_points]] 00:54). Candles are read by wick: an **expansion candle** has small wicks, a large body, forms its high/low early using little time or range and closes near its extreme; a **reversal candle** has a large opposing run that becomes its wick and returns toward its open ([[livestream_20261104_new_york_open_live_q_a_j8u9ch8gkdc]] 24:20; [[ttrades_fractal_model_trading_candle_2]] 00:23-00:34). The model trades candle 3 and candle 4, sometimes candle 2 with a small wick, never candle 5 by default ([[livestream_20261104_new_york_open_live_q_a_j8u9ch8gkdc]] 1:09:30). "Let the wick form, trade the body" is the working rule ([[livestream_20251007_candle_2_confirmations_live_lesson_q_a_uzpgxvypvgc]] 54:50).

**2024 (indicator launch, wick video).** The indicator labels C2 / C3 / C4 but the requirements are "taught in the course" ([[intro_to_ttrades_fractal_model_indicator_settings]] 06:16); the wick video teaches the wick 0.5 and respected vs disrespected wicks ([[master_candlestick_wicks_the_key_to_catching_reversals]] 00:47-02:13). The Sunday streams already say: let the reversal day form, trade the continuation the next day ([[livestream_20241006_ttrades_sunday_session_live_q_a_r7yw6ou1lds]] 21:00).

**2023 (ICT framing).** No candle numbers: a bullish candle "must move below the open to put in its low" (OLHC) and the open should then support price ([[open_high_low_close_ohlc_olhc_ict_concepts]] 00:03, 03:38); an inside bar defaults to continuation and an outside bar leans to the close direction ([[intraday_bias_simplified_ict_concepts]] 03:28; [[important_liquidity_levels_draw_on_liquidity_ict]] 05:32).

**Course change (explicit).** His first course version counted a C3 when price disrespected the EQ; he tightened it because people were confused, so the current C3 needs the stricter close ([[livestream_20260313_new_york_open_live_q_a_bmkromkeunu]] 22:00).

## Engine notes

- Numbering (engine 3.3) and the four closure types (3.4: reversal, continuation, inside bar, outside bar closing inside) follow rules 1 and 7 ([[fractal_model_fundamentals]] 00:46; [[candle_2_closure_foundation]] 00:57). The engine treats inside bars and outside bars as `neutral` (5.2 cases E, F); the 2023 teacher defaults an inside bar to trend and an outside bar to the close direction (rules 9, 11), and the 2026 teacher waits for a new sweep (rule 10) (inference: the engine matches 2026).
- C2 (engine 3.5) = reversal closure + POI + CISD, with body alignment as a quality check (`close > open`), matching rules 13-14, 20 and 25. The engine does not test reversal-candle shape (rule 19), sweep depth (rule 22), gap opens (rule 23) or SMT-swept C2s (rule 27) (inference).
- C3 (engine 3.6) uses the far body edge (`max(open, close)` bullish), which satisfies both "over the body" (rule 31) and "beyond the opening price" (rule 32); engine open question 6.
- "Strong candle 3" permission for C4 (engine 3.7) is `close[C3] > high[C2]`, i.e. rule 35; the guest's "over the body of C2" (GXT 04:54) is not used (inference).
- Wick size (engine 3.8): opposing run from the open, small <= 25% / large >= 50% of the 20-candle average range plus an "early" test; the trader accepted this on 2026-11-12 (engine open question 2; no TD id). The teacher gives no number (rules 51-55); the nearest are "about half the time" and EQ as the filter (inference).
- Only C3/C4 continuations are traded (engine 1.7, case D flagged `late`), matching rules 42-43; the 2026 "C5 if an objective remains" (rule 44) is not encoded (inference).
- The engine reads only closed bars (2.1), so "early C2" entries (rule 68) and entries inside the reversal candle (rule 57, small-wick C2) are not possible except via the reversal path (engine 7) (inference).
