# Ranges, EQ, premium / discount, ADR and the phases of price

## Definition

**EQ (equilibrium), 2025-2026.** EQ is the 0.5 of a candle or range, measured from wick high to wick low, never bodies; his fib carries only the 0.5 level ([[equilibrium_for_expansions]] 00:29-00:52; [[livestream_20260325_new_york_open_live_q_a_aqny1yzvfys]] 1:10:30). He uses it *opposite to ICT*: in an expansion the upper half of the previous candle must hold for price to trade higher and the lower half for lower, because expansions rarely retrace into discount or premium ([[equilibrium_for_expansions]] 00:55-01:34; [[livestream_20260821_new_york_open_live_q_a_4rnc3qxxc20]] 1:07:20). In 2026 the EQ is a mechanical wick-size test: if the next candle respects the previous candle's EQ the wick is small enough to expand; a close through it is a *soft* invalidation that keeps the bias but cuts targets back to the open or intraday liquidity ([[livestream_20261006_new_york_open_live_q_a_rtomj8urfnw]] 42:00; [[livestream_20260821_new_york_open_live_q_a_4rnc3qxxc20]] 1:01:10). For a candle with a large wick he halves the wick, not the candle ([[master_candlestick_wicks_the_key_to_catching_reversals]] 00:47; [[livestream_20260727_new_york_open_live_q_a_je7cd9hjube]] 1:01:40).

**Phases of price (2024-2026).** Price alternates four phases: expansion (one-sided, large range, shallow counter-moves), retracement (slow, shallow, small-range move against the trend that resolves with a close back through), consolidation (price held inside a high and low for a period; a large range turning into a small one) and reversal (expansion met with opposing expansion) ([[how_to_trade_expansion_phases_of_price]] 00:27; [[how_to_trade_retracement_phases_of_price]] 0:44; [[how_to_trade_consolidation_phases_of_price]] 00:25; [[how_to_trade_reversal_phases_of_price]] 00:33). He trades expansions; after about three expansion candles into an objective he expects a new phase.

**ADR (2025-2026).** The average daily range is read by eye from recent daily candles, not from an indicator, and is used as a "rope": how much range and time were spent forming the wick decides how much expansion is left ([[livestream_20261007_new_york_open_live_q_a_mflp3acpzc0]] 25:50; [[livestream_20260825_new_york_open_live_q_a_9h15zzvakpq]] 1:02:20).

**2022 (ICT-concepts era).** Premium / discount was classic ICT: draw a fib over the dealing range (nearest high and low containing price) or the displacement range (low that started the displacement to its high); long only in discount, short only in premium, OTE at 0.62-0.79 ([[choosing_dealing_ranges_displacement_ranges_ict_concepts]] 00:05-04:16; [[livestream_20220731_stream_q_a_ict_concepts_qgirecy2nz4]] 47:30).

**2023-2024.** Same split, now "the most prominent range", re-anchored with each new swing; the 0.5 is called EQ; OTE refined to 0.62-0.79 with 0.705 as its midpoint ([[discount_premium_ict_concepts]] 00:09-03:00; [[optimal_trade_entry_ote_ict_concepts]] 01:05). Discount/premium already softened to "raises probability, not required" ([[livestream_20230112_back_testing_live_session_fx_replay_8r6pxpeldiu]] 27:00).

**2026 dealing range.** The dealing range survives as a separate, larger range for entry location and R:R: measured from the extreme you trade from to the target, short in its upper half, long in its lower half ([[livestream_20260505_new_york_open_live_q_a_i2hhhhwdapq]] 30:40; [[livestream_20261111_new_york_open_live_q_a_mk9qoez3qzi]] 20:50). He has dropped OTE ([[livestream_20260728_new_york_open_live_q_a_yngx9oaefui]] 1:10:20).

## Engine notes

- **EQ formula.** `rules.md` 3.9 computes `EQ = (B[-1].high + B[-1].low) / 2`, wick to wick, and uses the upper half for longs, matching rules 1 and 11. It explicitly rejects an older rulepack that used the lower half for longs (inference: that rulepack followed the 2022-2023 ICT reading, rules 38-43).
- **Which midpoint.** The engine's `respect_level` picks the wick midpoint when `B[-1]` is a C2 closure whose closed wick is at least `closed_wick_large_frac` (0.5) of its range, else the whole-candle EQ (`rules.md` 3.9, open question 4). The teacher's split is "large wick → wick, expansion candle → whole candle" (rule 4) with the `trading_candle_3` exception (rule 6); the 50% threshold is the engine's proposal, not his. See RNG-1.
- **Respect test.** `cont_eq_respected` is a **gate**: any setup-timeframe close beyond `respect_level` (tolerance `eq_tolerance_frac = 0`) fails the continuation (`rules.md` stage c2). The 2026 teacher treats a close through daily EQ as a soft invalidation that keeps the bias and cuts targets (rule 29), and allows taps and shallow wicks (rules 15-17). The engine is stricter than the 2026 teacher (RNG-2, RNG-3).
- **Reversal target.** The reversal path's structural S1 is the EQ of `B[-1]`, S2 its far side (`rules.md` 8.3), matching rule 34. Exits default to an R multiple, so EQ is information, not the exit.
- **ADR.** The only ADR in the engine is `stop_size_ok`: mean of 14 closed daily ranges, stop at least 4% of it, advisory (`rules.md` 8.2, question 38). The teacher's ADR uses (range left vs draw, "done once ADR is made", gap excluded, rules 55-63) are not implemented; open question 36 already points at this gap (RNG-9, RNG-10).
- **Consolidation.** The engine recognises a consolidation only as a slow entry CISD (`cisd_max_bars` = 3 → `cont_not_inside_consolidation`, `cont_trigger_v_shape`; `rules.md` stage c4), which matches rule 83. Inside bars, double inside days, failed retracements and ranges between protected swings (rules 76-78) are not detected (RNG-15).
- **Phase count.** The engine trades only C3/C4 continuations, so the "3 expansion candles then new phase" rule (69) never binds separately (`rules.md` 5.4). No dealing-range location check exists (rules 47-49); the 2R reward check is the nearest proxy (inference).
