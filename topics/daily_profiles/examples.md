# Daily Profiles Examples

## London Reversal -> New York Continuation
- London session creates the wick of the daily candle by sweeping against the final direction.
- London prints the first CISD and establishes the intraday structural pivot.
- New York session continues from the shift rather than rebuilding the narrative from scratch.
- The best continuation comes when London did not already finish the day's expansion.

## New York Manipulation
- London stays mostly consolidative and leaves range liquidity intact.
- Around 8:30 or 9:30 EST, New York sweeps the active day's range high or low.
- A close through the opposing candle series confirms the directional delivery.
- Entry refines from the post-sweep anchor and targets previous-day liquidity.

## New York Reversal
- London leans toward a higher-timeframe level but fails to reach it.
- New York completes the run into that level and then shifts through CISD.
- The post-CISD swing becomes the structural invalidation for the reversal.
- The profile is strongest when the missed higher-timeframe level is obvious before New York opens.

## Seek and Destroy
- London sweeps both sides of Asia and never produces a clean hold.
- New York keeps rotating across the same range instead of extending away from it.
- Equilibrium and daily open behave like magnets.
- The profile is treated as a stand-aside or fade-only condition, not as a directional expansion setup.
