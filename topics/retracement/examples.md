# Retracement Examples

## Slow Pullback Into Fair Value Gap
- Price expands strongly in the direction of the higher-timeframe bias.
- The countertrend move overlaps candle-to-candle instead of displacing like a new expansion.
- The pullback reaches a fair value gap and the prior protected swing remains intact.
- A new with-trend protected swing confirms retracement completion and the trend resumes.

## Deep Pullback That Fails the Trend
- A pullback starts after expansion, but the countertrend move accelerates instead of staying slow.
- Price trades through the aligned point of interest and breaks the protected swing that defined the prior trend.
- The move is no longer treated as a normal retracement.
- The next classification should be consolidation or reversal risk rather than continuation.

## Retracement Skipped Entirely
- Expansion remains strong and price never offers a clean pullback into a valid area.
- No confirmed retracement forms before price reaches the next external objective.
- The model stands aside rather than forcing a late entry.
- A later consolidation or new expansion leg becomes the next tradable event.
