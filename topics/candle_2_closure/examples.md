# Candle 2 Closure Examples

## Bullish Candle 2 Reversal
- Price sweeps the previous candle low at a higher-timeframe point of interest.
- Candle 2 closes back above that low and inside the previous candle range.
- The closure marks the swing turn instead of a continuation breakdown.
- Candle 3 is expected to continue higher from that reversal.

## Bearish Candle 2 Reversal
- Price sweeps the previous candle high into a key level.
- Candle 2 closes back below that high and inside the prior range.
- The reversal closure becomes the anchor for lower-timeframe confirmation.
- The sequence stays valid only if Candle 3 follows through lower.

## Large-Wick Candle 2 Filter
- Candle 2 forms a clear rejection wick at the point of interest.
- The midpoint of that wick is marked as the integrity filter.
- Candle 3 must respect that midpoint for the swing to remain valid.
- If midpoint respect fails, the reversal is downgraded or rejected.
