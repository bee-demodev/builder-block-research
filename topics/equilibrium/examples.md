# Equilibrium Examples

## Wick-To-Wick Measurement

- Use the previous day or a significant higher-timeframe candle as the reference range.
- Measure wick high to wick low.
- Ignore candle bodies when computing the midpoint.

## Bullish Continuation Filter

- Mark the midpoint of the reference range.
- Keep the bullish idea active while price respects the upper half.
- Only promote the setup once a point of interest inside that half reacts with lower-timeframe closure and CISD.

## Bearish Continuation Filter

- Mark the midpoint of the reference range.
- Keep the bearish idea active while price respects the lower half.
- Reassess bias if lower-timeframe closes reclaim the wrong side of equilibrium.
