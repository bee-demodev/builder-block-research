# Expansion Examples

## Source-backed examples

### Example A
- Source: `Phases of Price - Part 2`
- Context: daily reversal leading to hourly expansion
- Features:
  - higher timeframe reversal
  - lower timeframe expansion
  - series of protected swings respected
  - alternation between large-range bars and small pullbacks

### Example B
- Source: `Aligning Candles for Expansion`
- Context: multi-timeframe candle alignment
- Features:
  - higher timeframe candle supports expansion
  - lower timeframe candle aligns in same direction
  - small opposing wicks
  - continuation opportunity after uncertainty resolves

### Example C
- Source: `Top-Down Analysis`
- Context: higher timeframe bias, intermediate structure, lower timeframe entry
- Features:
  - higher timeframe directional thesis
  - intermediate CISD / structure confirmation
  - lower timeframe protected swing entry

## Current machine-learning style takeaway
- Expansion should be modeled as a state with:
  - entry conditions
  - continuation conditions
  - exhaustion guards
  - invalidation conditions

It should not be modeled as a single candle pattern.
