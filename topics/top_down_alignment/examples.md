# Top-Down Alignment Examples

## Higher To Lower Workflow

- Start with the higher timeframe and set expansion higher, expansion lower, or consolidation.
- Drop to the intermediate timeframe and wait for structure or point-of-interest confirmation.
- Use the lower timeframe only after the first two layers agree.

## Clean Multi-Timeframe Trend

- Require at least two timeframes to print expansion-supporting candles in the same direction.
- Treat three aligned timeframes as the ideal trend stack.
- Expect cleaner continuation and better protected swings when the stack is aligned.

## Misalignment Filter

- Ignore lower-timeframe bullish setups when the higher timeframe is still expanding lower.
- Ignore lower-timeframe continuation ideas when the intermediate timeframe never confirmed at the point of interest.
- Downgrade the setup whenever a parent candle prints a large opposing wick and stops supporting expansion.
