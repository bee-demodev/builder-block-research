# Wick Logic Examples

## Expansion-Supporting Candle

- Measure the body and the wick opposite the candle direction.
- Keep the expansion read when the opposing wick is small and the close finishes near the directional extreme.
- Favor continuation targets beyond highs or lows rather than the opening price.

## Reversion Candle

- Mark candles where the opposing wick dominates the anatomy and the close drifts back toward the open.
- Treat those as poor immediate expansion candidates.
- Favor open-price or nearby-liquidity targets unless the next candle resets the structure.

## Wick Midpoint Hold-Fail Filter

- Mark 50 percent of the wick from the body edge to the wick extreme.
- If price respects the midpoint, keep the reversal read.
- If price closes through the midpoint, treat the wick as disrespected and drop the reversal thesis.
