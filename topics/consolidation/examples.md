# Consolidation Examples

## Sweep Low -> Confirm Higher
- Price compresses between a defined high and low after expansion.
- One side of the range is swept and price closes through the opposing candle series.
- A new protected swing forms away from the manipulated side.
- Entry is taken after confirmation, targeting liquidity beyond the opposite side.

## Clean Breakout -> Retest Continuation
- The market spends multiple rotations inside a stable range.
- Price breaks the range in the direction of bias and does not close back inside.
- A retest holds outside the range and the first new protected swing forms.
- The continuation is traded away from the range with the broken boundary as the key reference.

## Seek-and-Destroy Whipsaw
- London or another early session sweeps both sides of a session range without direction.
- Price keeps rotating back toward equilibrium and the daily open.
- No directional protected swing forms.
- The right move is to stand aside or only treat false breakouts as fades back into the middle.
