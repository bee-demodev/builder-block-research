# Protected Swings Examples

## Example A - Reversal to bearish trend
- Price sweeps a previous high.
- First attempt does not confirm because the close is insufficient.
- A later sweep closes below the up-close candle series.
- Protected high is confirmed.
- Retracements into key levels become sell candidates with invalidation above the protected high.

## Example B - Continuation protected highs
- Once a protected high exists, later reactions into important levels can form additional protected highs.
- Those newer swings become stepping stones for continuation entries and trailing invalidation.

## Example C - Lower timeframe refinement
- Hourly protected swing sets the directional framework.
- Lower timeframe waits for a smaller protected swing or CISD.
- Entry is refined only after the higher timeframe level is retested.
