# Continuation Examples

## Equilibrium Hold -> Trend Resumption
- Higher timeframe bias is already established in one direction.
- Price retraces into the respected half of a recent range and reacts near equilibrium.
- Lower timeframe closes back through the opposing candle sequence quickly.
- The first protected swing in the trend direction becomes the structural invalidation point.

## Sweep -> Continuation Anchor Retest
- A pullback sweeps short-term liquidity against the active bias.
- The reaction displaces back with trend and confirms the new swing.
- Entry is taken on the retest of the validated imbalance, order-block proxy, or breaker-style anchor.
- The initial objective is the next external liquidity pool in the direction of the move.

## Continuation Failure -> Stand Aside
- Price reacts from an anchor but rotates across the local range instead of expanding.
- The recovery takes too many candles and fails to protect the new swing quickly.
- A higher-timeframe objective has already been met, so continuation quality is downgraded.
- The model waits for the next clean post-sweep continuation instead of forcing an entry.
